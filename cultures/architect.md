# Architect culture

- Culture affinity: none (comes from subculture)
- DLC: Base

They commit themselves to build their own wonders, aspiring to serve a greater purpose.

- Has higher Production income.
- Collect Wonderstone to build impressive Monument to the Affinity.
- Monument grant Affinity incarnate unit property to Architect Units.
- Architect culture units deal damage matching the Empire's dominant Affinity.

A people dedicated to the creation of something greater than themselves, building Monuments to their chosen Affinities.

Culture tome: [Architect](../tomes/architect.md)

Culture buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Affinity Obelisks (unique building), Architect Institute (replaces Arcane Institute), Magitectural Archive (replaces Library), Town Hall II: Great Ziggurat (replaces Town Hall II), Town Hall III: Ascended Gardens (replaces Town Hall III), Town Hall IV: Pinnacle of the Incarnate (replaces Town Hall IV)
