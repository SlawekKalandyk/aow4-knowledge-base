# Dark culture

- Culture affinity: Shadow 1
- DLC: Base

They pursue power and often turn to dark and forbidden magic, caring little about the well-being of their people.

- Aggressive units that focus on damage over defense and healing.
- Exploit defeated enemy heroes, depending on the chosen subculture.

A dictatorial civilization where the strong and ruthless dominate the weak. They use forbidden magics to maintain control and weaken their enemies.

Culture buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Town Hall II: Dread Spire (replaces Town Hall II), Town Hall III: Stronghold (replaces Town Hall III), Town Hall IV: Black Palace (replaces Town Hall IV)

## Subculture: Cult of Death

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Shadow 1, Nature 1
- DLC: Base
- Subculture tome: [Dark - Cult of Death](../tomes/dark-cult-of-death.md)
- Units: [Immortal](../units/immortal.md), [Outrider](../units/outrider.md), [Pursuer](../units/pursuer.md), [Death Guard](../units/death-guard.md), [Mortalist](../units/mortalist.md), [Death Stalker](../units/death-stalker.md)
- Buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Crypt-Temple (replaces Monolith), Grave Obelisk (replaces Mana Obelisk), Soulvault (unique building)

Your people are familiar with death, sacrifice is the norm and a tool for their overlord to use:

- Units get power when friendly or hostile units are slain through Power from Death.
- Heroes in your Crypt can be turned into Embalmed Sacrifices.
- Use Embalmed Sacrifices to build powerful City Structures or cast Morbid sacrifice.

## Subculture: Cult of Tyranny

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Shadow 2
- DLC: Base
- Subculture tome: [Dark - Cult of Tyranny](../tomes/dark-cult-of-tyranny.md)
- Units: [Immortal](../units/immortal.md), [Outrider](../units/outrider.md), [Pursuer](../units/pursuer.md), [Tyrant Guard](../units/tyrant-guard.md), [Warlock](../units/warlock.md), [Tyrant Knight](../units/tyrant-knight.md)
- Buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Collector (replaces Market), Overlord's Tower (unique building), Shadowtrade Quarter (replaces Mint)

Your people value strength, tyranny is a virtue and privilege for those who prove their might:

- Units weaken enemy Status Resistance through Aura of tyranny and Exploit vulnerability.
- Capture heroes to control Free City through Hostage Diplomacy.
- -1 Whispering Stones.
- Gain City Stability when executing heroes in your Prison.
- Unique City structures provide greatly increased Gold at the cost of City Stability.
