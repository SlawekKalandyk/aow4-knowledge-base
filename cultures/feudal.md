# Feudal culture

- Culture affinity: Order 1
- DLC: Base

They hold honor and valor in high regard and fight to uphold their laws wherever they go.

- Low Unit Upkeep.
- Powerful cavalry units.

A hierarchical society where nobles reign over their subjects. They field exceptionally cheap to maintain armies and specialize in the use of cavalry.

Culture buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Town Hall II: Castle (replaces Town Hall II), Town Hall III: Citadel (replaces Town Hall III), Town Hall IV: Grand Estate (replaces Town Hall IV)

## Subculture: Monarchy

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Order 1, Materium 1
- DLC: Base
- Subculture tome: [Feudal - Monarchy](../tomes/feudal-monarchy.md)
- Units: [Archer](../units/archer.md), [Defender](../units/defender.md), [Militia](../units/militia.md), [Scout](../units/scout.md), [Aspirant Knight](../units/aspirant-knight.md), [Bannerman](../units/bannerman.md), [Longbow](../units/longbow.md), [Knight](../units/knight.md)
- Buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Militia Barracks (replaces Workshop), Retainer's Estate (unique building), Royal Smith (replaces Blacksmith)

Your people are loyal to the absolute rule of their Monarch, gaining power from the presence of their ruler:

- The Ruler gains Feudal monarchs dominion.
- Units gain For the Monarch.
- Certain units can promote into higher Tier units on attaining Legendary Rank.

## Subculture: Aristocracy

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Order 1, Nature 1
- DLC: Base
- Subculture tome: [Feudal - Aristocracy](../tomes/feudal-aristocracy.md)
- Units: [Archer](../units/archer.md), [Defender](../units/defender.md), [Militia](../units/militia.md), [Scout](../units/scout.md), [Aspirant Knight](../units/aspirant-knight.md), [Bannerman](../units/bannerman.md), [Liege Guard](../units/liege-guard.md), [Knight](../units/knight.md)
- Buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): House Hunting Grounds (replaces Store House), Lord's Manor (unique building), Serf Quarters (replaces Granary)

Your cities form powerful Houses, able to field efficient armies when led by their Liege Lords:

- Each city has a Feudal House led by its governor, who becomes the House's Liege lord.
- Units produced in Feudal cities become part of that city's Feudal House, gaining Experience as their Liege Lord gains Renown.
- Certain units can promote into higher Tier units on attaining Legendary Rank.
