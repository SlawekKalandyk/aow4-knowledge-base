# High culture

- Culture affinity: Order 2
- DLC: Base

They take great pride in their refined traditions and wish for others to reach similar heights.

- Has higher City Stability and Knowledge income.
- Units are strengthened with Awakened, revealing their hidden potential.
- Alignment Agenda.

A highly developed society whose members strive for harmony. When threatened, however, they wield their guiding light as a weapon.
