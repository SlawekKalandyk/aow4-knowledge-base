# Industrious culture

- Culture affinity: Materium 2
- DLC: Base

They have respect for smiths and other artisans, preferring a strong wall over a decorated palace.

- Has higher Production income.
- Through Bolstering, units get sturdier as they get hit in battle.
- Scout Prospecting.

Master builders who value grand cities and strong armor, preferring a defensive attitude over an aggressive one.
