# Mercantile culture

- Culture affinity: Shadow 1
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

They believe that gold lords over all and will trade with nearly anyone to maximize their profit.

A culture driven by commerce and profit, commanded by savvy entrepreneurs. Their cunning and resourcefulness allows them to found cities on water.
