# Mercantile culture

- Culture affinity: Shadow 1
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

They believe that gold lords over all and will trade with nearly anyone to maximize their profit.

A culture driven by commerce and profit, commanded by savvy entrepreneurs. Their cunning and resourcefulness allows them to found cities on water.

Culture buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Fortified Harbor (replaces Palisade Walls), Town Hall II: Merchants Court (replaces Town Hall II), Town Hall III: Guild Hall (replaces Town Hall III), Town Hall IV: Trade Palace (replaces Town Hall IV)
