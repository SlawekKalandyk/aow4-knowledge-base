# Nomad culture

- Culture affinity: Chaos 1
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

They are prone to wandering, either to conquer the lands beyond the horizon or to extract potent essences from magical sources.

- Mobile units that focus on maneuvering and flanking.
- Nomad Cities are able to pack up into a Nomad City Barge and relocate.
- Nodes in domain of their Cities are Overharvested over time, exhausting the Nodes to gain Essence in that city.
- Nomad Cities require 50% more Food to gain Population.

A resourceful people that migrate to thrive. They steer their land barges across the realm to settle in new lands.

Culture buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Sentry Nests (replaces Stone Walls), Town Hall II: Council Deck (replaces Town Hall II), Town Hall III: Cartography Bilge (replaces Town Hall III), Town Hall IV: Pinnacle Bridge (replaces Town Hall IV)

To your people, survival is an eternal conflict, one they excel at:

- Annex pillaged enemy provinces to gain Draft, Production, Knowledge, and Gold through Aggressive Expansion.
- Pillaging provinces provides Food.
- Movement grants units Empowering Momentum.
- Total War

## Subculture: Scavengers

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Chaos 1, Materium 1
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN
- Subculture tome: [Nomad - Scavengers](../tomes/nomad-scavengers.md)
- Units: [Dunerider](../units/dunerider.md), [Looter](../units/looter.md), [Wind Warrior](../units/wind-warrior.md), [Dustweaver](../units/dustweaver.md), [Strider](../units/strider.md), [Collector](../units/collector.md)
- Buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Loot Armory (replaces Blacksmith), Salvage Shop (replaces Workshop), Scavenger Vault (unique building)

Survival is measured in what can be scavenged from the ruined world, your people know this well:

- Use Wonder Scavenging to extract unique Essence from Ancient Wonder, further empowering your cities as they collect Essence.
- In combat, pickup loot from defeated units to heal and gain Looted Power.
