# Oathsworn culture

- Culture affinity: Order 1
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Driven by a strong sense of devotion, they proudly follow the rules of their oaths.

- Has higher Draft income.
- Swear Devotion to an oath that guides their people.

A society that functions through a strong sense of devotion, rewarding its rulers for their dedication to their chosen oath.

## Subculture: Oath of Strife

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Order 1, Chaos 1
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Units: [Honor Blade](../units/honor-blade.md), [Sworn Guard](../units/sworn-guard.md), [Wayfarer](../units/wayfarer.md), [Sealbearer](../units/sealbearer.md), [Vowkeeper](../units/vowkeeper.md), [Warbound](../units/warbound.md)

Your people follow a path of Strife, seeking to forge their strength in the fires of war:

- Gain grievances against Ruler with an overall better ranking.
- Razing City and Pillaging Province takes -1 Turn.
- Razing City and Pillaging Province yields Imperium.
- Culture units have Warriors soul.
- Become stronger as you gain Devotion to the Oath of Strife.

## Subculture: Oath of Righteousness

- Units: [Honor Blade](../units/honor-blade.md), [Sworn Guard](../units/sworn-guard.md), [Wayfarer](../units/wayfarer.md), [Sealbearer](../units/sealbearer.md), [Vowkeeper](../units/vowkeeper.md), [Avenger](../units/avenger.md)

Your people follow a path of Righteousness, working to be a positive force for good upon the world:

- Negative alignment gained is doubled.
- Culture units have Nobility.
- Become stronger as you gain Devotion to the Oath of Righteousness.

## Subculture: Oath of Harmony

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Order 1, Nature 1
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Units: [Honor Blade](../units/honor-blade.md), [Sworn Guard](../units/sworn-guard.md), [Wayfarer](../units/wayfarer.md), [Sealbearer](../units/sealbearer.md), [Vowkeeper](../units/vowkeeper.md), [Peacebringer](../units/peacebringer.md)

Your people follow a path of Harmony, seeking cooperation and to clear the world of threats:

- Gain +100 relations with Free City and Ruler.
- When vassalizing a conquered city, they start at Vassalage phase instead of at Tributary.
- Culture units have Harmonize.
- Become stronger as you gain Devotion to the Oath of Harmony.
