# Oathsworn culture

- Culture affinity: Order 1
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Driven by a strong sense of devotion, they proudly follow the rules of their oaths.

- Has higher Draft income.
- Swear Devotion to an oath that guides their people.

A society that functions through a strong sense of devotion, rewarding its rulers for their dedication to their chosen oath.

Culture buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Hall of Devotion (replaces Market), Paragon Quarters (unique building), Town Hall II: Oath Square (replaces Town Hall II), Town Hall III: Steel Bastion (replaces Town Hall III), Town Hall IV: Palace of Vows (replaces Town Hall IV), Training Hall (replaces Vendor)

## Subculture: Oath of Strife

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Order 1, Chaos 1
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Units: [Honor Blade](../units/honor-blade.md), [Sworn Guard](../units/sworn-guard.md), [Wayfarer](../units/wayfarer.md), [Sealbearer](../units/sealbearer.md), [Vowkeeper](../units/vowkeeper.md), [Warbound](../units/warbound.md)
- Can't take society traits: Fabled Hunters, Scions of Evil

Your people follow a path of Strife, seeking to forge their strength in the fires of war:

- Gain grievances against Rulers with an overall better ranking.
- Razing Cities and Pillaging Provinces takes -1 Turn.
- Razing Cities and Pillaging Provinces yields Imperium.
- Culture units have Warrior's Soul.
- Become stronger as you gain Devotion to the Oath of Strife.
