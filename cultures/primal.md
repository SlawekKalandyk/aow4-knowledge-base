# Primal culture

- Culture affinity: Nature 1
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

They worship powerful Animal Spirits and aim to shape the realms in their honor.

A people who worship powerful Animal Spirits and channel their Fury during battle. They receive blessings by altering their environment.

## Subculture: Mire Crocodile

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Nature 2
- DLC: PRIMALFURY
- Subculture tome: [Primal - Mire Crocodile](../tomes/primal-mire-crocodile.md)
- Units: [Primal Darter](../units/primal-darter.md), [Protector](../units/protector.md), [Spirit Tracker](../units/spirit-tracker.md), [Animist](../units/animist.md), [Primal Charger](../units/primal-charger.md), [Ancestral Warden](../units/ancestral-warden.md)

Your people worship the Mire Crocodile, bestowing benefits to your culture:

- Start in the Swamp.
- Swamp provinces are Favored Terrain and gain:

- +3 Food

- Access to the Primal - Mire Crocodile summon
- Units have:

- Disease immunity
- Swamp Walk
- Primal fury mod group crocodile
