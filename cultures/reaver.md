# Reaver culture

- Culture affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

They are opportunistic and materialistic, using old arcane technologies in any way that suits them.

A technologically advanced civilization where progress is everything. They use their advanced weaponry to conquer realms through Diplomacy or Conquest.

Culture buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Lodestone Foundry (replaces Mana Obelisk), Mana Forge (replaces Shrine), Town Hall II: Steel Hall (replaces Town Hall II), Town Hall III: Chained Spire (replaces Town Hall III), Town Hall IV: Ironclad Citadel (replaces Town Hall IV)

## Subculture: Federated

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Materium 1, Order 1
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Subculture tome: [Reaver - Federated](../tomes/reaver-federated.md)
- Units: [Mercenary](../units/mercenary.md), [Observer](../units/observer.md), [Pistoleer](../units/pistoleer.md), [Magelock](../units/magelock.md), [Sapper](../units/sapper.md), [Breacher](../units/breacher.md), [Magelock Cannon](../units/magelock-cannon.md)
- Buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Extraction Nexus (unique building)

Your people believe that all nations are stronger together; through strength and diplomacy, all shall share in the prosperity of their Federation:

- Gain Favors from defeating Marauders, destroying Infestations, and maintaining Supreme Vassalage with Free Cities. Use Favors to improve the Allegiance of your Vassals, rush War Coordination, trade with Free Cities, and recruit from Rally of the Lieges.
- Units recruited from Rally of the Lieges gain Federate Levy.
- Your units gain Federate Soldier.

## Subculture: Imperial

- Starting affinity (culture + subculture): Materium 1, Chaos 1
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Subculture tome: [Reaver - Imperial](../tomes/reaver-imperial.md)
- Units: [Harrier](../units/harrier.md), [Mercenary](../units/mercenary.md), [Observer](../units/observer.md), [Magelock](../units/magelock.md), [Overseer](../units/overseer.md), [Dragoon](../units/dragoon.md), [Magelock Cannon](../units/magelock-cannon.md)
- Buildings (aow4db site's hand-made culture map, not game data; details in [structures](../empire/structures.md)): Imperial Academy (unique building)

Your people know themselves to be the pinnacle of civilization, and thus all lesser domains are theirs to conquer:

- Gain War Spoils from fighting, raiding, and conquering Free Cities and Empires.
- Use War Spoils to empower your Cities.
- No Imperium income penalties from War without War Justification.
- Ranged units are specialized in focusing down targets Marked by Melee units through Focused Aggression.
