## Astral Empire Skill

- **Mana Infrastructure** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Mana City Structures cost -25% Gold and Production.
- **Supercharged Conduits** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Conduits grant 3 Production and 3 Food income.
- **Ancient Studies** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Ancient Wonders grant +5 Knowledge income for each Tier they have, when annexed to a City.
- **Transformative Expertise** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Enchantment and Transformation Spells cost -25% Mana and World Map Casting Points.
- **Grand Casting Reserves** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): Gain +30 World Map Casting Points.
- **Tactical Casting Reserves** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): Gain +30 Combat Casting Points.
- **Summoning Bonds** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium): - Your Magic Origin Units gain +2 Experience for each non-Magic Origin unit in the same army.; Your non-Magic Origin Units gain +1 Status Resistance for each Magic Origin unit in the same army.
- **Cosmic Breaches** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): [Combat] Damage Spells and [Combat] Debuff Spells in combat now apply Sundered Resistance to their targets.
- **Spell Warding** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): [Combat] Buff Spells and [Combat] Healing Spells in combat now apply Bolstered Resistance to the friendly targets.
- **Enchantment Attunement** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium): Upkeep from Unit Enchantment spells is reduced by -20%.
- **Alchemical Applications** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): Mana Resource Nodes and Pearl Resource Nodes grant +10 Gold and +10 Production income.
- **Extravagant Studies** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): Mana City Structures grant +5 Knowledge income.
- **Ingredient Experimentation** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium): Your Throne City gains +5 Gold and +5 Mana income for each Magic Material your Empire has access to.
- **Teleportation Mastery** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Units are restored to full Hit Points and regain 50% of their Move Points after using a Teleporter if they have not yet regained any Move Point this turn. Teleporters grant +15 Mana income.
- **Tower of Enlightenment** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): All Wizard Tower City Structures grant +5 City Stability, +10 Food, and +10 Knowledge income.  For Elder Vampires, Vampire Castle upgrades grant this bonus instead.
- **Astral Absorption** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): When an enemy player casts a spell in combat with you, gain 15 Combat Casting Points and 60 Mana.
- **Quickening** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Spells can now be cast on the first Turn of Combat.

## Chaos Empire Skill

- **War Industry** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Quarries grant +5 Draft income.
- **War Infrastructure** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Draft City Structures cost -25% Gold and Production.
- **Iron Rule** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Draft City Structures grant +10 City Stability income.
- **Specialized Troops** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Unit Research skills cost -25% Knowledge. Units unlocked through Research cost -25% Gold to draft in your Cities.
- **Battlefield Looting** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): Gain 3 Gold per Unit Tier of the Units killed in Combat.
- **Might Makes Right** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): - Tier 3, 4, and 5 units gain +5 Hit Point for each Tier 1 and 2 unit in the same army.; Tier 1 and 2 units gain +2 Experience per Turn for each Tier 3, 4, and 5 unit in the same army.
- **Chaotic Inspiration** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium): Whenever a new Research Skill is being researched, the Knowledge cost of another random Skill is reduced by -25%.
- **Otherworldly Reinforcements** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): - [World Map] Summon Spells cost -25% World Map Casting Points.; Combat Summon Spells cost -25% Combat Casting Point.
- **Skilled Raiders** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): Pillaging a Province takes -1 turns and yields +20% more rewards. Pillaging Units are healed for 20 Hit Points.
- **Destiny of War** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium): 30 Grievances against all other Empires. These cannot be traded or lost. Your Throne City gains +30 Gold and +30 Mana per active War you are involved in against another Empire.
- **Conquerors** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): Absorbing and Migrating Cities takes -2 Turns. Absorbed and Migrated Cities permanently gain +20 Food, +20 Production, and +20 City Stability.
- **Despoilers** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): Razing a City: ; Takes -2 Turns.; Gives 30 Gold per Population killed.; Gives 100 Food per Population killed to one of your cities.
- **First Blood Initiation** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium): Your Units gain +25% Critical Hit Chance. They lose 5% of this bonus for each Rank they have.
- **Lawless Lands** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium): While in unowned Provinces, your Units regenerate +15 Hit Points per turn and gain +5 Experience per turn.
- **Monstrous Elites** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Your Mythic Units gain +5 Hit Points for each Rank that they have, up to Legendary.
- **Resilient Hordes** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Your Tier 1 Unit gain +30 maximum Hit Points, +2 Defense, and Resistance. Your Tier 2 Units gain +20 maximum Hit Points, +1 Defense, and Resistance.
- **Tireless Armies** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Your Units gain Exhausted Immunity.

## General Empire Skill

- **Advanced Seafaring** (cost 175 Imperium): The movement cost of embarking is reduced while Embarked Units gain Very Fast Movement.
- **Basic Seafaring** (level 2, cost 100 Imperium): Unlocks the ability for Units to embark and use vessels to cross the water, and for Flying and Floating units to travel over water.
- **Excavation** (level 3, cost 125 Imperium): Unlocks the ability for Units to Excavate Earthen terrain in the Underground.
- **Road Building** (level 4, cost 125 Imperium): Unit can build roads on the World Map. Most terrain costs 6-8 Move Points while roads cost 5.  Road Building can be enabled in the Army panel. Roads are built on hexes along which the Army moves and cost 3 Gold per hex.
- **Diplomatic Focus** (level 5, cost 150 Imperium): Gain +1 Whispering Stone.
- **Advanced Sensing** (level 6, cost 150 Imperium): Your Units and Cities gain +2 Vision Range and +3 Sensing Range.
- **Wizard King** (level 6, cost 100 Imperium): Gain +10 [World Map] [Combat] Casting Points.
- **Forced March** (level 7, cost 175 Imperium): Unlocks the ability for your Armies to use Forced March.
- **Siege Specialization** (level 8, cost 175 Imperium): Gain +1 Siege Project slot.
- **Advanced Logistics** (level 9, cost 175 Imperium): Roads cost 3 Move Points instead of 5.  Units gain Fast Embark.
- **Teleporter Infrastructure** (level 10, cost 200 Imperium): Cities can now build Teleporters.  Outposts can build teleporters in a province they annex through a Work Camp.
- **Cosmic Affinity** (level 11, cost 250 Imperium): Gain +5 Affinity Points per Turn in every branch where you have at least 3 Affinity.
- **Focused Affinity** (level 11, cost 250 Imperium): Gain +50 Affinity Points per Turn in the branch of your highest Affinity.

## Materium Empire Skill

- **Industrial Infrastructure** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Production City Structures cost -25% Gold and Production to build.
- **Land Sculptors** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Terraforming Spells cost -25% Mana and World Map Casting Points.
- **Metropolitan Plans** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): [World Map] City Spells and City Structures Research cost -25% Knowledge. City Structures unlocked through Research cost -25% Gold to build.
- **Outpost Expertise** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Constructing Outposts takes -1 Turn and costs -50% Gold.
- **Mythical Alloys** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): Mines grant +5 Mana income.
- **Purified Gold** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): Mines grant +5 Gold income.
- **Growing Workforce** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium): Quarries grant +5 Food and +2 City Stability income.
- **Bastion Builders** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): City Defense Structures: ; Cost -25% Gold and Production.; Grant +5 City Stability and +5 Production.
- **Siege Masters** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): Gain +1 Siege Project slot. Siege Projects cost -25% less.
- **Consolidated Industry** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium): Province Improvements grant +2 City Stability for each adjacent Province Improvement of the same type.
- **Efficient Allocation** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium): Production City Structures grant +10 Gold income.
- **Metropolitan Society** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): The Throne City and Cities that share a border with the Throne City gain +10% in all types of income.
- **Logistical Centers** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium): Provinces adjacent to your Cities grant +100% income.
- **Formula Archives** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): You gain 1 Arcanium Ore, Fireforge Stone, and Focus Crystals.
- **Innovator Specialists** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Special Province Improvements grant +5 Knowledge.
- **Competitive Markets** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Cities gain an additional Guild Slot.
- **Wasteless Process** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Produce Merchandise converts an additional 75% in Cities.

## Nature Empire Skill

- **Food Infrastructure** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Food City Structures cost -25% Gold and Production to build.
- **Fruitful Integration** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Founding or Absorbing Cities takes -2 turns. Newly Founded or Absorbed Cities gain +1 Population.
- **Prosperous Lands** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Farms grant +3 Gold and +2 City Stability income.
- **Tree Keepers** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Foresters grant +2 Mana and +2 City Stability income.
- **Foraging** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): While in Friendly domains, your Units regenerate +10 Hit Points per turn and gain +2 Experience per turn.
- **Symbiotic Armies** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): Your non-Racial Units gain +5 Hit Points and +2 Experience per Turn for each Racial Unit in the same Army.
- **Cooperating Community** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium): Food City Structures grant +10 Production income.
- **Fields of Fertility** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium): Provinces in your domain without Resource Nodes generate +5 Food income.
- **Adapt and Overcome** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): Your Units gain Status Protection for 3 Turns whenever they gain the first Negative Status Effect that Turn in Combat.
- **Lasting Boons** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): Positive Status Effects on your Units which last at least 2 Turns, last an additional 1 Turn.
- **Expansive Reach** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium): Cities may expand to Provinces located 2 Provinces further from the center.
- **Sacred Waters** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): Coast, River, and Water Provinces grant +2 Knowledge income. Rivers cost 4 Move Points to traverse.
- **Spiritual Grounds** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): Food Resource Nodes grant +5 Knowledge income.
- **Plowshares To Swords** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium): Your Cities gain Draft income equal to 25% of their Food income.
- **Well Supplied** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Your Units gain Well Supplied at the start of their Turn in the World Map if they are within your Domain, which lasts for 5 Turns outside of your Domain.
- **Complete Harmony** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Each Population costs no Food upkeep and grants +3 City Stability income. Your Cities gain +15 maximum Population, raising it from 30 to 45.
- **Druidic Empire** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Each Population in your cities grants +1 Knowledge, +1 Mana, and +1 Gold income.

## Order Empire Skill

- **Aligned Relations** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): You gain +300 Relations with every Free City whose alignment does not oppose yours.
- **Pacification** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Destroying an Infestation grants a stacking +20 City Stability to all your Cities for 10 Turns.
- **Diplomatic Expertise** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): - You gain +1 Whispering Stone.; Whispering Stones grant +1 Allegiance.
- **Gold Infrastructure** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Gold City Structures cost -25% Gold and Production.
- **Cultural Exchangers** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): - Gain +4 Knowledge per active Wizards Bond, Treaty, or Declaration with other Empires.; Gain +4 Knowledge per Trade Treaty with Free Cities.
- **Spiritual Conviction** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): Cities gain +10 Mana for each City Stability above Neutral.
- **Dutiful Watch** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium): - Cities gain +1 City Stability per Unit tier of each friendly Unit inside the City. (Heroes count as Tier 5).; Friendly Units inside your Cities gain +5 Experience Per Turn.
- **Oaths of Vengeance** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): Completing a Bounty grants you: ; +25% more rewards.; +200% better Empire Relations with the Empire that posted it.
- **Shared Prosperity** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): Gold City Structures grant +10 City Stability and +5 Production income.
- **Benevolent Conquerors** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium): Converting conquered Cities into Vassals takes -2 Turns and does not give a Relations penalty.
- **Bonds of Brotherhood** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium): - You gain +5 Imperium income per Defensive Pact you have with other Rulers.; You gain +15 Imperium income per Alliance you have with other Rulers instead.
- **Blessings and Wards** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): - [Combat] Healing Spells and [Combat] Buff Spells cost -25% Mana and Combat Casting Points.; [World Map] Friendly Army Spells cost -25% Mana and World Map Casting Points.
- **Call to Arms** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium): Units from Rally of the Lieges: ; Cost -50% Gold.; Gain +2 starting ranks.
- **Lead by Example** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium): Armies led by Heroes gain +5 Morale for each level of Renown they have.
- **Absolute Loyalty** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Vassals grant an additional +20% income when at Supreme Vassalage.
- **Utopian Society** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Cities gain +10% income when at the highest City Stability level.
- **Order of Knightly Oaths** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Units of Legendary Rank gain a buff depending on their unit type. ; Shield Units gain Order of the Unbreakable.; Polearm Units gain Order of the Tyrantslayer.; Shock Units gain Order of the Thunderer.; Skirmisher Units gain Order of the Windborne.; Support Units gain Order of the Soulwarden.; Battle Mage Units gain Order of the Warmage.; Ranged Units gain Order of the Titanslayer.; Fighter Units gain Order of the Berserker.; Mythic Units gain Order of the Indomitable.; Magic Fighter Units gain Order of the Spellblade.

## Order Repeatable Rite

- **Rite of Allegiance** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Instantly gain 20 Allegiance with all non-hostile Free Cities.
- **Rite of Wealth** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Instantly gain 2000 Gold.
- **Rite of the Banners** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Call a Rally of the Lieges event, where you have 20 additional Recruitment Points.

## Repeatable Astral Rite

- **Rite of Astral Abundance** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Instantly gain 750 Mana.
- **Rite of Conjuration** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Create a random Magic Material near your Throne City.
- **Rite of Spell Storm** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Gain 150 World Map Casting Points and Combat Casting Points until the end of the turn.

## Repeatable Chaos Rite

- **Rite of Chosen Warriors** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Your Units all immediately gain enough Experience to level up.  Does not affect Heroes and units above Legendary rank.
- **Rite of Fanaticism** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): All of your Units gain the following for 5 Turns: ; Resurgence; Martyr

## Repeatable General Rite

- **Rite of the Last Stand** (level 5, cost 175 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Each city in your Empire loses -1 Population, but immediately summons 3 Tier I Units.

## Repeatable Materium Rite

- **Rite of Industry** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): All of your Cities immediately gain 500 Production.
- **Rite of Stoic Defense** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): All of your current Units: ; Heal to their maximum Hit Points.; Gain +1 Defense and +1 Resistance, which does not stack with subsequent uses.

## Repeatable Nature Rite

- **Rite of Expansive Growth** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Increase the City Cap by +1.
- **Rite of Awakening** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Instantly gain 15 Knowledge, 15 Mana, and 15 Gold for each Population in your empire's Cities.
- **Rite of Nature's Guardians** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Immediately summon a White Wolf with Resurgence and Animal Guardian and an Entwined Scourge with Resurgence and Plant Guardian in your Throne City.

## Shadow Empire Skill

- **Finders Keepers** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): Resource rewards from Pickups, except Astral Echoes, are increased by +50%.
- **Wonder Reavers** (level 1, cost 100 Imperium): When clearing an Ancient Wonder gain: ; 70 Knowledge for a Bronze Ancient Wonder.; 150 Knowledge for a Silver Ancient Wonder.; 300 Knowledge for a Gold Ancient Wonder.
- **Extracted Essence** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Gain 2 Mana per Unit Tier of the Units killed in Combat.
- **Turncoat Masters** (level 2, cost 125 Imperium): Routing Units are Captured if your side is victorious in Combat.  Units recruited in this way have Low Maintenance.
- **Hexes and Curses** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): - [World Map] Enemy Army Spells cost -25% World Map Casting Points.; [Combat] Debuff Spells cost -25% Combat Casting Points.
- **Research Infrastructure** (level 3, cost 150 Imperium): Knowledge City Structures cost -25% Gold and Production.
- **Seeing Stones** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium): Free Cities with a Whispering Stone assigned instantly provide you with Vision Range. Free Cities or Vassals with a Whispering Stone assigned grant you +15 Knowledge.
- **Interrogation Studies** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): Knowledge City Structures grant +5 Mana and +5 Gold income.
- **Shadow Binding** (level 5, cost 200 Imperium): Magic Origin base Unit Upkeep is reduced by -20%.
- **Court of Whispers** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium): Gain the ability to assign Whispering Stones to other empires' Vassals to gain their tribute. Gain +1 Whispering Stone.
- **Dark Vigor** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): Your Armies regenerate +15 Hit Points per Turn in hostile Domain.
- **Feeding on Fear** (level 7, cost 300 Imperium): Units heal 10 Hit Points for every enemy unit that Routed during combat.
- **Unnatural Recovery** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium): After winning a battle, you can spend Mana to resurrect any of your non-hero units that died.
- **Exalted by Shadows** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Your Heroes gain +200% Experience from combat.
- **Headhunters** (level 9, cost 400 Imperium): Your non-Hero Units do +20% damage to Heroes.
- **Death Casting** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Gain 10 Combat Casting Points whenever a Unit dies in combat.  Doesn't affect units with: ; Combat Summon; Non-Vital
- **Eyes Everywhere** (level 10, cost 500 Imperium): Immediately reveal the full World Map. Your Throne City gains infinite Sensing Range. Armies in Sensing Range reveal their numbers and allegiance.

## Shadow Repeatable Rite

- **Rite of Forbidden Knowledge** (level 4, cost 175 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Instantly gain 400 Knowledge.
- **Rite of the Exalted** (level 6, cost 250 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): All your Heroes instantly gain +1 Rank.
- **Rite of Swift Shadows** (level 8, cost 350 Imperium, repeatable, each repeat costs +50 Imperium): Your Units immediately regain all of their Move Points and gain Universal Camouflage for 2 Turns.
