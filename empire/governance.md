# Governors (governance)

## Elder Vampire Governor

While Governor, the governed city gains benefits based on the Renown level of this Hero:

- Renown level 1
Each Population grants +1 Food, +1 Draft, and +1 Knowledge.
- Renown level 2
Acquiring new Population and Thralls requires -20% less Food
- Renown level 3
Each Mine grants +2 Imperium
- Renown level 4
Units cost -20% less Gold, Draft and Souls to recruit.
- Level 1: Each Population grants +1 Food, +1 Draft, and +1 Knowledge.
- Level 2: Acquiring new Population and Thralls requires -20% less Food.
- Level 3: Each Mine grants +2 Imperium.
- Level 4: Units cost -20% less Gold, Draft and Souls to recruit.
