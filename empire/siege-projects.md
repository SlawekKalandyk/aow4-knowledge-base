# Siege projects

- **Powder Keg Diplomacy** (cost 150 Gold, fortification damage 2, needs research, DLC SILVERSEAS): At the start of combat: Wall Obstacles are damaged. Wall Obstacles have a 25% chance of being destroyed. 6 to 12 enemy units gain Ember Dust.
- **Conqueror's Edict** (cost 60 Mana, fortification damage 4, unlocked by [Tome of the Warlord](../tomes/tome-of-the-warlord.md) (tier 3), DLC RISEFROMRUIN): At the start of combat random hexes are set On Fire. During battle all friendly units gain +15% Critical Hit chance. If victorious, Razing and Migrating this city requires 1 fewer Turns.
- **Foresee Downfall** (cost 70 Mana, fortification damage 0, needs research, DLC ARCHONPROPHECY): At the start of combat: Friendly units gain: Precognition; +10% Critical Hit Chance for the duration of combat. Enemy units gain: A 10% Fumble chance for the duration of combat.
- **Ritual of Calamity** (cost 100 Gold 150 Mana, fortification damage 0, needs research, DLC WAYSOFWAR): At the start of every Turn during the siege, a random Province Improvement of the besieged city is pillaged, granting you rewards. At the start of combat: All defenders gain a stack of Ghostfire for 3 Turns. 2 Accursed Ogres appear on the attacker's side.
- **Construct Siege Guns** (cost 200 Gold, fortification damage 2, needs research, DLC EMPIRESANDASHES): At the start of the battle, gain 2 Magelock Cannon units on the attacker's side until the end of battle. Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- **Exploit Networks** (cost , fortification damage 2, needs research, DLC SILVERSEAS): At the start of combat, all enemy units gain 1 Leverage. When the walls are breached, gain 50 Gold per tier of the besieged City.
- **Construct Great Bombard** (cost 150 Gold, fortification damage 3, needs research, DLC EMPIRESANDASHES): At the start of the battle, gain Great Bombard on the attacker's side until the end of battle. Great Bombard is an immobile Siegecraft Unit. Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- **Wizard's Bombardment** (cost 200 Mana, fortification damage 0, needs research, DLC Base): During combat, whenever you cast a Combat Spell, an astral comet strikes a random enemy unit: Enemy units within a 1 hex radius suffer 5 Lightning Damage, 5 Frost Damage, and 5 Fire Damage. Obstacles within a 1 hex radius are destroyed. Requires a captured Fallen Tower Ancient Wonder.
- **Subjugating Raid** (cost , fortification damage 0, unlocked by [Tome of Subjugation](../tomes/tome-of-subjugation.md) (tier 3), DLC Base): When the walls are breached, the besieged City loses -2 Population. Closest owned City gains +2 Population. At the start of the battle, all enemy units have -10 Morale until the end of battle.
- **Arcane Accumulation** (cost 100 Mana, fortification damage 0, unlocked by [Tome of Astral Convergence](../tomes/tome-of-astral-convergence.md) (tier 4), DLC Base): At the start of combat, gain 50 Combat Casting Points. During combat, all enemy Units suffer -1 Resistance.
- **Create Earthshatter Engines** (cost 200 Gold 100 Mana, fortification damage 5, needs research, DLC Base): At the start of the battle, all Wall obstacles are damaged. At the start of the battle, gain 2 Earthshatter Engine units on the attacker's side until the end of battle. Earthshatter Engines are Siegecraft Units with short range, area of effect attacks that can destroy obstacles and knock enemy units out of Defense Mode. Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- **Unleash the War Hounds** (cost 50 Mana, fortification damage 0, unlocked by [Tome of the Horde](../tomes/tome-of-the-horde.md) (tier 1), DLC Base): At the start of the battle, gain 6 War Hound units on the attacker's side until the end of battle.
- **Sow Confusion** (cost 20 Mana, fortification damage 0, unlocked by [Tome of Mayhem](../tomes/tome-of-mayhem.md) (tier 2), DLC Base): At the start of the battle, enemy units: Are displaced by 5 hexes. Suffer Misfortune for 3 Turns. Suffer Immobilized for 1 Turn.
- **Raiding Party** (cost , fortification damage 0, available by default, DLC Base): When the walls are breached, gain 50 Gold per tier of the besieged City. When the walls are breached, you gain -5 Alignment.
- **Burn Battlements** (cost 100 Gold, fortification damage 0, available by default, DLC Base): Battlement positions have 25% chance of being On Fire at the start of combat.
- **Undermining the Walls** (cost 100 Gold, fortification damage 0, available by default, DLC Base): All Wall obstacles are damaged at the start of combat. Wall obstacles have a 25% chance of being destroyed at the start of combat.
- **Tower Bombardment** (cost 100 Mana, fortification damage 0, available by default, DLC Base): At the start of combat, a random Tower Unit takes 20 Physical Damage, repeats 4 times.
- **Incite Rebellion** (cost 150 Gold 150 Mana, fortification damage 0, needs research, DLC Base): After 1 Turns in battle, 3 enemy non-Hero units are Mind-Controlled until the end of combat. The city suffers -50 City Stability while it is besieged.
- **Headlong Assault** (cost , fortification damage 5, available by default, DLC Base): Units besieging the city take 20 Physical Damage and suffer Exhausted at the start of combat.
- **Construct Onagers** (cost 200 Gold, fortification damage 0, available by default, DLC Base): At the start of the battle, gain 2 Onagers on the attacker's side until the end of battle. Onagers are immobile Siegecraft Units with long range, area of effect attacks that are great for destroying obstacles, but are inaccurate against Units. Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- **Construct Devastator Spheres** (cost 100 Gold 100 Mana, fortification damage 3, unlocked by [Tome of Devastation](../tomes/tome-of-devastation.md) (tier 3), DLC Base): At the start of battle, the attacker gains 2 Devastator Sphere until the end of battle. Devastator Spheres are Siegecraft Units that can detonate themselves into target obstacles or Unit to deal massive damage and destroy obstacles. Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- **Harass Defenders** (cost 60 Gold, fortification damage 0, available by default, DLC Base): Units defending the city take 20 Physical Damage at the start of combat.
- **Fumigation** (cost 40 Mana, fortification damage 0, unlocked by [Tome of Alchemy](../tomes/tome-of-alchemy.md) (tier 1), DLC EMPIRESANDASHES): At the start of combat units defending the city: Suffer 16 Blight Damage. Suffer 2 Weakened.
- **Dragon Attack** (cost 150 Mana, fortification damage 5, unlocked by [Tome of Dragons](../tomes/tome-of-dragons.md) (tier 3), DLC DRAGONLORDS): At the start of combat: Units defending the city take 26 Fire Damage. Units defending the city have a base 120% of suffering Burning. Random positions are On Fire. Most Battlement Structure obstacles are On Fire.
- **Soul Siphon Ritual** (cost 150 Mana, fortification damage 0, needs research, DLC Base): When the walls are breached, you gain +40 Souls per Tier of the besieged City; At the start of the battle: Gain 6 Decaying Zombie units. All enemy units gain Soulbound.
- **Construct Bolt Repeaters** (cost 160 Gold, fortification damage 2, unlocked by [Tome of Artificing](../tomes/tome-of-artificing.md) (tier 2), DLC Base): At the start of the battle, gain 2 Bolt Repeater units on the attacker's side until the end of battle. Bolt Repeaters are immobile Siegecraft Units with long range area of effect attacks that are repeating, unlike Onagers. Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- **Revels of Blood** (cost 50 Mana, fortification damage 0, needs research, DLC Base): At the start of the battle, attacking units gain 10 Morale and defending units are inflicted with 2 Bleeding for 3 Turns
- **Tremor Ritual** (cost 100 Gold 100 Mana, fortification damage 3, needs research, DLC Base): In battle, every 2 Turns a random enemy unit is struck by an earthquake: Enemy non-flying, non-floating units within 2-hex radius take 10 Physical Damage. Enemy non-flying, non-floating units within 2-hex radius have 90% chance of suffering Slowed for 3 Turns. Obstacles, including Walls, Towers, and Battlements within 2-hex radius are destroyed.
