- **Allfather**: The "Allfather" is one of the names given to a supreme being shrouded in mystery. Worshiped as a force of creation, they are known by many different identities throughout the cosmos. Some say they shaped the mythical Well of Creation while others believe that the being and the Well form a holy duality that cannot be separated.  The Archons Lore have spread the faith of the "Allfather" to many Worlds, the being's manifestation morphing toward the perception of the various races. While the Archons Lore claim to be the "Allfather's" celestial servants, there are no trustworthy sources that would demonstrate the being's intentions or any will to interfere with the dealings of mortals.
- **Alliances**: The Ruler is less inclined toward hostile actions, is keen to act more in favor of good relations with other Rulers and cities and will not break their Alliances.
- **Ambition**: Each Hero has an Ambition, which sets goals for them to complete and gain Renown. Each includes a Minor Ambition, which can be repeated any number of times, and a Major Ambition, which can only be completed once.  Completing these ambitions grants Renown, while completing the Major Ambition additionally grants a unique hero property.  A hero's Ambition may be changed through an event should that hero fail to complete their goals in a set period of time.

Around that time, however, the Ancient Krazura mysteriously vanished and any record of their civilization and works became lost in the ages to follow. Only after the opening of Grexolis Lore and the return of the Endailon Lore is their history and heritage being rediscovered.
- **Ancient Wonder**: An Ancient Wonder is a Location that contains adventure and rewards. It can only be explored by a single Army led by a Hero.  When cleared, it can be annexed by a City. When annexed it will provide City Income and special Unit in the Request Military Aid. An annexed Ancient Wonder counts as a type of Province Improvement.  Ancient Wonders can be Bronze, Silver, or Gold, depending on rarity, difficulty and value.
