# Glossary (in-game concept tooltips)

- **AI Personality**: The AI personality is an optional setting which determines the personality of a Ruler when you encounter them as an AI controlled Ruler in different sessions. This setting does not affect your empire when you are playing as this Ruler. The personality affects the choices and behavior of AI Rulers in terms of Diplomacy, Research, Economy, and Military. When using the Default option, the system will pick a personality based on the starting Affinity and Alignment of your custom Ruler when it is AI controlled.
- **Abandoned Egg Cluster**: An egg cluster from an aquatic creature, beset by predators. At least one of them seems ready to hatch. Moving your army onto it will allow the creature to join your ranks.
- **Absorbing**: Absorbing a City makes it part of your Empire under your direct control, retaining its original race and culture.
- **Accuracy**: Accuracy is the likelihood of attacks landing successfully. If the attack does not land, it misses or grazes the target. Grazes deal half damage. Misses deal no damage. Misses can hit random adjacent units next to the target.
- **Accursed Armors**: When a melee range attack hits, the attacker has a 60% chance of being inflicted with Misfortune for 3 Turns.
- **Action Point**: Units use Action Points to attack and use abilities. Units get 3 Action Points per Turn, and can use up to 2 of them to move. Abilities use action points in different ways: single-use action Ability costs a single action point. The unit's turn ends afterwards. repeating action Ability triggers once per remaining action point. The unit's turn ends afterwards. full action Ability costs 3 action points. The unit's turn ends afterwards. free action Ability costs 0 action points. leave-one action Ability costs 0 action points. The unit retains 1 action point afterwards. Ability requires 3 action points. The unit retains 1 action point afterwards.
- **Adaptable**: Non-hero units gain +30% Experience from combat.
- **Adept Hero Skill**: Adept skills are unlocked by acquiring 2 other skills in the same category.
- **Adherent**: Cities have +10 City Stability.
- **Advisor**: The Advisor shows new tutorial and advice messages. Advice messages contain tips about the current situation of your empire. It can be accessed through the ? banner located near the bottom right of the screen.
- **Aerelazaliar**: Having hatched only ten cosmic cycles ago, Aerelazaliar would be considered "young" among his Dragonkin. Perhaps it is his youth that drives his curiosity and kindness towards those his kin would deem inferior. He pays their comments no mind, for Aerelazaliar's gaze drifts beyond his nature of creation and destruction and he dares to question "why". The Starbred Salamanders were created in Aerelazaliar's own image. It is rare for a Dragon to create their own people, and many wonder what the consequences of Aerelazaliar's "vain splurge in divinity" will be. His people, however, are forever grateful for their draconic father's care.
- **Aeri Kyastir**: As a direct descendant of the first Syrons, Aeri grew up in reverence of the history of the Gloomwatch. Yet, as her talents as a Wizard grew, her Torment of Shadows made her question her assigned burden as a cosmic protector. Instead, she decided to offer sanctuary to those who choose simpler lives... Though in the Astral Sea, that in itself is a challenge. The Free Syrons consist mainly of veterans, and people who declined the duty and honor of the Gloomwatch in favor of a calmer life on the Worlds Within. Yet, when threatened, they will stand their ground next to any other battle-hardened warrior.
- **Affinity**: The Affinity of an Empire is an indication of its strengths, specializations, and available Tomes. The Affinity types are: Order Affinity; Chaos Affinity; Nature Affinity; Materium Affinity; Astral Affinity; Shadow Affinity. Starting Affinity points are determined by faction creation choices. Affinity points are obtained by selecting the respective Tomes during Research. Having more Affinity points will increase the rate at which you unlock new Empire Skills in Empire Development. To unlock higher tier Tomes, you will need to have a certain amount of Affinity points that match with the Affinity of those tomes.
- **Affinity Check**: When the empire of a player has reached a high enough Affinity score, this can unlock special character actions in story events. Within those actions the player Ruler or one of their heroes attempts to channel the respective Affinity to perform a challenging action. Each Affinity check has a strength X, which determines how the option behaves: The option is hidden as long as the empire Affinity is lower than X. 50% base chance to succeed when the empire Affinity is exactly X. This chance increases by 10% for every Empire Affinity Point above X.
- **Affinity Province Improvement**: Affinity Province Improvements are built from the Production Queue and replace an existing Province Improvement. Only one can be built per City, and an Empire can only build one of each. If the Seed, Root and Heart of the same affinity have been built in the Empire, the Magic Victory Spell is unlocked.
- **Afflicting Miasma**: Units passing through or ending their turn on this hex will be inflicted with Weakened. Units on this hex have Afflicted.
- **Age of Astral**: Channel the Age of Astral to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling: Your Astral Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points. Each turn in combat, a random enemy takes 15 mixed Fire, Lightning, and Frost, damage and gets 2 Sundered Resistance and 2 Status Vulnerability.
- **Age of Chaos**: Channel the Age of Chaos to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling: Your Chaos Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points. Each Turn in combat, a random enemy unit sustains 15 damage of a random type and has a base 90% chance of receiving a random Negative Status Effect.
- **Age of Materium**: Channel the Age of Materium to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling: Your Materium Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points. Each turn in combat, a friendly unit gains Bolstered Defense and Bolstered Resistance. Repeats 2-4 times.
- **Age of Nature**: Channel the Age of Nature to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling: Your Nature Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points. Every 2 turns in combat, a Tier II-III nature unit is summoned on your side.
- **Age of Order**: Channel the Age of Order to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling: Your Order Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points. Each turn in combat, a random ally gains 3 random Positive Status Effects and loses all Negative Status Effects.
- **Age of Shadow**: Channel the Age of Shadow to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling: Your Shadow Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points. Each turn in combat, a random corpse is reanimated as a Decaying Zombie until the end of combat.
- **Aggressive Expansion**: Aggressive Expansion rewards Province Annexation of provinces belonging to other factions. Annexing a hostile province to a city grants a one time reward of Production, Draft, and Gold. These rewards scale with the population of the city the province was taken from.

In order to protect his people, he united the splintered factions of Athla under one banner and started the search for a new home. - **Alignment**: Determines how Free Cities and other Empires see you. Affected by Ruler traits and various actions. Affects relations with Free Cities and Empires with Neutral or better Alignment. In addition a Good Alignment (or better) will increase the chance of positive random events occurring, while an Evil Alignment (or worse) will make negative random events more likely. High Culture has an Alignment Agenda. Empire Relations for each Alignment level: 100 to 130: Pure Good: +300; 40 to 99: Very Good: +200; 10 to 39: Good: +100; 9 to -9: Neutral: No effects; -10 to -39: Evil: -100; -40 to -99: Very Evil: -200; -100 to -130: Pure Evil: -300
- **Alignment Agenda**: The High Culture has an Alignment Agenda, which grants bonuses for the following alignment levels: Pure Good: +25 City Stability in all Cities. Neutral: +10 Food and +10 Production per City Stability level above Neutral in all Cities. Pure Evil: Units start Combat in an Awakened state.
- **Allegiance**: Allegiance represents the commitment of a Free City to an Empire. Allegiance is primarily gained by giving a Whispering Stone to the Free City Lord which starts Negotiations. Increasing allegiance will result in better Diplomatic States with the city, eventually leading to a Pact of Vassalage and Pact of Integration.
- **Allfather**: The "Allfather" is one of the names given to a supreme being shrouded in mystery. Worshiped as a force of creation, they are known by many different identities throughout the cosmos. Some say they shaped the mythical Well of Creation while others believe that the being and the Well form a holy duality that cannot be separated. The Archons have spread the faith of the "Allfather" to many Worlds, the being's manifestation morphing toward the perception of the various races. While the Archons claim to be the "Allfather's" celestial servants, there are no trustworthy sources that would demonstrate the being's intentions or any will to interfere with the dealings of mortals.

Ancient Wonders can be Bronze, Silver, or Gold, depending on rarity, difficulty and value.
