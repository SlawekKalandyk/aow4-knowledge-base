## Ring of Escape

(Tier I; DLC Base)


- **Slip Away**: When this unit's HP Hit Points are reduced to zero, it is displaced by 3 hexes and heals +15 Temporary Hit Points.  Works once per battle. Doesn't work when Embarked.

## Ring of Flanking

(Tier I; DLC Base)


- **Flanker**: Unit deals +25% damage when Flanking.

## Ring of Opportunity

(Tier I; DLC Base)


- **Opportunist**: Deal +30% damage on Opportunity Attacks.

## Ring of Resistance

(Tier I; DLC Base)

Grants +1 Resistance.

## Ring of the Inspiring Killer

(Tier I; DLC Base)


- **Inspiring Killer**: This unit grants double the morale bonus to itself and its allies when killing an enemy.

## Ring of the Vicious Killer

(Tier I; DLC Base)


- **Vicious Killer**: This unit inflicts increased morale penalties when killing an enemy. Affects enemy units up to 3 hexes.

## Eternal Frost

(Tier III; DLC SILVERSEAS)


- **Eternal Hoarfrost**: When an enemy units becomes Frozen, heal friendly units in a 2-hex radius for 15 Temporary Hit Points.

## Murder Ring

(Tier III; DLC Base)


- **Frenzy**: When this unit lands an attack, it gains a stack of Strengthened. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Killing Momentum**: Unit regains 1 Action Point when killing another unit with a Magic, Melee, or Missile attack.  Works once per Turn.

## Ring of Deflection

(Tier III; DLC Base)


- **Ring of Deflection**: Grants +3 Defense and +3 Resistance against Missile and Magic attacks.

## Ring of Regeneration

(Tier III; DLC Base)


- **Natural Regeneration**: Heals 10% of its maximum Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points at the end of its turn in combat, though not more than the unit started combat with. Heals 10 HP Hit Points at the start of your turn on the world map.

## Wind Barrier Ring

(Tier III; DLC Base)


- **Wind Barrier**: This unit is 60% harder to hit by Missile and ranged Magic attacks.

## Fire Immunity Ring
