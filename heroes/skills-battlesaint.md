# Hero skills: Battlesaint

Battlesaints gain Fervor through particular actions that they spend for powerful support abilities or to empower themselves and smite their enemies.

Battlesaints gain Fervor through particular actions that they spend for powerful support abilities or to empower themselves and smite their enemies.

Compatible weapons:

- Great Weapon
- One Handed Weapon
- Shield
- Fist Weapon
- Polearm Weapon
- Magic Staff Weapon
- Magic Orb Weapon
- Skirmisher Weapon

## Battlefield Restoration

(type normal; requires Healing Support; can't be taken with Immortal Saint, Smite; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Battlefield Restoration** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, full action): All friendly units gain: +1 Regeneration per stack of Fervor consumed. Enter Defense Mode. Cooldown: 3 Turns

## Bless

(type normal; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Bless** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Target friendly unit gains +1 Fortune. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Support

## Blessed Body

(type normal; requires any of Support Resistance I, Martyr's Blood; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Blessed Body**: This unit is healed for +10 Temporary Hit Point whenever it uses a support ability.

## Bolstering Support

(type normal; requires any of Cleanse, Support Resistance I; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

Support abilities grant 1 Bolstered Resistance to affected units.

## Cleanse

(type normal; requires Rallying Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Cleanse** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit: Has all Negative Status Effects removed. Gains +3 Status Resistance for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support

## Cleric

(type normal; can't be taken with Paladin; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Cleric**: This and adjacent friendly units gain +1 Resistance. At the end of its turn, this unit gains +1 Fervor.

## Cleric's Fervor

(type normal; requires Cleric; can't be taken with Saint's Fervor, Warrior's Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Cleric's Fervor** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Caster loses all stacks of Fervor and the target unit gains: +10 Temporary Hit Point per stack of Fervor consumed. Does not generate Fervor. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support

## Condemnation

(type normal; requires any of Resistant Vanguard, Herald of Portents; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Condemnation** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Target enemy unit: Suffers Condemned until the end of combat. Base 90% chance of suffering 2 Weakened for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Debuff

## Defense

(type normal; requires any of Fervorous Martyr, Purifying Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

- +1 Defense
- +5% Evasion

## Defensive Master

(type normal; requires any of Weakening Aura, Inner Grace; can't be taken with Support Resistance III, Healing Support; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Defensive Master**: Always end your Turn in Defense Mode.

## Fervorous Disciple

(type normal; requires any of Cleric's Fervor, Saint's Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Fervorous Disciple**: Support abilities grant +1 Fervor. Entering Defense Mode grants +3 Fervor.

## Fervorous Martyr

(type normal; requires any of Saint's Fervor, Warrior's Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Fervorous Martyr**: Gain +1 Fervor whenever this unit takes damage.

## Fortunate Support

(type normal; requires any of Revive, Support Resistance II; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

Support abilities grant:

- +1 Fortune to affected units.

## Greater Defense

(type normal; requires any of Radiant Retribution, Resistant Vanguard; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

- +2 Defense
- +10% Evasion

## Healing Support

(type normal; requires any of Fortunate Support, Spiritual Guide; can't be taken with Support Resistance III, Defensive Master; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

Support abilities now heal targets for 10 Temporary Hit Points in addition to their regular effects.

## Herald of Portents

(type normal; requires Warded Armor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Herald of Portents**: This and adjacent friendly units gain 10% Critical Hit Chance.

## Immortal Saint

(type normal; requires any of Healing Support, Support Resistance III, Defensive Master; can't be taken with Battlefield Restoration, Smite; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Immortal Saint**: 1 Turn after this unit dies, it: Comes back to life with 40% Hit Points. Gains +5 Fervor.

## Inner Grace

(type normal; requires any of Greater Defense, Condemnation; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Inner Grace**: At the start of its turn, the unit gains Grace if it doesn't already have it.

## Martyr's Blood

(type normal; requires any of Fervorous Disciple, Fervorous Martyr; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Martyr's Blood**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 33% or lower: This unit gains Steadfast. All friendly units gain Grace and Fortune.

## Paladin

(type normal; can't be taken with Cleric; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Paladin**: This and adjacent friendly units gain +1 Defense. Landing attacks grants +1 Fervor. Effect is doubled for non repeating attacks.

## Purifying Fervor

(type normal; requires Warrior's Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Warrior's Fervor** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Caster loses all stacks of Fervor and gains: +1 Strengthened per stack of Fervor consumed. +5 Temporary Hit Point per stack of Fervor consumed. Dispels 1 Negative Status Effect per stack of Fervor consumed. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support

## Radiant Retribution

(type normal; requires any of Martyr's Blood, Defense; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Radiant Retribution**: When this unit takes damage, adjacent enemies: Suffer 5 Spirit Damage

## Rallying Fervor

(type normal; requires Cleric's Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Cleric's Fervor** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Caster loses all stacks of Fervor and the target unit gains: +10 Temporary Hit Point per stack of Fervor consumed. +1 Rally per stack of Fervor consumed. Does not generate Fervor. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support

## Regenerating Support

(type normal; requires Cleanse; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

Support abilities grant:

- +1 Regeneration to affected units.

## Resistant Vanguard

(type normal; requires any of Defense, Warded Armor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Resistant Vanguard**: This and adjacent friendly units gain +1 Resistance and +1 Status Resistance.

## Revive

(type normal; requires any of Regenerating Support, Bolstering Support; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Revive** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, full action): Target friendly corpse is revived with 60% of its max Hit Points. Cooldown: 3 Turns. Requires: Support

## Saint's Fervor

(type normal; requires any of Cleric, Paladin; can't be taken with Cleric's Fervor, Warrior's Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Saint's Fervor** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Caster loses all stacks of Fervor and the target unit gains: +5 Temporary Hit Point per stack of Fervor consumed. Does not generate Fervor. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support

## Smite

(type normal; requires Defensive Master; can't be taken with Battlefield Restoration, Immortal Saint; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Smite** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Lose all stacks of Fervor. Target enemy unit suffers 10 Spirit Damage per stack of Fervor consumed. Does not generate Fervor. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic

## Spiritual Guide

(type normal; requires any of Support Resistance II, Warding Bond; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Spiritual Guide**: This and adjacent friendly units gain: -50% less negative Morale. +50% more positive Morale.

## Support Resistance I

(type normal; requires any of Rallying Fervor, Fervorous Disciple; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

The hero gains:

- +1 Resistance
- +2 Status Resistance

When the unit enters Defense Mode these bonuses are given to adjacent allies.

## Support Resistance II

(type normal; requires any of Bolstering Support, Blessed Body; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

The hero gains:

- +1 Resistance
- +2 Status Resistance

When the unit enters Defense Mode these bonuses are given to adjacent allies.

## Support Resistance III

(type normal; requires any of Spiritual Guide, Weakening Aura; can't be taken with Defensive Master, Healing Support; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

The hero gains:

- +2 Resistance
- +3 Status Resistance

When the unit enters Defense Mode these bonuses are given to adjacent allies.

## Warded Armor

(type normal; requires Purifying Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)

Gain +2 Resistance.

## Warding Bond

(type normal; requires any of Blessed Body, Radiant Retribution; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Warding Bond** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Damage sustained by the target friendly unit is transferred to this unit with a 50% reduction. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Support

## Warrior's Fervor

(type normal; requires Paladin; can't be taken with Cleric's Fervor, Saint's Fervor; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Warrior's Fervor** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Caster loses all stacks of Fervor and gains: +1 Strengthened per stack of Fervor consumed. +5 Temporary Hit Point per stack of Fervor consumed. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support

## Weakening Aura

(type normal; requires any of Warding Bond, Greater Defense; DLC ARCHONPROPHECY)


- **Weakening Aura**: At the end of the turn inflict Weakened to adjacent enemies.
