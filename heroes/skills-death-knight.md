# Hero skills: Death Knight

A Hero Class that specializes in high damage Melee attacks, backed up by Debuff and summoned Undead units.

Compatible weapons:

- Great Weapon
- One Handed Weapon
- Shield
- Fist Weapon
- Polearm Weapon
- Skirmisher Weapon

## Berserker's Rage

(type normal; requires any of Reckless Rage, Execute; can't be taken with Grim Executioner; DLC Base)


- **Berserker's Rage**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 33% or lower, it gains for 1 Turn: Berserk; Steadfast

## Bone Wyvern Keeper

(type normal; requires Soul Keeper; DLC Base)


- **Bone Wyvern Keeper**: Summon a Bone Wyvern at the start of battle.

## Critical Momentum

(type normal; requires Keen Edge; can't be taken with Execute; DLC Base)


- **Critical Momentum**: When this hero makes a Critical Hit it: Heals 10 Temporary Hit Points; Gains Hastened

## Curse

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Curse** (damage 10 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Curse target unit. Sundered Defense: Inflict 2x Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Sundered Resistance: Inflict 2x Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns. Weakened: Inflict 2x Weakened for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 3 Turns. Requires: Debuff, Magic, Single Shot

## Dark Ritual

(type normal; requires any of Mass Curse, Frost Curse, Infested Summons; DLC Base)


- **Dark Ritual** (damage 10 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Enemies in a 1-hex radius sustain damage. All corpses in a 1-hex radius turn into Decaying Zombie units. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Magic

## Execute

(type normal; requires any of Sundering Strikes, Keen Edge; can't be taken with Reckless Rage, Critical Momentum; DLC Base)


- **Execute** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Slight chance of instantly killing an injured target. This chance increases the lower the target's Hit Points are. Cooldown: 2 Turns

## Fighting

(type normal; requires Fighting; DLC Base)

Melee attacks and Magic Strikes gain +10% damage.

## Fighting

(type normal; requires Second Wind; DLC Base)

Melee attacks and Magic Strikes gain +10% damage.

## Frenzy

(type normal; requires Knight; DLC Base)


- **Frenzy**: When this unit lands an attack, it gains a stack of Strengthened. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Frost Curse

(type normal; requires Hex of Disruption; can't be taken with Mass Curse; DLC Base)

Modify and enhance Curse:

- Curse now deals 30 Frost Damage.
- Curse gains 120% base chance of inflicting Frozen for 1 Turn.

## Grim Executioner

(type normal; requires any of Execute, Critical Momentum; can't be taken with Berserker's Rage; DLC Base)

When this unit kills another, it gains 3 Fortune.

## Hex of Decay

(type normal; requires Plague Magic; DLC Base)

Debuff abilities:

- Inflict Decaying for 3 Turn.

## Hex of Disruption

(type normal; requires Weakening Magic; DLC Base)

Debuff abilities:

- Gain 90% base chance of inflicting Disrupted for 2 Turn.

## Infested Summons

(type normal; requires Soul Keeper; DLC Base)

Units summoned by this hero have:

- Aura of Disease
- Immunity to Diseased

The hero gains immunity to Diseased.

## Infused Sweep

(type normal; requires any of Fighting, Vigor; DLC Base)


- **Infused Sweep** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage in a 1-hex cone. Sundered Defense: Inflict Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Sundered Resistance: Inflict Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Debuff, Magic, Base

## Keen Edge

(type normal; requires Frenzy; can't be taken with Sundering Strikes; DLC Base)

+20% Critical Hit Chance.

## Killing Momentum

(type normal; requires any of Willbreaker, Warseeker; DLC Base)


- **Killing Momentum**: Unit regains 1 Action Point when killing another unit with a Magic, Melee, or Missile attack. Works once per Turn.

## Knight

(type normal; DLC Base)

- +1 Defense
- +10 maximum Hit Points

## Mass Curse

(type normal; requires Hex of Decay; can't be taken with Frost Curse; DLC Base)

Modify and enhance Curse:

- Curse now affects all targets in a 1-hex radius.

## Melee Mage

(type normal; requires Dark Ritual; DLC Base)


- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.

## Plague Magic

(type normal; requires Curse; DLC Base)

Attacks and Debuff abilities:

- Gain 90% base chance of inflicting Diseased for 3 Turn.

## Raise Undead

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Raise Undead** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target: Friendly Undead corpse is revived with 60% of its total Hit Points. Non-Undead corpse is raised as a Zombie. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Support, Summoning

## Reckless Rage

(type normal; requires Sundering Strikes; can't be taken with Execute; DLC Base)


- **Reckless Rage** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Gain: +30% attack damage for 3 Turn; 1 Sundered Defense; 1 Sundered Resistance. Can only be used once per battle.

## Second Wind

(type normal; requires Knight; DLC Base)


- **Second Wind** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): +1 hex of movement for 1 Turn. Slippery for 1 Turn. Heal for 15 Temporary Hit Point. This ability is reusable after each kill. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Second Wind

## Soul Keeper

(type normal; requires Raise Undead; DLC Base)


- **Soul Keeper**: Summon a Zombie at the start of battle.

## Sundering Strikes

(type normal; requires Frenzy; can't be taken with Keen Edge; DLC Base)

Makes base attacks gain:

- Base 60% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense.
- Demolisher.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Terrifying Aura

(type normal; requires any of Vigor, Infused Sweep; can't be taken with Weakening Aura; DLC Base)


- **Terrifying Aura**: At the end of this unit's turn, adjacent enemy units lose -5 Morale. Doesn't work on units with this ability.

## Undying Warrior

(type normal; requires any of Grim Executioner, Berserker's Rage; DLC Base)


- **Resurgence**: When the unit dies in combat, it is revived at the end of combat if its owner is victorious. Revived units are brought back with 50% of their maximum Hit Points.

## Unyielding

(type normal; requires any of Weakening Aura, Terrifying Aura, Undying Warrior, Melee Mage; DLC Base)


- **Unyielding**: At the start of its turn, the unit dispels 1 Negative Status Effect.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires Vigor; DLC Base)

+15 maximum Hit Points.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires Second Wind; DLC Base)

+15 maximum Hit Points.

## Warseeker

(type normal; requires Unyielding; can't be taken with Willbreaker; DLC Base)


- **Warseeker**: When this unit kills an enemy: Gains Warseeker for 2 Turns. This effect stacks up to 3 times.

## Weakening Aura

(type normal; requires any of Fighting, Infused Sweep; can't be taken with Terrifying Aura; DLC Base)


- **Weakening Aura**: At the end of the turn inflict Weakened to adjacent enemies.

## Weakening Magic

(type normal; requires Curse; DLC Base)

Attacks and Debuff abilities:

- Gain 90% base chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turn.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Willbreaker

(type normal; requires Unyielding; can't be taken with Warseeker; DLC Base)

Gain:

- Vicious Killer
- Willbreaker
