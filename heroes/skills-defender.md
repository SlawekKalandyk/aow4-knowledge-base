# Hero skills: Defender

A Hero Class that specializes in defending its allies, survivability, and retaliation.

A Hero Class that specializes in defending its allies, survivability, and retaliation.

Compatible weapons:

- One Handed Weapon
- Shield
- Fist Weapon
- Polearm Weapon
- Skirmisher Weapon

## Adaptive Armor

(type normal; requires any of Defense, Warded Armor; DLC Base)


- **Adaptive Armor**: Once per Turn, when this unit is damaged by Magic attacks or spells it gains: Bolstered Resistance; Status Protection

## Aggressive Push

(type normal; requires Pushing Attack; can't be taken with Consolidating Push; DLC Base)

Modify and enhance Pushing Attack:

- Pushing Attack now displaces and replaces.
- Pushing Attack now deals 40 Physical Damage base.

## Bladestorm

(type normal; requires any of Aggressive Push, Consolidating Push; DLC Base)


- **Bladestorm**: At the start of this units' turn adjacent enemy units suffer 10 Physical Damage and Bleeding for 3 Turns

## Braced Defense

(type normal; requires Weakening Strikes; can't be taken with Defensive Master; DLC Base)

While in Defense Mode, this unit gains Charge Resistance.

## Consolidating Push

(type normal; requires Pushing Attack; can't be taken with Aggressive Push; DLC Base)

Modify and enhance Pushing Attack:

- Pushing Attack now heals the caster for +20 Temporary Hit Points.

## Defense

(type normal; requires any of Resistance, Warding Bond; DLC Base)

- +1 Defense
- +5% Evasion

## Defense

(type normal; requires any of Vigor, Resistant Vanguard; DLC Base)

- +1 Defense
- +5% Evasion

## Defensive Master

(type normal; requires Weakening Strikes; can't be taken with Braced Defense; DLC Base)


- **Defensive Master**: Always end your Turn in Defense Mode.

## Fighting

(type normal; requires Greater Defense; DLC Base)

Melee attacks gain +10% damage.

## Frenzied Retaliation

(type normal; requires Retaliator; can't be taken with Invigorating Retaliations; DLC Base)

When making a Retaliation Attack attack, this unit gains Strengthened.

## Frontline Fighter

(type normal; requires Sentinel; DLC Base)


- **Frontline Fighter**: Gain +1 Defense and +1 Resistance against non-flanking attacks.

## Graceful Defense

(type normal; requires any of Greater Defense, Weakening Aura, Greater Resistance; DLC Base)

Gain +1 Grace on entering Defense Mode.

## Greater Defense

(type normal; requires any of Consolidating Push, Defense; DLC Base)

- +2 Defense
- +10% Evasion

## Greater Fighting

(type normal; requires any of Fighting, Graceful Defense; can't be taken with Greater Vigor; DLC Base)

Melee attacks gain +20% damage.

## Greater Resistance

(type normal; requires any of Resistance, Adaptive Armor; DLC Base)

- +2 Resistance
- +3 Status Resistance

## Greater Vigor

(type normal; requires any of Graceful Defense, Vigor; can't be taken with Greater Fighting; DLC Base)

+30 maximum Hit Points.

## Invigorating Retaliations

(type normal; requires Retaliator; can't be taken with Frenzied Retaliation; DLC Base)

When making a Retaliation Attack attack this unit gains Regeneration.

## Leaden Blows

(type normal; requires Sentinel; DLC Base)

Makes base Melee attacks gain:

- +2 Physical Damage.
- Base 90% chance of inflicting Slowed.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Offensive Vanguard

(type normal; requires Shieldbearer; can't be taken with Resistant Vanguard; DLC Base)


- **Offensive Vanguard**: This unit is adjacent to a friendly Hero with Offensive Formation. Attacks deal +10% more damage.

## Pure Defense

(type normal; requires any of Adaptive Armor, Stunning Bash; can't be taken with Reflecting Defense; DLC Base)

When entering Defense Mode, this unit and all adjacent allies dispel 1 Negative Status Effect.

## Pushing Attack

(type normal; requires any of Leaden Blows, Frontline Fighter; DLC Base)


- **Pushing Attack** (damage 30 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Strike the target enemy unit, knocking them back and dealing damage. Enter Defense Mode after use. Push Back: Target is pushed back by 1 hex. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Melee, Single Shot

## Rallying Cry

(type normal; requires any of Greater Fighting, Greater Vigor; DLC Base)


- **Rallying Cry** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): The caster and adjacent friendly units gain: Rally for 3 Turn. +2 Bolstered Defense for 3 Turn. +2 Bolstered Resistance for 3 Turn. Vigilant. -. This unit then enters Defense Mode. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Support

## Reflecting Defense

(type normal; requires Stunning Bash; can't be taken with Pure Defense; DLC Base)

When entering Defense Mode, this unit gains Damage Deflection until the start of the next turn.

## Reinforced Armor

(type normal; requires any of Resistant Vanguard, Offensive Vanguard; can't be taken with Warded Armor; DLC Base)


- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.

## Resistance

(type normal; requires any of Frontline Fighter, Vigor; DLC Base)

- +1 Resistance
- +2 Status Resistance

## Resistance

(type normal; requires any of Warding Bond, Defense; DLC Base)

- +1 Resistance
- +2 Status Resistance

## Resistant Vanguard

(type normal; requires Shieldbearer; can't be taken with Offensive Vanguard; DLC Base)


- **Resistant Vanguard**: This and adjacent friendly units gain +1 Resistance and +1 Status Resistance.

## Retaliator

(type normal; requires any of Bladestorm, Greater Defense; DLC Base)

Gain +1 Retaliation Attack.

## Sentinel

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Sentinel**: +20% damage via Retaliation Attacks and Opportunity Attacks.

## Shieldbearer

(type normal; DLC Base)

When entering Defense Mode, this unit and all adjacent allies receive +2 Defense until the start of the next turn.

## Stunning Bash

(type normal; requires any of Warded Armor, Reinforced Armor; DLC Base)


- **Stunning Bash** (accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Base 120% chance of target unit becoming Stunned. Enter Defense Mode after use. Cooldown: 1 Turns

## Taunt

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Taunt** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Target enemy unit is inflicted with a base 90% chance of becoming Taunted for 2 Turns. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Debuff

## Tireless Retaliator

(type normal; requires any of Frenzied Retaliation, Invigorating Retaliations; DLC Base)

When entering Defense Mode, this unit gains Tireless Retaliation until the start of the next turn.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires any of Greater Resistance, Pure Defense; DLC Base)

+15 maximum Hit Points.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires Taunt; DLC Base)

+15 maximum Hit Points.

## Warded Armor

(type normal; requires any of Resistant Vanguard, Offensive Vanguard; can't be taken with Reinforced Armor; DLC Base)

Gain +2 Resistance.

## Warding Bond

(type normal; requires any of Resistance, Vigor, Defense; DLC Base)


- **Warding Bond** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Damage sustained by the target friendly unit is transferred to this unit with a 50% reduction. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Support

## Weakening Aura

(type normal; requires any of Defense, Resistance; DLC Base)


- **Weakening Aura**: At the end of the turn inflict Weakened to adjacent enemies.

## Weakening Strikes

(type normal; requires any of Reflecting Defense, Pure Defense; DLC Base)

Unit's attacks gain:

- A base 90% chance of inflicting Weakened.

This effect is increased for Retaliation Attacks.
