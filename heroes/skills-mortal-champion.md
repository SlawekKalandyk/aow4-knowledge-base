# Hero skills: Mortal Champion

## Champion of Perseverance

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Champion of Perseverance**: This unit grants +4 Status Resistance to adjacent friendly units.

## Champion of War

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Champion of War**: This unit grants +20% Critical Hit Chance to adjacent friendly units.

## Command

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Command** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly non-Hero unit regains its Action Points. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support, Can't be refreshed

## Coordinated Command

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Coordinated Command** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly non-Hero unit regains its Action Points. This effect jumps to 1 additional target within 3 hexes. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support, Can't be refreshed

## Decisive Command

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Decisive Command** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Target friendly non-Hero unit regains its Action Points. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support, Can't be refreshed

## Defensive Formation

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Defensive Formation**: While army leader, all non-hero units in the army grant: ; +1 Defense and +1 Resistance to adjacent friendly units.

## Endurance Training

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Endurance Training**: While army leader, all non-hero units in the army gain: ; +10 HP Hit Points; +2 Status Resistance

## Experienced Leader

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Experienced Leader**: While army leader, all non-Hero units in the army gain: ; +15 Experience each Turn.

## Foraging Training

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Foraging Training**: While army leader, all non-hero units in the army gain: ; Regenerate +10 HP Hit Points per Turn.

## Logistics Training

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Logistics Training**: While army leader, all non-Hero units in army have: ; -20% base Unit Upkeep

## Offensive Formation

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Offensive Formation**: While army leader, all non-Hero units in the army grant: ; +10% damage to the attacks of adjacent friendly units.
