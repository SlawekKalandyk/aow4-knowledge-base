# Hero skills: Primal subculture avatars

## Avatar of the Ash Sabertooth

(type normal; DLC PRIMALFURY)


- **Avatar of the Ash Sabertooth**: At the start of combat in a province with Ashlands, all friendly units with Ash Sabertooth's Boon start combat with 3 Fury of the Ash Sabertooth.
