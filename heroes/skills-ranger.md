# Hero skills: Ranger

## Barrage Attacks

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

The third Missile attack in a turn does an additional 15 Physical Damage to the target and adjacent enemies.

## Big Game Hunter

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

Missile attacks now do +1 Physical Damage for each Unit tier of the target.
Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Blood Trail

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

Flanking attacks now inflict Bleeding and Marked.
Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Critical Second Wind

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Critical Second Wind**: When the hero does a Critical Hit, it gains Strengthened and heals 10 Temporary Hit Points.

## Critical Skirmisher

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Critical Skirmisher**: When the hero does a Critical Hit, subsequent Critical Hit will do an additional +10% damage. This effect stacks up to 5 times.

## Culling Hunter

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

Missile attacks now do +2 Physical Damage per Negative Status Effect on the target (maximum +5).
Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Defense

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

- +1 Defense
- +10% Evasion

## Defense

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

- +1 Defense
- +10% Evasion

## Disruptive Snapshot

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

Snapshot now:

- Cancels Defense Mode
- Removes Retaliation Attack

## Ensnaring Hunter

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

Missile attacks now have a base 90% chance of inflicting Immobilized.
Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Evasive Strikes

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

Melee attacks no longer trigger retaliations.

## Exploit Weakness

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

The hero now does +20% damage to units with a Negative Status Effect.

## Fatal Strikes

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

Melee attacks now do more damage as the target loses health, up to +50%.

## Flanking Skirmisher

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

Flanking attacks now deal +20% more damage.

## Focused Aim

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

If this hero is not Engaged in Melee when its turn starts, then it gains +30% Accuracy on its Missile attacks until it moves.

## Focused Hunt

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

If this hero is not Engaged in Melee when its turn starts, then it gains +20% Critical Hit Chance on its Missile attacks until it moves.

## Go For The Kill

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)


- **Go For The Kill** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Attacks deal +50% damage until the end of the current turn. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Support

## Grim Executioner

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

When this unit kills another, it gains 3 Fortune.

## Invigorating Takedown

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

Killing an enemy unit grants the hero 1 Strengthened and 2 Regeneration

## Keen Edge

(type normal; DLC Base)

+20% Critical Hit Chance.

## Kill Shot

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

Killing an enemy unit resets all ability cooldowns. Can only trigger once per turn.

## Knockback Retaliations

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Knockback Retaliations**: Melee retaliation attacks now knock targets back 1 hex.

## Knockback Snapshot

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

Snapshot now knocks targets back 1 hex and can be used while in an enemy Zone of Control.

## Martial Expertise

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

The hero gains:

- Melee and Missile attacks deal +10% damage
- +1 Defense
- +10% Evasion

## Mastered Focus

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

If this hero is not Engaged in Melee when its turn starts, then it gains +20% damage on its Missile attacks until it moves.

## Melee Training

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)

Melee attacks now inflict Sundered Defense.

## Miasma Shot

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

Sniper Shot and Scatter Shot now leave Afflicting Miasma and apply Weakened to targets in a 1-hex radius.

## Opening Strikes

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)


- **Opening Strikes**: Melee attacks now do up to +50% extra damage; this bonus is reduced as the target loses health.

## Piercing Shot

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

Sniper Shot and Scatter Shot now bypass 50% of the target's Defense.

## Plague Shot

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)

Missile attacks now have a base 90% chance of inflicting Diseased on all enemies in a 1-hex radius.

## Ranger 2

## Scatter Shot

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Scatter Shot** (damage 18 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage in a 1-hex radius. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Missile, Base

## Skirmisher Training

(type normal; DLC Base)

The hero gains:

- Slippery
- Swift

## Slip Away

(type normal; requires ; DLC Base)


- **Slip Away**: When this unit's HP Hit Points are reduced to zero, it is displaced by 3 hexes and heals +15 Temporary Hit Points.  Works once per battle. Doesn't work when Embarked.

## Snapshot

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Snapshot** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, free action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Missile, Base

## Sniper Shot

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Sniper Shot** (damage 40 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 6, full action): Fire a ranged attack at the target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot

## Thorn Shot

(type normal; requires , ; DLC Base)
