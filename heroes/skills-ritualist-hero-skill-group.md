# Hero skills: Ritualist - Hero Skill Group

## Empowering Bond

(type normal; requires Support Resistance II; DLC Base)


- **Empowering Bond** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Link with the target friendly unit, while active: ; Target gains +2 Retaliation Attacks, +30 Accuracy, and Frenzy. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 0 Turns Requires: Support

## Enveloping Wildgrowth

(type normal; requires any of Grasping Wildgrowth, Support Resistance I; can't be taken with Living Wildgrowth; DLC Base)

Enemy units in the area of effect of Hostile Wildgrowth and Protective Wildgrowth:

- Have a base 90% chance of gaining Blind.
- Have a base 90% chance of gaining Immobilized.

## Healing Support II

(type normal; requires any of Reincarnate, Master of Healing, Swift Leader; can't be taken with Lifeseeder, Rituals of Strength, Protector's Fury; DLC Base)

Support abilities now heal targets for 10 Temporary Hit Points in addition to their regular effects.

## Inner Grace

(type normal; requires any of Support Resistance II, Attuned Wildgrowth; DLC Base)


- **Inner Grace**: At the start of its turn, the unit gains Grace if it doesn't already have it.

## Master of Healing

(type normal; requires any of Inner Grace, Empowering Bond; DLC Base)

While army leader, all units heal +15 HP Hit Points per World Map Turn.

## Protector's Fury

(type normal; requires any of Master of Healing, Reincarnate, Polymorph; can't be taken with Healing Support II, Rituals of Strength, Lifeseeder; DLC Base)

Support abilities now also apply Steadfast.

## Support Resistance I

(type normal; requires any of Hostile Wildgrowth, Protective Wildgrowth; DLC Base)

The hero gains:

- +1 Resistance
- +2 Status Resistance

When the unit enters Defense Mode these bonuses are given to adjacent allies.

## Support Resistance II

(type normal; requires any of Reclaiming Attacks, Regenerating Support; DLC Base)

The hero gains:

- +1 Resistance
- +2 Status Resistance

When the unit enters Defense Mode these bonuses are given to adjacent allies.

## Support Resistance III

(type normal; requires Bolstering Support; DLC Base)

The hero gains:

- +2 Resistance
- +3 Status Resistance

When the unit enters Defense Mode these bonuses are given to adjacent allies.
