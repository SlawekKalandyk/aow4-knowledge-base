# Hero skills: Spellblade

A Hero Class that uses both Melee and Magic attacks to deal magic damage from a distance and physical damage when the enemy gets close.

Compatible weapons:

- Great Weapon
- One Handed Weapon
- Shield
- Fist Weapon
- Polearm Weapon
- Skirmisher Weapon
- Ranged Weapon

## Adaptive Armor

(type normal; requires Melee Mage; DLC Base)


- **Adaptive Armor**: Once per Turn, when this unit is damaged by Magic attacks or spells it gains: ; Bolstered Resistance; Status Protection

## Aethermancy

(type normal; requires any of Devastating Evocation, Sundering Evocation; DLC Base)

All attacks:

- Gain 60% base chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn.

## Arcane Skirmisher

(type normal; DLC Base)

- Melee attacks and Magic Strikes grant Prepared Spell for 2 Turn.
- Other Magic attacks grant Prepared Strike for 2 Turn.

## Arcfire Burst

(type normal; requires Melee Mage; DLC Base)


- **Arcfire Burst** (damage 15 Lightning 15 Fire, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, full action): All enemies within 2 hexes of you take: ; 15 Fire Damage; 15 Lightning Damage  Enter defense mode. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Magic

## Cascading Evocation

(type normal; requires Fire Evoker; can't be taken with Sundering Evocation, Devastating Evocation; DLC Base)

Modify and enhance Fire Evocation:

- Fire Evocation cascades to adjacent enemy units and is repeated.
- Fire Evocation inflicts guaranteed Burning.

## Critical Second Wind

(type normal; requires any of Grim Executioner, Resistance; DLC Base)


- **Critical Second Wind**: When the hero does a Critical Hit, it gains Strengthened and heals 10 Temporary Hit Points.

## Critical Skirmisher

(type normal; requires any of Spellblade I, Keen Edge, Skirmisher Training; can't be taken with Flanking Skirmisher; DLC Base)


- **Critical Skirmisher**: When the hero does a Critical Hit, subsequent Critical Hit will do an additional +10% damage. This effect stacks up to 5 times.

## Devastating Evocation

(type normal; requires any of Fire Evoker, Lightning Evoker; can't be taken with Cascading Evocation, Sundering Evocation; DLC Base)

Modify and enhance this hero's Evocation:

- Fire Evocation now deals 24 Fire Damage.
- Lightning Evocation now deals 27 Lightning Damage.

## Electromancy

(type normal; requires any of Cascading Evocation, Devastating Evocation, Sundering Evocation; DLC Base)

All attacks:

- Gain 90% base chance of inflicting Electrified for 3 Turn

## Evasive Strikes

(type normal; requires any of Keen Edge, Skirmisher Training, Vigor; can't be taken with Knockback Retaliations; DLC Base)

Melee attacks and Magic Strikes no longer trigger retaliations.

## Fatal Strikes

(type normal; requires any of Critical Second Wind, Resistance, Retaliator; can't be taken with Opening Strikes; DLC Base)

Melee attacks and Magic Strikes now do more damage as the target loses health, up to +50%.

## Fire Evoker

(type normal; can't be taken with Lightning Evoker; DLC Base)


- **Fire Evocation** (damage 12 Fire, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage in a 1-hex radius. Burning: Base 90% chance of inflicting Burning for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Magic, Single Shot

## Flanking Skirmisher

(type normal; requires any of Spellblade I, Keen Edge; can't be taken with Critical Skirmisher; DLC Base)

Flanking attacks now deal +20% more damage.

## Frontline Fighter

(type normal; requires Arcane Skirmisher; can't be taken with Melee Training; DLC Base)


- **Frontline Fighter**: Gain +1 Defense and +1 Resistance against non-flanking attacks.

## Go For The Kill

(type normal; requires any of Weaver, Opening Strikes, Fatal Strikes; DLC Base)


- **Go For The Kill** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Attacks deal +50% damage until the end of the current turn. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Support

## Grim Executioner

(type normal; requires any of Flanking Skirmisher, Critical Skirmisher, Evasive Strikes; DLC Base)

When this unit kills another, it gains 3 Fortune.

## Keen Edge

(type normal; requires any of Melee Training, Frontline Fighter; DLC Base)

+20% Critical Hit Chance.

## Knockback Retaliations

(type normal; requires any of Skirmisher Training, Vigor; can't be taken with Evasive Strikes; DLC Base)


- **Knockback Retaliations**: Melee and Magic Strike retaliation attacks now knock targets back 1 hex.

## Lightning Evoker

(type normal; can't be taken with Fire Evoker; DLC Base)


- **Lightning Evocation** (damage 15 Lightning, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage to target enemy unit and 2 others within 3 hexes. Electrified: Base 90% chance of inflicting Electrified for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Magic

## Melee Mage

(type normal; requires any of Wildfire Strikes, Spell Penetration, Quick Phase; DLC Base)


- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.

## Melee Training

(type normal; requires Arcane Skirmisher; can't be taken with Frontline Fighter; DLC Base)

Melee attacks and Magic Strikes now inflict Sundered Resistance.
Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Opening Strikes

(type normal; requires any of Spellblade Expertise, Critical Second Wind, Resistance; can't be taken with Fatal Strikes; DLC Base)


- **Opening Strikes**: Melee attacks and Magic Strikes now do up to +50% extra damage; this bonus is reduced as the target loses health.

## Pyromancy

(type normal; requires any of Cascading Evocation, Devastating Evocation; DLC Base)

All attacks:

- Gain 90% base chance of inflicting Burning for 3 Turn.

## Quick Phase

(type normal; requires Aethermancy; DLC Base)


- **Quick Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Makes the caster teleport to target hex. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Teleport, Movement

## Resistance

(type normal; requires any of Resistance, Grim Executioner; DLC Base)

- +1 Resistance
- +2 Status Resistance

## Resistance

(type normal; requires any of Critical Skirmisher, Evasive Strikes, Knockback Retaliations; DLC Base)

- +1 Resistance
- +2 Status Resistance

## Retaliator

(type normal; requires any of Resistance, Vigor; DLC Base)

Gain +1 Retaliation Attack.

## Skirmisher Training

(type normal; requires any of Melee Training, Frontline Fighter; DLC Base)

The hero gains:

- Slippery
- Swift

## Spell Penetration

(type normal; requires Electromancy; DLC Base)

All attacks ignore 1 Resistance and 1 Status Resistance.

## Spellblade Expertise

(type normal; requires any of Adaptive Armor, Spellblade II, Grim Executioner; DLC Base)

- Melee and Magic attacks gain +10% damage.
- +1 Defense
- +10% Evasion

## Spellblade I

(type normal; requires Melee Training; DLC Base)

Melee and Magic attacks gain +10% damage.

## Spellblade II

(type normal; requires Flanking Skirmisher; DLC Base)

Melee and Magic attacks gain +10% damage.

## Sundering Evocation

(type normal; requires Lightning Evoker; can't be taken with Cascading Evocation, Devastating Evocation; DLC Base)

Modify and enhance Lightning Evocation:

- Lightning Evocation is now also a debuff ability.
- Lightning Evocation inflicts guaranteed Sundered Defense.
- Lightning Evocation inflicts guaranteed Sundered Resistance.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires Knockback Retaliations; DLC Base)

+15 maximum HP Hit Points.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires Frontline Fighter; DLC Base)

+15 maximum HP Hit Points.

## Weaver

(type normal; requires any of Arcfire Burst, Spellblade Expertise; DLC Base)


- **Weaver** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): - Refresh all your ability cooldowns and once-per-combat abilities.; Enter Defense Mode. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Can only be used once per battle. Starts on cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Can't be refreshed

## Wildfire Strikes

(type normal; requires Pyromancy; DLC Base)

Base attacks gain:

- 2 Fire Damage to the target and adjacent enemies.
- 60% base chance of inflicting Burning on the target and adjacent enemies.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
