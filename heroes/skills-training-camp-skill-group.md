# Hero skills: Training Camp Skill Group

## Chaotic Rebuke

(type normal; requires Pure Defense; DLC GIANTKINGS)


- **Chaotic Rebuke**: Enemy attackers within 3 hexes have a base 60% chance of gaining a random status effect.
