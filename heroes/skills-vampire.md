# Hero skills: Vampire

## Deep Bond

(type normal; requires ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

- The Drain Familiar heal is increased to 30 Temporary Hit Points.
- Drain Familiar grants the targeted unit:

- 3 Regeneration for 3 Turns.
- Resurgence.

## Deep Hunger

(type normal; requires , ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

Reduce the cooldown of your Blood Drain ability by 1 Turn.

## Drain Familiar

(type normal; requires , ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)


- **Drain Familiar** (damage 25 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Deal unblockable damage to target friendly unit. Then the caster: ; Heals for 25 Temporary Hit Points.; Gains 2 Strengthened for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 1 Turns

## Draining Bite

(type normal; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)


- **Draining Bite** (damage 28 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, leave-one action): Deal damage to target unit. Blood Drain: Heal for 50% of damage dealt if target isn't immune to Bleeding. Signature Power: +10% damage per Signature Skill learned. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Melee, Blood Drain, Single Shot

## Draining Charm

(type normal; requires ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

- Blood Drain abilities now have a chance of inflicting Mind-Controlled for 3 Turns instead of Stunned.
- Still attempts to inflict Stunned against Control Loss Immunity units.

## Mesmerizing Drain

(type normal; requires ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

- Chance of inflicting Draining Charm increases to base 90%.
- Instead of Mind-Controlled, it now inflicts Dominated.

## Mist Away

(type normal; requires , ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)


- **Mist Away**: When this unit's HP Hit Points are reduced to zero, it is displaced by 3 hexes and heals +20 Temporary Hit Points. Works once per battle.

## Profane Strength

(type normal; requires ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

- Drain Familiar grants the targeted unit 3 Strengthened.
- Drain Familiar gains the Support tag.

## Reaching Drain

(type normal; requires ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

Siphon Blood gains +2 range.

## Sanguine Extraction

(type normal; requires , ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

Your Blood Drain ability:

- Ignores 50% Defense.
- Ignores 50% Resistance.

## Siphon Blood

(type normal; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)


- **Siphon Blood** (damage 18 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 3, leave-one action): Deal damage to target unit. Blood Drain: Heal for 50% of damage dealt if target isn't immune to Bleeding. Signature Power: +10% damage per Signature Skill learned. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Blood Drain, Magic, Missile, Single Shot

## Staggering Bite

(type normal; requires ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

Draining Bite:

- Now removes Defense Mode and Retaliation Attacks unless the target has Charge Resistance.
- Gains Momentum, increasing damage by 20% per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.

## Stunning Drain

(type normal; requires , ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

Blood Drain abilities gain a base 60% chance of inflicting Stunned for 1 Turn.

## Vampiric Flight

(type normal; requires ; DLC THRONESOFBLOOD)

When not mounted this unit gains:

- Flying movement.
- Fast Movement.
- Slippery.
- 30% evasion against Missile and Magic attacks.
