# Hero skills: Warrior

A Hero Class that specializes in charge attacks, dealing damage, and gaining strength through fighting.

Compatible weapons:

- Great Weapon
- One Handed Weapon
- Shield
- Fist Weapon
- Polearm Weapon
- Skirmisher Weapon

## Berserker

(type normal; can't be taken with Knight; DLC Base)

- Melee attacks gain +10% damage.
- +10 maximum Hit Points.

## Berserker's Rage

(type normal; requires any of Reckless Rage, Execute; can't be taken with Grim Executioner; DLC Base)


- **Berserker's Rage**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 33% or lower, it gains for 1 Turn: Berserk; Steadfast

## Brutal Charge

(type normal; requires Prepared Charge; can't be taken with Guarded Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a charge attack after moving, then it:

- Displaces the target unit and move onto its hex.
- Has a base 90% chance of inflicting Stunned.

## Brutal Parry

(type normal; requires Undying Warrior; DLC Base)


- **Brutal Parry**: Attackers within 1 hexes suffer 5 Physical Damage and have a base 90% chance of suffering Bleeding for 3 Turns.

## Cleaving Sweep

(type normal; requires any of Fighting, Vigor; DLC Base)


- **Cleaving Sweep** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to units in a 1-hex cone. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base

## Committed Charge

(type normal; requires any of Weakening Charge, Relentless Charge; can't be taken with Taunting Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a Charge attack after moving, they gain +1 Retaliation Attack.

## Critical Momentum

(type normal; requires Keen Edge; can't be taken with Execute, Reckless Rage; DLC Base)


- **Critical Momentum**: When this hero makes a Critical Hit it: Heals 10 Temporary Hit Points; Gains Hastened

## Defense

(type normal; requires any of Committed Charge, Vigor; DLC Base)

- +1 Defense
- +5% Evasion

## Distracting Charge

(type normal; requires Guarded Charge; can't be taken with Relentless Charge, Weakening Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a Charge attack after moving, they now inflict Distracted.

## Execute

(type normal; requires any of Sundering Strikes, Keen Edge; can't be taken with Reckless Rage, Critical Momentum; DLC Base)


- **Execute** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Slight chance of instantly killing an injured target. This chance increases the lower the target's Hit Points are. Cooldown: 2 Turns

## Fighting

(type normal; requires any of Cleaving Sweep, Reckless Rage; DLC Base)

Melee attacks gain +10% damage.

## Fighting

(type normal; requires any of Prepared Charge, Second Wind; DLC Base)

Melee attacks gain +10% damage.

## Frenzy

(type normal; requires Berserker; DLC Base)


- **Frenzy**: When this unit lands an attack, it gains a stack of Strengthened. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Graceful Charge

(type normal; requires Sweeping Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a Charge attack after moving, they gain +1 Grace.

## Grim Executioner

(type normal; requires any of Execute, Critical Momentum; can't be taken with Berserker's Rage; DLC Base)

When this unit kills another, it gains 3 Fortune.

## Guarded Charge

(type normal; requires Prepared Charge; can't be taken with Brutal Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a Charge attack after moving, they enter Defense Mode.

## Keen Edge

(type normal; requires Frenzy; can't be taken with Sundering Strikes; DLC Base)

+20% Critical Hit Chance.

## Killing Momentum

(type normal; requires any of Willbreaker, Warseeker; DLC Base)


- **Killing Momentum**: Unit regains 1 Action Point when killing another unit with a Magic, Melee, or Missile attack. Works once per Turn.

## Knight

(type normal; can't be taken with Berserker; DLC Base)

- +1 Defense
- +10 maximum Hit Points

## Prepared Charge

(type normal; requires Knight; DLC Base)

If this hero is not Engaged in Melee when its turn starts, then its next charge attack will do 20% extra damage.

## Reckless Rage

(type normal; requires any of Vigor, Sundering Strikes; can't be taken with Critical Momentum, Execute; DLC Base)


- **Reckless Rage** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Gain: +30% attack damage for 3 Turn; 1 Sundered Defense; 1 Sundered Resistance. Can only be used once per battle.

## Relentless Charge

(type normal; requires any of Brutal Charge, Fighting; can't be taken with Weakening Charge, Distracting Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a Charge attack after moving, they now inflict 2x Sundered Defense.

## Second Wind

(type normal; requires any of Knight, Berserker; DLC Base)


- **Second Wind** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): +1 hex of movement for 1 Turn. Slippery for 1 Turn. Heal for 15 Temporary Hit Point. This ability is reusable after each kill. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Second Wind

## Sundering Strikes

(type normal; requires Frenzy; can't be taken with Keen Edge; DLC Base)

Makes base attacks gain:

- Base 60% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense.
- Demolisher.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Sweeping Charge

(type normal; requires any of Taunting Charge, Committed Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a Charge attack after moving, they now inflict 8 Physical Damage to enemies adjacent to the target.

## Taunting Charge

(type normal; requires any of Distracting Charge, Weakening Charge; can't be taken with Committed Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a Charge attack after moving, they now have a base 90% chance of inflicting Taunted.

## Undying Warrior

(type normal; requires any of Berserker's Rage, Grim Executioner; DLC Base)


- **Resurgence**: When the unit dies in combat, it is revived at the end of combat if its owner is victorious. Revived units are brought back with 50% of their maximum Hit Points.

## Unyielding

(type normal; requires any of Vigor, Fighting; DLC Base)


- **Unyielding**: At the start of its turn, the unit dispels 1 Negative Status Effect.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires any of Fighting, Berserker's Rage; DLC Base)

+15 maximum Hit Points.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires any of Relentless Charge, Cleaving Sweep; DLC Base)

+15 maximum Hit Points.

## Vigor

(type normal; requires any of Second Wind, Frenzy; DLC Base)

+15 maximum Hit Points.

## Warseeker

(type normal; requires any of Unyielding, Vigor, Brutal Parry; can't be taken with Willbreaker; DLC Base)


- **Warseeker**: When this unit kills an enemy: Gains Warseeker for 2 Turns. This effect stacks up to 3 times.

## Weakening Charge

(type normal; requires any of Guarded Charge, Brutal Charge; can't be taken with Distracting Charge, Relentless Charge; DLC Base)

If the hero uses a Charge attack after moving, they now inflict guaranteed 2x Weakened.

## Willbreaker

(type normal; requires any of Graceful Charge, Defense, Unyielding; can't be taken with Warseeker; DLC Base)

Gain:

- Vicious Killer
- Willbreaker
