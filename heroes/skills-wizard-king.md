# Hero skills: Wizard King

## Amplified Evocation

(type normal; requires Evoker's Ritual; can't be taken with Empowered Attuned Godir; DLC Base)


- **Amplified Evocation**: Damage Spells cast in combat where this unit is present deal +20% damage.

## Amplified Healing

(type normal; requires Enchanter's Ritual; can't be taken with Empowered Attuned Godir; DLC Base)


- **Amplified Healing**: - Healing Spells cast in combat where the Wizard King is present heal +50% more.; Unlock the Arcane Renewal spell.

## Attuned Godir

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Attuned Godir**: Whenever you cast a spell, this unit gains: ; +1 Strengthened; +1 Regeneration

## Channeling Ritual

(type normal; DLC Base)


- **Channeling Ritual** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, full action): Allows you to cast an additional Spell this Turn. This effect does not stack. Cooldown: 2 Turns Starts on cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Can't be refreshed

## Defensive Ritual

(type normal; requires Channeling Ritual; can't be taken with Quickened Ritual; DLC Base)


- **Channeling Ritual** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): - Allows you to cast an additional Spell this Turn.; Heal for 10 Temporary Hit Points.; Enter defense mode.; This effect does not stack. Cooldown: 1 Turns Starts on cooldown Requires: Can't be refreshed, Support

## Empowered Attuned Godir

(type normal; requires any of Evoker's Ritual, Enchanter's Ritual; can't be taken with Amplified Evocation, Amplified Healing; DLC Base)


- **Empowered Attuned Godir**: Whenever you cast a spell, this unit gains: ; +2 Strengthened; +2 Regeneration; +1 Bolstered Resistance  This replaces Attuned Godir.

## Enchanter's Ritual

(type normal; requires Attuned Godir; can't be taken with Evoker's Ritual; DLC Base)


- **Enchanter's Ritual**: Adds an effect to Channeling Ritual: ; Healing Spells and Buff Spells become Overcharged for 1.

## Evoker's Ritual

(type normal; requires Attuned Godir; can't be taken with Enchanter's Ritual; DLC Base)


- **Evoker's Ritual**: Adds an effect to Channeling Ritual: ; Damage Spells and Debuff Spells become Overcharged for 1 Turn.

## Quickened Ritual

(type normal; requires Channeling Ritual; can't be taken with Defensive Ritual; DLC Base)


- **Channeling Ritual** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Allows you to cast an additional Spell this Turn. This effect does not stack. Cooldown: 2 Turns Starts on cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Can't be refreshed
