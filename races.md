# Races (appearances)

In AoW4 a race is an appearance plus form traits. Appearances are cosmetic; the gameplay comes from form traits (see [traits/form](traits/form/_index.md)).

- **Dwarfkin** (Base): Stout, hardy and stubborn beings who are used to living in the most rugged regions of their world.
- **Owlkin** (SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES): Wide-eyed birds of prey who puff up their feathers when threatened. Their hoots signal reverence or distress.
- **Elfkin** (Base): Slender, pointy-eared beings whose kin were the first to inhabit the realms.
- **Feline** (Base): Cat-like beings who move with feline grace.
- **Goblinoid** (Base): Small, hunched beings who love to rampage around in groups. Some are strangely fascinated by sheep.
- **Avian** (EMPIRESANDASHES): Feathered, beaked beings whose families are usually defined as "Nests". Their sing-song calls can carry over entire forests.
- **Syron** (ELDRITCHREALMS): Slender, pale beings whose origins lie within the dark depths of the Astral Sea.
- **Ogrekin** (WAYSOFWAR): Imposing beings whose teeth can grow to sizes almost as impressive as their horns.

- **Insectoid** (ELDRITCHREALMS): Carapaced beings who communicate through a combination of sound and subtle chemical odors.
- **Halfling** (Base): Stocky, happy-go-lucky beings who enjoy wild adventures just as much as a well-deserved nap.
- **Human** (Base): Humanoid beings who are organized and entrepreneurial, albeit impatient at times.
- **Molekin** (Base): Beings who have evolved from underground dwellers and retain their heightened sense of touch.
- **Orcoid** (Base): Passionate and strong beings who have faced great prejudice as a result of their kin's history.
- **Ratkin** (Base): Rodent-like beings whose groups are often referred to as mischiefs.
- **Toadkin** (Base): Stout amphibian-like beings, born with vocal sacs that allow them to communicate through loud croaking.
- **Lizardfolk** (DRAGONLORDS): Slender, scaled creatures who use their tongues to get a sense of their surroundings.
- **Harefolk** (RISEFROMRUIN): Buck-toothed beings, who consider the length of their ears to be a sign of wisdom and grace.
- **Lupine** (PRIMALFURY): Wolf-like beings who communicate through barks and howls.
- **Goatkin** (PRIMALFURY): Goat-like beings who take pride in their elaborate horns.
- **Ancient** (ARCHONPROPHECY): Venerable humanoid beings, corrupted ages ago. They overcame their decay through magical enhancements.
- **Elysian** (ARCHONPROPHECY): Touched by a higher power, these humanoid beings have ascended both their bodies and minds.
- **Sharkkin** (SILVERSEAS): Hulking landsharks with deadly maws. They take pride in their fins, while their scars command respect.
- **Aqualon** (SILVERSEAS): An aquatic people possessing uncanny beauty, who share ancestry with mythical creatures from the Astral Seas.
