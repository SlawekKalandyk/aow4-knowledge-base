# Rulers

## Ruler origins

### Champion

DLC: Base

A Ruler risen from the ranks of their people.

A Ruler risen from the ranks of their people.

- Mortal Champions have access to unique army leadership hero skills.
- Mortal Champion Rulers gain +20% Experience.
- Start with +100 Relation with Free Cities.
- Mortal Champion Rulers start with the Spur to Action ability in Combat.
- Mortal Champions are Champion Governors.

### Wizard King

DLC: Base

Origin spells: [Arcane Renewal](spells/arcane-renewal.md)

A powerful Wizard seeking dominion over a new realm.

A powerful Wizard seeking dominion over a new realm.

- Wizard Kings have access to unique Combat Spell enhancing hero skills.
- All Conduits grant +2 Manas.
- Start with +10 Combat Casting Points and +10 World Map Casting Points.
- Wizard Kings start with the Channeling Ritual ability in Combat.
- Wizard Kings are Wizard Governors.

### Dragon Lord

DLC: DRAGONLORDS

A mighty Dragon seeking new realms to shape in their vision.

A mighty Dragon seeking new realms to shape in their vision.

- Dragon Lords have access to unique combat Hero Skills.
- Dragon Lords gain powerful transformation Hero Signature Skills based on empire Affinities.
- Gain extra Gold through their Dragon Hoard.
- Dragon Lords are Dragon Governors.
- Dragon Lords have a 30 Gold Unit Upkeep and cannot equip most Hero Items.

### Elder Vampire

DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Origin spells: [Desecration Ritual](spells/desecration-ritual.md), [Pall of Darkness Ritual](spells/pall-of-darkness-ritual.md), [Blood Moon Ritual](spells/blood-moon-ritual.md), [Vivisection Ritual](spells/vivisection-ritual.md), [Soul Drain Ritual](spells/soul-drain-ritual.md), [Blood Transfer Ritual](spells/blood-transfer-ritual.md)

A deathless Vampire who drains the life from enemies under cover of night.

A deathless Vampire who drains the life from enemies under cover of night.

- An Undead unit with an aversion to light.
- Has access to Vampire Castle: Foundation which replaces the Wizard Tower: Foundation and grants the use of Thralls to cast powerful ritual Spells.
- Drains 20% of the combat Experience earned by Turned Vampire heroes.
- Faction is immune to Sunless terrain's penalties to City Stability and Morale.
- Elder Vampires are Elder Vampire Governors.
- Elder Vampires have a 5 Gold Unit Upkeep

### Eldritch Sovereign

DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

Origin spells: [Astral Wisp Ritual](spells/astral-wisp-ritual.md), [Mage Bane Ritual](spells/mage-bane-ritual.md), [Astral Siphoner Ritual](spells/astral-siphoner-ritual.md), [Clairvoyance Ritual](spells/clairvoyance-ritual.md), [Phantasmal Ritual](spells/phantasmal-ritual.md), [Mass Phantasmal Ritual](spells/mass-phantasmal-ritual.md), [Dark Knowledge Ritual](spells/dark-knowledge-ritual.md), [Quickening Ritual](spells/quickening-ritual.md), [Eldritch Rituals](spells/eldritch-rituals.md), [Enthrall Population](spells/enthrall-population.md)

An eldritch sorcerer commanding powers beyond mortal comprehension.

An eldritch sorcerer commanding powers beyond mortal comprehension.

- Eldritch Sovereigns have access to powerful control Hero Skills.
- Eldritch Sovereigns gain powerful unique Hero Signature Skills.
- Use Thralls to cast powerful ritual Spells.
- Start with Clairvoyance Ritual and Enthrall Population.
- Eldritch Sovereigns are Eldritch Governors.
- Eldritch Sovereigns have a 5 Mana Unit Upkeep and cannot equip Leg and Mount items.

### Giant King

DLC: GIANTKINGS

Origin spells: [Summon Ice Runestele](spells/summon-ice-runestele.md), [Summon Lava Runestele](spells/summon-lava-runestele.md), [Summon Storm Runestele](spells/summon-storm-runestele.md), [Summon Rock Runestele](spells/summon-rock-runestele.md)

A formidable Giant who harnesses the elemental force of the Fire, Rock, Storm, or Frost Clan to refashion realms.

A formidable Giant who harnesses the elemental force of the Fire, Rock, Storm, or Frost Clan to refashion realms.

- Capital city starts with Wizard Tower: Item Forge.
- Has access to a Runestele spell that slowly alters terrain to the Giant's preference.
- Gains economic bonuses from preferred terrain.
- Giant Kings are Giant King Governors.
- Giant Kings have a 5 Mana Unit Upkeep and cannot equip Mount items.

## Ruler subtypes

- **Astral Dragon** (Dragon Lord, Astral 1, DLC DRAGONLORDS): Astral dragon claw item; Gain an additional Astral Affinity point. Breath and Claws deal Lightning Damage.
- **Chaos Dragon** (Dragon Lord, Chaos 1, DLC DRAGONLORDS): Chaos dragon claw item; Gain an additional Chaos Affinity point. Breath and Claws deal Fire Damage.
- **Materium Dragon** (Dragon Lord, Materium 1, DLC DRAGONLORDS): Materium dragon claw item; Gain an additional Materium Affinity point. Breath and Claws deal Physical Damage.
- **Nature Dragon** (Dragon Lord, Nature 1, DLC DRAGONLORDS): Nature dragon claw item; Gain an additional Nature Affinity point. Breath and Claws deal Blight Damage.
- **Order Dragon** (Dragon Lord, Order 1, DLC DRAGONLORDS): Order dragon claw item; Gain an additional Order Affinity point. Breath and Claws deal Spirit Damage.
- **Shadow Dragon** (Dragon Lord, Shadow 1, DLC DRAGONLORDS): Shadow dragon claw item; Gain an additional Shadow Affinity point. Breath and Claws deal Frost Damage.
- **Rock Giant King** (Giant King, DLC GIANTKINGS): An adamant Giant who sculpts realms by raising mountains. Capital city starts with Wizard Tower: Item Forge. Has the Summon Rock Runestele spell. Provinces with Mountains or Stalagmites provide +3 Production. Giant Kings are Giant King Governors. Giant Kings have a 5 Mana Unit Upkeep and cannot equip Mount items.
- **Fire Giant King** (Giant King, DLC GIANTKINGS): A searing Giant who cleaves realms by molding lava. Capital city starts with Wizard Tower: Item Forge. Has the Summon Lava Runestele spell. Can build Farms on Ashlands. Provinces with Lava or Lava Chasms provide +2 Production and don't apply the extra City Stability penalty normally found in these. Giant Kings are Giant King Governors. Giant Kings have a 5 Mana Unit Upkeep and cannot equip Mount items.
- **Storm Giant King** (Giant King, DLC GIANTKINGS): A thunderous Giant who levels realms by flattening peaks. Capital city starts with Wizard Tower: Item Forge. Has the Summon Storm Runestele spell. Provinces with Rivers or Water provide +2 Food and +1 Mana. Giant Kings are Giant King Governors. Giant Kings have a 5 Mana Unit Upkeep and cannot equip Mount items.
- **Frost Giant King** (Giant King, DLC GIANTKINGS): An icy Giant who freezes realms by shaping glaciers. Capital city starts with Wizard Tower: Item Forge. Has the Summon Ice Runestele spell. Can build Farms on Snow. Provinces with Snow or Ice provide +2 Manas. Giant Kings are Giant King Governors. Giant Kings have a 5 Mana Unit Upkeep and cannot equip Mount items.
- **Elder Vampire Pureblood** (Elder Vampire, DLC THRONESOFBLOOD): A Vampire descended from the Archvampires of Drevanya. Has unique appearance options.
- **Elder Vampire Hybrid** (Elder Vampire, DLC THRONESOFBLOOD): A Vampire descended from one of the many races who inhabit the Astral Sea. Can be of any form.
