# Abducting Cyclone

- Type: [Combat] Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 15 Mana
- Casting points: 20
- Tome: Tome of Winds
- Granted by: [Followers of Materium](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Pulls the closest enemy within 3 hexes to target empty hex. They have a base 90% chance of becoming Stunned for 1 Turn.
