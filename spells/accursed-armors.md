# Accursed Armors

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of Calamity
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Enchanted units gain:

- When a melee range attack hits, the attacker has a 60% chance of being inflicted with Misfortune for 3 Turns.
- +1 Defense.
