# Accursed Blades

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Tome: Tome of the Damned
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit

Enchanted units attacks gain:

- +1 Fire Damage.
- +1 Frost Damage.
- A base 60% chance of inflicting Ghostfire for 3 Turns.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
