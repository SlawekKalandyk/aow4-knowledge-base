# Adaptive Armor

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of Transmutation
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Once per Turn, whenever enchanted units are damaged by Magic attacks or spells, they gain:

- Bolstered Resistance, increasing their resistance to magical damage.
- Status Protection, reducing the chance that the unit will be affected by Negative Status Effects.
