# Age of Astral

- Type: [World Map] Spell Victory
- Casting cost: 800 Mana
- Upkeep: 50 Mana
- Casting points: 400
- DLC: Base

Channel the Age of Astral to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling:

- Your Astral Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, [World Map] World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points.
- Each turn in combat, a random enemy takes 15 mixed Fire, Lightning, and Frost, damage and gets 2 Sundered Resistance and 2 Status Vulnerability.
