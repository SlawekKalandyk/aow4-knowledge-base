# Age of Chaos

- Type: [World Map] Spell Victory
- Casting cost: 800 Mana
- Upkeep: 50 Mana
- Casting points: 400
- DLC: Base

Channel the Age of Chaos to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling:

- Your Chaos Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, [World Map] World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points.
- Each Turn in combat, a random enemy unit sustains 15 damage of a random type and has a base 90% chance of receiving a random Negative Status Effect.
