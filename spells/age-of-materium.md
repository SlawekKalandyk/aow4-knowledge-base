# Age of Materium

- Type: [World Map] Spell Victory
- Casting cost: 800 Mana
- Upkeep: 50 Mana
- Casting points: 400
- DLC: Base

Channel the Age of Materium to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling:

- Your Materium Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, [World Map] World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points.
- Each turn in combat, a friendly unit gains Bolstered Defense and Bolstered Resistance. Repeats 2-4 times.
