# Age of Order

- Type: [World Map] Spell Victory
- Casting cost: 800 Mana
- Upkeep: 50 Mana
- Casting points: 400
- DLC: Base

Channel the Age of Order to reach the Magic Victory. While channeling:

- Your Order Affinity Spells cost -20% Mana, World Map Casting Points, and Combat Casting Points.
- Each turn in combat, a random ally gains 3 random Positive Status Effects and loses all Negative Status Effects.
