# Agonize

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Tome: Tome of Torment
- Granted by: [Dreadful Vampires](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Enemy units in a 1-hex radius:

- Sustain 40 Frost Damage
- Have a base 90% chance of gaining 3 stacks of Torment until end of combat. On failure, inflict 1 stack of Torment instead.
