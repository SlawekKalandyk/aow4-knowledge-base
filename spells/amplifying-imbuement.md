# Amplifying Imbuement

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Tome: Tome of Amplification
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Makes base attacks of enchanted units:

- Deal +3 Lightning Damage.
- Deal 3 Lightning Damage to 2 other targets within 2 hexes.
- Have a base 30% chance of inflicting Sundered Resistance on each target hit for 3 Turns.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
