# Animal Kinship

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of Beasts
- Granted by: [Followers of Nature](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Beasts](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Nature](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Makes the target race more bestial and connected to animals. When adjacent to a friendly Animal, both units are granted:

- +10% damage
- +10% Critical Hit Chance
