# Artica's Ice Age

- Type: World Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Up to 40 random Provinces gain an Ice Age Hazard. At the start of the next turn, those hazards expire and:

- Enemy units sustain 20 Frost Damage.
- Provinces are destroyed.

- The province:

- Gains Snow and Ice.
- Loses Chasm, Swamp, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, Fungus Fields, and Gloom.
