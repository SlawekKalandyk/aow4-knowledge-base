# Ascended Rebirth

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 40
- Tome: Tome of the Archon
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

Target dead non-Hero and non-Celestial unit:

- Revives as a Vigil under the caster's control until the end of combat.
- On revive, all friendly units in a 2-hex radius are healed for 20 Temporary Hit Points.
