# Aspect of the Root

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Glades
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Grants enchanted units:

- The Aspect of the Root ability, which allows units to heal themselves in battle.
