# Astral Attunement

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- Tome: Tome of Astral Convergence
- Granted by: [Masters of Astral](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of the Ethereal](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Links the target race to the Astral Sea, granting them:

- The Ethereal unit type.
- Attunement: Astral Omen, granting a random Positive Status Effect when a spell is cast in combat.
