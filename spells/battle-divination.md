# Battle Divination

- Type: Combat Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Tome: Tome of Prophecies
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

When cast and for the next 2 Turns, apply effect to the battle in the following order:

- All friendly units gain +25% Evasion for the turn.
- All friendly units gain Precognition.
- All friendly units gain +20% damage for the turn.
