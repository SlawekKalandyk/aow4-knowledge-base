# Battlefield Reanimation

- Type: [Combat] Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 50 Souls
- Casting points: 65
- Tome: Tome of the Eternal Lord
- DLC: Base

- All friendly Undead units come back to life with 50% of their total HP Hit Points.
- Non-undead corpses come back to life as Decaying Zombies under your control until the end of battle.
