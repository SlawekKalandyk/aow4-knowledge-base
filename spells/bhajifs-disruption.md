# Bhajif's Disruption

- Type: [Combat] Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Granted by: [Secret Scroll of Bhajif](../world/relics.md) (relic)
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

- Enemies in a 1-hex radius:
- Become Disrupted for 2 Turns.
- Become Mana Drained for 1 Turn.

- Additionally:
- All active combat enchantments are dispelled.
- For the next 2 Turns, Combat Spells cannot be cast.
