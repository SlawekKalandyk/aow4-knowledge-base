# Bind Gold Ancient Wonder

- Type: [World Map] Sustained World Spell
- Casting cost: 400 Mana
- Casting points: 300
- DLC: Base

- Target owned and annexed Gold Ancient Wonder becomes bound.
- While bound, it grants +50 Knowledge.
- A number of bound Golden Ancient Wonders are required for Magic Victory.
- This spell is canceled if the Bound Ancient Wonder is occupied or changes ownership.
