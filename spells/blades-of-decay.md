# Blades of Decay

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of Cycles
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units:

- Inflict Decaying, a damage-over-time effect that decreases healing received.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
