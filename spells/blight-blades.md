# Blight Blades

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Roots
- Granted by: [Initiates of Nature](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Flora](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units deal:

- +2 Blight Damage.
- +10% damage against Poisoned or Decaying units.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
