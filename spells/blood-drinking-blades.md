# Blood Drinking Blades

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of the Blood Rite
- Granted by: [Sanguine Vampires](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit

Attacks of enchanted units:

- Deal +2 Physical Damage.
- When striking units with Bleeding, heal for 3 Temporary Hit Points.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
