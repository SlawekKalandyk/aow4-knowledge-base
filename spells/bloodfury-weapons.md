# Bloodfury Weapons

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Revelry
- Granted by: [Masters of Calamity](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Demons](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Sanguine Vampires](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Ranged Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Grants enchanted units:

- +2 Physical Damage on attacks.
- On kill, adjacent enemies to the target gain 2 Bleeding for 3 Turns.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
