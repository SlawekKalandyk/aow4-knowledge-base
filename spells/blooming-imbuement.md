# Blooming Imbuement

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of Cycles
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units:

- Gain a base 60% chance of inflicting Life Seed for 3 Turns.
- Deal +20% damage against Decaying units.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
