# Bred for War

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of the Warband
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

Transformed units become stronger and more battle ready gaining:

- +10 HP Hit Points.
- +10% Morale resistance to morale loss, plus +10% for each adjacent friendly unit.
