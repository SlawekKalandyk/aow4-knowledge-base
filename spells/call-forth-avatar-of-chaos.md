# Call Forth Avatar of Chaos

- Type: Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 50
- Tome: Tome of the Chaos Lord
- DLC: Base

Summons an avatar of your Ruler into a battle. The avatar has the same abilities as your Ruler and spawns with full Action Points. All Fiends gain +10 Morale.

Cannot be used in battles where your Ruler is present.
