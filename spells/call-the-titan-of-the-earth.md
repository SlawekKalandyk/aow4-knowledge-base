# Call the Titan of the Earth

- Type: [Combat] Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 200 Mana
- Casting points: 65
- Tome: Tome of the Creator
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Earth Titan](../units/earth-titan.md)

- Summons an Earth Titan onto the target unoccupied hex.
- All adjacent enemies lose their Defense Mode and Retaliation Attack.
