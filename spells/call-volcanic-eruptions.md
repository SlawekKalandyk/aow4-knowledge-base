# Call Volcanic Eruptions

- Type: [Combat] Combat Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Granted by: [Volcano](../world/world-structures.md) (world structure)
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

For 3 turns, at the start of your turn, a marker is placed near an enemy. Then, after 3 Turns the ground will erupt, and units within 1 of the marker will suffer 10 Physical Damage and 10 Fire Damage. Random affected hexes will gain On Fire.
