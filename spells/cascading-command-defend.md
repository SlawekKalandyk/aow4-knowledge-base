# Cascading Command: Defend

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- Tome: Tome of the Construct
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

Target friendly Construct or Linked Minds unit:

- Gains Defensive Masters for 1 Turn.
- Has 2 Negative Status Effects dispelled.
- This effect then applies to adjacent friendly Construct or Linked Mind units and is repeated.
