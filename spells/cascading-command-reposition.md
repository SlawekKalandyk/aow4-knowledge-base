# Cascading Command: Reposition

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- Tome: Tome of the Construct
- Granted by: [Masters of Constructs](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

Target friendly Construct or Linked Minds unit gains:

- Hastened
- Strengthened
- This effect then cascades to adjacent friendly Construct or Linked Mind units and is repeated.
