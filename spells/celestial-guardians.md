# Celestial Guardians

- Type: [World Map] Friendly Army Spell
- Casting cost: 200 Mana
- Casting points: 200
- Tome: Tome of the Archon
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

Target leader of an army gains Celestial Guardians for 1 World Map Turn, conjuring 2 Tier III Archon units at the start of combat for the duration.
