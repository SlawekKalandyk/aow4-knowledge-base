# City Wards

- Type: [World Map] Sustained City Spell
- Casting cost: 45 Mana
- Upkeep: 10 Mana
- Casting points: 45
- Tome: Tome of Abjuration
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Target owned city or non-hostile free city:

- Gains +10 City Stability.
- Gains +10 Fortification Health if not under siege.
- If targeting a free city with an owned Whispering Stone, gain +2 Allegiance per turn.
