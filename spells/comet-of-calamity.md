# Comet of Calamity

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 40
- Tome: Tome of Calamity
- Granted by: [Masters of Calamity](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Units in a 2-hex radius:

- Sustain 15 Fire Damage.
- Sustain 15 Frost Damage.
- Have a 50% base chance of becoming Frozen for 1 Turn.
- Gain 2 stacks of Ghostfire for 3 Turns.
