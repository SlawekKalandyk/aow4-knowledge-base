# Compounding Defense

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Tome: Tome of the Construct
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Fighter Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

When adjacent to another unit with Compounding Defense this unit gains:

- +1 Defense
- +1 Resistance
