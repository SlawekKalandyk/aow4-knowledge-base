# Condemnation

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 5 Mana
- Casting points: 10
- Tome: Tome of Zeal
- Granted by: [Initiates of Order](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of the Cleansing Flame](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Target enemy unit:

- Sustains 15 Spirit Damage.
- Becomes Condemned until the end of battle.
