# Conjure Amplification Pylon

- Type: Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 45 Mana
- Casting points: 30
- Tome: Tome of Amplification
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Amplification Pylon](../units/amplification-pylon.md)

Conjures an Amplification Pylon, a stationary structure that deals damage and increases the damage of your spells by +50%.
This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
