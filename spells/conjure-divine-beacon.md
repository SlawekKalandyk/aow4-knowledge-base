# Conjure Divine Beacon

- Type: [Combat] Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- Tome: Tome of the Beacon
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Divine Beacon](../units/divine-beacon.md)

Summons a Divine Beacon. Upon casting and at the start of each turn, friendly units in a 2-hex radius:

- Heal +15 Temporary Hit Points.
- Gain +5 Morale.

This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
