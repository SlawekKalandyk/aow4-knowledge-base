# Consecrating Firestorm

- Type: [World Map] World Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Tome: Tome of the Cleansing Flame
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

In target Province:

- Enemy units sustain 20 Spirit Damage.
- The province gains Consecrating Firestorm for 3 Turns
- If it has an enemy owned Province Improvement, it is Pillaged.
