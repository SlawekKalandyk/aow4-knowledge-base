# Constricting Focus

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of the Tentacle
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS
- Applies to: Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Base Magic attacks of enchanted units:

- Deal +2 Physical Damage
- Gain base 30% chance of inflicting Constricted for 1 Turn.
- If unsuccessful, inflicts Status Vulnerability.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
