# Cosmic Inspiration

- Type: [World Map] City Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Granted by: [Broken Wizard Throne](../world/world-structures.md) (world structure)
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

- Requires target owned City with 3 or more Provinces in its Domain adjacent to the Broken Wizard's Throne.
- Each City can only be targeted once by this Spell.
- Immediately unlocks a random Spell that you do not have access to yet.
