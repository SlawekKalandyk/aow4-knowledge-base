# Cosmic Overdrive

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 180 Mana
- Casting points: 180
- Tome: Tome of the Archmage
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Elemental, Ethereal, Magic Origin, Fey, Merfolk, Umbral Demon

Grants enchanted units:

- +20% damage.
- Negative Status Effects last -1 Turn, with a minimum of 1.
- If enchanted unit is a non-mythic Magic Origin unit, it gains Very Fast Movement.
