# Cosmic Wake

- Type: [World Map] City Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Target Province:

- If it contains an enemy Province Improvement and no Ancient Wonder, it is removed and the Population is lost.
- Enemy Units in the province sustain 4 Fire Damage, Frost Damage, Blight Damage, Spirit Damage, Lightning Damage, and Physical Damage.
