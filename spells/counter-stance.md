# Counter Stance

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 5 Mana
- Casting points: 10
- Tome: Oathsworn - Strife, Oathsworn - Righteousness, Oathsworn - Harmony
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Friendly units in a 1-hex radius gain, for 1 Turn:

- First Strike
- An extra Retaliation Attack
- Defensive Masters
