# Crushing Earth

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 45 Mana
- Casting points: 30
- Tome: Tome of Terramancy
- Granted by: [Masters of Creation](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Materium](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Target enemy unit:

- Sustains 50 Physical Damage.
- Suffers 4 Sundered Defense for 3 Turns.
- Has a base 90% chance of becoming Stunned for 1 Turn.
