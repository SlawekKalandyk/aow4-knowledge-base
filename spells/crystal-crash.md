# Crystal Crash

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Tome: Crystal Pact
- DLC: GIANTKINGS

Enemy units in a 1-hex radius:

- Sustain 15 Physical Damage.
- Sustain 15 Lightning Damage.
- Have a base 90% chance of inflicting Volatile Charge for 2 Turns.
