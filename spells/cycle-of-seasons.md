# Cycle of Seasons

- Type: [World Map] Sustained City Spell
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Upkeep: 12 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of Cycles
- DLC: Base

All battles that take place in target city's domain gain Cycle of Seasons, granting a different effect each battle turn in the following order:

- Winter: All enemies sustain 10 Frost.
- Spring: All friendly units become 1 Strengthened.
- Summer: All friendly units heal +10 Temporary Hit Points.
- Autumn: All enemies become 1 Weakened.
