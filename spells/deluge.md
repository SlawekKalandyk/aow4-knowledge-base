# Deluge

- Type: [World Map] Terraforming Spell
- Casting cost: 60 Mana
- Casting points: 60
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Target habitable and unowned land Province which does not contain an Ancient Wonder, Landmark, or Diggable Earth:

- Is terraformed to water.
- Loses all Resource Nodes and Magic Materials on it.
- Has a chance of spawning its own Resource Node or Magic Material.
