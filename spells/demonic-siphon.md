# Demonic Siphon

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 160 Mana
- Casting points: 160
- Tome: Tome of the Chaos Lord
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Dragon, Elemental, Fiend, Accursed Fiend

Whenever another unit dies in battle, enchanted unit gains:

- Infernal Might for 3 Turns.
- +5 Morale.

Does not trigger on the death of Combat Summons, Tower, and Siegecraft units.
