# Demonic Summoning

- Type: [World Map] Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of the Demon Gate
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Skald](../units/skald.md), [Nightmare](../units/nightmare.md), [Accursed Blade](../units/accursed-blade.md), [Inferno Hound](../units/inferno-hound.md), [Immolated Soul](../units/immolated-soul.md), [Infernal Juggernaut](../units/infernal-juggernaut.md), [Chaos Eater](../units/chaos-eater.md), [Accursed Trickster](../units/accursed-trickster.md)

Target one of your Free City Vassals:

- Summon 2 random Fiends under your control, one will be Tier III and the other Tier IV.
- The Vassal loses:

- A Vassalage Phase diplomatic stage.
- 1 Population.

Cannot be cast on a Denying Tributary.
