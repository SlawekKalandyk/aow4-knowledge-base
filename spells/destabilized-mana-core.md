# Destabilized Mana Core

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- Tome: Tome of the Dreadnought
- Granted by: [Masters of Constructs](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

Units in 2-hex radius are Marked. After 1 Turn the Mana Core explodes inflicting:

- 24 Physical Damage
- 24 Lightning Damage.
- 60% base chance of receiving Disrupted.
