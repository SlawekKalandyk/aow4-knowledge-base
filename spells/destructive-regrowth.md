# Destructive Regrowth

- Type: [World Map] Terraforming Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of Nature's Wrath
- DLC: Base

On Target Province:

- Enemy units sustain 20 Blight Damage and suffer -2 Blight Protection for 2 Turn.
- If it contains an enemy Province Improvement, it becomes Pillaged.

- You are not granted its rewards nor do you lose Alignment.

- The province gains Forest if on the Surface or Mushroom Forest if in the Underground.
