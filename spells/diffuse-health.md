# Diffuse Health

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Healing Spell
- Casting cost: 45 Mana
- Casting points: 30
- Tome: Tome of Cycles
- Granted by: [Masters of Flora](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Nature](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)

Target enemy unit:

- Sustains 30 Blight Damage.

Friendly units in a 2-hex range.

- Heal +15 Temporary Hit Points.
