# Disrupting Blades

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 180 Mana
- Casting points: 180
- Tome: Tome of Severing
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units gain:

- A base 60% chance of inflicting Disrupted for 2 Turns.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
