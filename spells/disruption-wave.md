# Disruption Wave

- Type: [Combat] Buff/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 300 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of the Archmage
- Granted by: [Masters of Antimagic](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

- Enemy units have a base 120% chance of becoming Disrupted for 2 Turns.
- Dispels 2 Positive Status Effects from enemies.
- Dispels 2 Negative Status Effects from allies.
