# Domain of Death

- Type: [World Map] Sustained City Spell
- Casting cost: 60 Souls
- Upkeep: 5 Mana
- Casting points: 50
- Tome: Tome of the Great Transformation
- DLC: Base

Target friendly city:

- Has +20 City Stability.
- Friendly Undead units in the domain deal +10% damage.
- Enemy units in the domain become Soulbound.
