# Draconian Transformation

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- Tome: Tome of Dragons
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS

Turn target race into Draconians, which grants them:

- Dragon unit type.
- +10 HP Hit Points.
- Natural Regeneration, which makes the unit regain health faster.
