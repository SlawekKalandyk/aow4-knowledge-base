# Draconic Vitality

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of Evolution
- Granted by: [Followers of the Dragon](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS

Target race is imbued with Dragon-like vitality, granting them:

- +5 Hit Point regeneration per World Map Turn.
- +3 Hit Points per Unit Rank.
- +3 Hit Points per Hero Level, up to Level 10.
