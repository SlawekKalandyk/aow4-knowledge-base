# Dungeon Hazard

- Type: [World Map] Enemy Army Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of the Dungeon Depths
- DLC: GIANTKINGS

Target enemy army:

- Sustains 15 Physical Damage, doubled if cast on a Dungeon Province.
- For 1 World Map Turn, at the start of combat units in this army become Slowed for 3 Turn.
