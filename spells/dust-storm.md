# Dust Storm

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 15 Mana
- Casting points: 20
- Tome: Tome of Winds
- Granted by: [Followers of Materium](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)

Enemy units in a 2-hex radius:

- Sustain 10 Physical Damage.
- Have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
