# Earth Shatter

- Type: [World Map] Terraforming Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of Terramancy
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Stone Spirit](../units/stone-spirit.md)

In target habitable or uninhabitable Province:

- Enemy Units sustain 10 Physical Damage.
- The province loses any Mountain, Stalagmites, and Diggable Earth.
- Summon a Stone Spirit on the target hex.
