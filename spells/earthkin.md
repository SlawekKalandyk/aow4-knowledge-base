# Earthkin

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of Rock
- Granted by: [Masters of Creation](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Materium](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Makes rock growths develop on the target race, granting them:

- +1 Defense.
- Mountain Camouflage, which allows them to hide on Mountain terrain.
- Mountain Walk, which allows them to traverse Mountain terrain faster.
