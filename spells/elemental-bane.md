# Elemental Bane

- Type: [World Map] Sustained City Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Upkeep: 10 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

Targets a city, and whenever you fight in that domain, a Gremlin is summoned next to one of your units every Turn.
Up to 5 Gremlins can be summoned per battle.
