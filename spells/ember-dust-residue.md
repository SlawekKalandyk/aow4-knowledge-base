# Ember Dust Residue

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Piracy
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Enchanted units attacks:

- Deal +1 Fire Damage. Increased for Single Shots.
- The third strike from a base attack has a base 90% chance of inflicting Ember Dust.
- Single Shot base attacks instead get a base 60% chance of inflictiing Ember Dust.
