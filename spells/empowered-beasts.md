# Empowered Beasts

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Tome: Tome of Vigor
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Animal

Makes enchanted units:

- Deal +20% damage.
- Gain +10 Hit Points.
- Gain Demolisher, making them able to destroy reinforced obstacles.
- Decreased number of units in formation.
