# Empowered Strikes

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 70 Mana
- Casting points: 70
- Tome: Tome of Discipline
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Makes base Melee attacks and Magic Strikes of enchanted units:

- Deal +2 Physical Damage.
- Third attack in a turn has a 60% base chance of inflicting Stunned for 1 Turn

Enchantment doesn't apply to units without a repeating base melee attack.
