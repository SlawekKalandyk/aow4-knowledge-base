# Eternal Ice Blades

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 180 Mana
- Casting points: 180
- Tome: Tome of Eternal Ice
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit

Enchanted unit's attacks:

- Deal +2 Frost Damage.
- Inflict Chilled for 3 Turns.
- This unit gains Regeneration when it inflicts Chilled.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
