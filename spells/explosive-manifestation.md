# Explosive Manifestation

- Type: [Combat] Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 40
- Tome: Tome of Astral Convergence
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Astral Serpent](../units/astral-serpent.md), [Astral Siphoner](../units/astral-siphoner.md)

Target non-occupied hex:

- Conjure an Astral Serpent or Astral Siphoner unit at random that lasts for 3 Turns.
- All adjacent units sustain 10 Fire Damage, 10 Lightning Damage, and 10 Frost Damage.
