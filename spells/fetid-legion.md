# Fetid Legion

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of the Great Transformation
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Grants enchanted units:

- +10 HP Hit Points.
- Weakening Aura, which inflicts Weakened to adjacent enemies at the end of the turn.
