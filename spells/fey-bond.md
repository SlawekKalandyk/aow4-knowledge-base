# Fey Bond

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 70 Mana
- Casting points: 70
- Tome: Tome of the Sprite
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Ranged Unit, Polearm Unit, Support Unit, Shock Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Enchanted units:

- On combat start, gain +10 Morale.
- When at neutral morale, gain +2 Status Resistance.
- When at high morale, gain +4 Status Resistance.
