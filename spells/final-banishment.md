# Final Banishment

- Type: [Combat] Healing/Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 50
- Tome: Tome of Severing
- Granted by: [Masters of Antimagic](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

All non-hero corpses on the map are destroyed. For each corpse destroyed, friendly units:

- Gain +1 Bolstered Resistance
- Heals +5 Temporary Hit Points
