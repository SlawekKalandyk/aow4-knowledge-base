# Flameburst Weapons

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 160 Mana
- Casting points: 160
- Tome: Tome of Devastation
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Ranged Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Grants enchanted units:

- +20% Critical Hit Chance.
- Killing a unit causes it to explode, adjacent enemies:

- Sustain 15 Fire Damage.
- Gain 2 Burning for 3 Turns.
