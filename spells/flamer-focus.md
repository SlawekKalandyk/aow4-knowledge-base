# Flamer Focus

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 140 Mana
- Casting points: 140
- Tome: Tome of Chaos Channeling
- Granted by: [Followers of the Dragon](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit

Enchanted units gain:

- The Fire Bomb ability, which deals magical damage in an area.
- The damage of this ability increases with the unit's tier.
