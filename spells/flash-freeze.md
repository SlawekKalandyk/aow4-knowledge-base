# Flash Freeze

- Type: [World Map] Terraforming Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of the Cold Dark
- DLC: Base

Target Province:

- For 1 World Map Turns enemy Units in the Province suffer:

- -2 Status Resistance.
- -2 Frost Protection

- The province gains Snow and Ice.
- The province loses Chasm, Swamp, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, Fungus Fields, and Gloom.
