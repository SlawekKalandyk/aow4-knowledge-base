# Flesh Carved Runes

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of the Blood Rite
- Granted by: [Sanguine Vampires](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Carve profane sigils onto the flesh of your race, granting them:

- +5 Hit Point regeneration on the World Map.
- Attack and Debuff abilities:

- Ignore 1 Status Resistance per stack of Bleeding on the target.
