# Flowing Imbuement

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Hydromancy
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Enchanted unit's base attacks:

- Deal +2 Physical Damage.
- On the third strike, push the unit away 1 hex and inflict Wet.
- Single Strike attacks have a base 90% chance of triggering this effect instead.
