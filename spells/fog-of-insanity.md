# Fog of Insanity

- Type: [World Map] Sustained City Spell
- Casting cost: 200 Mana
- Upkeep: 20 Mana
- Casting points: 200
- Tome: Tome of Oblivion
- DLC: Base

Target friendly City. Any battle in the Domain of the City now has the Fog of Insanity battle enchantment, which causes enemies to have a base 20% chance of gaining Insanity every Turn.
