# Fortress of Vines

- Type: [World Map] Sustained World Spell
- Casting cost: 300 Mana
- Upkeep: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 300
- Tome: Tome of Paradise
- DLC: Base

Your empire gains:

- Your non-hero units in your domain earn 10 Experience each Turn.
- Enemy units in this domain spend 4 more Move Points per hex moved.
- A Living Vine spawns next to an enemy each turn during battle in your domain.
