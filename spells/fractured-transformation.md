# Fractured Transformation

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

Target race becomes fractured:

- Attacks have a base 60% chance of inflicting Disrupted. This effect is increased for Single Shot attacks.
- Gains Mana Starved.
- Has all its other enchantments and transformations disabled. For each disabled gain:

- 5 HP Hit Points
- +5% damage
