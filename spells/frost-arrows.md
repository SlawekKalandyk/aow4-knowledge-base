# Frost Arrows

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Cryomancy
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Skirmisher Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units gain:

- +1 Frost Damage.
- A base 60% chance of inflicting Chilled.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
