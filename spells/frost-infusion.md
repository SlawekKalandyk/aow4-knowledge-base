# Frost Infusion

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Cryomancy
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units deal:

- +2 Frost Damage.
- +10% damage against Frozen or Chilled units.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
