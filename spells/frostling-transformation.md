# Frostling Transformation

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 350 Mana
- Casting points: 350
- Tome: Tome of the Cold Dark
- Granted by: [Masters of Shadow](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Makes winter run through the target race's veins, granting them:

- 3 Frost Protection.
- Immunity to Chilled.
- +10 Morale while on Snow or Ice.
- Arctic Walk.
- Snow Camouflage
