# Gaia's Chosen

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- Tome: Tome of Paradise
- DLC: Base

Infuse the target race with the blessing of Nature. They gain:

- The Plant unit type.
- Negative Status Effects last -1 Turn, with a minimum of 1.
- +3 Status Resistance.
- +20 Hit Points.
