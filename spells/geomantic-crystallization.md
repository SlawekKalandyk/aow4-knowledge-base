# Geomantic Crystallization

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- Tome: Tome of Geomancy
- DLC: GIANTKINGS

Turn target race into resonating crystals that self infuse with environmental magics, gaining:

- The Elemental unit type.
- Resistance to a specific damage type based on the terrain the unit is on.
- Melee attackers sustain damage based on terrain the unit is on.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
