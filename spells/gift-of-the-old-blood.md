# Gift of the Old Blood

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- Tome: Tome of the Crimson Reign
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Transform your units as they ritually imbibe blood from an Exarch, granting them:

- The Undead unit type.
- Lifedrinker.
- Base attacks have a base 60% chance of inflicting Blood Parasite for 3 Turns. This effect is increased for Single Shot attacks.
