# Gloom Strider

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- Tome: Tome of Corruption
- Granted by: [Masters of Corruption](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

Turns target race into Umbral beings, which grants them:

- The Umbral Demon unit type.
- Floating.
- Fast Movement.

Mounted units lose their mounts.

Heroes lose their Mount and Leg equipment slots.

This transformation is incompatible with Pure Soul.
