# Golden Horde

- Type: [World Map] Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 300 Mana
- Casting points: 300
- Tome: Tome of Chaos Channeling
- DLC: Base

- Summons a full army of random non-Scout Tier I units that can be produced in a city on the target hex.
- Summon units start at Champion rank.
- If cast within an enemy Province, instantly pillages it.
