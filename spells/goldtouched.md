# Goldtouched

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 450 Mana
- Casting points: 450
- Tome: Tome of the Golden Realm
- Granted by: [The Rotten Gold](../world/relics.md) (relic), [Followers of Alchemy](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Materium](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Makes the target race gain an affinity for gold, granting them:

- +2 Resistance.
- +1 Gold per Population in their Cities.
