# Grand Protection

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 40
- Tome: Tome of Prosperity
- Granted by: [Masters of Prosperity](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Friendly units in a 2-hex radius gain:

- 2 Grace
- 2 Regeneration for 3 Turns
- 2 Bolstered Defense for 3 Turns
- 2 Bolstered Resistance for 3 Turns
