# Guided Projectiles

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Scrying
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit

Makes base attacks of enchanted units:

- Fire 1 hex further without Long Range Accuracy penalties.
- Ignore Accuracy penalties from the Obscuring condition caused by units that are in the way and certain terrain.
