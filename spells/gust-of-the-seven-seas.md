# Gust of the Seven Seas

- Type: [World Map] World Spell
- Casting cost: 240 Mana
- Casting points: 240
- Granted by: [Conch of Fair Winds](../world/relics.md) (relic)
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

All your Armies regain all their movement points if they are on water. Half if they are on land.
A unit cannot benefit from this spell more than once per turn.
