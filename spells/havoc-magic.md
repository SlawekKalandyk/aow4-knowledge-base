# Havoc Magic

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of Pandemonium
- Granted by: [Followers of Fey](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Chaos](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Demons](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Grants base Magic attacks of enchanted units:

- Base 60% chance of inflicting a random Negative Status Effect.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
