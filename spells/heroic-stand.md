# Heroic Stand

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- Tome: Tome of Virtue
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

Target friendly unit:

- Has their Negative Status Effects removed.
- Gains Steadfast for 1 Turn.
- Ignores the penalties from Casualties for 1 Turn.

Can only affect a unit once per battle.
