# Ice Coffin

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 15 Mana
- Casting points: 20
- Tome: Tome of Cryomancy
- Granted by: [Initiates of Shadow](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Corruption](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Frost](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Target enemy:

- Sustains 10 Frost Damage.
- Has a base 90% chance of becoming Frozen. If unsuccessful, inflicts Chilled.
