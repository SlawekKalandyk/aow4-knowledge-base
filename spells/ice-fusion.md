# Ice Fusion

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- Tome: Tome of Eternal Ice
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Target race becomes one with ice, gaining:

- The Elemental unit type.
- +4 Frost Protection.
- -2 Fire Protection
- Immunity to Frozen and Chilled.
- Rime Healing.
- Water Walk.
