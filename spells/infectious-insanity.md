# Infectious Insanity

- Type: [Combat] Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 40
- Tome: Tome of Pandemonium
- Granted by: [Followers of Chaos](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Demons](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Target Unit has:

- 90% chance of being inflicted with Infectious Insanity for 2 Turns.
- If resisted, the target is inflicted with Insanity for 1 Turn.
