# Infernal Jaws

- Type: [Combat] Damage Spell
- Casting cost: 10 Mana
- Casting points: 15
- Tome: Tome of Gluttony
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Target enemy sustains:

- 15 Physical Damage.
- 15 Fire Damage.

If the enemy dies, adjacent friendly units:

- Heal 20 Temporary Hit Points.
- If they have Demonic Hunger or Imp's Hunger, gain Gorged.
