# Inner Mastery

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of Discipline
- Granted by: [Masters of Prosperity](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Grants the target race mastery over their own internal energies, granting them:

- +1 Status Resistance
- +20% healing received in combat.
