# Joy Siphoners

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 250 Mana
- Casting points: 250
- Tome: Tome of the Doomherald
- DLC: Base

Target race becomes a living morale siphon, gaining:

- +4 Morale when attacking an enemy.
- Demoralizer

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
