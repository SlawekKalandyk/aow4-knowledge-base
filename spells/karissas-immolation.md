# Karissa's Immolation

- Type: [Combat] Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Target enemy unit sustains 60 Fire Damage. If it dies to this damage and is not a hero or Combat Summon unit, it turns into an Immolated Soul under your control that can be acquired at the end of combat for a Mana cost.
