# Keeper's Mark

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Tome: Tome of Sanctuary
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Enchanted units:

- Gain Faithful, reducing Unit Upkeep.
- When taking fatal damage for the first time, this unit gains:

- Steadfast for 1 Turn.
- Pacified for 1 Turn.
