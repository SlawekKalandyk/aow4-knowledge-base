# Lava Burst

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 40
- Tome: Tome of the Crucible
- Granted by: [Masters of Creation](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Materium](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Targets a 2-hex radius:

- All units sustain 30 Fire Damage.
- All units are inflicted with Burning.
- All units are Slowed.
- The ground is set On Fire.
