# Leafskin

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 250 Mana
- Casting points: 250
- Tome: Tome of Glades
- DLC: Base

Makes the target race one with the forest, granting them:

- Forest Walk
- Forest Camouflage
- When in a province with Forest or Mushroom Forest:

- +10% Evasion
- +10% Accuracy
- +10% Critical Hit Chance
