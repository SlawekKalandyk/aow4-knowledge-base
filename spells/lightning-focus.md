# Lightning Focus

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Evocation
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units:

- Deal +2 Lightning Damage.
- Gain base 30% chance of inflicting Electrified, a damage-over-time effect.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
