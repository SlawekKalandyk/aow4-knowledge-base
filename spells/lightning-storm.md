# Lightning Storm

- Type: [Combat] Combat Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Tome: Tome of the Stormborne
- Granted by: [Masters of Storms](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

- All units become Wet for 3 Turns
- Up to 2 random enemy units:

- Sustain 30 Lightning Damage.
- Have a base 90% chance of becoming Electrified for 3 Turns.

This spell repeats at the start of each turn for the next 6 Turns.
