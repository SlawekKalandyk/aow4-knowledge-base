# Living Shadows

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 250 Mana
- Casting points: 250
- Tome: Tome of Shades
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Wreathes target race in swirling shadows, granting them:

- +10% Evasion.
- Fleeting.
- When this unit's Hit Points drop below 60%:

- It becomes Obscured for 1 Turn.
- Adjacent enemies have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
