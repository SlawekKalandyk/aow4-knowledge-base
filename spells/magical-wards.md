# Magical Wards

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of Warding
- Granted by: [Masters of Antimagic](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Corruption](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Inscribes magical wards onto the target race, granting:

- +2 Lightning Protection
- +2 Fire Protection
- +2 Frost Protection
