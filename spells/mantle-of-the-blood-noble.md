# Mantle of the Blood Noble

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 140 Mana
- Casting points: 140
- Tome: Tome of the Crimson Reign
- Granted by: [Sanguine Vampires](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Applies to: Tier III, Tier IV, Tier V

Grants enchanted units:

- Coagulate
- At the start of combat, for each friendly non-summon Tier I and Tier II unit:

- +5 Hit Points
- +5% healing received in combat.
