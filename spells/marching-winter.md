# Marching Winter

- Type: [World Map] Terraforming Spell
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Upkeep: 12 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of the Cold Dark
- DLC: Base

Target friendly City gains:

- Every Turn, 2 Provinces within or adjacent to the Domain:

- Gain Snow and Ice.
- Lose Chasm, Swamp, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, Fungus Fields, and Gloom.

- Provinces in the domain with Snow or Ice provide +2 Food and +2 Production income.
