# Mark of Misfortune

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Mayhem
- Granted by: [Followers of Chaos](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Demons](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units:

- Inflict Misfortune.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
