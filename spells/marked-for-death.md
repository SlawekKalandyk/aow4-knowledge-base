# Marked for Death

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 35 Souls
- Casting points: 40
- Tome: Tome of the Reaper
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Decaying Zombie](../units/decaying-zombie.md)

Target unit:

- Sustains 15 unblockable Physical Damage.
- Loses -10 Morale.
- Has a Decaying Zombie spawn adjacent to them.
- Is afflicted with Visions of Death until the end of combat.
