# Mass Condemnation

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 15 Mana
- Casting points: 20
- Tome: Tome of the Inquisition
- Granted by: [Followers of Order](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of the Cleansing Flame](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Enemies in a 1-hex radius:

- Sustain 10 Spirit Damage.
- Become Condemned until the end of battle.
