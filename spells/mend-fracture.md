# Mend Fracture

- Type: [World Map] World Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

Decreases the Devouring Winds value by 220, temporarily reducing the chance of a Cataclysm.
The cost of this spell is reduced based on the amount of Artifacts of the Unmakers the caster has.

This spell does not contribute to the Devouring Winds and cannot Backfire.
