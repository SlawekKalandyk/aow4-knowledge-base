# Merfolk Transformation

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 350 Mana
- Casting points: 350
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Target race gains:

- Merfolk.
- Fast Movement.
- When on the water units lose their legs and gain a merfolk tail.

Special notes:

- Incompatible with transformations that give Flying or disable mounts.
- Mounted units lose their mounts.
- Heroes lose their mount equipment slots.
