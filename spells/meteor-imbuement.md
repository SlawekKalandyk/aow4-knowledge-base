# Meteor Imbuement

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 160 Mana
- Casting points: 160
- Tome: Tome of the Crucible
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Makes base attacks of enchanted units:

- Deal +4 Fire Damage to the target and adjacent enemies.
- Gain Demolisher, making them able to destroy reinforced obstacles.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
