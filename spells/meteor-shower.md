# Meteor Shower

- Type: Combat Enchantment
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 50
- Tome: Tome of the Crucible
- DLC: Base

Up to 2 random enemy units and enemies adjacent to those:

- Sustain 10 Fire Damage and 10 Physical Damage.

This spell repeats at the start of each turn for the next 5 Turns.
