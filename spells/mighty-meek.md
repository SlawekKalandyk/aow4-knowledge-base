# Mighty Meek

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Tome: Tome of the Beacon
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Tier I, Tier II

Grants enchanted units:

- Faithful, which reduces Unit Upkeep.
- +2 Status Resistance.
- +1 Spirit Damage on attacks for each Unit Tier of the target.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
