# Monstrous Rebirth

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 45 Mana
- Casting points: 30
- Tome: Tome of Devastation
- DLC: Base

Target friendly Tier I, II, or III non-Magic Origin, non-Construct unit:

- Transforms into a Warbreed.
- Heals 60 Temporary Hit Points.
- Dies at the end of combat.
