# Naga Transformation

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 600 Mana
- Casting points: 600
- Tome: Tome of the Stormborne
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

Target race gains the body of a Naga, granting:

- Fast Movement
- Merfolk
- Slip Away
- Immunity to Electrified
- +2 Blight Protection
- +2 Lightning Protection

Mounted units lose their mounts.

Heroes lose their Mount and Leg equipment slots.
