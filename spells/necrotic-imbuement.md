# Necrotic Imbuement

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Necromancy
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Grants attacks of enchanted units:

- A base 90% chance of inflicting Decaying, a damage-over-time effect that decreases healing received.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
