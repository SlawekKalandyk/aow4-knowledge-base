# Nightveil Focus

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 100
- Tome: Tome of the Cold Dark
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Enchanted units gain:

- Attacks deal +2 Frost Damage.
- Attacks have a base 60% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn.
- Universal Camouflage.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
