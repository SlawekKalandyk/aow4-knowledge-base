# Noctus' Mastery

- Type: [World Map] World Spell
- Casting cost: 45 Mana
- Casting points: 45
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Target friendly city is destroyed. Then for each population of the destroyed city:

- Gain +50 Knowledge.
- Gain +20 Soul.
- Gain a random Skeleton unit.
- Each skeleton gained has a small chance of being a Corrupt Soul instead.
