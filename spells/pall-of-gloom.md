# Pall of Gloom

- Type: [World Map] Terraforming Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Eldritch Pact
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

Target Province and adjacent Provinces:

- Gain Gloom
- Lose Chasm, River, Sand, Snow, Ice, Ashlands, Swamp, Cavern Floor, and Dungeon.
- Enemy Units suffer -1 Frost Protection and -1 Blight Protection for 2 Turn.
