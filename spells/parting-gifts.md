# Parting Gifts

- Type: [Combat] Combat Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Tome: Tome of Cycles
- Granted by: [Masters of Flora](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Nature](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Until the end of battle, whenever a friendly unit dies, all other friendly units within 2 hexes heal +15 Temporary Hit Points.
