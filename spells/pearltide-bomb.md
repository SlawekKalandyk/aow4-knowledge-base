# Pearltide Bomb

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Conjure a Pearltide Bomb on target hex:

- All enemy Units within 5 hexes of the Pearltide Bomb suffer Wet for 3 Turns.
- After 1 Turn:

- The enemy unit on the hex of the Pearltide Bomb suffers 25 Physical Damage and 25 Lightning Damage.
- Enemy units within a 2 hex radius of the Pearltide Bomb instead suffer 10 Physical Damage and 10 Lightning Damage.

Can only be used in water battles.
