- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of Teleportation
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Grants enchanted units:

- The Charged Phase ability, which allows them to teleport in battle.
