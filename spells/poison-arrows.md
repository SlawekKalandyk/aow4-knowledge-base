# Poison Arrows

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Roots
- Granted by: [Initiates of Nature](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Flora](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Skirmisher Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units:

- Deal +1 Blight Damage.
- Gain base 60% chance of inflicting Poisoned, a damage-over-time effect.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
