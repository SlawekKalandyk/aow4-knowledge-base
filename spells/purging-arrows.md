# Purging Arrows

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 70 Mana
- Casting points: 70
- Tome: Tome of Enchantment
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Skirmisher Unit

Grants attacks of enchanted units:

- +10% damage against Magic Origin units.
- Base 60% chance of removing 1 Positive Status Effect from the target.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
