# Raiders of the Sea

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Piracy
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Ranged Unit, Polearm Unit, Support Unit, Shock Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Enchanted units:

- Increase resources gained from Pillaging a Province by 100%. Does not stack.
- Gain Sailor, increasing combat speed and damage when fighting on water.
