# Rapid Evolution Enchantment

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Evolution
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS
- Applies to: Units that Evolve, Units that have Evolved

Grants enchanted units:

- 15 XP Experience per Turn.
- Slip Away healing them once per battle if their HP Hit Points would be reduced to zero.
