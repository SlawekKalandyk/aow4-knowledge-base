# Rending Shadows

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- Tome: Tome of Shades
- Granted by: [Masters of Calamity](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Enemy units in a 1-hex radius:

- Sustain 16 Physical Damage.
- Have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
- Have a base 90% chance of gaining Sundered Defense for 3 Turn.
