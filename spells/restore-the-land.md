# Restore the Land

- Type: [World Map] Terraforming Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Fertility
- DLC: Base

Target and adjacent Provinces:

- Gains Grassland if it is on the Surface or Fungus Fields and Mushroom Forest if it is in the Underground.
- Loses Chasm, Swamp, Snow, Ice, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, Stalagmites, and Gloom.
