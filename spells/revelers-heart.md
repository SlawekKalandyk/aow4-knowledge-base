# Reveler's Heart

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 250 Mana
- Casting points: 250
- Tome: Tome of Revelry
- Granted by: [Followers of Chaos](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Demons](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Overwhelms the target race with an intense fervor, granting them:

- +50% Morale from all sources.
- +10% damage when at High Morale.
