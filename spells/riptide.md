# Riptide

- Type: [Combat] Damage Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- Tome: Tome of Hydromancy
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Units in a 1-hex radius:

- Sustain 12 Physical Damage.
- Become Wet for 3 Turns.
- If on the outer radius, they are pushed 1-hex away from the center.
- Targets that were already Wet have the above effects doubled.
