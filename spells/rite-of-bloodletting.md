# Rite of Bloodletting

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 10 Mana
- Casting points: 15
- Tome: Tome of the Blood Rite
- Granted by: [Sanguine Vampires](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Target friendly unit sustains 5 unblockable Physical Damage. Then all enemy units within 2 hexes:

- Sustain 12 Physical Damage.
- Gain 2 Bleeding for 3 Turns.
