# Rite of Life Leeching

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 15 Mana
- Casting points: 20
- Tome: Tome of the Blood Rite
- Granted by: [Sanguine Vampires](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Target friendly unit sustains 5 unblockable Physical Damage. Then all adjacent friendly units gain:

- Strengthened for 3 Turns.
- Life Steal for 3 Turns.
