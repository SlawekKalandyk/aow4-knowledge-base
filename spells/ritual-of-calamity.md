# Ritual of Calamity

- Type: [World Map] City Spell
- Casting cost: 
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

- At the start of every Turn during the siege, a random Province Improvement of the besieged city is pillaged, granting you rewards.
- At the start of combat:

- All defenders gain a stack of Ghostfire for Turns.
- Accursed Ogres appear on the attacker's side.
