# Rock Blast

- Type: [Combat] Damage Spell
- Casting cost: 10 Mana
- Casting points: 15
- Tome: Tome of Rock
- Granted by: [Initiates of Materium](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Creation](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Target enemy unit:

- Suffers 24 Physical Damage.
- Has its Defense Mode canceled.
- Has its Retaliation Attacks removed.
