# Rotting Explosion

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 10 Souls
- Casting points: 15
- Tome: Tome of Necromancy
- DLC: Base

Target friendly Zombie or Skeleton unit explodes, all enemies in a 2-hex radius:

- Sustain 10 Frost Damage and 10 Blight Damage.
- Gain 2 Decaying for 3 Turns.
