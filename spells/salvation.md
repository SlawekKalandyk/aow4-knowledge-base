# Salvation

- Type: [Combat] Healing/Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Tome: Tome of Sanctuary
- Granted by: [Masters of Order](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Target friendly unit:

- Heals all of its Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points.
- Dispels its Negative Status Effects.
- Gains 3 Bolstered Resistance.
