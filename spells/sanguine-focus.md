# Sanguine Focus

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of the Blood Rite
- Granted by: [Sanguine Vampires](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Applies to: Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Attacks of enchanted units gain:

- +1 Physical Damage.
- A base 60% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
