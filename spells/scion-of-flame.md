# Scion of Flame

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 450 Mana
- Casting points: 450
- Tome: Tome of Chaos Channeling
- DLC: Base

Makes the target race the embodiment of fire, granting them:

- 4 Fire Protection.
- Vengeful Flames, which damages Melee attackers.
- Fiery Wake, which ignites flammable terrain such as Flora Obstacles.
- Immunity to Burning.
- Lava Walk.
- Cities of this race ignore the City Stability penalty from Chasm and Lava in their Domain.
