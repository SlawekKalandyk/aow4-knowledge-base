# Scorpion Venom Weapons

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of the Sand Stalkers
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Ranged Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Attacks of enchanted units gain:

- A base 60% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns.
- A base 60% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns.
- +2 Blight Damage when flanking.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
