# Searing Blades

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Pyromancy
- Granted by: [Masters of the Cleansing Flame](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit

Makes attacks of enchanted units deal:

- +2 Fire Damage.
- +10% damage against targets that are Burning.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
