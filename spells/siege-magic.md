# Siege Magic

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Artificing
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Grants enchanted units:

- +10% damage
- Siege Breaker, dealing extra fortification damage during a siege.
- Demolisher, making them able to destroy reinforced obstacles.
