# Sleep of Oblivion

- Type: [Combat] Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 100 Mana
- Casting points: 40
- Tome: Tome of Oblivion
- Granted by: [Masters of Corruption](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Shadow](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Target non-Hero unit dies. After 2 Turns, they come back to life with 75% of their total Hit Points and are inflicted with Insanity.

While dead this way the unit cannot be revived or have its corpse destroyed.
