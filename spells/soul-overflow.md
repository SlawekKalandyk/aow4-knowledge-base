# Soul Overflow

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 15 Souls
- Casting points: 20
- Tome: Tome of Souls
- Granted by: [Followers of Shadow](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Necromancy](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Friendly units in a 1-hex radius:

- Gain 1 Strengthened.
- Gain +20 maximum Hit Points until the end of battle.
- Have 2 Negative Status Effects removed.
