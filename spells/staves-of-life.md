# Staves of Life

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 90 Mana
- Casting points: 90
- Tome: Tome of Fertility
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Support Unit

Makes Attack or Support abilities of enchanted units:

- Heal a random friendly unit within 3 hexes of it for 15 Temporary Hit Point.
