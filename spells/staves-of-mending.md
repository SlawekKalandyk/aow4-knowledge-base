# Staves of Mending

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Faith
- Granted by: [Initiates of Order](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Support Unit

Grants enchanted units:

- Faithful, which reduces base Unit Upkeep.
- Mending Touch, a healing ability for use in battle.
