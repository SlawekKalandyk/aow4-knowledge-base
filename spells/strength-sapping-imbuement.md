# Strength Sapping Imbuement

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Burning Passion
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Enchanted units attacks gain:

- +1 Fire Damage.
- Base attacks have a base 60% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns. On success gain Strengthened for 3 Turns.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
