# Summon Astral Reflection

- Type: [Combat] Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 50
- Tome: Tome of the Astral Mirror
- Granted by: [Masters of Astral](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of the Ethereal](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Summons an Astral Reflection of target friendly unit onto an unoccupied adjacent hex.

Cannot target Heroes, Combat Summons, and Mythic Units.
