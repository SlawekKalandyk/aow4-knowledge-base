# Summon Calamity Dragon

- Type: [World Map] Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 300 Mana
- Casting points: 300
- Tome: Tome of Calamity
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Summons: [Calamity Dragon](../units/calamity-dragon.md)

Target owned non-ruined Province turns to Ruins and Desolate terrain and then summons a Calamity Dragon, a Mythic Unit unit with strong offensive capabilities.
