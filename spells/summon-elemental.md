# Summon Elemental

- Type: [World Map] Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 150 Mana
- Casting points: 150
- Tome: Tome of Geomancy
- DLC: GIANTKINGS
- Summons: [Magma Spirit](../units/magma-spirit.md), [Snow Spirit](../units/snow-spirit.md), [Stone Spirit](../units/stone-spirit.md), [Storm Spirit](../units/storm-spirit.md), [Tide Spirit](../units/tide-spirit.md)

Summon a Tier III Elemental unit on target hex. The Elemental summoned changes based on the terrain type.
