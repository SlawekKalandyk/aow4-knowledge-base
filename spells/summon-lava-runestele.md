# Summon Lava Runestele

- Type: [World Map] Terraforming Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Upkeep: 5 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Granted by: [Giant King](../rulers.md) (ruler origin)
- DLC: GIANTKINGS

Summon a Lava Runestele on target Province, which will appear on an empty hex and terraform surrounding provinces every Turn.
