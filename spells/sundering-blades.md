# Sundering Blades

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 80
- Tome: Tome of Enchantment
- DLC: Base
- Applies to: Shield Unit, Polearm Unit, Shock Unit, Fighter Unit

Grants attacks of enchanted units:

- Base 90% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense, reducing enemy Defense.
- Demolisher, making them able to destroy reinforced obstacles.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
