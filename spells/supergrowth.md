# Supergrowth

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 350 Mana
- Casting points: 350
- Tome: Tome of Vigor
- Granted by: [Masters of Beasts](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Flora](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Nature](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Makes the target race grow in mass and stature, granting them:

- +10 Hit Points.
- +1 Retaliation Attack.
- Decreased number of units in formation.

This transformation is incompatible with Spawnkin.
