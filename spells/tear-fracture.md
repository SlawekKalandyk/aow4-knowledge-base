# Tear Fracture

- Type: [Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 45 Mana
- Casting points: 30
- Granted by: [Well Tear](../world/relics.md) (relic)
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

## Variant 1 of 2 (cost 45 Mana)

Target hex becomes marked, Units in 2-hex radius of the marked hex have a 60% base chance of receiving Disrupted. After 1 Turn, the marked hex explodes and inflicts the following in a 2-hex radius:

- Units suffer 48 Physical Damage.
- Units have 60% base chance of suffering Mana Drained.
- Each hex has 20% chance of creating a Mana Fracture obstacle, which have Draining and spread to adjacent hexes when spells are cast.

This spell cannot Backfire.
