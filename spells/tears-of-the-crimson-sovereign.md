# Tears of the Crimson Sovereign

- Type: Combat Enchantment
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Tome: Tome of the Crimson Reign
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

When cast and for the next 2 Turns:

- Up to 3 enemy units:

- Sustain 10 Physical Damage.
- Have a base 90% chance of gaining Blood Parasite for 3 Turns.

- Up to 3 friendly units:

- Heal for 10 Temporary Hit Points.
- Gain Fortune for 3 Turns.
