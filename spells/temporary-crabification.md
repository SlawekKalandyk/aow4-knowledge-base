# Temporary Crabification

- Type: [Combat] Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Target friendly non-Hero Unit:

- Transforms into a Lightning Rune Crab until the end of the battle.
- Has its HP Hit Points reset.
- Loses all Negative Status Effects and Positive Status Effects.
- Loses all [World Map] Unit Enchantments, [World Map] Race Transformations, and ranks until the end of the battle.

Does not work in lava battles.
