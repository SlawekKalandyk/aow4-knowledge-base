# Tormenting Imbuement

- Type: [World Map] Unit Enchantment
- Casting cost: 140 Mana
- Casting points: 140
- Tome: Tome of Torment
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Applies to: Ranged Unit, Support Unit, Battle Mage Unit, Skirmisher Unit, Magic Fighter Unit

Attacks of enchanted units:

- Have a base 90% chance of inflicting Torment until the end of combat.

Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
