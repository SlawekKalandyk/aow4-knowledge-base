# Totem of the Wild

- Type: [Combat] Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 80 Mana
- Casting points: 35
- Tome: Tome of Vigor
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Totem of the Wild](../units/totem-of-the-wild.md)

Summons a Totem of the Wild, which spawns a random Tier I or II animal unit when it's created and at the start of your next 2 Turns.
