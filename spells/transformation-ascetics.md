# Transformation Ascetics

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 
- DLC: Base

- Units of this Race cannot have any non-Cultural [World Map] Unit Enchantments.
- Units of this Race cannot have any [World Map] Race Transformation.
- Non-Hero Units of this Race gain +5 maximum HP Hit Points and deal +1 damage (doubled for non-repeating) with their attacks per Rank.
- Hero Units of this Race gain +5 maximum HP Hit Points and deal +1 damage (doubled for non-repeating) with their attacks per Signature Skill they have.
