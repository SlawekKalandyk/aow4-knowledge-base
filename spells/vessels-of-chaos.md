# Vessels of Chaos

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 350 Mana
- Casting points: 350
- Tome: Tome of Pandemonium
- Granted by: [Masters of Chaos](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade), [Masters of Demons](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Turns the target race into a conduit for chaotic energies, granting them:

- +10% damage for each of the target's Negative Status Effects. Stacks up to 3 times.
