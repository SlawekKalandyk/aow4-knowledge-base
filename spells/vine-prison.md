# Vine Prison

- Type: [Combat] Combat Summon Spell
- Casting cost: 30 Mana
- Casting points: 25
- Tome: Tome of Roots
- Granted by: [Initiates of Nature](../world/free-cities.md) (free city), [Masters of Flora](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base
- Summons: [Living Vine](../units/living-vine.md)

Summon 5 Living Vine units randomly in a 2-hex radius which live for 2 Turns. These don't deal damage but have a chance of inflicting Immobilized on enemies.
