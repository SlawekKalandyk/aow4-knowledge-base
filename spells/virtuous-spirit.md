# Virtuous Spirit

- Type: [World Map] Minor Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 250 Mana
- Casting points: 250
- Tome: Tome of Virtue
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

Gives target race strength in moments of weakness, giving them:

- +2 Spirit Protection
- When this unit drops below 60% of their maximum HP Hit Points:

- Ignores 50% of its Casualties.
- Gains +1 Defense.
- Gains +1 Resistance.
