# Wightborn

- Type: [World Map] Major Race Transformation
- Casting cost: 300 Mana 100 Souls
- Casting points: 300
- Tome: Tome of the Great Transformation
- Granted by: [The Rotten Gold](../world/relics.md) (relic), [Masters of Shadow](../world/free-cities.md) (free city)
- DLC: Base

Gives the target race immortality beyond life, granting them:

- The Undead unit type.
- Life Steal, which restores Temporary Hit Points upon each attack.
