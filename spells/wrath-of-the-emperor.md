# Wrath of the Emperor

- Type: [World Map] Enemy Army Spell
- Casting cost: 120 Mana
- Casting points: 120
- Tome: Tome of the God Emperor
- DLC: Base

Target enemy army:

- Sustains 20 Spirit Damage.
- Becomes Demoralized for 1 Turn.
- Becomes Condemned for 1 Turn.
- If cast in enemy territory, the army additionally sustains +50% damage.
