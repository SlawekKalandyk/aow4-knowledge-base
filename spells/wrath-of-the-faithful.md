# Wrath of the Faithful

- Type: [Combat] Damage Spell
- Casting cost: 10 Mana
- Casting points: 15
- Tome: Tome of Faith
- Granted by: [Initiates of Order](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: Base

Target enemy unit sustains 10 Spirit Damage plus 5 Spirit Damage per each friendly Faithful unit in battle (up to 8).
