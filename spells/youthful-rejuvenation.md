# Youthful Rejuvenation

- Type: [Combat] Healing/Buff Spell
- Casting cost: 10 Mana
- Casting points: 15
- Tome: Tome of Evolution
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS

Target unit:

- Heals for 25 Temporary Hit Points.
- Gains 2 stacks of Strengthened.

Units with Evolve or Product of Evolution:

- Gain Resurgence until the end of combat.
