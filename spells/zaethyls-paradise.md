# Zaethyl's Paradise

- Type: World Spell
- Casting cost: 200 Mana
- Casting points: 200
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

All Provinces:

- Gain Grassland and Forest if on the Surface or Fungus Fields and Mushroom Forest if they are in the Underground.
- Lose Chasm, Swamp, Snow, Ice, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, Stalagmites, and Gloom.

Additionally, all your Provinces with Grassland or Forest gain double income.
