# Zealous Ignition

- Type: [Combat] Buff/Debuff Spell
- Casting cost: 45 Mana
- Casting points: 30
- Tome: Tome of the Cleansing Flame
- Granted by: [Masters of the Cleansing Flame](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, always), [Masters of the Cleansing Flame](../world/free-cities.md) (free city, possible upgrade)
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

Target unit:

- Gains Zeal until the end of battle.
If target already has Zeal, gain 2 Strengthened for 3 Turns.
- Enemy units in a 2-hex radius suffer Condemned until the end of battle.
- Create Cleansing Flames on the target hexes for 3 Turns.
