# Tomes

Affinity = affinity points the tome grants. Unlock rule (in-game tutorial): For every two tomes you pick, you will unlock the next tier of tomes. Higher tier tomes also require a certain amount Affinity to be unlocked.

Needs = Affinity needed (player-provided, not in the game data): tier 3 = 3, tier 4 = 6, tier 5 = 8 points in the tome's affinity; a dual-affinity tome counts both affinities combined, in any split. Tiers 1-2 need no affinity.
