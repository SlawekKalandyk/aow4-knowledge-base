# Barbarian

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to rulers with the Barbarian culture. They specialize in speed and aggression.

## Unlocks

- [Song of the Reckless](../spells/song-of-the-reckless.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): For 3 Turns, target friendly unit gains:
  
  - Berserk
  - 3 Strengthened
  
  Does not affect units with Control Loss Immunity.
- [Vision of Victory](../spells/vision-of-victory.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Friendly units in a 1-hex radius gain 3 Fortune.
- [Brutal Mark](../spells/brutal-mark.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Barbarian units:
  
  - Savage Strike, which adds Blight Damage on critical hits.
- Ritual of Alacrity (): Units standing on the center of this city or outpost restore 50% Hit Points, 100% Move Points, and remove Exhausted from Forced March. The city or outpost has a 3 Turn cooldown before it can use this again.
- Forest of Stakes (Initial City Upgrade): - +7 Food income.; +7 Production income.; +7 Draft per adjacent Forester.; Enemy Units in this Domain get Demoralized.; Counts as a Forester.
