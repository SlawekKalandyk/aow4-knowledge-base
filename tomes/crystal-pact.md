# Crystal Pact

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: GIANTKINGS

These boons can be purchased from a Crystal Dwelling by reaching a Crystal or Eternal pact with them. They will give you access to Lithorine units and the power of the Eternals.

## Unlocks

- [Lithorine Needler](../units/lithorine-needler.md) (Unit): A Ranged Unit with a ranged attack that applies Volatile Charge. Draftable in cities.
- [Lithorine Core](../units/lithorine-core.md) (Unit): A Support Unit that can link to units and take their damage for them. Draftable in cities.
- [Lithorine Simulacrum](../units/lithorine-simulacrum.md) (Unit): A Mythic Unit with a strong charge attack and the ability to create a chain of lightning that can trigger Volatile Charge. Draftable in cities.
- [Lithorine Gemcaster](../units/lithorine-gemcaster.md) (Unit): A Battle Mage Unit that inflicts Volatile Charge in a large area. Draftable in cities.
- [Crystal Crash](../spells/crystal-crash.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 15 Physical Damage.
  - Sustain 15 Lightning Damage.
  - Have a base 90% chance of inflicting Volatile Charge for 2 Turns.
- Eternal Embassy (City Structure): - 15 Mana
  - 15 Knowledge
  - 10 Imperium
- [Crystallize Essence](../spells/crystallize-essence.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Revive target non-Lithorine non-Hero unit as a Lithorine of its Tier.
  
  If victorious in combat, units revived can be kept for a Mana cost.
- [Lithorine Shardlancer](../units/lithorine-shardlancer.md) (Unit): A Polearm Unit that immobilizes enemies. Draftable in cities.
- [Crystal Focus](../spells/crystal-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Attacks of enchanted units gain:
  
  - A base 60% chance of applying Volatile Charge.
