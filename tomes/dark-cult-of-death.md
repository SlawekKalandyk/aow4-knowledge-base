# Dark - Cult of Death

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to Rulers with the Dark culture.

## Unlocks

- [Morbid Sacrifice](../spells/morbid-sacrifice.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Units in friendly armies in the target Province:
  
  - Restore all HP Hit Points.
  - Gain Universal Camouflage for 3 Turns.
  - Gain +15 Morale until the end of their next combat.
  - Gain +20 HP Hit Points until the end of their next combat.
- [Deathwaltz](../spells/deathwaltz.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Regains its Action Points and Health.
  - Gains Resurgence.
  - Gains Doomed, dying at the end of this turn.
- [Mark of the Death Cult](../spells/mark-of-the-death-cult.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Dark units:
  
  - Power from Death
- Mausoleum (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Knowledge income.; +3 Knowledge per adjacent Conduit.; Counts as a Research Post.
