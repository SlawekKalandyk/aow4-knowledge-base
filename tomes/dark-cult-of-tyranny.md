# Dark - Cult of Tyranny

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to Rulers with the Dark culture.

## Unlocks

- [Baneful Curse](../spells/baneful-curse.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius gain:
  
  - Base 90% chance of inflicting 2 Weakened for 3 Turns.
  - Base 90% chance of inflicting 2 Misfortune for 3 Turns.
- [Tyrannical Exploitation](../spells/tyrannical-exploitation.md) ([World Map] City Spell): Target Vassal City:
  
  - No longer gains Allegiance
  - Vassal Income is increased by +2% for each point of Allegiance Income instead.
- [Mark of Tyranny](../spells/mark-of-tyranny.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Dark units:
  
  - Cult of Tyranny
- Dark Forge (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Gold. +5 Production per adjacent Quarry, Mine, or Forester. Counts as a Mine.
