# Eldritch Pact

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

These boons can be purchased from an Umbral Dwelling by reaching an Eldritch Pact with them. They help your Empire adapt to the Umbral Abyss and spread Gloom.

## Unlocks

- [Ritual of the Gloomveil](../spells/ritual-of-the-gloomveil.md) ([World Map] Sustained World Spell): All Units in your Empire gain Umbral Malady Immunity.
- Altar of Marching Gloom (Special Province Improvement): - +10 Mana income.
  - Every Turn, 1 Province within a radius equal to the City Tier:
  
  - Gains Gloom.
  - Loses Chasm, River, Swamp, Sand, Snow, Ice, and Ashlands.
  
  - Counts as a Conduit.
- [Umbral Flesh](../spells/umbral-flesh.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Gives the target race blessings of the Umbral Abyss, granting them:
  
  - Umbral Malady Immunity
  - While in Gloom terrain units gain:
  
  - +10 Morale.
  - +12 Hit Point per Turn on the world map.
  
  - Cities and Outposts of the race:
  
  - Gain +2 Knowledge income for each province with Gloom terrain.
  - Ignore City Stability penalties from Gloom terrain.
  
  - Hero units gain Gloom Shepherd.
  
  This transformation is incompatible with Pure Soul.
- [Summon Umbral Juggernaut](../spells/summon-umbral-juggernaut.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons an Umbral Juggernaut on target hex.
- [Umbral Exile](../spells/umbral-exile.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target unit has a base 60% chance of:
  
  - Being immediately removed from combat.
  - Appearing at a random location in the Umbral Abyss after battle.
  - If unsuccessful, inflicts Stunned for 1 Turn instead.
- [Umbral Rift](../spells/umbral-rift.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Create an Umbral Rift in the target Umbral Abyss province, and another nearby on the Surface layer.
- [Pall of Gloom](../spells/pall-of-gloom.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target Province and adjacent Provinces:
  
  - Gain Gloom
  - Lose Chasm, River, Sand, Snow, Ice, Ashlands, Swamp, Cavern Floor, and Dungeon.
  - Enemy Units suffer -1 Frost Protection and -1 Blight Protection for 2 Turn.
- Altar of Marching Gloom (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Mana income.; Every Turn, 1 Province within a radius equal to the City Tier: ; Gains Gloom.; Loses Chasm, River, Swamp, Sand, Snow, Ice, and Ashlands. ; Counts as a Conduit.
