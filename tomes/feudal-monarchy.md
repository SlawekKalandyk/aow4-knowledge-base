# Feudal - Monarchy

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to rulers with the Feudal culture. They specialize in cheap troops and leadership.

## Unlocks

- [Call Militia](../spells/call-militia.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon 2 Militia in the target owned City.
- [Monarch's Decree](../spells/monarchs-decree.md) ([Combat] Combat Enchantment): For 2 Turns, all friendly units gain the benefit from For the Monarch as though the Monarch were present.
- [Call to Glory](../spells/call-to-glory.md) ([Combat] Combat Enchantment): For 3 Turns all friendly units gain:
  
  - +5 Morale at the start of your Turn.
- Farmstead (Initial City Upgrade): - +15 Food income.; +5 Food per adjacent Farm.; Counts as a Farm.
