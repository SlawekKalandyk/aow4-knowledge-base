# High

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to rulers with the High culture. They specialize in buffing allies and diplomacy.

## Unlocks

- [Awaken Inner Radiance](../spells/awaken-inner-radiance.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Friendly units in a 1-hex radius become Awakened for 3 Turns.
  If already Awakened, a unit gains Strengthened instead.
- [Warding Blessing](../spells/warding-blessing.md) ([Combat] Healing/Buff Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Heals +20 Temporary Hit Points
  - Gains 2 Bolstered Resistance
- [Dormant Enchantment](../spells/dormant-enchantment.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-High units:
  
  - Dormant: Guardian if a Polearm Unit or Fighter Unit.
  - Dormant: Shield of Light if a Shield Unit, Shock Unit, or Mythic Unit.
  - Dormant: Seeking Arrows if a Ranged Unit or Skirmisher Unit.
  - Dormant: Radiant Light if a Battle Mage Unit or Magic Fighter Unit.
  - The Awaken ability and Dormant: Radiant Light if a Support Unit.
- Alignment Agenda: The High Culture has an Alignment Agenda, which grants bonuses for the following alignment levels:
  
  - Pure Good: 25 City Stability in all Cities.
  - Neutral: 10 Food and 10 Production per City Stability level above Neutral in all Cities.
  - Pure Evil: Units start Combat in an Awakened state.
- Sunshrine (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Knowledge income. +3 Knowledge per adjacent Research Post. Friendly Units in this Domain are Encouraged. Counts as a Research Post.
