# Industrious

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to rulers with the Industrious culture. They specialize in defenses and exploiting natural resources.

## Unlocks

- [Steelfury Chant](../spells/steelfury-chant.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): All friendly units lose all stacks of Bolstered Defense and Bolstered Resistance. For each stack lost they gain:
  
  - Strengthened
  - Fortune
- [Rune of Industry](../spells/rune-of-industry.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Industrious units:
  
  - Bolstering, which increases defense when they sustain damage.
- [Bolstering Chant](../spells/bolstering-chant.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Heals +20 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Gains 2 Bolstered Defense.
- Scout Prospecting (): When Industrious Scout Units stand within a friendly or neutral Province that contains a Cliff, Mountain or Stalagmite, the player that owns the Scout Unit can prospect the Province for Production or Gold rewards.
  
  Each Province can only be prospected once.
- Builder's Quarters (Initial City Upgrade): +15 Production income. +5 Production per adjacent Quarry. Counts as a Quarry.
