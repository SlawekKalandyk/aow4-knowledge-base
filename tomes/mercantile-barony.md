# Mercantile - Barony

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

This research is available to Rulers of the Mercantile Barony subculture.

## Unlocks

- [Shadow Negotiations](../spells/shadow-negotiations.md) ([World Map] Sustained World Spell): Target Free City:
  
  - Allegiance gain from Whispering Stones is increased by +1.
  - Other Rulers' Allegiance gain is reduced by -30%.
- [Mark Bounty](../spells/mark-bounty.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Suffers Bounty until the end of combat.
  - Suffers 3 stacks of Marked for 3 Turns.
- [Bounty Hunter's Writ](../spells/bounty-hunters-writ.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Mercantile, units:
  
  - Bounty Hunter
  - Support units gain Assign Bounty instead.
- Trade District (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Gold. For each Trade Route connecting to or from this city, the city gains: +10 Gold
