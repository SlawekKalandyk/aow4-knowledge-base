# Mercantile - Cartel

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

This research is available to Rulers of the Mercantile Cartel subculture.

## Unlocks

- [Bribes and Incentives](../spells/bribes-and-incentives.md) ([World Map] Sustained World Spell): Target Infestation becomes neutral towards you and will not launch attacks against you for 10 Turns.
- [Scry Leverage](../spells/scry-leverage.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Suffers 2 Leverage.
  
  This effect jumps to 2 additional targets within 3 hexes.
- [Signia of the Cartel](../spells/signia-of-the-cartel.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Mercantile units:
  
  - Observant
- Privateers Camp (Initial City Upgrade): +15 Gold. +10 City Stability for 10 Turns after Raiding a Supply Route. +5 City Stability for 10 Turns after Pillaging a province. Counts as a Mine.
