# Mystic - School of Attunement

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to rulers with the Mystic culture. They specialize in magic damage and spellcasting.

## Unlocks

- [Magic Shield](../spells/magic-shield.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target unit gains:
  
  - 2 Bolstered Defense for 3 Turns.
  - 2 Bolstered Resistance for 3 Turns.
- [Astral Divination](../spells/astral-divination.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Create and reveal new Astral Echoes in provinces near your domain.
- [Scroll of Attunement](../spells/scroll-of-attunement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Mystic units:
  
  - Attunement: Star Blades, which increases damage dealt when spells are cast.
- Astral Echoes (): Mysterious whispers of the Astral Sea, only visible to and collectible by Mystics.
- Mystic Abbey (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Mana income.; +3 Knowledge per adjacent Conduit or Research Post.; Counts as a Conduit.
