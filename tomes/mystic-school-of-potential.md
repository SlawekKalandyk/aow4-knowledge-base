# Mystic - School of Potential

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to rulers with the Mystic culture. They specialize in magic damage and spellcasting.

## Unlocks

- [Arcane Studies](../spells/arcane-studies.md) ([World Map] City Spell): For 10 Turns, target owned city:
  
  - Contributes 1 Arcane Inspiration to a random spell each Turn.
- [Cosmic Ablation](../spells/cosmic-ablation.md) ([Combat] Damage Spell): Target unit sustains:
  
  - 10 Lightning Damage
  - 10 Fire Damage
  - 10 Frost Damage
- [Ciphers of Dissonance](../spells/ciphers-of-dissonance.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Mystic units:
  
  - Ciphers of Dissonance, which inflicts Dissonance on enemy units.
- Spell Arcanist Library (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Knowledge income.; +3 Knowledge per adjacent Conduit or Research Post.; Counts as a Research Post.
