# Mystic - School of Summoning

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: Base

This research is available to rulers with the Mystic culture. They specialize in magic damage and spellcasting.

## Unlocks

- [Scroll of Astral Connection](../spells/scroll-of-astral-connection.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Mystic non-Magic Origin units:
  
  - Astral Connection.
- [Astral Divination](../spells/astral-divination.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Create and reveal new Astral Echoes in provinces near your domain.
- [Conjure Summoning Rift](../spells/conjure-summoning-rift.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Conjures a Summoning Rift on an empty hex in the target Province. This structure:
  
  - Allows summoning spells to be cast on or adjacent to it.
  - Provides vision in 5-hex radius.
  - Units in this province gain regenerate +15 Hit Points per Turn.
  - During combat in this province friendly [Combat] Combat Summon Spells are 20% cheaper to cast.
- [Conjure Astral Ward](../spells/conjure-astral-ward.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Summon an Immobile Astral Ward.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- Astral Echoes (): Mysterious whispers of the Astral Sea, only visible to and collectible by Mystics.
- Astral Manalith (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Mana income. +5 Mana per adjacent Conduit or Research Post. Counts as a Conduit.
