# Nomad - Scavengers

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

This research is available to Rulers with the Nomad culture. They specialize in mobility.

## Unlocks

- [Improvised Remedy](../spells/improvised-remedy.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): All friendly units with Looted Power:
  
  - Heal for 10 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Have their Negative Status Effects removed.
- [Empowering Trophies](../spells/empowering-trophies.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target friendly unit with Scavenger:
  
  - Gains +1 Looted Power
- [Scavenger's Bags](../spells/scavengers-bags.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grant non-Culture units:
  
  - Scavenger
- Scavenging Camp (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Gold. +10 Gold for this and each adjacent province containing a Resource Node. Counts as a Mine.
