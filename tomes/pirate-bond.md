# Pirate Bond

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

These boons can be purchased from a Free Captain by reaching various bond levels with them. Granting pirate themed spell and units.

## Unlocks

- Conjure Pirate Captain (Combat Summon Spell): Conjure a Free Captain into combat as a Combat Summon. Cannot be used when that captain is already in combat.
- [Conjure Pirate Cannon Tower](../spells/conjure-pirate-cannon-tower.md) (Combat Summon Spell): Conjure a Pirate Cannon Tower, a Tier III long range Tower unit.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- Pirate Smuggling Den (City Structure): Trade Routes in this city going over Ocean provinces grant +3 Gold for each Ocean province they cross.
- [Construct Pirate Cove](../spells/construct-pirate-cove.md) (World Spell): Place a Pirate Cove Infestation on target water province. It is neutral towards you.
- [Wavemaster](../units/wavemaster.md) (Unit): A magical fighter who specializes in displacing the enemy around the battlefield.
