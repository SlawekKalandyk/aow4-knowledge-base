# Primal - Dune Serpent

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

This research is available to rulers with the Primal culture. They specialize in spiritual communion and empowerment.

## Unlocks

- [Serpent Primal Communion](../spells/serpent-primal-communion.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grant non-Culture units:
  
  - Dune Serpent's Boon
  - Sand Walk
- [Conjure Primal Serpent](../spells/conjure-primal-serpent.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Summon a Tier 2 Primal Serpent.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Ancestral Harmony](../spells/ancestral-harmony.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Heals 15 Temporary Hit Points.
  - If Fury of the Tunneling Spider is active, heal twice the amount.
- Dune Serpent Den (): Can see Dune Serpent Den nodes which when annexed to a city:
  
  - Grant +15 City Stability.
  - Allow a Dune Serpent Temple to be built.
- Dune Megalith (Initial City Upgrade): +4 Gold per adjacent Sand Province. Counts as a Conduit.
