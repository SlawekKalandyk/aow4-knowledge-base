# Primal - Mire Crocodile

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

This research is available to rulers with the Primal culture. They specialize in spiritual communion and empowerment.

## Unlocks

- [Crocodile Primal Communion](../spells/crocodile-primal-communion.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grant non-Culture units:
  
  - Mire Crocodile's Boon
  - Swamp Walk
  - Status Effect Immunity: Diseased
- [Conjure Primal Crocodile](../spells/conjure-primal-crocodile.md) (Combat Summon Spell): Summon a Tier 2 Primal Crocodile.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Ancestral Harmony](../spells/ancestral-harmony.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Heals 15 Temporary Hit Points.
  - If Fury of the Tunneling Spider is active, heal twice the amount.
- Mire Crocodile Den (): Can see Mire Crocodile Den nodes which when annexed to a city:
  
  - Grant +15 City Stability.
  - Allow a Mire Crocodile Temple to be built.
- Mire Megalith (Initial City Upgrade): - +6 Food per adjacent Swamp Province.; Counts as a Conduit.
