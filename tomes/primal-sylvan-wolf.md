# Primal - Sylvan Wolf

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

This research is available to rulers with the Primal culture. They specialize in spiritual communion and empowerment.

## Unlocks

- [Ancestral Harmony](../spells/ancestral-harmony.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Heals 15 Temporary Hit Points.
  - If Fury of the Tunneling Spider is active, heal twice the amount.
- [Wolf Primal Communion](../spells/wolf-primal-communion.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grant non-Culture units:
  
  - Sylvan Wolf's Boon
  - Forest Walk
- [Conjure Primal Wolf](../spells/conjure-primal-wolf.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Summon a Tier 2 Primal Wolf.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- Sylvan Wolf Den: Can see Sylvan Wolf Den nodes which when annexed to a city:
  
  - Grant +15 City Stability.
  - Allow a Sylvan Wolf Temple to be built.
- Sylvan Megalith (Initial City Upgrade): +6 Draft per adjacent Forest Province; Counts as a Conduit.
