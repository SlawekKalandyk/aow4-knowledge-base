# Reaver - Federated

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

This research is available to rulers with the Reaver culture. They use Magelock technology.

## Unlocks

- [Revitalizing Alliance](../spells/revitalizing-alliance.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Heal target friendly unit:
  
  - Federate Soldier units:
  
  - +10 Temporary Hit Points
  - +5 Temporary Hit Points for each friendly Federate Levy in battle.
  
  - Federate Levy units:
  
  - +10 Temporary Hit Points
  - +5 Temporary Hit Points for each friendly Federate Soldier in battle.
  
  This effect jumps to 2 additional targets within 3 hexes.
- [Banner of the Federation](../spells/banner-of-the-federation.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Reaver units:
  
  - Federate Soldier
- [Sundering Barrage](../spells/sundering-barrage.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Suffer 10 Physical Damage.
  - Have a 90% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense for 3 Turn
- Reaver Manufactorum (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Gold.; +5 Gold income per Vassal.; Counts as a Mine.
