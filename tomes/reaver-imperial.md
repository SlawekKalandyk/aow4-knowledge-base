# Reaver - Imperial

- Tier: 0
- Affinity granted: 
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

This research is available to rulers with the Reaver culture. They use Magelock technology.

## Unlocks

- [Engraving of Focus](../spells/engraving-of-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants non-Reaver units:
  
  - Focused Aggression.
- [Suppressing Barrage](../spells/suppressing-barrage.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Suffer 10 Physical Damage.
  - Have their Defense Mode canceled and Retaliation Attacks removed.
- [Designate Target](../spells/designate-target.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target enemy gains:
  
  - 2 Marked
  - Immobilized
- Imperial Warfactory (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Production. 10 Draft per adjacent Quarry, Forester, or Mine. Counts as a Quarry.
