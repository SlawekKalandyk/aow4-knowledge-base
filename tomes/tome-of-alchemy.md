# Tome of Alchemy

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Materium 1, Nature 1
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

Inflict your enemies with Negative Status Effects and mitigate them on your own units. Tinctures and a powerful ranged unit will help you manipulate the tides of battle.

## Unlocks

- Material Refinery (City Structure): - +5 Knowledge income.
  - +5 Gold income.
  - +5 Knowledge income per Magic Material in this city's Domain.
  - +5 Gold income per Magic Material in this city's Domain.
- Fumigation (Siege Project): At the start of combat units defending the city:
  
  - Suffer 16 Blight Damage.
  - Suffer 2 Weakened.
- [Antimagic Tincture](../spells/antimagic-tincture.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Friendly units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Have 2 Negative Status Effects dispelled.
  - Gain 2 Status Protection.
- [Afflictor](../units/afflictor.md) (Unit): Ranged Unit that can create Miasmas to debilitate enemies.
- [Disperse Afflicting Miasma](../spells/disperse-afflicting-miasma.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Create Afflicting Miasma in a 1-radius.
- [Mysterious Tonic](../spells/mysterious-tonic.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Distribute Tonic, granting Positive Status Effects while removing a negative one.
- Alchemist's Lab (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Knowledge.; +3 Knowledge per adjacent unique Province Improvement.; Counts as a Research Post.
