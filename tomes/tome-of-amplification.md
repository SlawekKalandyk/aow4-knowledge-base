# Tome of Amplification

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 Astral (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Enhance your mages and your spellcasting with more advanced arcane magic.

## Unlocks

- [Amplify Minds](../spells/amplify-minds.md) ([World Map] Sustained City Spell): Target owned city gains:
  
  - +20 Knowledge income
  - -10 City Stability
- [Astral Blood](../spells/astral-blood.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes magic begin to flow through the veins of the target race, granting them:
  
  - Attunement: Fortune, which increases Critical Hit Chance when spells are cast.
- [Chain Lightning](../spells/chain-lightning.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Sustains 30 Lightning Damage.
  - Is inflicted with 2 Electrified.
  - This effect passes on to another enemy within 3 hexes.
  - Passes on up to 2 times.
- [Conjure Amplification Pylon](../spells/conjure-amplification-pylon.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Conjures an Amplification Pylon, a stationary structure that deals damage and increases the damage of your spells by +50%.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Amplifying Imbuement](../spells/amplifying-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes base attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Deal +3 Lightning Damage.
  - Deal 3 Lightning Damage to 2 other targets within 2 hexes.
  - Have a base 30% chance of inflicting Sundered Resistance on each target hit for 3 Turns.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- Resonance Fields (Initial City Upgrade): +5 Mana. +5 World Map Casting Points. +5 Combat Casting Points. Counts as a Conduit.
