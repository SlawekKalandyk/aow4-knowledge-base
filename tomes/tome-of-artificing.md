# Tome of Artificing

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Specialize in siegecraft, expertly crafted weapons, and Golems.

## Unlocks

- Artisan Fortification (City Structure): A Tower Structure that grants the city Bolt Repeater Towers during siege combat.
- [Siege Magic](../spells/siege-magic.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - +10% damage
  - Siege Breaker, dealing extra fortification damage during a siege.
  - Demolisher, making them able to destroy reinforced obstacles.
- [Iron Golem](../units/iron-golem.md) (Unit): Highly defensive Shield Unit that is resistant to Negative Status Effects and protects allies from them.
- [Artisan Armaments](../spells/artisan-armaments.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - +25% Critical Hit Chance.
- Construct Bolt Repeaters (Siege Project): At the start of the battle, gain 2 Bolt Repeater units on the attacker's side until the end of battle.
  - Bolt Repeaters are immobile Siegecraft Units with long range area of effect attacks that are repeating, unlike Onagers.
  - Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- Golem Mine (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Gold.; +5 Production per adjacent Quarry.; Spawns an Iron Golem on the owner's side for Combat in this Domain.; Counts as a Mine.
