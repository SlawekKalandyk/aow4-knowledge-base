# Tome of Astral Convergence

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Astral (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Become stronger the more spells you cast and summon creatures from the Astral Sea.

## Unlocks

- [Cascading Power](../spells/cascading-power.md) ([World Map] Sustained World Spell): Whenever you cast a spell in Tactical Combat, add a Stack of Cascading Power to that combat.
- [Astral Shattering](../spells/astral-shattering.md) (World Spell): Target Province:
  
  - If it contains an enemy Province Improvement and no Ancient Wonder, it becomes ruins.
  - Spawns a powerful marauding Army of Astral Sea Units.
- Arcane Accumulation (Siege Project): - At the start of combat, gain 50 Combat Casting Points.
  - During combat, all enemy Units suffer -1 Resistance.
- [Explosive Manifestation](../spells/explosive-manifestation.md) (Combat Summon Spell): Target non-occupied hex:
  
  - Conjure an Astral Serpent or Astral Siphoner unit at random that lasts for 3 Turns.
  - All adjacent units sustain 10 Fire Damage, 10 Lightning Damage, and 10 Frost Damage.
- [Astral Attunement](../spells/astral-attunement.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Links the target race to the Astral Sea, granting them:
  
  - The Ethereal unit type.
  - Attunement: Astral Omen, granting a random Positive Status Effect when a spell is cast in combat.
