# Tome of Beasts

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Nature 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Walk beside the Animals of nature. Specialize in summoning and buffing Animals and become stronger when standing next to them.

## Unlocks

- [Call of the Wild](../spells/call-of-the-wild.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Friendly Animal and Cavalry units in a 1-hex radius gain:
  
  - 2 Bolstered Defense
  - 2 Strengthened
- [Wildspeaker](../units/wildspeaker.md) (Unit): A Support Unit that summons and empowers Animals.
- [Call Wild Animal](../spells/call-wild-animal.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): - Choose a Tier I or Tier II Animal unit to add to your army.
  - The available Animals depend on the type of terrain the spell is cast on.
- [Animal Kinship](../spells/animal-kinship.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes the target race more bestial and connected to animals. When adjacent to a friendly Animal, both units are granted:
  
  - +10% damage
  - +10% Critical Hit Chance
- [Mark as Prey](../spells/mark-as-prey.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target enemy:
  
  - Becomes Distracted.
  - Suffers 3 Sundered Defense.
- Wildlife Sanctuary (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Food. +5 Draft per adjacent Province with Forest. Unlocks the production of various Animal Units. Counts as a Forester.
