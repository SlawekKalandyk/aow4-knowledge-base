# Tome of Burning Passion

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Strengthen your units with Demonic wrath, seduce your enemies with fiendish lust, and engulf both in infernal flames.

## Unlocks

- [Thrill of Combat](../spells/thrill-of-combat.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target friendly unit gains:
  
  - Berserk for 3 Turns.
  - 5 Infernal Might for 3 Turns.
- [Fiery Heart](../spells/fiery-heart.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Target race gains a fiery core, gaining:
  
  - +2 Fire Protection
  - +10% Critical Hit chance
  - Fire Hazard Immunity
- [Strength Sapping Imbuement](../spells/strength-sapping-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units attacks gain:
  
  - +1 Fire Damage.
  - Base attacks have a base 60% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns. On success gain Strengthened for 3 Turns.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Blazing Aura](../spells/blazing-aura.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - Blazing Aura, burning and damaging nearby enemies.
- [Conjure Orb of Desire](../spells/conjure-orb-of-desire.md) (Combat Summon Spell): Conjure an Orb of Desire on target hex that Taunts enemies and burns attackers.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Temptress](../units/temptress.md) (Unit): A Magic Fighter Unit that can inflict Dominated on enemy units.
- House of Passion (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Draft.; +6 Gold per adjacent Farm.; Counts as a Quarry.
