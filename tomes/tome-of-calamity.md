# Tome of Calamity

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Chaos 1, Shadow 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 combined Chaos + Shadow (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Bring calamity to the realm and burn your enemies in cold Ghostfire.

## Unlocks

- [Accursed Armors](../spells/accursed-armors.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - When a melee range attack hits, the attacker has a 60% chance of being inflicted with Misfortune for 3 Turns.
  - +1 Defense.
- [Accursed Imbuement](../spells/accursed-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Deal +1 Fire Damage.
  - Deal +1 Frost Damage
  - Inflict Ghostfire for 3 Turns.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- Ritual of Calamity (Siege Project): - At the start of every Turn during the siege, a random Province Improvement of the besieged city is pillaged, granting you rewards.
  - At the start of combat:
  
  - All defenders gain a stack of Ghostfire for 3 Turns.
  - 2 Accursed Ogres appear on the attacker's side.
- [Desecrate Land](../spells/desecrate-land.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target Province:
  
  - If it contains a Province Improvement, it becomes Pillaged.
  
  - You are granted its rewards and lose Alignment.
  
  - The province gains Ashlands.
  - The province loses River, Grassland, Swamp, Snow, Ice, Sand, Cavern Floor, and Gloom.
- [Comet of Calamity](../spells/comet-of-calamity.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Units in a 2-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 15 Fire Damage.
  - Sustain 15 Frost Damage.
  - Have a 50% base chance of becoming Frozen for 1 Turn.
  - Gain 2 stacks of Ghostfire for 3 Turns.
- [Summon Calamity Dragon](../spells/summon-calamity-dragon.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Target owned non-ruined Province turns to Ruins and Desolate terrain and then summons a Calamity Dragon, a Mythic Unit unit with strong offensive capabilities.
- Accursed Shrine (Initial City Upgrade): - When placed, alter terrain into Desolate.; Per adjacent Ruin province gain: ; +3 Draft; +3 Food; +3 Gold; +3 Knowledge; +3 Mana; +3 Production ; Allows the drafting of Accursed Ogre, Accursed Blade, and Accursed Trickster; Counts as a Conduit.
