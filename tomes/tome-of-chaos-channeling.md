# Tome of Chaos Channeling

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Chaos (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Become the master of chaos by exploiting your accumulated chaotic powers from previous Chaos Affinity Tomes.

## Unlocks

- [Flamer Focus](../spells/flamer-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - The Fire Bomb ability, which deals magical damage in an area.
  - The damage of this ability increases with the unit's tier.
- [Summon Flame Incarnate](../spells/summon-flame-incarnate.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a non-Fiend Tier V Mythic Unit with Fire and Burning-related abilities on target hex.
- [Scion of Flame](../spells/scion-of-flame.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes the target race the embodiment of fire, granting them:
  
  - 4 Fire Protection.
  - Vengeful Flames, which damages Melee attackers.
  - Fiery Wake, which ignites flammable terrain such as Flora Obstacles.
  - Immunity to Burning.
  - Lava Walk.
  - Cities of this race ignore the City Stability penalty from Chasm and Lava in their Domain.
- [Fan the Inferno](../spells/fan-the-inferno.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): All enemy units:
  
  - Sustain 5 Fire Damage that ignores 50% of Resistance.
  - Are inflicted with Burning.
- [Abyssal Flames](../spells/abyssal-flames.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target Province:
  
  - Enemy Units suffer -2 Fire Protection for 1 World Map Turn.
  - The provinces gain Ashlands.
  - The provinces lose River, Grassland, Swamp, Snow, Ice, Sand, Cavern Floor, and Gloom.
  - Summon a Magma Spirit on the targeted hex.
- [Golden Horde](../spells/golden-horde.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): - Summons a full army of random non-Scout Tier I units that can be produced in a city on the target hex.
  - Summon units start at Champion rank.
  - If cast within an enemy Province, instantly pillages it.
