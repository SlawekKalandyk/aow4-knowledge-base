# Tome of Cryomancy

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- DLC: Base

Specialize in dealing Frost Damage and inflicting Chilled and Frozen.

## Unlocks

- [Blizzard](../spells/blizzard.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): All units in target enemy army:
  
  - Sustain 20 Frost Damage.
  - Suffer -1 Frost Protection for 1 World Map Turns.
- [Summon Lesser Snow Spirit](../spells/summon-lesser-snow-spirit.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Lesser Snow Spirit, a Magic Fighter Unit with attacks that inflict Chilled.
- [Frost Infusion](../spells/frost-infusion.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units deal:
  
  - +2 Frost Damage.
  - +10% damage against Frozen or Chilled units.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [White Witch](../units/white-witch.md) (Unit): Grants access to a Battle Mage Unit that inflicts Chilled and Frozen.
- [Ice Coffin](../spells/ice-coffin.md) ([Combat] Damage Spell): Target enemy:
  
  - Sustains 10 Frost Damage.
  - Has a base 90% chance of becoming Frozen. If unsuccessful, inflicts Chilled.
- [Frost Arrows](../spells/frost-arrows.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units gain:
  
  - +1 Frost Damage.
  - A base 60% chance of inflicting Chilled.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- School of Cryomancy (Initial City Upgrade): - +8 Knowledge.; +3 Mana per adjacent Snow or Ice Province.; Counts as a Research Post.
