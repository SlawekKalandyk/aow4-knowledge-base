# Tome of Discipline

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Materium 1, Order 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Channel your inner strength to empower your attacks and cleanse your body.

## Unlocks

- [Focus Aim](../spells/focus-aim.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - The Focus Aim ability, which allows the unit to make an attack that cannot miss
- [Empowered Strikes](../spells/empowered-strikes.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes base Melee attacks and Magic Strikes of enchanted units:
  
  - Deal +2 Physical Damage.
  - Third attack in a turn has a 60% base chance of inflicting Stunned for 1 Turn
  
  Enchantment doesn't apply to units without a repeating base melee attack.
- [Inner Mastery](../spells/inner-mastery.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Grants the target race mastery over their own internal energies, granting them:
  
  - +1 Status Resistance
  - +20% healing received in combat.
- [Mantra of Purification](../spells/mantra-of-purification.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Heals 15 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Has 3 Negative Status Effect dispelled.
- [Monk](../units/monk.md) (Unit): Mobile Magic Fighter Unit that can use devastating combo attacks and heal itself.
- Monastery (Initial City Upgrade): - 10 City Stability.; 10 Draft; Per adjacent Farm: ; 2 City Stability; 3 Draft ; Counts as a Conduit
