# Tome of Dragons

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Nature 1, Chaos 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 combined Nature + Chaos (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS

Revel in the power of Dragons and let their flames empower your people.

## Unlocks

- Dragon Infusion (Empire Bonus): Non-Racial Dragon units can be ranked up rapidly by exchanging Gold to gain XP Experience.
- [Draconian Transformation](../spells/draconian-transformation.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Turn target race into Draconians, which grants them:
  
  - Dragon unit type.
  - +10 HP Hit Points.
  - Natural Regeneration, which makes the unit regain health faster.
- Dragon Attack (Siege Project): - At the start of combat:
  
  - Units defending the city take 26 Fire Damage.
  - Units defending the city have a base 120% of suffering Burning.
  - Random positions are On Fire.
  - Most Battlement Structure obstacles are On Fire.
- [Purifying Flame](../spells/purifying-flame.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Friendly units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Heal for 25 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Have their Negative Status Effects removed.
- [Dragonstrike Infusion](../spells/dragonstrike-infusion.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Dragonstrike.
  - The damage type is determined by your empire's Dominant Affinity and increases with the unit's tier.
- Call young Dragon ([World Map] Summon Spell): Choose a Tier III Young Dragon to add to your army:
  
  - Young Fire Dragon
  - Young Frost Dragon
  - Young Obsidian Dragon
  - Young Golden Dragon
  
  These units may Evolve into Adult Dragons.
- Wyvern Eyrie (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Gold.; +5 Gold per adjacent Farm Province.; Unlocks the production of various Wyvern Units. Alignment influences which Wyverns can be drafted.; Counts as a Mine.
