# Tome of Enchantment

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Improve your units with [World Map] Unit Enchantments and specialize in Physical Damage.

## Unlocks

- [Awakened Tools](../spells/awakened-tools.md) ([World Map] City Spell): Target owned city has:
  
  - +20 Production
  - +20 Draft
  - -10 City Stability
- [Sundering Blades](../spells/sundering-blades.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Base 90% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense, reducing enemy Defense.
  - Demolisher, making them able to destroy reinforced obstacles.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Purging Arrows](../spells/purging-arrows.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - +10% damage against Magic Origin units.
  - Base 60% chance of removing 1 Positive Status Effect from the target.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Summon Copper Golem](../spells/summon-copper-golem.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Copper Golem, a Tier I Polearm Unit.
- [Spell-Tempered Shields](../spells/spell-tempered-shields.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - +1 Resistance.
  - When entering Defense Mode, this unit and all adjacent allies receive +1 Resistance until the start of the next turn.
- Runecarver's Camp (Initial City Upgrade): - +15 Draft.; +3 Mana per adjacent Quarry.; Unit deployment location.; Counts as a Quarry.
