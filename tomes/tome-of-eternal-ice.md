# Tome of Eternal Ice

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Embrace the power of frost and freeze your enemies while making your own units eternal.

## Unlocks

- [Whiteout](../spells/whiteout.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target and adjacent Province:
  
  - Gain Whiteout for 3 Turns.
  - Water provinces gain Ice for 3 Turns.
  - Alter land provinces to Snow and Ice.
  
  - Loses Chasm, Swamp, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, and Gloom.
- [Ice Focus](../spells/ice-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - Base 60% chance of inflicting Chilled with all attacks.
  - The Ice Nova ability.
  - The damage of this ability increases with the unit's tier.
- [Eternal Ice Blades](../spells/eternal-ice-blades.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted unit's attacks:
  
  - Deal +2 Frost Damage.
  - Inflict Chilled for 3 Turns.
  - This unit gains Regeneration when it inflicts Chilled.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Ice Fusion](../spells/ice-fusion.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Target race becomes one with ice, gaining:
  
  - The Elemental unit type.
  - +4 Frost Protection.
  - -2 Fire Protection
  - Immunity to Frozen and Chilled.
  - Rime Healing.
  - Water Walk.
- [Deep Freeze](../spells/deep-freeze.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Sustains 40 Frost Damage.
  - Has a base 120% chance of becoming Frozen for 1 Turn. If resisted, gains 2 Chilled for 3 Turns.
  
  Adjacent enemy units:
  
  - Sustain 20 Frost Damage.
  - Has a base 90% chance of becoming Frozen for 1 Turn. If resisted, gains Chilled for 3 Turns.
- [Summon Ancient Hoarfrost Manta](../spells/summon-ancient-hoarfrost-manta.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon an Ancient Hoarfrost Manta, a Mythic Unit that supports your units as it freezes your enemies.
- Permafrost Nexus (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Knowledge; +7 Mana; In this city's domain: ; Provinces gain Whiteout.; 2 provinces per turn: ; Gain Snow and/or Ice.; Provinces with Water gain Ice.; Lose Chasm, Swamp, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, and Gloom. ; Counts as a Research Post.
