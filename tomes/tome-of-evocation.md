# Tome of Evocation

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Grants excellent and cheap attack spells to any aspiring wizard.

## Unlocks

- [Lightning Torrent](../spells/lightning-torrent.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): In target enemy army:
  
  - All units sustain 20 Lightning Damage.
  - All units suffer -1 Lightning Protection for 1 World Map Turns.
- [Evoker](../units/evoker.md) (Unit): A Battle Mage Unit with offensive chain lightning magic.
- [Lightning Focus](../spells/lightning-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Deal +2 Lightning Damage.
  - Gain base 30% chance of inflicting Electrified, a damage-over-time effect.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Summon Lesser Storm Spirit](../spells/summon-lesser-storm-spirit.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Lesser Storm Spirit on target hex.
- [Lightning Blades](../spells/lightning-blades.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units deal:
  
  - +2 Lightning Damage.
  - +10% damage against Electrified units.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Fulmination](../spells/fulmination.md) ([Combat] Damage Spell): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 15 Lightning Damage.
  - Have a 60% chance of becoming Electrified.
- Channeling Tower (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Mana.; +3 Mana per adjacent Conduit.; Counts as a Conduit.
