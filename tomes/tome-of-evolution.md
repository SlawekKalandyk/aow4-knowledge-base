# Tome of Evolution

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Nature 1, Chaos 1
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS

Take young primal forces and nurture them into formidable allies.

## Unlocks

- [Draconic Vitality](../spells/draconic-vitality.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Target race is imbued with Dragon-like vitality, granting them:
  
  - +5 HP Hit Point regeneration per World Map Turn.
  - +3 HP Hit Points per Unit Rank.
  - +3 HP Hit Points per Hero Level, up to Level 10.
- [Summon Wyvern Fledgling](../spells/summon-wyvern-fledgling.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Wyvern Fledgling to target hex.
- [Youthful Rejuvenation](../spells/youthful-rejuvenation.md) ([Combat] Healing/Buff Spell): Target unit:
  
  - Heals for 25 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Gains 2 stacks of Strengthened.
  
  Units with Evolve or Product of Evolution:
  
  - Gain Resurgence until the end of combat.
- [Rapid Evolution Enchantment](../spells/rapid-evolution-enchantment.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - 15 XP Experience per Turn.
  - Slip Away healing them once per battle if their HP Hit Points would be reduced to zero.
- [Slither Hatchling](../units/slither-hatchling.md) (Unit): A Skirmisher Unit that evolves into a stronger unit.
