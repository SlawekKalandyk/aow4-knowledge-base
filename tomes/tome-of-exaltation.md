# Tome of Exaltation

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Order (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Convert your people to Celestials and use their faith to smite your enemies.

## Unlocks

- [Resurrect Unit](../spells/resurrect-unit.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Returns target dead friendly unit to full Temporary Hit Points.
- Temple of the Exalted (City Structure): City gains:
  
  - +30 City Stability
  - +30 Mana
- [Shrine of Smiting](../units/shrine-of-smiting.md) (Unit): A Mythic Unit whose power scales with the number of Faithful units on the battlefield.
- [Angelic Transformation](../spells/angelic-transformation.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Turns the target race into angelic beings, granting them:
  
  - The Celestial unit type.
  - Flying Movement, which increases mobility.
  - Faithful, which reduces Unit Upkeep.
  - Decreased number of units in formation.
- [Ascended Warriors](../spells/ascended-warriors.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Non-Hero units in target army gain 1 Rank.
  
  Cannot promote units above Legendary rank.
- Conduit Ruler's Statue (Initial City Upgrade): - Can only be built once in your Throne City.; Allied Empires and Free Cities with a Supreme Vassalage grant 3 Imperium.; Ruler respawns 1 Turn faster.; Spells can be cast even if the Ruler is in the void.; Counts as a Conduit.
