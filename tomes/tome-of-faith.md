# Tome of Faith

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- DLC: Base

Heal and support your units through the power of the Faithful.

## Unlocks

- [Army Heal](../spells/army-heal.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Units in target army heal 25 HP Hit Points.
- [Faithful Whispers](../spells/faithful-whispers.md) ([World Map] Sustained City Spell): Allegiance with target Free City increases 30% faster.
- Convent (City Structure): For each City Stability level above Unstable the city gains:
  
  - +5 Knowledge.
  - +5 Mana.
- [Staves of Mending](../spells/staves-of-mending.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Faithful, which reduces base Unit Upkeep.
  - Mending Touch, a healing ability for use in battle.
- [Summon Lesser Light Spirit](../spells/summon-lesser-light-spirit.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Lesser Light Spirit Support Unit.
- [Wrath of the Faithful](../spells/wrath-of-the-faithful.md) (Combat Spell): Target enemy unit sustains 10 Spirit Damage plus 5 Spirit Damage per each friendly Faithful unit in battle (up to 8).
- Abbey (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Knowledge; +3 Knowledge per adjacent Farm.; Grants Status Protection at the start of the next Combat to all friendly Units on this hex. 3 Turn cooldown.; Counts as a Research Post.
