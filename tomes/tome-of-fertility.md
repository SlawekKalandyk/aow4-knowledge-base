# Tome of Fertility

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Nature 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Thrive on the bountiful spoils of nature. Provide your empire with ample Food and heal your units in battle.

## Unlocks

- [Staves of Life](../spells/staves-of-life.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes Attack or Support abilities of enchanted units:
  
  - Heal a random friendly unit within 3 hexes of it for 15 Temporary Hit Point.
- [Restore the Land](../spells/restore-the-land.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target and adjacent Provinces:
  
  - Gains Grassland if it is on the Surface or Fungus Fields and Mushroom Forest if it is in the Underground.
  - Loses Chasm, Swamp, Snow, Ice, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, Stalagmites, and Gloom.
- [Blossom of Life](../spells/blossom-of-life.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Friendly units in a 2-hex radius gain 3 Regeneration.
- Temple of Fertility (City Structure): - +10 Food
  - +3 Food per Population
  - +2 Draft per Population
- [Summon Nymph](../spells/summon-nymph.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Nymph onto the target world hex.
- Grasslands Preference (Tome Passive): Provinces with Grassland give +1 City Stability.
- Bountiful Fields (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Food income. Per adjacent Province with Grasslands or Fungal Fields: +3 Food. +3 Mana. Counts as a Farm.
