# Tome of Fey Mists

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Nature 1, Astral 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

Conjure otherworldly mists to protect and strengthen your units.

## Unlocks

- [Feytouched](../spells/feytouched.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes the target race connect with Fey powers, granting them:
  
  - Fey Guile
  - The ability to ignore:
  
  - Vision penalties from Misty.
  - Accuracy penalties from Clinging Mist.
- [Lingering Mists](../spells/lingering-mists.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Target province becomes Misty for 3 Turns.
- [Fey Embrace](../spells/fey-embrace.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): In a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Place Mist clouds.
  - Friendly units gain 2 random Positive Status Effects.
- [Staves of Mist](../spells/staves-of-mist.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - The Fey Blessing ability.
- [Summon Mistling](../spells/summon-mistling.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Mistling, a Magic Fighter Unit with attacks that inflict random Negative Status Effects.
- Feywater Pond (Initial City Upgrade): - Provinces in this city's domain become Misty.; +3 Mana per adjacent Forester.; Counts as a Conduit.
