# Tome of Glades

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Nature 2
- DLC: Base

Create and protect forests. Specialize in healing and making use of forests.

## Unlocks

- [Aspect of the Root](../spells/aspect-of-the-root.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - The Aspect of the Root ability, which allows units to heal themselves in battle.
- [Animate Flora](../spells/animate-flora.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Target Flora Obstacle transforms into a Floral Stinger unit under your control.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Summon Entwined Protector](../spells/summon-entwined-protector.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons an Entwined Protector, a Shield Unit with healing abilities.
- [Leafskin](../spells/leafskin.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes the target race one with the forest, granting them:
  
  - Forest Walk
  - Forest Camouflage
  - When in a province with Forest or Mushroom Forest:
  
  - +10% Evasion
  - +10% Accuracy
  - +10% Critical Hit Chance
- [Glade Runner](../units/glade-runner.md) (Unit): Ranged Unit that marks and weakens enemies.
- [Create Forest](../spells/create-forest.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target and adjacent Provinces:
  
  - Gain Forest on the Surface
  - Gain Mushroom Forest in the Underground.
- Forest Preference (Tome Passive): Provinces with Forest or Mushroom Forest give +1 City Stability.
- Sacred Meadow (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Food.; +10 City Stability.; +5 Food and +5 City Stability per adjacent Conduit.; Grants Encouraged at start of next Combat to friendly Units on this hex, 5 Turn cooldown.; Counts as a Farm.
