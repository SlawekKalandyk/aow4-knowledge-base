# Tome of Gluttony

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Gain control over the infernal domain of Gluttony, making your units harder to kill and stronger as they feed on mortal essences.

## Unlocks

- [Corrupt Pact](../spells/corrupt-pact.md) ([World Map] Sustained World Spell): Target Free City:
  
  - Has its Relations to other rulers reduced by 200.
  - 80% of its Food income is given to your cities.
- [Infernal Jaws](../spells/infernal-jaws.md) ([Combat] Damage Spell): Target enemy sustains:
  
  - 15 Physical Damage.
  - 15 Fire Damage.
  
  If the enemy dies, adjacent friendly units:
  
  - Heal 20 Temporary Hit Points.
  - If they have Demonic Hunger or Imp's Hunger, gain Gorged.
- [Demonic Hunger](../spells/demonic-hunger.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Target race is charged with infernal forces of gluttony, gaining:
  
  - When killing a non-Combat Summon enemy unit, gain a permanent stack of Gorged which:
  
  - At 10 stacks turns into a permanent stack of Demonic Satiation.
  - Cannot be gained when Demonic Satiation is at 10 stacks.
  
  - When pillaging a province, heal for 25% of their maximum Hit Points.
- [Illusory Feast](../spells/illusory-feast.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Becomes Distracted for 1 Turn.
  - Has a base 90% chance of becoming Stunned for 1 Turn.
- [Summon Gluttonous Imp](../spells/summon-gluttonous-imp.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Gluttonous Imp on target hex.
- Hungering Maw (Initial City Upgrade): - 10% of Food is converted to Mana.; When victorious in battle, this city gains 25 Food.; Counts as a Farm.
