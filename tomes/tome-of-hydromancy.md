# Tome of Hydromancy

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Nature 1, Materium 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Rush your allies, manipulate your enemies, and inflict the Wet status effect.

## Unlocks

- [Revitalizing Rush](../spells/revitalizing-rush.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Units in target friendly army:
  
  - Heal 10 Hit Points.
  - At the start of the next combat, units are Hastened for 3 Turns.
- [Riptide](../spells/riptide.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 12 Physical Damage.
  - Become Wet for 3 Turns.
  - If on the outer radius, they are pushed 1-hex away from the center.
  - Targets that were already Wet have the above effects doubled.
- [Flowing Imbuement](../spells/flowing-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted unit's base attacks:
  
  - Deal +2 Physical Damage.
  - On the third strike, push the unit away 1 hex and inflict Wet.
  - Single Strike attacks have a base 90% chance of triggering this effect instead.
- [Staves of the River](../spells/staves-of-the-river.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted unit's Support abilities:
  
  - Heal 5 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Dispel 1 status effect.
- [Summon Lesser Tide Spirit](../spells/summon-lesser-tide-spirit.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Lesser Tide Spirit on target hex.
- [Flowbinder Archer](../units/flowbinder-archer.md) (Unit): Ranged Unit that inflicts Wet and Immobilized on its enemies.
- Meditation Fountain (Initial City Upgrade): +5 Knowledge; +5 Mana. Per adjacent owned province with River or Ocean: +1 Knowledge; +1 Mana; Counts as a Research Post
