# Tome of Mayhem

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Cause chaos on the battlefield by randomly displacing enemies and inflicting Misfortune.

## Unlocks

- [Incite Revolution](../spells/incite-revolution.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Target enemy city:
  
  - Loses a border province and the population is lost.
  - Spawns a Brigand Camp on land provinces.
  - Spawns a Pirate Cove on Ocean provinces.
- Sow Confusion (Siege Project): At the start of the battle, enemy units:
  
  - Are displaced by 5 hexes.
  - Suffer Misfortune for 3 Turns.
  - Suffer Immobilized for 1 Turn.
- [Curse of Misfortune](../spells/curse-of-misfortune.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Targets in a 2-hex radius suffer 2 Misfortune.
- [Summon Gremlin](../spells/summon-gremlin.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Gremlin onto the target world hex. This unit is a disruptive and fiendish Magic Fighter Unit.
- [Mark of Misfortune](../spells/mark-of-misfortune.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Inflict Misfortune.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
