# Tome of Nature's Wrath

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Nature 2
- DLC: Base

Grants powerful spells that unleash the uncontrollable power of nature.

## Unlocks

- [Frenzying Imbuement](../spells/frenzying-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Frenzy
  - Life Steal
- [Awaken the Forest](../spells/awaken-the-forest.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Target Province with Forest:
  
  - Loses Forest.
  - An Army of Animals and Plants is summoned under your control.
- [Awaken Instincts](../spells/awaken-instincts.md) ([Combat] Healing/Buff Spell): All friendly units in a 2-hex radius:
  
  - Heal +25 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Regain all Action Points.
  - Become Berserk for 2 Turns.
  
  Does not affect units with Control Loss Immunity.
- [Summon Horned God](../spells/summon-horned-god.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons the Horned God, a Tier V Mythic Unit with potent summoning abilities.
- [Destructive Regrowth](../spells/destructive-regrowth.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): On Target Province:
  
  - Enemy units sustain 20 Blight Damage and suffer -2 Blight Protection for 2 Turn.
  - If it contains an enemy Province Improvement, it becomes Pillaged.
  
  - You are not granted its rewards nor do you lose Alignment.
  
  - The province gains Forest if on the Surface or Mushroom Forest if in the Underground.
- [Devolve](../spells/devolve.md) ([Combat] Damage Spell): Target non-Hero enemy:
  
  - Has a base 90% chance of being transformed into a random Tier I animal until the end of battle.
  - If resisted, inflicts Stunned instead.
