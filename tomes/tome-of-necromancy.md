# Tome of Necromancy

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Harvest Souls from your enemies and create Undead creatures. Specialize in expendable units and starting your Soul economy.

## Unlocks

- Skeleton Reanimation (Empire Bonus): When killing racial units, gain the option to spend Souls to create Skeletons after combat.
- [Soul Collection](../spells/soul-collection.md) ([World Map] Sustained World Spell): - Gain +10 Souls income.
  - Costs 30 Gold upkeep.
- [Necrotic Imbuement](../spells/necrotic-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - A base 90% chance of inflicting Decaying, a damage-over-time effect that decreases healing received.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Necromancer](../units/necromancer.md) (Unit): Necromancer strengthen the Undead and create them from corpses.
- [Rotting Explosion](../spells/rotting-explosion.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target friendly Zombie or Skeleton unit explodes, all enemies in a 2-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 10 Frost Damage and 10 Blight Damage.
  - Gain 2 Decaying for 3 Turns.
- [Necrotize](../spells/necrotize.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Sustains 10 Frost Damage and 10 Blight Damage
  - Gains 2 stacks of Decaying for 3 Turns.
  - When it dies, it becomes a Decaying Zombie under your control until the end of combat.
- Soul Harvest (Tome Passive): Gain Souls when enemies die in battle.
  
  Unlocks the ability to animate City Ruin and to animate Heroes in your crypt as your undead servants.
- Soulwell (Initial City Upgrade): +5 Souls. +3 Mana per adjacent Research Post or Conduit. Counts as a Research Post.
