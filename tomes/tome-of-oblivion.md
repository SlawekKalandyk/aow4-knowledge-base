# Tome of Oblivion

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Shadow (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Use powerful magic capable of sending your enemies to the nothingness of oblivion. Specialize in inflicting Insanity and making parts of the world and the battlefield inhospitable.

## Unlocks

- [Fog of Insanity](../spells/fog-of-insanity.md) ([World Map] Sustained City Spell): Target friendly City. Any battle in the Domain of the City now has the Fog of Insanity battle enchantment, which causes enemies to have a base 20% chance of gaining Insanity every Turn.
- [Devouring Void](../spells/devouring-void.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): - Conjures a Devouring Void in a 1-hex radius.
  - Each turn, the radius increases by 1 hex.
  - Lasts for 2 Turns.
- [Summon Living Fog](../spells/summon-living-fog.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Living Fog unit onto the target world hex.
- [Sleep of Oblivion](../spells/sleep-of-oblivion.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target non-Hero unit dies. After 2 Turns, they come back to life with 75% of their total HP Hit Points and are inflicted with Insanity.
  
  While dead this way the unit cannot be revived or have its corpse destroyed.
- [Ritual of Somnia](../spells/ritual-of-somnia.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): In the target enemy army:
  
  - At the start of each battle, all units have a base 90% chance of becoming Stunned for 2 Turns.
  - Lasts 1 World Map Turns.
