# Tome of Pandemonium

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 Chaos (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Thrive in the chaos of battle and specialize in inflicting random Negative Status Effects on your enemies and dealing more damage to enemies that are already inflicted.

## Unlocks

- [Vessels of Chaos](../spells/vessels-of-chaos.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Turns the target race into a conduit for chaotic energies, granting them:
  
  - +10% damage for each of the target's Negative Status Effects. Stacks up to 3 times.
- [Havoc Magic](../spells/havoc-magic.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants base Magic attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Base 60% chance of inflicting a random Negative Status Effect.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Summon Chaos Eater](../spells/summon-chaos-eater.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Chaos Eater, a close-range Battle Mage Unit that thrives on eating Negative Status Effects.
- [Infectious Insanity](../spells/infectious-insanity.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target Unit has:
  
  - 90% chance of being inflicted with Infectious Insanity for 2 Turns.
  - If resisted, the target is inflicted with Insanity for 1 Turn.
- [Mass Hysteria](../spells/mass-hysteria.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Inflicts a random Negative Status Effect on all units in a 1-hex radius.
