# Tome of Paradise

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Nature 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Nature (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Create a lush green paradise for your people. Focus on economy, healing, and buffs.

## Unlocks

- [Gaia's Chosen](../spells/gaias-chosen.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Infuse the target race with the blessing of Nature. They gain:
  
  - The Plant unit type.
  - Negative Status Effects last -1 Turn, with a minimum of 1.
  - +3 Status Resistance.
  - +20 HP Hit Points.
- Blessing of Paradise (Empire Bonus): Your cities gain +5% income per City Stability above Neutral.
- [Fortress of Vines](../spells/fortress-of-vines.md) ([World Map] Sustained World Spell): Your empire gains:
  
  - Your non-hero units in your domain earn 10 XP Experience each Turn.
  - Enemy units in this domain spend 4 more Move Points per hex moved.
  - A Living Vine spawns next to an enemy each turn during battle in your domain.
- [Exhilarating Pollen](../spells/exhilarating-pollen.md) ([Combat] Buff/Debuff Spell): All friendly units gain +15 Morale.
  All enemy units have a base 90% chance of becoming Distracted.
- [Enchanted Bloom](../spells/enchanted-bloom.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target friendly City gains:
  
  - Every Turn, 2 Provinces within or adjacent to the Domain:
  
  - Gain Grassland and Forest if on the Surface, or Fungus Fields and Mushroom Forest if in the Underground.
  - Lose Chasm, Swamp, Sand, Snow, Ice, Ashlands, and Gloom.
  - Provinces within the domain with either Grasslands, Forests, Fungus Fields, or Mushroom Forest features gain: +5 Food, +5 City Stability
- [Nature's Bounty](../spells/natures-bounty.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): All friendly units in the target Province gain +15 max HP Hit Points and regenerate 15 HP Hit Points in neutral and friendly domain for the next 2 World Map Turns.
- Garden of Bliss (Initial City Upgrade): - +15 City Stability.; +7 Food per adjacent Grasslands Province.; Convert 10% of Food income into Mana.; Counts as a Farm.
