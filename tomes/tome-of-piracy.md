# Tome of Piracy

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Materium 1, Chaos 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Dominate the seas with highly mobile skirmishers, use explosive Ember Dust, and plunder and pillage your enemies.

## Unlocks

- Powder Keg Diplomacy (Siege Project): At the start of combat:
  
  - Wall Obstacles are damaged.
  - Wall Obstacles have a 25% chance of being destroyed.
  - 6 to 12 enemy units gain Ember Dust.
- [Raiders of the Sea](../spells/raiders-of-the-sea.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units:
  
  - Increase resources gained from Pillaging a Province by 100%. Does not stack.
  - Gain Sailor, increasing combat speed and damage when fighting on water.
- [Plundering Blades](../spells/plundering-blades.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units attacks:
  
  - Deal +2 Physical Damage.
  - Have a base 60% chance of Rob the target.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Ember Dust Residue](../spells/ember-dust-residue.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units attacks:
  
  - Deal +1 Fire Damage. Increased for Single Shots.
  - The third strike from a base attack has a base 90% chance of inflicting Ember Dust.
  - Single Shot base attacks instead get a base 60% chance of inflictiing Ember Dust.
- [Ember Cloud](../spells/ember-cloud.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
  - Become inflicted with Ember Dust.
- [Swashbuckler](../units/swashbuckler.md) (Unit): Fast and mobile unit that can apply Ember Dust.
- Pirate Haven (Initial City Upgrade): +15 Gold; +4 Gold per adjacent owned Ocean Province. Pillaging a Province grants the city +15 City Stability for 10 Turns. Counts as a Mine.
