# Tome of Prophecies

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Order 1, Astral 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 combined Order + Astral (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

Prevent enemy attacks and foresee the downfall of your enemies.

## Unlocks

- [Prescient Circlets](../spells/prescient-circlets.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Gain Precognition when entering Defense Mode.
- [Fateful Imbuement](../spells/fateful-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - +20% Critical Hit Chance.
  - Faithful.
- Foresee Downfall (Siege Project): At the start of combat:
  
  - Friendly units gain:
  
  - Precognition
  - +10% Critical Hit Chance for the duration of combat.
  
  - Enemy units gain:
  
  - A 10% Fumble chance for the duration of combat.
- [Battle Divination](../spells/battle-divination.md) ([Combat] Combat Enchantment): When cast and for the next 2 Turns, apply effect to the battle in the following order:
  
  - All friendly units gain +25% Evasion for the turn.
  - All friendly units gain Precognition.
  - All friendly units gain +20% damage for the turn.
- [Oracle](../units/oracle.md) (Unit): A Support Unit that can foresee the future and use that knowledge to strengthen and protect other units.
- Conduit Temple of the Prophet (Initial City Upgrade): - +15 Mana.; +5 Knowledge per adjacent Conduit.; Counts as a Conduit.
