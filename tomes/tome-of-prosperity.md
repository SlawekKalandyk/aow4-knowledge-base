# Tome of Prosperity

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Order 1, Nature 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 combined Order + Nature (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Bring forth prosperity to your empire and grant your units healing Grace.

## Unlocks

- [Staves of Grace](../spells/staves-of-grace.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - Inner Grace
  - Their Support abilities grant 1 Grace.
  - The Cleansing Rain ability.
- [Blessed Armors](../spells/blessed-armors.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - Inner Grace
  - +1 Resistance
- Garden of Affluence (City Structure): - +10 City Stability
  - Per Farm and Forester in the domain:
  
  - +2 Gold income
  - +2 Mana income
- [Grand Protection](../spells/grand-protection.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Friendly units in a 2-hex radius gain:
  
  - 2 Grace
  - 2 Regeneration for 3 Turns
  - 2 Bolstered Defense for 3 Turns
  - 2 Bolstered Resistance for 3 Turns
- [Summon Prosperity Dragon](../spells/summon-prosperity-dragon.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Prosperity Dragon, a Mythic Unit unit with strong protective and healing abilities.
- Conduit Shrine of Prosperity (Initial City Upgrade): - +3 City Stability per adjacent Farm or Forester.; +7 Food per adjacent Farm or Forester.; 10% of Food income in the city is converted to Gold.; Allows the drafting of Blessed Dragon, Radiant Guardian, and Righteous Judge.; Counts as a Conduit.
