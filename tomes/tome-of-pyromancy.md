# Tome of Pyromancy

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- DLC: Base

Specialize in high Fire Damage by inflicting and exploiting Burning.

## Unlocks

- [Fiery Imbuement](../spells/fiery-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Attacks of enchanted units gain:
  
  - +1 Fire Damage.
  - A base 60% chance of inflicting Burning.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Ignite](../spells/ignite.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Sustains 25 Fire Damage.
  - Is inflicted with Burning.
  - The ground is set On Fire.
- [Immolate](../spells/immolate.md) ([Combat] Damage Spell): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 10 Fire Damage.
  - Sustain an extra 15 Fire Damage if they are Burning.
- [Summon Lesser Magma Spirit](../spells/summon-lesser-magma-spirit.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Lesser Magma Spirit on target hex.
- [Pyromancer](../units/pyromancer.md) (Unit): A Battle Mage Unit that spreads fire and inflicts Burning.
- [Searing Blades](../spells/searing-blades.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units deal:
  
  - +2 Fire Damage.
  - +10% damage against targets that are Burning.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- Ritual Pyre (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Mana income.; +3 Mana per adjacent Forester.; Allows the drafting of Inferno Puppies.; Counts as a Forester.
