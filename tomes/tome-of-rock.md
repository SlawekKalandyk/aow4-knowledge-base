# Tome of Rock

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- DLC: Base

Smash your enemies with the force of stone. Specialize in increasing your defenses and dealing Physical Damage.

## Unlocks

- [Obsidian Weapons](../spells/obsidian-weapons.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units gain:
  
  - +1 Physical Damage.
  - Gain a base 60% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Earthkin](../spells/earthkin.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes rock growths develop on the target race, granting them:
  
  - +1 Defense.
  - Mountain Camouflage, which allows them to hide on Mountain terrain.
  - Mountain Walk, which allows them to traverse Mountain terrain faster.
- [Summon Lesser Stone Spirit](../spells/summon-lesser-stone-spirit.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Lesser Stone Spirit, a Shield Unit with good defenses.
- [Stone Skin](../spells/stone-skin.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target unit gains Stone Skin for 3 Turns.
- [Gargoyle](../units/gargoyle.md) (Unit): A flying Shock Unit unit that can harden itself to soften enemy attacks.
- [Rock Blast](../spells/rock-blast.md) ([Combat] Damage Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Suffers 24 Physical Damage.
  - Has its Defense Mode canceled.
  - Has its Retaliation Attacks removed.
- Central Quarry (Initial City Upgrade): - +15 Production.; +5 Production per adjacent Quarry.; Counts as a Quarry.
