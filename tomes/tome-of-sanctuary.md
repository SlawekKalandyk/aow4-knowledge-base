# Tome of Sanctuary

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- DLC: Base

Create a safe haven for all believers. Specialize in healing and buffing defenses.

## Unlocks

- [Summon Light Spirit](../spells/summon-light-spirit.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Light Spirit, a Support Unit with strong healing capabilities.
- Healing Spires (City Structure): Tower Structure
  
  - Adds 4 Healing Spires in Combat during a Siege.
  - Friendly units in the domain heal +12 HP Hit Points per Turn.
  - +10 City Stability
- [Keeper's Mark](../spells/keepers-mark.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units:
  
  - Gain Faithful, reducing Unit Upkeep.
  - When taking fatal damage for the first time, this unit gains:
  
  - Steadfast for 1 Turn.
  - Pacified for 1 Turn.
- [Salvation](../spells/salvation.md) ([Combat] Healing/Buff Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Heals all of its HP Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points.
  - Dispels its Negative Status Effects.
  - Gains 3 Bolstered Resistance.
- [Consecrated Domain](../spells/consecrated-domain.md) ([World Map] Sustained City Spell): Target friendly City, friendly units in the Domain gain:
  
  - +2 Resistance.
  - +20 Morale at the start of battle.
- [Anointed People](../spells/anointed-people.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Gives the target race divine protection, granting them:
  
  - +3 Status Resistance
  - +3 Spirit Protection
- Conduit Sanctuary (Initial City Upgrade): - +15 Mana.; Pillaging Province Improvements in this domain takes +2 Turns and yields -30% resources.; Counts as a Conduit.
