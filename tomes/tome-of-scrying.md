# Tome of Scrying

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Keep an eye on your enemies and let nothing pass you by. Specialize in gaining more vision and Truesight and inflicting Marked.

## Unlocks

- Tower of True Sight (City Structure): - +10 City Stability
  - +10 Knowledge
  - +6 Vision Range
  - True Sight making it possible to spot camouflaged enemies near the city.
- [Guided Projectiles](../spells/guided-projectiles.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes base attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Fire 1 hex further without Long Range Accuracy penalties.
  - Ignore Accuracy penalties from the Obscuring condition caused by units that are in the way and certain terrain.
- [Summon Watcher](../spells/summon-watcher.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Watcher, a Battle Mage Unit with increased vision capabilities.
- [Mental Mark](../spells/mental-mark.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target Enemies in a 1-hex radius gain:
  
  - 2 Marked.
  - 3 Sundered Resistance.
- [Scry Enemy](../spells/scry-enemy.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): All enemies in the target army provide you with vision for 10 Turns.
