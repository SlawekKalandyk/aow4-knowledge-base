# Tome of Severing

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Materium 1, Shadow 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 combined Materium + Shadow (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

Wield powerful nullification magic to sever the very essence of summoned creatures.

## Unlocks

- [Severing Golem](../units/severing-golem.md) (Unit): A caster Mythic Unit based on ancient designs that can remove friendly Negative Status Effects and enemy enchantments.
- [Final Banishment](../spells/final-banishment.md) (Combat Spell): All non-hero corpses on the map are destroyed. For each corpse destroyed, friendly units:
  
  - Gain +1 Bolstered Resistance
  - Heals +5 Temporary Hit Points
- [Disrupting Blades](../spells/disrupting-blades.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units gain:
  
  - A base 60% chance of inflicting Disrupted for 2 Turns.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Null Shield](../spells/null-shield.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes it so when enchanted unit enters Defense Mode, it and adjacent friendly units gain:
  
  - +5 Status Resistance
- [Conjure Spellward](../spells/conjure-spellward.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Conjures a Spellward on an empty hex in the target Province. This structure:
  
  - Functions as a Spell Jammer for this and adjacent provinces.
  - Provides vision in 3-hex radius.
- [Astral Severance](../spells/astral-severance.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target Magic Origin Unit:
  
  - Has a base 60% chance of being killed.
  - If unsuccessful, inflicts Disrupted and 4 stacks of Decaying.
