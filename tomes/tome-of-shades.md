# Tome of Shades

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Materium 1, Shadow 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

Strike from the shadows, blind your foes and find their weak points to deliver the perfect, fatal blow.

## Unlocks

- [Living Shadows](../spells/living-shadows.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Wreathes target race in swirling shadows, granting them:
  
  - +10% Evasion.
  - Fleeting.
  - When this unit's Hit Points drop below 60%:
  
  - It becomes Obscured for 1 Turn.
  - Adjacent enemies have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
- Shade Network (City Structure): - +10 Knowledge income.
  - +10 Knowledge for each non-owned city that shares a border with this City.
- [Rending Shadows](../spells/rending-shadows.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 16 Physical Damage.
  - Have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
  - Have a base 90% chance of gaining Sundered Defense for 3 Turn.
- [Shadow Weapons](../spells/shadow-weapons.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Deal +3 Frost Damage when Flanking or attacking Blind units.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Shade](../units/shade.md) (Unit): Stealthy Skirmisher Unit that specializes in finishing off weakened enemies.
