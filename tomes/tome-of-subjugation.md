# Tome of Subjugation

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 Order (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Expands and subjugates cities from other races. Specializes in lowering enemy Morale and conquering cities.

## Unlocks

- Baron's Palace (City Structure): This city gains:
  
  - +10 City Stability
  - +20 Draft
  
  Your Throne City gains:
  
  - +20 Gold
  - +20 Food
  - +5 Imperium
  
  Can only be built in cities of another race.
- [Final Ultimatum](../spells/final-ultimatum.md) (Combat Spell): Target Routing non-Hero enemy unit:
  
  - Has a base 90% chance of becoming permanently mind controlled.
  - If successful, it gains +60 Morale.
  - If resisted, the unit dies instead.
- Subjugating Raid (Siege Project): - When the walls are breached, the besieged City loses -2 Population.
  - Closest owned City gains +2 Population.
  - At the start of the battle, all enemy units have -10 Morale until the end of battle.
- [Intimidation Aura](../spells/intimidation-aura.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Intimidating Aura, which reduces Morale of nearby enemies.
- [Subjugator](../units/subjugator.md) (Unit): A heavy Shock Unit that specializes in lowering Morale.
