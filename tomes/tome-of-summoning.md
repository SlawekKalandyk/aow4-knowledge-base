# Tome of Summoning

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Call forth, control, and enhance Magic Origin units.

## Unlocks

- [Summon Astral Serpent](../spells/summon-astral-serpent.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons an Astral Serpent, a Magic Fighter Unit that can teleport into the front lines in an explosive manner.
- [Arcane Supercharge](../spells/arcane-supercharge.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): For 3 Turns, target friendly Magic Origin unit gains:
  
  - 5 Strengthened
  - 3 Bolstered Defense
  - 3 Bolstered Resistance
  - Static Charge
- [Conjure Astral Keeper](../spells/conjure-astral-keeper.md) (Combat Summon Spell): Summons an Astral Keeper, a Support Unit that heals units even in death.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Arcane Restoration](../spells/arcane-restoration.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Heals all friendly Magic Origin units +25 Temporary Hit Points.
- [Arcane Bond](../spells/arcane-bond.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target enemy Magic Origin unit has a base 90% chance of becoming Dominated for 2 Turns.
  
  If unsuccessful, deals 30 Lightning Damage instead.
- Summoning Well (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Mana.; +2 Mana and +2 Knowledge per adjacent Conduit or Research Post.; Combat Summon Spells in the domain cost -50% less mana.; Counts as a Conduit.
