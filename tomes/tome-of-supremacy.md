# Tome of Supremacy

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Order (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Lead your people to glorious victory. Specialize in increasing Morale and managing a big empire.

## Unlocks

- [Anthem of Victory](../spells/anthem-of-victory.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): All allied units gain:
  
  - 3 Strengthened.
  - +15 Morale.
- [Supreme Magic](../spells/supreme-magic.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): For enchanted units:
  
  - Grants Zeal, which makes attacks deal extra Spirit Damage.
  - Attacks ignore 2 Status Resistance.
  - Killing a unit causes it to explode, dealing 20 Spirit Damage to adjacent enemy units.
- [Condemn Army](../spells/condemn-army.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): Target enemy army:
  
  - Takes 20 Spirit Damage
  - Becomes Condemned for 5 World Map Turns.
- Monument of Supremacy (City Structure): - +30 Draft
  - +5 City Stability per Monument of Supremacy built in your empire
- [Exemplar](../units/exemplar.md) (Unit): A flying Shield Unit that excels at increasing the Morale of your units.
