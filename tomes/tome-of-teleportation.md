# Tome of Teleportation

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 Astral (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Exploit spatial magic to its full potential. Strategically reposition your units in battle in a mere instant.

## Unlocks

- Astral Trade Relay (City Structure): - +15 Gold income.
  - +4 Gold income for each Astral Trade Relay built in your empire.
- [Phasing Enchantment](../spells/phasing-enchantment.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - The Charged Phase ability, which allows them to teleport in battle.
- [Emergency Teleportation](../spells/emergency-teleportation.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Teleport the closest allied unit within 4 hexes to target empty hex.
  That unit heals +30 Temporary Hit Points and has all Negative Status Effects removed.
- [Summon Phase Beast](../spells/summon-phase-beast.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Phase Beast onto the target world hex.
- [Mass Recall](../spells/mass-recall.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Makes target friendly army teleport back to the nearest owned city.
- Chrono Gate (Initial City Upgrade): - +3 Mana per adjacent Conduit or Research Post.; +3 Knowledge per adjacent Conduit or Research Post.; Grants Evasion to visiting Armies until their next combat.; Functions as a Teleporter.
