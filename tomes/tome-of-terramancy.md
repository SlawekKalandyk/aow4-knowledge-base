# Tome of Terramancy

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 Materium (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Manipulate the earth at will. Specialize in dealing Physical Damage and hindering enemy Movement.

## Unlocks

- [Ley Line Focus](../spells/ley-line-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - At the start of the turn and until the unit moves this unit has:
  
  - +30% damage
  - +2 Resistance.
- [Summon Rock Giant](../spells/summon-rock-giant.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Rock Giant, a Mythic Unit that throws boulders that hinder enemies and crushes them with earthquake like smashes.
- Tremor Ritual (Siege Project): - In battle, every 2 Turns a random enemy unit is struck by an earthquake:
  
  - Enemy non-flying, non-floating units within 2-hex radius take 10 Physical Damage.
  - Enemy non-flying, non-floating units within 2-hex radius have 90% chance of suffering Slowed for 3 Turns.
  - Obstacles, including Walls, Towers, and Battlements within 2-hex radius are destroyed.
- [Earth Shatter](../spells/earth-shatter.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): In target habitable or uninhabitable Province:
  
  - Enemy Units sustain 10 Physical Damage.
  - The province loses any Mountain, Stalagmites, and Diggable Earth.
  - Summon a Stone Spirit on the target hex.
- [Seismic Shock](../spells/seismic-shock.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Deals 30 Physical Damage to units in a 1-hex radius and inflicts Slowed.
  This spell deals double damage to obstacles.
- [Crushing Earth](../spells/crushing-earth.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Sustains 50 Physical Damage.
  - Suffers 4 Sundered Defense for 3 Turns.
  - Has a base 90% chance of becoming Stunned for 1 Turn.
- Excavated Ley Line (Initial City Upgrade): - +5 Production.; +5 Mana.; +5 World Map Casting Points.; +4 Mana per adjacent Quarry.; Counts as a Conduit.
