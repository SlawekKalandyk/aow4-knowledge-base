# Tome of the Archmage

- Tier: 5
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- DLC: Base

Reach the pinnacle of the arcane arts by bending space and time to your will.

## Unlocks

- [Disruption Wave](../spells/disruption-wave.md) ([Combat] Buff/Debuff Spell): - Enemy units have a base 120% chance of becoming Disrupted for 2 Turns.
  - Dispels 2 Positive Status Effects from enemies.
  - Dispels 2 Negative Status Effects from allies.
- [Cosmic Overdrive](../spells/cosmic-overdrive.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - +20% damage.
  - Negative Status Effects last -1 Turn, with a minimum of 1.
  - If enchanted unit is a non-mythic Magic Origin unit, it gains Very Fast Movement.
- [Time Stop](../spells/time-stop.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target enemy units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Become Stunned for 1 Turn.
  - Become Distracted for 1 Turn.
  - Gain 5 Marked for 3 Turns.
- [Astral Travel](../spells/astral-travel.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Makes your leader teleport to the target world hex.
