# Tome of the Archon

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Order (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

Summon Celestial allies and ascend your units to the ranks of the Archons.

## Unlocks

- [Holy Aura](../spells/holy-aura.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Holy Aura, granting status protection to adjacent friendly units and damaging adjacent enemies.
- [Celestial Guardians](../spells/celestial-guardians.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Target leader of an army gains Celestial Guardians for 1 World Map Turn, conjuring 2 Tier III Archon units at the start of combat for the duration.
- [Ascended Rebirth](../spells/ascended-rebirth.md) ([Combat] Healing Spell): Target dead non-Hero and non-Celestial unit:
  
  - Revives as a Vigil under the caster's control until the end of combat.
  - On revive, all friendly units in a 2-hex radius are healed for 20 Temporary Hit Points.
- [Pure Soul](../spells/pure-soul.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Purify the souls of target race, granting them:
  
  - At the start of each turn in combat, dispel 1 Negative Status Effect.
  - If the Negative Status Effect removed had a positive counter, gain the Positive Status Effect.
  
  This transformation is incompatible with Umbral Flesh and Gloom Strider.
- [Summon Astra](../spells/summon-astra.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon an Astra Mythic Unit, an Angel unit with strong attacks and healing abilities.
- Archon Gate (Initial City Upgrade): - +20 City Stability; Unit deployment location.; Allows the drafting of Titans.; Functions as a Teleporter.
