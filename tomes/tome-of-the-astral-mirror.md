# Tome of the Astral Mirror

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Astral (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Create Astral Reflections of your units and leader and reflect damage back onto attackers.

## Unlocks

- Throne of Mirrors (City Structure): - When a fight occurs in the city's domain, the player gains an Astral Reflection of their Ruler if they are not present themselves.
  - Your Ruler gains 10 Experience per Turn per owned City with this city structure built.
- [Mirror Veil](../spells/mirror-veil.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes enchanted units:
  
  - Reflect 30% of non- Physical Damage sustained back onto attackers.
- [Summon Astral Reflection](../spells/summon-astral-reflection.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Summons an Astral Reflection of target friendly unit onto an unoccupied adjacent hex.
  
  Cannot target Heroes, Combat Summons, and Mythic Units.
- [Summon Mirror Mimic](../spells/summon-mirror-mimic.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Mirror Mimic, a Mythic Unit that can take the form of other units, gaining their abilities and healing themselves.
- [Astral Revelation](../spells/astral-revelation.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Units and Cities you control gain +7 Vision Range.
