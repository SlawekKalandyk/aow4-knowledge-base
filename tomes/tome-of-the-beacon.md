# Tome of the Beacon

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Brings out the inner radiance of the Faithful to smite enemies and bolster allies.

## Unlocks

- [Mighty Meek](../spells/mighty-meek.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Faithful, which reduces Unit Upkeep.
  - +2 Status Resistance.
  - +1 Spirit Damage on attacks for each Unit Tier of the target.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Summon Blessed Soul](../spells/summon-blessed-soul.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Blessed Soul Shield Unit that can rally your units and condemn the enemy.
- [Covenant of the Faith](../spells/covenant-of-the-faith.md) ([World Map] Sustained City Spell): Units recruited through Rally of the Lieges from the target Vassal have Faithful.
  Target Vassal city grants the player +15 Mana each Turn.
- [Chaplain](../units/chaplain.md) (Unit): Faithful Support Unit that bolsters allies.
- [Conjure Divine Beacon](../spells/conjure-divine-beacon.md) (Combat Summon Spell): Summons a Divine Beacon. Upon casting and at the start of each turn, friendly units in a 2-hex radius:
  
  - Heal +15 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Gain +5 Morale.
  
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
