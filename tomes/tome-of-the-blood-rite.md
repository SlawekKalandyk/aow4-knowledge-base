# Tome of the Blood Rite

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Chaos 1, Shadow 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Perform forbidden and dangerous rites on your units to bleed your enemies and strengthen your allies.

## Unlocks

- [Flesh Carved Runes](../spells/flesh-carved-runes.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Carve profane sigils onto the flesh of your race, granting them:
  
  - +5 HP Hit Point regeneration on the World Map.
  - Attack and Debuff abilities:
  
  - Ignore 1 Status Resistance per stack of Bleeding on the target.
- [Rite of Bloodletting](../spells/rite-of-bloodletting.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target friendly unit sustains 5 unblockable Physical Damage. Then all enemy units within 2 hexes:
  
  - Sustain 12 Physical Damage.
  - Gain 2 Bleeding for 3 Turns.
- [Sanguine Focus](../spells/sanguine-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Attacks of enchanted units gain:
  
  - +1 Physical Damage.
  - A base 60% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Blood Cultist](../units/blood-cultist.md) (Unit): A Battle Mage Unit that inflicts a Blood Parasite on its enemies and can empower themselves at the cost of their lives.
- [Blood Drinking Blades](../spells/blood-drinking-blades.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Deal +2 Physical Damage.
  - When striking units with Bleeding, heal for 3 Temporary Hit Points.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Rite of Life Leeching](../spells/rite-of-life-leeching.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target friendly unit sustains 5 unblockable Physical Damage. Then all adjacent friendly units gain:
  
  - Strengthened for 3 Turns.
  - Life Steal for 3 Turns.
- Blood Altar (Initial City Upgrade): - +15 Draft income.; +5 Draft per adjacent Farm.; When victorious in battle gain 10 Mana.; Counts as a Conduit.
