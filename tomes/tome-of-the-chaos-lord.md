# Tome of the Chaos Lord

- Tier: 5
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- DLC: Base

Unleash chaos forces upon the world. Specialize in offensive buffs.

## Unlocks

- [Call Forth Avatar of Chaos](../spells/call-forth-avatar-of-chaos.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Summons an avatar of your Ruler into a battle. The avatar has the same abilities as your Ruler and spawns with full Action Points. All Fiends gain +10 Morale.
  
  Cannot be used in battles where your Ruler is present.
- Incite Rebellion (Siege Project): - After 1 Turns in battle, 3 enemy non-Hero units are Mind-Controlled until the end of combat.
  - The city suffers -50 City Stability while it is besieged.
- [Demonic Onslaught](../spells/demonic-onslaught.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): All attacking units gain Killing Momentum and Hastened for 3 Turns.
- [Demonic Siphon](../spells/demonic-siphon.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Whenever another unit dies in battle, enchanted unit gains:
  
  - Infernal Might for 3 Turns.
  - +5 Morale.
  
  Does not trigger on the death of Combat Summons, Tower, and Siegecraft units.
