# Tome of the Cleansing Flame

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Order 1, Chaos 1
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

Utilize the zeal of your troops to bathe the battlefield in cleansing flame that punishes the condemned and blesses the faithful.

## Unlocks

- Temple of the Pyre (City Structure): City gains:
  
  - Ignore income and stability penalties from being under Siege.
  - At the start of combat in this city's Domain, enemy units suffer Condemned.
- [Consecrating Firestorm](../spells/consecrating-firestorm.md) ([World Map] World Spell): In target Province:
  
  - Enemy units sustain 20 Spirit Damage.
  - The province gains Consecrating Firestorm for 3 Turns
  - If it has an enemy owned Province Improvement, it is Pillaged.
- [Pyre Templar](../units/pyre-templar.md) (Unit): A zealous Polearm Unit whose sweeping strikes inflict Burning.
- [Pureflame Staves](../spells/pureflame-staves.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes base Magic attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Gain base 60% chance of inflicting Condemned
  - Create Cleansing Flames on the target hex for 3 Turns
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Zealous Ignition](../spells/zealous-ignition.md) ([Combat] Buff/Debuff Spell): Target unit:
  
  - Gains Zeal until the end of battle.
  If target already has Zeal, gain 2 Strengthened for 3 Turns.
  - Enemy units in a 2-hex radius suffer Condemned until the end of battle.
  - Create Cleansing Flames on the target hexes for 3 Turns.
- [Flame Blessed Champions](../spells/flame-blessed-champions.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes base Melee attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Gain base 60% chance of inflicting Burning
  - Create Cleansing Flames on the target hex for 3 Turns
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- Pyreshrine (Initial City Upgrade): - +15 Gold.; +5 Draft per adjacent Forester.; Units produced in this city gain Faithful.; Flameherald's Consecration is added to all battles in this domain.; Counts as a Forester.
