# Tome of the Cold Dark

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- DLC: Base

Spread Arctic terrain throughout the world and envelop your units with its power, so they may be empowered by its presence.

## Unlocks

- [Flash Freeze](../spells/flash-freeze.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target Province:
  
  - For 1 World Map Turns enemy Units in the Province suffer:
  
  - -2 Status Resistance.
  - -2 Frost Protection
  
  - The province gains Snow and Ice.
  - The province loses Chasm, Swamp, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, Fungus Fields, and Gloom.
- [Marching Winter](../spells/marching-winter.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target friendly City gains:
  
  - Every Turn, 2 Provinces within or adjacent to the Domain:
  
  - Gain Snow and Ice.
  - Lose Chasm, Swamp, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, Fungus Fields, and Gloom.
  
  - Provinces in the domain with Snow or Ice provide +2 Food and +2 Production income.
- [Nightveil Focus](../spells/nightveil-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - Attacks deal +2 Frost Damage.
  - Attacks have a base 60% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn.
  - Universal Camouflage.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Frostling Transformation](../spells/frostling-transformation.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes winter run through the target race's veins, granting them:
  
  - 3 Frost Protection.
  - Immunity to Chilled.
  - +10 Morale while on Snow or Ice.
  - Arctic Walk.
  - Snow Camouflage
- [Summon Frost Giant](../spells/summon-frost-giant.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Frost Giant, a Mythic Unit with attacks that inflict Frozen.
- Snow Preference (Tome Passive): Provinces with Snow give +2 City Stability.
- Arctic Farming (Tome Passive): Able to build Farms on Snow terrain.
  
  Farms on Snow terrain gain +5 Food if your race has Arctic Adaptation.
- Frostspire (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Knowledge.; +3 Knowledge per adjacent Snow or Ice Province.; Allows for the drafting of some Tier I, Tier II, and Tier III frost themed units.; Counts as a Research Post.
