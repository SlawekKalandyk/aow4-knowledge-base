# Tome of the Cosmos

- Tier: 5
- Affinity granted: Astral 1, Shadow 1, Chaos 1, Nature 1, Materium 1, Order 1
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Use your mastery over the magic of the universe to empower your units and your economy, and summon a mighty Avatar of the Cosmos itself.

## Unlocks

- Prismatic Tower (City Structure): - Reveal the Map.
  - +25 [World Map] World Map Casting Point.
  - +25 Combat Casting Point.
  - For each Affinity at or above 5 the city gains +50 of the resource based on that affinity.
  
  - Astral Affinity - Mana
  - Chaos Affinity - Draft
  - Materium Affinity - Production
  - Nature Affinity - Food
  - Order Affinity - Gold
  - Shadow Affinity - Knowledge
  
  Can only be built in the Throne City.
- [Cosmos Awakening](../spells/cosmos-awakening.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - +1 damage of each damage channel.
  - +1 protection of each damage channel.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Summon Avatar of the Cosmos](../spells/summon-avatar-of-the-cosmos.md) ([World Map] Sustained World Spell): Summon the Avatar of the Cosmos, a Mythic Unit that is empowered by your own affinities.
