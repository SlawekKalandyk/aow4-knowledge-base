# Tome of the Creator

- Tier: 5
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 8 Materium (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Shape the world, summon slumbering titans, and become the master of the earth.

## Unlocks

- Create Earthshatter Engines (Siege Project): - At the start of the battle, all Wall obstacles are damaged.
  - At the start of the battle, gain 2 Earthshatter Engine units on the attacker's side until the end of battle.
  - Earthshatter Engines are Siegecraft Units with short range, area of effect attacks that can destroy obstacles and knock enemy units out of Defense Mode.
  - Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- [Shaper's Touch](../spells/shapers-touch.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Natural Regeneration
  - +10 HP Hit Points
  - +2 Lightning Protection
- [Tectonic Shatter](../spells/tectonic-shatter.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): - All enemies sustain 30 Physical Damage.
  - All enemies have a base 60% chance of becoming Stunned.
  - All obstacles on the map have a 50% chance of being destroyed.
- [Call the Titan of the Earth](../spells/call-the-titan-of-the-earth.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): - Summons an Earth Titan onto the target unoccupied hex.
  - All adjacent enemies lose their Defense Mode and Retaliation Attack.
