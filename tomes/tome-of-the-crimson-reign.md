# Tome of the Crimson Reign

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Chaos 1, Shadow 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 combined Chaos + Shadow (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Embrace a forgotten, corrupt magic that empowers your high tier units and spreads Blood Parasites onto your enemies through eldritch undeath.

## Unlocks

- [Gift of the Old Blood](../spells/gift-of-the-old-blood.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Transform your units as they ritually imbibe blood from an Exarch, granting them:
  
  - The Undead unit type.
  - Lifedrinker.
  - Base attacks have a base 60% chance of inflicting Blood Parasite for 3 Turns. This effect is increased for Single Shot attacks.
- Crimson Court (City Structure): - Tier III, IV, and V units you own gain:
  
  - +5 Hit Point regeneration on the world map.
  - Gain 10 Experience per turn on the world map.
  
  - Gain Crimson Court Magistrate, letting you gain mana from Vassal and when Migrating, Razing, or Pillaging.
- [Mantle of the Blood Noble](../spells/mantle-of-the-blood-noble.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Coagulate
  - At the start of combat, for each friendly non-summon Tier I and Tier II unit:
  
  - +5 Hit Points
  - +5% healing received in combat.
- [Tears of the Crimson Sovereign](../spells/tears-of-the-crimson-sovereign.md) (Combat Enchantment): When cast and for the next 2 Turns:
  
  - Up to 3 enemy units:
  
  - Sustain 10 Physical Damage.
  - Have a base 90% chance of gaining Blood Parasite for 3 Turns.
  
  - Up to 3 friendly units:
  
  - Heal for 10 Temporary Hit Points.
  - Gain Fortune for 3 Turns.
- [Summon Blood Exarch](../spells/summon-blood-exarch.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Blood Exarch Mythic Unit with powerful support and offensive abilities that devastate low tier units.
