# Tome of the Crucible

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- DLC: Base

Bury your enemies in lava and shape the land using the destructive forces of both earth and fire.

## Unlocks

- [Meteor Imbuement](../spells/meteor-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes base attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Deal +4 Fire Damage to the target and adjacent enemies.
  - Gain Demolisher, making them able to destroy reinforced obstacles.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- Crucible Battlements (City Structure): Battlement Structure
  
  - Battlements grant Missile and Magic attacks:
  
  - Deals +4 Fire Damage.
  - Sets affected hexes On Fire.
  - Vantage Point
  
  - The City ignores negative City Stability penalties from annexing provinces with Chasm or Lava.
- [Meteor Shower](../spells/meteor-shower.md) ([Combat] Combat Enchantment): Up to 2 random enemy units and enemies adjacent to those:
  
  - Sustain 10 Fire Damage and 10 Physical Damage.
  
  This spell repeats at the start of each turn for the next 5 Turns.
- [Lava Burst](../spells/lava-burst.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Targets a 2-hex radius:
  
  - All units sustain 30 Fire Damage.
  - All units are inflicted with Burning.
  - All units are Slowed.
  - The ground is set On Fire.
- [Pyroclastic Eruption](../spells/pyroclastic-eruption.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target Province:
  
  - If it contains an enemy Province Improvement, it will be Pillaged.
  
  - You will not gain its rewards or lose Alignment.
  
  - Enemy Units in the province sustain 20 Fire Damage.
  - The province gains Ashlands.
  - The province loses River, Grassland, Swamp, Snow, Ice, Sand, Cavern Floor, and Gloom.
- Great Foundry (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Gold.; Per adjacent Mine: ; +5 Draft.; +3 Gold. ; Counts as a Mine.; Allows for the drafting of Magma Spirit.
