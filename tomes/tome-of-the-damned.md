# Tome of the Damned

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Chaos 1, Shadow 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

Apply Ghostfire to your enemies and use it as a conduit to summon Accursed Wisps.

## Unlocks

- [Curse of the Damned](../spells/curse-of-the-damned.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Target province gains Curse of the Damned for 3 Turns.
- [Accursed Blades](../spells/accursed-blades.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units attacks gain:
  
  - +1 Fire Damage.
  - +1 Frost Damage.
  - A base 60% chance of inflicting Ghostfire for 3 Turns.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Accursed Undertow](../spells/accursed-undertow.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Enemy units in a 2 hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 8 Fire Damage.
  - Sustain 8 Frost Damage.
  - Suffer Ghostfire.
  - Are pulled towards the target location.
- [Mark of the Damned](../spells/mark-of-the-damned.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units attacks gain:
  
  - Inflict Ghostfire on units that do not have it.
  - Inflict Damned
- [Summon Accursed Lamenter](../spells/summon-accursed-lamenter.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon an Accursed Lamenter on to target hex.
- Accursed Grave (Initial City Upgrade): +15 Mana; Per adjacent owned Ocean province: +1 Knowledge. +2 Gold. Fights on this city's domain add an Accursed Lamenter on your side. Counts as a Conduit
