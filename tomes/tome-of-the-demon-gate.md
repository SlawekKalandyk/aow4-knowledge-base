# Tome of the Demon Gate

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Chaos (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Open rifts to summon Fiends and burn the world in your wake.

## Unlocks

- [Sacrificial Slaughter](../spells/sacrificial-slaughter.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target friendly unit explodes and enemy units within 2 hexes:
  
  - Sustain 20 Fire Damage plus 5 Fire Damage per Tier of the sacrificed unit.
  - Suffer one random Negative Status Effect per Tier of the sacrificed unit.
- [Gremlin Ambushers](../spells/gremlin-ambushers.md) ([World Map] Sustained City Spell): Targets a city, and whenever you fight in that domain, a Gremlin is summoned next to one of your units every Turn.
  Up to 5 Gremlins can be summoned per battle.
- [Fight for Power](../spells/fight-for-power.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Two Fiend units of the same Tier in the target army fight each other. One dies and the other transforms into a Fiend with a Tier one level higher than before.
- [Demonic Summoning](../spells/demonic-summoning.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Target one of your Free City Vassals:
  
  - Summon 2 random Fiends under your control, one will be Tier III and the other Tier IV.
  - The Vassal loses:
  
  - A Vassalage Phase diplomatic stage.
  - 1 Population.
  
  Cannot be cast on a Denying Tributary.
- [Demonkin](../spells/demonkin.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Turns the target race into demonic beings, which grants them:
  
  - The Infernal Fiend unit type.
  - Flying, increasing mobility.
  - Frenzy, increasing damage as they attack.
  - Cities of this race ignore the City Stability penalty from Chasm and Lava in their Domain.
- [Summon Balor](../spells/summon-balor.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Target city loses 2 Population and spawns a Balor, a mythic unit that wreaks havoc on your foes.
- Demon Gate (Initial City Upgrade): - Unlocks the production of various Infernal Fiend Units.; Unit deployment location.; Functions as a Teleporter.
