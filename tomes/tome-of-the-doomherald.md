# Tome of the Doomherald

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Torment your enemies with a Morale-reducing effect and by exploiting enemies with Low Morale.

## Unlocks

- [Joy Siphoners](../spells/joy-siphoners.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Target race becomes a living morale siphon, gaining:
  
  - +4 Morale when attacking an enemy.
  - Demoralizer
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Prelude of Doom](../spells/prelude-of-doom.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): Target enemy army gains 3 Demoralized for 1 Turn.
- [Cruel Weaponry](../spells/cruel-weaponry.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - +30% damage against units with Morale of "Low" or worse.
- [Summon Corrupt Soul](../spells/summon-corrupt-soul.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Corrupt Soul, a Magic Fighter Unit that exploits low enemy unit Morale.
- [Cause Despair](../spells/cause-despair.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Enemies in a 1-hex radius have a base 90% chance of losing -15 Morale.
- Doomdepth Trench (Initial City Upgrade): -5 City Stability. +10 Mana. +10 Knowledge. +3 Mana and Knowledge for each Alignment level below Neutral. Counts as a Conduit.
