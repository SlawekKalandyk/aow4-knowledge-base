# Tome of the Dreadnought

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Materium 1, Chaos 1
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

Call upon the power of mighty machines and obliterate your enemies with powerful barrages.

## Unlocks

- [Destabilized Mana Core](../spells/destabilized-mana-core.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Units in 2-hex radius are Marked. After 1 Turn the Mana Core explodes inflicting:
  
  - 24 Physical Damage
  - 24 Lightning Damage.
  - 60% base chance of receiving Disrupted.
- [Warding Metals](../spells/warding-metals.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - +2 Resistance
- Construct Great Bombard (Siege Project): At the start of the battle, gain Great Bombard on the attacker's side until the end of battle.
  
  - Great Bombard is an immobile Siegecraft Unit.
  - Only one type of Siegecraft unit can be brought into Combat.
- [Tuning Kits](../spells/tuning-kits.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - The Overcharge ability, which can grant a Construct unit Overcharged until the end of battle.
- [Pinning Barrage](../spells/pinning-barrage.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Units in a 2-hex radius:
  
  - Suffer 20 Physical Damage.
  - Become Marked.
  - Have a base 60% chance of becoming Immobilized.
- [Ironclad](../units/ironclad.md) (Unit): An armored Mythic Unit with a powerful tri-barreled cannon able to load and fire specialized shells at significant range.
- War Foundry (Initial City Upgrade): - +20 Draft.; +5 Draft per adjacent Quarry or Mine Province.; Construct Units produced in the owner city gain +2 Ranks.
