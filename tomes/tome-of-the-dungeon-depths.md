# Tome of the Dungeon Depths

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- DLC: GIANTKINGS

Build a grand Dungeon Underground, fill it with structures that will enrich you, and protect it with eternal clay soldiers and traps.

## Unlocks

- [Raiders of the Deep](../spells/raiders-of-the-deep.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - Cave Walk
  - While in the Underground:
  
  - +1 Defense
  - +1 Resistance
  
  - While above ground:
  
  - Deal +10% damage
- [Dungeon Hazard](../spells/dungeon-hazard.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): Target enemy army:
  
  - Sustains 15 Physical Damage, doubled if cast on a Dungeon Province.
  - For 1 World Map Turn, at the start of combat units in this army become Slowed for 3 Turn.
- Silent Barracks (Special Province Improvement): - +1 Fortification Health per adjacent Dungeon Province.
  - +3 City Stability per adjacent Dungeon Province.
  - A Clay Arbalest, Clay Charger, or a Clay Defender joins combat on your side when fighting in this city's domain (does not function in lava).
  - Counts as a Quarry.
- Clay Forge (Special Province Improvement): - +5 Production per adjacent Dungeon Province.
  - +5 Draft per adjacent Dungeon Province.
  - Allows the drafting of the Clay Arbalest, Clay Charger, and Clay Defender units.
  - Counts as a Quarry.
- Underground Vault (Special Province Improvement): - +10 Gold
  - +3 Gold per adjacent Dungeon Province.
  - +1 Binding Essence per adjacent Dungeon Province
  - Counts as a Mine
- Dungeoneering Conclave (Initial City Upgrade): - +5 Knowledge; Every Turn, a Province in the Domain of a City: ; Gains Dungeon. ; Gain +2 Production per adjacent Dungeon Province.; Counts as a Research Post.
