# Tome of the Eternal Lord

- Tier: 5
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 8 Shadow (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Become the leader of an eternal realm and command unending armies.

## Unlocks

- [Battlefield Reanimation](../spells/battlefield-reanimation.md) (Combat Summon Spell): - All friendly Undead units come back to life with 50% of their total Hit Points.
  - Non-undead corpses come back to life as Decaying Zombies under your control until the end of battle.
- [True Death Magic](../spells/true-death-magic.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - The Curse of the Reaper ability, which has a chance to instantly kill the target.
- [Raise Undead Army](../spells/raise-undead-army.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a full army stack of low-Tier Undead units onto a target world hex.
- [Withering Mist](../spells/withering-mist.md) (Combat Enchantment): Each turn, for 3 Turns, all enemies:
  
  - Sustain 15 Frost Damage.
  - Have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
  - Have a base 90% chance of becoming Weakened for 3 Turns.
- Soul Harvest (Tome Passive): Gain Souls when enemies die in battle.
  
  Unlocks the ability to animate City Ruin and to animate Heroes in your crypt as your undead servants.
