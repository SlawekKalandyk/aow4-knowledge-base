# Tome of the God Emperor

- Tier: 5
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 8 Order (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Become a god to be worshiped. Your mere presence inspires your troops and your cities. Specialize in buffing your units.

## Unlocks

- [Mass Revive](../spells/mass-revive.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Targets all friendly dead units with Faithful or Zeal. They come back to life with 50% of their total HP Hit Points.
- [Wrath of the Emperor](../spells/wrath-of-the-emperor.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): Target enemy army:
  
  - Sustains 20 Spirit Damage.
  - Becomes Demoralized for 1 Turn.
  - Becomes Condemned for 1 Turn.
  - If cast in enemy territory, the army additionally sustains +50% damage.
- [Divine Protection](../spells/divine-protection.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Units in target friendly army gain Resurgence for 1 Turns.
- [Exalted Champion](../spells/exalted-champion.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): For 3 Turns, makes target friendly unit:
  
  - Deal +100% damage.
  - Gain +5 Bolstered Defense.
  - Gain +5 Bolstered Resistance.
  - Gain +5 Status Protection.
