# Tome of the Golden Realm

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Materium (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Become a prosperous empire and gain large amounts of gold with new infrastructure and by turning your very enemies into gold.

## Unlocks

- Reagent Refinery (City Structure): - +10 Gold
  - +10 Food per Magic Material in the Domain.
  - +10 Draft per Magic Material in the Domain.
- [Gilding Blast](../spells/gilding-blast.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Grants units in a 1-hex radius a 90% chance of becoming Gilded for 1 Turn.
- Luxury Markets (City Structure): Buy Now is 25% cheaper and can be used 2 times per World Map Turn.
- [Golden Golem](../units/golden-golem.md) (Unit): A Polearm Unit that turns enemies into gold... and brings home the spoils.
- [Goldtouched](../spells/goldtouched.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes the target race gain an affinity for gold, granting them:
  
  - +2 Resistance.
  - +1 Gold per Population in their Cities.
- Bazaar of Wonders (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Gold. +5 Gold per unique adjacent Province Improvement. Counts as a Mine.
