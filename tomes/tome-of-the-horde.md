# Tome of the Horde

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Turn your cheapest units into large, deadly armies. Specialize in summoning and buffing low tier units.

## Unlocks

- Unleash the War Hounds (Siege Project): - At the start of the battle, gain 6 War Hound units on the attacker's side until the end of battle.
- [Blaze of the Horde](../spells/blaze-of-the-horde.md) ([Combat] Damage Spell): Target enemy unit suffers:
  
  - 25 Fire Damage.
  - +5 Fire Damage for each adjacent friendly unit
- [Summon Irregulars](../spells/summon-irregulars.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a random non-Scout Tier I unit that can be produced in a city on the target hex.
- [Spawnkin](../spells/spawnkin.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Makes the target race smaller and more numerous, granting them:
  
  - Increased number of units in formation.
  - +20% damage for non-hero units.
  - +10% Evasion for hero units.
  - Incompatible with Supergrowth.
- [Fury of the Horde](../spells/fury-of-the-horde.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Tier I and Tier II friendly units in a 2-hex radius gain:
  
  - 2 stacks of Strengthened.
- [Houndmaster](../units/houndmaster.md) (Unit): A Ranged Unit that summons a War Hound to debilitate enemies.
- Mob Camp (Initial City Upgrade): - +7 Food.; +7 Draft.; Unit deployment location.; Tier I Units are cheaper by 20%.; Counts as a Forester.
