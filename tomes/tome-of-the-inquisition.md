# Tome of the Inquisition

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Hunt down those who do not agree with your authority. Inflict Condemned on enemies and restrict their Movement.

## Unlocks

- [Summon Lightbringer](../spells/summon-lightbringer.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Lightbringer Battle Mage Unit that can convert other units to your cause.
- [Burden of Guilt](../spells/burden-of-guilt.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): Target enemy army:
  
  - Sustains 20 Spirit Damage.
  - Loses 50% of its Move Points on the World Map.
- [Inquisitor's Mark](../spells/inquisitors-mark.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes attacks of enchanted units have a:
  
  - Base 90% chance of inflicting Condemned until end of combat, reducing enemy Status Resistance.
  - Base 90% chance of inflicting Weakened, reducing enemy damage.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- Tribunal (City Structure): - +20 City Stability
  - +1 Knowledge per Population
- [Mass Condemnation](../spells/mass-condemnation.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Enemies in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 10 Spirit Damage.
  - Become Condemned until the end of battle.
- [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md) (Unit): Grants access to an aggressive Skirmisher Unit that can inflict Condemned and Stunned.
- Tithe Collector (Initial City Upgrade): +10 Gold. +2 Gold per adjacent Farm or Forester. Counts as a Mine.
