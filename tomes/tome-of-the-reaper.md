# Tome of the Reaper

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 6 Shadow (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Extract Souls in brutal fashion and use them to bring Undead terrors into the world. Use instant kill effects and ultimate Undead synergy.

## Unlocks

- [Harvest Population](../spells/harvest-population.md) ([World Map] City Spell): Target friendly City:
  
  - No longer gains any Food income.
  - Gains +30 Souls income.
  - Has -20 City Stability
- [Summon Reaper](../spells/summon-reaper.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Reaper, a Tier V Mythic Unit that can instantly kill units and grows stronger when enemies die.
- [Greater Reanimation](../spells/greater-reanimation.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Resurrects target non-Hero unit:
  
  - As a Corrupt Soul if not Undead.
  - With 100% of its total Hit Points if Undead.
  
  If a Corrupt Soul or enemy Undead survives this combat, the controller will have the option of spending Souls to keep the unit permanently.
- Soul Siphon Ritual (Siege Project): - When the walls are breached, you gain +40 Souls per Tier of the besieged City
  - At the start of the battle:
  
  - Gain 6 Decaying Zombie units.
  - All enemy units gain Soulbound.
- [Marked for Death](../spells/marked-for-death.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target unit:
  
  - Sustains 15 unblockable Physical Damage.
  - Loses -10 Morale.
  - Has a Decaying Zombie spawn adjacent to them.
  - Is afflicted with Visions of Death until the end of combat.
- Soul Harvest (Tome Passive): Gain Souls when enemies die in battle.
  
  Unlocks the ability to animate City Ruin and to animate Heroes in your crypt as your undead servants.
