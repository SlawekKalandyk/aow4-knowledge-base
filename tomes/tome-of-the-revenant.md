# Tome of the Revenant

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Order 1, Shadow 1
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

Raise an army of corrupted and undead Archons and use their knowledge to make your undead come back again and again, while protecting them from those who would use the powers of light and fire.

## Unlocks

- [Unholy Champion](../spells/unholy-champion.md) ([Combat] Buff/Debuff Spell): Target friendly unit gains:
  
  - +50% damage.
  - Undying.
  - Armor of Despair
  
  On cast, enemy units in a 2-hex radius lose 10 Morale.
- [Revenant Whispers](../spells/revenant-whispers.md) ([World Map] Enemy Army Spell): For 1 World Map Turn, target enemy army gains:
  
  - 2 Demoralized.
  - When attacked, conjure 3 Skeletons with Unholy Champion on the attacker side.
- [Undead Resentment](../spells/undead-resentment.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - +2 Spirit Protection
  - +2 Fire Protection
  - Vicious Killer
- Chamber of the Rite (City Structure): Undead units drafted in this city gain Natural Regeneration.
- [Summon Fallen Angel](../spells/summon-fallen-angel.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Fallen Angel Battle Mage Unit, that causes decay and corruption.
- Soul Harvest (Tome Passive): Gain Souls when enemies die in battle.
  
  Unlocks the ability to animate City Ruin and to animate Heroes in your crypt as your undead servants.
- Necropolis (Initial City Upgrade): - +8 Soul; +2 Soul per adjacent Research Post.; Allows the drafting of Skeletons, Corrupt Souls and Undead Titans.; Counts as a Research Post.
