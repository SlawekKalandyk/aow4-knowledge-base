# Tome of the Sand Stalkers

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Materium 1, Nature 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

Spread deserts, stalk and blind your enemies, and summon a Guardian of the Desert to protect you.

## Unlocks

- [Scorpion Venom Weapons](../spells/scorpion-venom-weapons.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Attacks of enchanted units gain:
  
  - A base 60% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns.
  - A base 60% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns.
  - +2 Blight Damage when flanking.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Sandstorm](../spells/sandstorm.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Conjure a Sandstorm in a 2-hex radius that lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Dunestalkers](../spells/dunestalkers.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Target race becomes one with the desert, granting them:
  
  - Sand Walk
  - Sand Camouflage
  - When on Sand terrain gain:
  
  - Fast Movement
  - +20% Flanking damage
- [Desertification](../spells/desertification.md) ([World Map] Terraforming Spell): Target and adjacent surface Provinces:
  
  - Gain Sand.
  - Lose Chasm, Swamp, Snow, Ice, Ashlands, and Gloom.
- [Summon Sand Scorpion](../spells/summon-sand-scorpion.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Sand Scorpion, a Shield Unit unit with a debilitating poison.
- Sand Preference (Tome Passive): Provinces with Sand give +2 City Stability.
- Desert Farming (Tome Passive): Able to build Farms on Sand terrain.
  
  Farms on Sand terrain gain +5 Food if your race has Desert Adaptation.
- Grand Bazaar (Initial City Upgrade): +7 Gold income. Per adjacent Province with Sand: +2 Gold. +3 Food. Counts as a Mine.
