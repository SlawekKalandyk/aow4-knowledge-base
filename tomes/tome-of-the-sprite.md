# Tome of the Sprite

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Astral 1, Nature 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Use the powers of small Fey to support your units and befuddle your enemies.

## Unlocks

- [Catnap](../spells/catnap.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Target friendly army loses all their movement points then heals by 1 HP Hit Point per movement point lost.
- [Captivating Lights](../spells/captivating-lights.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Becomes Distracted for 1 Turn.
  - Has its Defense Mode canceled.
  - Has its Retaliation Attacks removed.
- [Fairy Dust](../spells/fairy-dust.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units:
  
  - Gain the Fairy Dust ability.
- [Fey Bond](../spells/fey-bond.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units:
  
  - On combat start, gain +10 Morale.
  - When at neutral morale, gain +2 Status Resistance.
  - When at high morale, gain +4 Status Resistance.
- [Summon Sprite](../spells/summon-sprite.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Morning Sprite Support Unit or an Evening Sprite Battle Mage Unit.
- Fey Woods (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Food.; +5 Draft.; +2 Mana per adjacent Forester.; Counts as a Forester.
