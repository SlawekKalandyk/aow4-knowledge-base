# Tome of the Stormborne

- Tier: 4
- Affinity granted: Nature 1, Astral 1
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

Control the storms to do your bidding and turn your people into powerful Naga.

## Unlocks

- [Downpour](../spells/downpour.md) ([World Map] World Spell): Target and adjacent Provinces:
  
  - Alters terrain to Swamp, if land.
  - Becomes Downpouring for 3 Turns.
  
  Cannot be used underground.
- [Lightning Storm](../spells/lightning-storm.md) ([Combat] Combat Enchantment): - All units become Wet for 3 Turns
  - Up to 2 random enemy units:
  
  - Sustain 30 Lightning Damage.
  - Have a base 90% chance of becoming Electrified for 3 Turns.
  
  This spell repeats at the start of each turn for the next 6 Turns.
- Stormport (City Structure): - +10 Mana
  - Each Trade Route originating from this city gains:
  
  - +2 Mana for each Ocean Province it passes through.
  - +1 Gold for each Ocean Province it passes through.
  
  Requires an Ocean Province Improvement in order to build.
- [Bounty of the Sea](../spells/bounty-of-the-sea.md) ([World Map] Sustained City Spell): Target owned City gains:
  
  - Double the base resources from Coastal Province Improvement.
- [Naga Transformation](../spells/naga-transformation.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Target race gains the body of a Naga, granting:
  
  - Fast Movement
  - Merfolk
  - Slip Away
  - Immunity to Electrified
  - +2 Blight Protection
  - +2 Lightning Protection
  
  Mounted units lose their mounts.
  
  Heroes lose their Mount and Leg equipment slots.
- [Stormbringer](../units/stormbringer.md) (Unit): Offensive Magic Fighter Unit whose attacks arc to other enemy units.
