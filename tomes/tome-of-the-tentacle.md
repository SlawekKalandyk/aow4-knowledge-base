# Tome of the Tentacle

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Shadow 1, Astral 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

Conjure tentacled mutations and creatures from the depths to restrain and constrict your enemies, keeping them right where you want them.

## Unlocks

- [Retaliating Growths](../spells/retaliating-growths.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target friendly unit gains Retaliating Growths for 2 Turns.
- Tendril Labyrinth (City Structure): City gains:
  
  - +10 Gold
  - +10 City Stability
  - When besieged, conjure 3 Tentacle units adjacent to enemy units at the start of battle.
- [Constricting Focus](../spells/constricting-focus.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Base Magic attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Deal +2 Physical Damage
  - Gain base 30% chance of inflicting Constricted for 1 Turn.
  - If unsuccessful, inflicts Status Vulnerability.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Conjure Tentacle](../spells/conjure-tentacle.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Summon an Immobile Fighter Unit with Hyper-Awareness.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Constrictor](../units/constrictor.md) (Unit): A Polearm Unit capable of pulling in and restraining opponents.
