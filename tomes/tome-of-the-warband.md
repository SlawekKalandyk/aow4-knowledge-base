# Tome of the Warband

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Chaos 1, Materium 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

Build strong martial armies and mentor your units through the ranks.

## Unlocks

- [Commander's Call](../spells/commanders-call.md) ([Combat] Healing/Buff Spell): Friendly units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Heal 15 Temporary Hit Points
  - Gain 15 Morale
- [Bred for War](../spells/bred-for-war.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Transformed units become stronger and more battle ready gaining:
  
  - +10 HP Hit Points.
  - +10% Morale resistance to morale loss, plus +10% for each adjacent friendly unit.
- Mentorship (Empire Bonus): All heroes under your control gain:
  
  - While army leader, the non-Hero unit with the least XP Experience in the army gains +40 XP Experience per World Map Turn.
- [Training Regimen](../spells/training-regimen.md) ([World Map] City Spell): Target owned city:
  
  - Gains 40 Draft.
- [Lieutenant](../units/lieutenant.md) (Unit): A Fighter Unit unit that empowers others in battle through their own actions.
