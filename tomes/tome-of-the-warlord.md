# Tome of the Warlord

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Chaos 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 Chaos (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

Conquer the world with powerful warriors and infernal allies.

## Unlocks

- Gladiatorial Pit (City Structure): - +20 Draft
  - +10 Gold
  - +10 City Stability
- [Might of the Battlefield](../spells/might-of-the-battlefield.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): All friendly units in a 2-hex radius gain:
  
  - Infernal Might for 3 Turns.
  - 15 Morale.
  
  Doubled for Tier I, Tier II, and Tier III units.
- Conqueror's Edict (Siege Project): - At the start of combat random hexes are set On Fire.
  - During battle all friendly units gain +15% Critical Hit chance.
  - If victorious, Razing and Migrating this city requires 1 fewer Turns.
- [Relentless Might](../spells/relentless-might.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units gain:
  
  - +1 Retaliation Attack.
  - Infernal Might when it attacks.
- [Summon War Golem](../spells/summon-war-golem.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a War Golem, an infernal siege weapon that specializes in destroying enemy fortifications.
- [Warlord](../units/warlord.md) (Unit): A powerful Mythic Unit, a master of melee combat that commands others in combat.
- War Forge (Initial City Upgrade): +20 Draft income. Per available Ore type Magic Material available to your empire gain: +5 Gold. +10 Draft; Counts as a Mine.
