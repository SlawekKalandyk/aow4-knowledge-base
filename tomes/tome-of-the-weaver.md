# Tome of the Weaver

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Nature 1, Shadow 1
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 combined Nature + Shadow (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

Inflict and exploit the Immobilized status effect and strike fear into your enemies.

## Unlocks

- [Spider's Embrace](../spells/spiders-embrace.md) ([World Map] Major Race Transformation): Target race weaves themselves with spiders gaining:
  
  - The Spider unit type.
  - Fast Movement.
  - +10 Hit Points.
  - All attacks gain a base 30% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Increased for Single Shots.
  
  Mounted units lose their mounts.
  
  Heroes lose their Mount and Leg equipment slots.
- [Conjure Weaver Spiders](../spells/conjure-weaver-spiders.md) ([Combat] Combat Summon Spell): Conjure 2 Weaver Spiders on target and adjacent hex.
  This summon lasts for 3 Turns.
- [Dreadful Bind](../spells/dreadful-bind.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Units in a 2-hex radius:
  
  - Have a base 90% chance of becoming Immobilized for 1 Turn.
  - Gain 2 Remorse for 3 Turns.
- [Dread Hunters](../spells/dread-hunters.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units:
  
  - Gain Demoralizer
  - Attacks deal +20% damage against Immobilized or Frozen targets.
- [Priest of the Weave](../units/priest-of-the-weave.md) (Unit): A Support Unit that can rebirth dead units as spiders and transfer negative effects from friendly units to enemies.
- Spider's Nest (Initial City Upgrade): - +20 Food.; +5 Draft per adjacent Forester or Forest.; Unlocks various Spider units.; Counts as a Forester.
