# Tome of Torment

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Shadow 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 Shadow (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

Torment your enemies by turning their very actions against them and make your units empowered by pain.

## Unlocks

- Lens of Anguish (City Structure): - +5 Mana.
  - +10 Combat Casting Points.
  - [Combat] Damage Spells and [Combat] Debuff Spells apply an extra 5 Morale loss.
  
  Can only be built in the Throne City.
- [Agonize](../spells/agonize.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 40 Frost Damage
  - Have a base 90% chance of gaining 3 stacks of Torment until end of combat. On failure, inflict 1 stack of Torment instead.
- [Tormenting Imbuement](../spells/tormenting-imbuement.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - Have a base 90% chance of inflicting Torment until the end of combat.
  
  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- [Pain Bringer](../units/pain-bringer.md) (Unit): Mythic Unit that torments their enemies and can easily flank them and drain their vitality.
- [Painbound](../spells/painbound.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Target race embraces pain and when hit by an attack gain:
  
  - 2 Morale.
  - A random positive status effect.
- Conduit Basilica of Lamentation (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Mana income.; +10 Knowledge income.; +10 City Stability; +3 Mana per adjacent Farm.; +2 Knowledge per adjacent Forester.; Counts as a Conduit.
