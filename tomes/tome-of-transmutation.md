# Tome of Transmutation

- Tier: 3
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: 3 Materium (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Change physical substances using vast amounts of mana. Specialize in debuffing enemies, buffing allies, and changing your economy.

## Unlocks

- [Steel Skin](../spells/steel-skin.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Transmutes the target race's skin into steel, granting them:
  
  - +2 Physical Protection.
  - +2 Blight Protection
  - -2 Lightning Protection
- [Transmuter](../units/transmuter.md) (Unit): A Battle Mage Unit that turns enemies to gold and can steal their armor and grant it to friendly units.
- [Melt Armor](../spells/melt-armor.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target enemies in a 1-hex radius:
  
  - Gain 3 Sundered Defense.
  - Sustain 20 Fire Damage.
- [Transmute Resources](../spells/transmute-resources.md) ([World Map] Sustained City Spell): Target owned city converts their Mana income, gaining Gold, Production, and Food income equal to 75% of the Mana income.
- [Adaptive Armor](../spells/adaptive-armor.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Once per Turn, whenever enchanted units are damaged by Magic attacks or spells, they gain:
  
  - Bolstered Resistance, increasing their resistance to magical damage.
  - Status Protection, reducing the chance that the unit will be affected by Negative Status Effects.
- Transmutation Circle (Initial City Upgrade): Once built, can replicate the effects and income of a Magic Material. Counts as a Mine.
