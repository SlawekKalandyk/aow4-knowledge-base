# Tome of Virtue

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

Create a virtuous army and make them fight strong until the end.

## Unlocks

- House of Charity (City Structure): The city loses 10% of its Gold income, and gains double that amount as Food and Production.
- [Heroic Stand](../spells/heroic-stand.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Has their Negative Status Effects removed.
  - Gains Steadfast for 1 Turn.
  - Ignores the penalties from Casualties for 1 Turn.
  
  Can only affect a unit once per battle.
- [Virtuous Spirit](../spells/virtuous-spirit.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Gives target race strength in moments of weakness, giving them:
  
  - +2 Spirit Protection
  - When this unit drops below 60% of their maximum HP Hit Points:
  
  - Ignores 50% of its Casualties.
  - Gains +1 Defense.
  - Gains +1 Resistance.
- [Summon Vigil](../spells/summon-vigil.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Vigil Battle Mage Unit, a unit that delivers holy fire to purify their enemies.
- [Paladin](../units/paladin.md) (Unit): A Shield Unit with healing capabilities and a powerful smite.
