# Tome of Warding

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Astral 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Specialize in magic that protects your units from damage and retaliates against enemies that attack you.

## Unlocks

- [Magical Wards](../spells/magical-wards.md) ([World Map] Minor Race Transformation): Inscribes magical wards onto the target race, granting:
  
  - +2 Lightning Protection
  - +2 Fire Protection
  - +2 Frost Protection
- [Static Shields](../spells/static-shields.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Enchanted units:
  
  - Gain Static Shield when they enter Defense Mode.
- [Summon Phantasm Warrior](../spells/summon-phantasm-warrior.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Phantasm Warrior, a Shield Unit that strikes with Lightning Damage.
- [Staves of Warding](../spells/staves-of-warding.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Makes Support abilities of enchanted units:
  
  - Grant +2 Bolstered Resistance to affected units.
- [Mark of Invulnerability](../spells/mark-of-invulnerability.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Target friendly unit:
  
  - Becomes Invulnerable for 1 Turn.
  - Has their Negative Status Effects removed.
  
  Cannot be used on a unit more than once per battle.
