# Tome of Winds

- Tier: 2
- Affinity granted: Materium 2
- DLC: Base

Harness the powers of the wind to gain boosts of speed and disrupt enemies.

## Unlocks

- [Summon Wind Rager](../spells/summon-wind-rager.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summon a Wind Rager, a fast and disruptive Magic Fighter Unit
- [Favorable Winds](../spells/favorable-winds.md) ([World Map] Friendly Army Spell): Target friendly army regains all their Movement points if they are on water. Half if they are on land.
  A unit cannot benefit from this spell more than once per turn.
- [Dust Storm](../spells/dust-storm.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Enemy units in a 2-hex radius:
  
  - Sustain 10 Physical Damage.
  - Have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
- [Seeker Arrows](../spells/seeker-arrows.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants Missile attacks of enchanted units:
  
  - +1 Range.
- [Abducting Cyclone](../spells/abducting-cyclone.md) ([Combat] Debuff Spell): Pulls the closest enemy within 3 hexes to target empty hex. They have a base 90% chance of becoming Stunned for 1 Turn.
- [Zephyr Archer](../units/zephyr-archer.md) (Unit): A Ranged Unit with extra range and an area of effect ability.
