# Tome of Zeal

- Tier: 1
- Affinity granted: Order 2
- Affinity needed to unlock: none (player-provided rule, not in the game data)
- DLC: Base

Rile up your fanatic population for a common goal. Use units with Zeal and inflict Condemned on enemies.

## Unlocks

- [Legion of Zeal](../spells/legion-of-zeal.md) ([World Map] Unit Enchantment): Grants enchanted units:
  
  - Zeal, which makes attacks deal extra Spirit Damage.
- [Inspiring Chant](../spells/inspiring-chant.md) ([Combat] Buff Spell): Friendly units in a 2-hex radius:
  
  - Gain +10 Morale.
  - Become 2 Strengthened if they have Zeal.
- [Fanatical Workforce](../spells/fanatical-workforce.md) ([World Map] City Spell): For 3 Turns, target owned city:
  
  - Gains +60 Production income.
  - Loses -20 Food income.
- [Condemnation](../spells/condemnation.md) ([Combat] Damage/Debuff Spell): Target enemy unit:
  
  - Sustains 15 Spirit Damage.
  - Becomes Condemned until the end of battle.
- [Summon Zealot](../spells/summon-zealot.md) ([World Map] Summon Spell): Summons a Zealot onto the target world hex. A Zealot is a reckless Fighter Unit that is advantageous against Condemned units.
- Circle of Zealotry (Initial City Upgrade): - +10 Draft per positive or negative level of alignment.; +2 City Stability per adjacent Province Improvement.; Unit deployment location.; Counts as a Quarry.
