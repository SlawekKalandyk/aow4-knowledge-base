# Mark of the Conflux

- Type: destiny trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Astral 2
- DLC: Base
- Earned when: Have over 1000 + (25 x Current Turn Number) Mana stored

## Effects

- +2 Astral Affinity
- Cities of this race gain +1 City Stability for each 70 Mana in their controller's global Mana reserve up to a maximum of +30 City Stability.
- Cities of this race generate -1 Mana for each Population they have.

## Description

With spellcraft as a part of their daily lives, these people have integrated the application of mana deep into their society.
