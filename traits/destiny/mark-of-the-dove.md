# Mark of the Dove

- Type: destiny trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: reputation4 10
- DLC: Base
- Earned when: Has Met Other Players; Has equal or over a 'High' Imperium Stored; No wars with Free City or Rulers; No battles for 5 or more turns

## Effects

- +10 Alignment
- Cities of this race gain +2 City Stability for each Population.
- Units of this race earn -50% XP Experience in combat.

## Description

Their society thrives in peacefulness and pacifism.
The people thrive in peacefulness and pacifism.
