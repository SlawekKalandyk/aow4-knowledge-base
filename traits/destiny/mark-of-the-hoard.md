# Mark of the Hoard

- Type: destiny trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 2
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- +2 Materium Affinity
- Cities of this race gain +1 City Stability for each 70 Gold in their controller's global Gold reserve up to a maximum of +30 City Stability.
- Cities of this race generate -1 Gold for each Population they have.

## Description

Driven by the desire to possess material wealth, they value gold above anything else.
