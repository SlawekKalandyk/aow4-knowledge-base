# Mark of the Owl

- Type: destiny trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Shadow 2
- DLC: Base
- Earned when: 'High' Knowledge income; 3 or more Ancient Wonders in domain

## Effects

- +2 Shadow Affinity
- Cities of this race gain +15 Knowledge from cleared Ancient Wonders in their Domain.
- Cities of this race have -20 City Stability when there is no cleared Ancient Wonder in their Domain.

## Description

Their society is dedicated to unraveling the secrets of the past. This makes them keen scholars of ancient sites, but also restless when bound to their ordinary lives.
