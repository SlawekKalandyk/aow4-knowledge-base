# Mark of the Sword

- Type: destiny trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Chaos 2
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- +2 Chaos Affinity
- Cities of this race generate +10% Draft for each War their Ruler is engaged in.
- Cities of this race lose -10 City Stability for each Empire or Free City Lord that has a diplomatic state better than neutral with their Ruler (vassalage excluded).

## Description

Their society thrives during times of war, and see in foreign empires only opportunities of conquest.
