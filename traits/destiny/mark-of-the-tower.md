# Mark of the Tower

- Type: destiny trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Order 2
- DLC: Base
- Earned when: Has equal or over a 'High' Imperium Stored; Have 1 or more Cities of your race with a 'Town Hall IV' structure; Have 3 or less Cities of your race

## Effects

- +2 Order Affinity
- Cities of this race with City Tier 3 or higher generate +5 Imperium.
- Cities of this race with City Tier 2 or lower generate -10 Imperium.

## Description

Residing in their high halls, they look down on all less developed dwellings.
