# Mark of the Wildspeaker

- Type: destiny trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Nature 2
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- +2 Nature Affinity
- Cities of this race gain +5 City Stability for each friendly Animal unit within their Domain up to a maximum of +30 City Stability.
- Cities of this race have -20 City Stability when there is no friendly Animal unit within their Domain.

## Description

Their society has fostered an intimate bond with the native animals of the realm.
This race has fostered an intimate bond with the native animals of the realm.
