# Arctic Adaptation

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Desert Adaptation, Desolate Adaptation, Swamp Adaptation, Underground Adaptation
- Grants spells: [Earthshape: Snow](../../spells/earthshape-snow.md)

## Effects

- Gain Arctic Walk, increasing world map movement speed on snow and ice.
- Able to build Farms on Snow terrain.
- Start in the arctic.
- Unlock the Earthshape: Snow spell.

## Description

Moving around and settling in cold areas becomes easier.
The harsh cold no longer bothers them.
