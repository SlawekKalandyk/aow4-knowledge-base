# Ashland Hunters

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Forest Hunters, Sand Hunters, Snow Hunters, Water Hunters

## Effects

- Ashland Camouflage
- Has 6 Combat Speed when on Ashlands.

## Description

Cannot be easily detected and has increased combat movement speed when in Ashlands.
Hunting in the Ashlands taught them to conceal their steps and swiftly rush their prey.
