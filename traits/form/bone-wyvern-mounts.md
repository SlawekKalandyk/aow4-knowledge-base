# Bone Wyvern Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 4
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Gain Flying movement.
- +4 Blight Protection.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Bone Wyvern mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Bone Wyvern mount.
They bring doom from the sky, mounted on their bone wyverns.
