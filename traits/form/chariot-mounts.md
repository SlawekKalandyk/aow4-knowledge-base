# Chariot Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

## Effects

- +5 HP Hit Points.
- Reduces members in the unit to one, granting immunity to Casualties.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Chariot mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Chariot mount.
Their beasts and troops have been trained in the art of chariot warfare.
