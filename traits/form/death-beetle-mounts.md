# Death Beetle Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

## Effects

- +5 HP Hit Points.
- Gain the Pestilent Escape ability.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Death Beetle mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Death Beetle mount.
They have tamed the tunneling Death Beetles.
