# Defensive Tactics

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Defense
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Overwhelm Tactics, Pack Tactics, Uncooperative

## Effects

- +1 Defense, +1 Resistance and +10% Evasion when standing next to a friendly unit with Defensive Tactics. This does not stack.

## Description

Units become tougher when standing next to allies.
They have trained to cover any gaps in their defensive formations.
