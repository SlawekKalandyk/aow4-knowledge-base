# Desolate Adaptation

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Arctic Adaptation, Desert Adaptation, Swamp Adaptation, Underground Adaptation

## Effects

- Gain Desolate Walk, increasing world map movement speed on Ashlands.
- Able to build Farms on Ashland terrain.
- Start in desolate terrain.
- Unlock the Earthshape: Ashland spell.
- Cities ignore the City Stability penalty from Lava and Chasm terrain in their Domain.

## Description

Moving around and settling in Ashland areas becomes easier.
They thrive on barren lands.
