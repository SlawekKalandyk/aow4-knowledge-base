# Dire Bear Mount

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES

## Effects

- +10 HP Hit Points.
- Gains the Overwhelm passive.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Dire Bear mount

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Dire Bear mount
They made friends with the Dire Bears.
