# Dread Spider Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- +5 HP Hit Points.
- Gains the Web ability.
- Gains Swift.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Spider mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Spider mount.
They share a deep kinship with Spiders.
