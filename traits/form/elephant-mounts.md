# Elephant Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

## Effects

- +15 HP Hit Points.
- Gain the Juggernaut ability.
- Gains Large Target.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with an Elephant Mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain an Elephant mount
They share a deep kinship with mighty Elephants.
