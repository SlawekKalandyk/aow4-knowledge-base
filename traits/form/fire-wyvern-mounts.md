# Fire Wyvern Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 4
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Gain Flying movement.
- +4 Fire Protection.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Fire Wyvern mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Fire Wyvern mount.
They soar the scorching winds, mounted on their fire wyverns.
