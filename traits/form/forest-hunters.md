# Forest Hunters

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Ashland Hunters, Snow Hunters, Sand Hunters, Water Hunters

## Effects

- Forest Camouflage
- Has 6 Combat Speed when on Forest or Mushroom Forest.

## Description

Cannot be easily detected and has increased combat movement speed when in Forests.
Their fellowship with forests hastens them in combat and hides their presence among trees.
