# Fractured Eagle Mount

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 3
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base
- Note: disabled in game data

## Effects

- Gain Flying movement.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Fractured Eagle mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Fractured Eagle mount.
They bring doom from the sky, mounted on their fractured eagles.
