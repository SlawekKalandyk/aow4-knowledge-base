# Gold Wyvern Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 4
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS

## Effects

- Gain Flying movement.
- +4 Spirit Protection.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Gold Wyvern mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Gold Wyvern mount.
They soar through the skies on grand golden wyverns.
