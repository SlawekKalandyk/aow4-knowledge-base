# Graceful Judge Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR

## Effects

- +5 HP Hit Points.
- Gain the Touch of Grace ability.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Graceful Judge mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Graceful Judge mount.
They share a deep kinship with the honorable Gracious Judges.
