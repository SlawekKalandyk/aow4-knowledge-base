# Griffon Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 3
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

## Effects

- Gain Flying movement.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Griffon Mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Griffon mount.
They soar through the skies on the noble griffons.
