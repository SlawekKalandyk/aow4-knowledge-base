# Hoarfrost Manta Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 3
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

## Effects

- Gain Flying movement.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Hoarfrost Manta mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Hoarfrost Manta mount.
They glide the seas mounted on Hoarfrost Mantas.
