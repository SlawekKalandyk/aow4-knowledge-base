# Infernal Juggernaut Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

## Effects

- +10 HP Hit Points.
- +4 Fire Protection.
- Gain the Juggernaut ability.
- Gains Large Target.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with an Infernal Juggernaut mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain an Infernal Juggernaut mount.
They have bound to their will the Infernal Juggernauts of Obbadoth.
