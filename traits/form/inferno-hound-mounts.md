# Inferno Hound Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base
- Note: disabled in game data

## Effects

- +5 HP Hit Points.
- +4 Fire Protection.
- Gains the Pack Hunter passive ability.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with an Inferno Hound mount

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain an Inferno Hound mount.
They have bound to their will the Inferno Hounds of Obbadoth.
