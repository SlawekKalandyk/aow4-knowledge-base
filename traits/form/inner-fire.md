# Inner Fire

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Defense
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Inner Frost, Inner Lightning, Inner Radiance, Poisonous

## Effects

- +3 Fire Protection.
- Melee attackers sustain 3 Fire Damage.

## Description

Melee attackers sustain Fire Damage.
Their boiling blood scorches anyone who dares to wound them.
