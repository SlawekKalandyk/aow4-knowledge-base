# Inner Frost

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Defense
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Inner Fire, Inner Lightning, Inner Radiance, Poisonous

## Effects

- +3 Frost Protection.
- Melee attackers sustain 3 Frost Damage.

## Description

Melee attackers sustain Frost Damage.
Their skin is cold to the touch, causing frostbite to those who get too close.
