# Inner Lightning

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Defense
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Inner Fire, Inner Frost, Inner Radiance, Poisonous

## Effects

- +3 Lightning Protection.
- Melee attackers sustain 3 Lightning Damage.

## Description

Melee attackers sustain Lightning Damage.
Lightning runs through their veins, zapping those who dare to harm them.
