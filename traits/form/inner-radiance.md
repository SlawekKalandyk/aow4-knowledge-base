# Inner Radiance

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Defense
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Inner Fire, Inner Frost, Inner Lightning, Poisonous

## Effects

- +3 Spirit Protection.
- Melee attackers sustain 3 Spirit Damage.

## Description

Melee attackers sustain Spirit Damage.
Their bodies can barely contain their divine origin, sacred light radiates from within them like a purifying flame.
