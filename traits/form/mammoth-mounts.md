# Mammoth Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

## Effects

- +10 Hit Points.
- +4 Frost Protection.
- Gain the Juggernaut ability.
- Gains Large Target.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Mammoth mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Mammoth mount.
They have tamed the furry Mammoths from the tundra.
