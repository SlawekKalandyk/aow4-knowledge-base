# Nightmare Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- +5 HP Hit Points.
- Gains the Intimidating Aura ability.
- Gains the Vicious Killer ability.
- Gains the Lava Walk ability.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Nightmare mount

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Nightmare mount.
They have tamed the Flaming Horses of the Inferno.
