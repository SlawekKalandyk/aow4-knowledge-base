# Overwhelm Tactics

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Offense
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Defensive Tactics, Pack Tactics, Uncooperative

## Effects

- +20% Critical Hit Chance when standing next to a friendly unit with Overwhelm Tactics. This does not stack.

## Description

Units deal more damage when fighting in groups.
They work together to bring down even the largest of foes.
