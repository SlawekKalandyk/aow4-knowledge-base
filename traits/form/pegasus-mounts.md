# Pegasus Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 3
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: HERALDOFGLORY

## Effects

- Gain Flying movement.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Pegasus mount

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Pegasus mount.
They soar through the sky on majestic Pegasi.
