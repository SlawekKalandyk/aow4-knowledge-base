# Poisonous

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Defense
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Inner Fire, Inner Frost, Inner Lightning, Inner Radiance

## Effects

- +2 Blight Protection.
- Melee attackers have a base 60% chance of becoming Poisoned.
- Immunity to becoming Poisoned.

## Description

Melee attackers have a chance of being poisoned.
They have poison glands which they use to ward off attackers.
