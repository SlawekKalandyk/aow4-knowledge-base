# Sand Hunters

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Ashland Hunters, Forest Hunters, Snow Hunters, Water Hunters

## Effects

- Sand Camouflage
- Has 6 Combat Speed when on Sand.

## Description

Cannot be easily detected and has increased combat movement speed when in Sand.
Desert winds obscure their movements yet quicken their steps when stalking their quarry.
