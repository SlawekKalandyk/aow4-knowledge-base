# Seamare Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

## Effects

- Gain Amphibious movement.
- Grants the Slippery passive ability.
- +2 Lightning Protection.
- +2 Blight Protection.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Seamare Mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Seamare mount.
They roam the seas mounted on majestic seamares.
