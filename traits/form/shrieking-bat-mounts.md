# Shrieking Bat Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 3
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

## Effects

- Gain Flying movement.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Shrieking Bat mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Shrieking Bat mount.
They darken the sky, mounted on their shrieking bats.
