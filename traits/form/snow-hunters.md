# Snow Hunters

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Ashland Hunters, Forest Hunters, Sand Hunters, Water Hunters

## Effects

- Snow Camouflage
- Has 6 Combat Speed when on Snow or Ice.

## Description

Cannot be easily detected and has increased combat movement speed when in Snow.
Enemies barely notice them in snowy lands, despite the frost enhancing their speed in combat.
