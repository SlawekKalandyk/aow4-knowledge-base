# Storm Seamare Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: WITCHOFTHEWAVES

## Effects

- Gain Amphibious movement.
- Grants the Slippery passive ability.
- +2 Lightning Protection.
- +2 Frost Protection.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Storm Seamare Mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Storm Seamare mount.
They descend upon the sea like an oncoming tempest, mounted on Storm Seamares.
