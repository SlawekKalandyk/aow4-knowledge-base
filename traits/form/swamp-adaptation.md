# Swamp Adaptation

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Arctic Adaptation, Desert Adaptation, Desolate Adaptation, Underground Adaptation

## Effects

- Gain Swamp Walk and River Walk, increasing world map movement speed in swamps and rivers.
- Ignore the -2 Food penalty from building Farms in Swamps.
- Start in the swamps.
- Unlock the Earthshape: Swamp spell.

## Description

Moving around and settling in swamp areas becomes easier.
Living around or on water is second nature to them.
