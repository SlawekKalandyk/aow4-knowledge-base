# Swiftfoot Raptor Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- +5 Hit Points.
- Grants the Slippery passive ability.
- Units have Very Fast Movement.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Raptor mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Raptor mount.
They have tamed the elusive Swiftfoot Raptors.
