# Uncooperative

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: -1
- Group: Flaws
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Defensive Tactics, Overwhelm Tactics, Pack Tactics

## Effects

- 10% Fumble chance when adjacent to another friendly unit.

## Description

Chance to deal reduced or no damage when adjacent to a friendly unit.
They are bound by pride, and know little of fighting as one.
