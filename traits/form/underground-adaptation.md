# Underground Adaptation

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Arctic Adaptation, Desert Adaptation, Desolate Adaptation, Swamp Adaptation

## Effects

- Gain Cave Walk, increasing world map movement speed in most underground terrain.
- Can build Farms instead of Huts in Fungus Fields, Underground Ashlands, and Underground Snow.
- Start in the underground.
- Underground Cities gain +1 Province Annex Range.
- Cities ignore the City Stability penalty from Lava and Chasm terrain in their Domain.

## Starting bonus

- excavation

## Description

Moving around and settling underground becomes easier.
They can thrive underground as well as anyone else could above ground.
