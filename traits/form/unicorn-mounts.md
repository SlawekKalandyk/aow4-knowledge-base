# Unicorn Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- +5 HP Hit Points.
- Gains the Phase ability.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a Unicorn mount.

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Unicorn mount.
They share a deep kinship with Unicorns.
