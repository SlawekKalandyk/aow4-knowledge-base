# Water Hunters

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 1
- Group: Adaptations
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Ashland Hunters, Forest Hunters, Sand Hunters, Snow Hunters

## Effects

- Water Camouflage.
- Has 6 Combat Speed when on Water.

## Description

Cannot be easily detected and has increased combat movement speed when in Water.
They are deadly in water, which conceals them and propels them at great speeds in battle.
