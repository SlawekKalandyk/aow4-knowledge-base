# White Wolf Mounts

- Type: form trait
- Point cost: 2
- Group: Mounts
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- +5 Hit Points.
- Gains the Pack Hunter passive ability.
- Grants the Enfeebling Howl ability.
- Additional units become Cavalry
- Ruler starts with a White Wolf mount

## Description

Mounted units are more common and gain a Wolf mount.
They have made companions of the White Wolves.
