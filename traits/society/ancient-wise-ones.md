# Ancient Wise Ones

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Astral 1
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Gifted Casters

## Effects

- When a Tome is unlocked, a random skill from that Tome costs -60% Knowledge.

## Starting bonus

- 1 random research skill is already unlocked.

## Description

They have been around since the start of history, honing their minds in the pursuit of arcane knowledge.
They harbor knowledge dating back to the beginning of time.
