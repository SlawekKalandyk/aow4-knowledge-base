# Apex Predators (evil act)

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Nature 1, Alignment -10
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Devotees of Good

## Effects

- Gain 50 Food on successfully Pillaging a Province Improvement.
- Gain 50 Food on successfully Razing a conquered City per Province Improvement it has.
- Gain 30 Food on successfully Migrating a conquered City per Population. But the City loses additional Population afterward.
- Vassals grant +10 Food income per Population.
- -10 Alignment.

## Starting bonus

- Starting Units gain +1 rank.

## Description

They believe themselves to be at the top of the food chain. To be weak is to be prey.
