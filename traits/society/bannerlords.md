# Bannerlords

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Order 1
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Chosen Destroyers

## Effects

- Rally of the Lieges occur twice as often

## Starting bonus

- Start with the nearest Free City revealed and gain +25 Allegiance with them.

## Description

They value and cultivate good relationships with their vassals, raising their banners in times of war.
