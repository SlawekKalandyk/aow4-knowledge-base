# Children of the Waves

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Nature 1
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Incompatible with: Experienced Seafarers, Transformation Ascetics

## Effects

- Cities gain +2 City Stability, +2 Production, and +2 Mana for each Water province in their Domain.

## Starting bonus

- Start with Merfolk Transformation active.
- basic_seafaring

## Description

Their people are as at home among the waves as they are upon the land.
