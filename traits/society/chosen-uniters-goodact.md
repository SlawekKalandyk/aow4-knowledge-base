# Chosen Uniters

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: reputation4 10, Order 1
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Scions of Evil

## Effects

- Gain an additional +10% income from Vassals.
- +10 Alignment.
- Units recruited through Rally of the Lieges gain +1 Rank upon recruitment.
- Gain +1 Whispering Stone.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Shield Unit or Polearm Unit.

## Description

They strive to build a realm based on cooperation, but will not hesitate to defend it.
They believe it is their calling to unite all societies.
