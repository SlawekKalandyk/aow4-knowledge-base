# Cult of Personality

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Shadow 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Heroes gain a bonus Skill Point whenever they gain a Renown Level.
- Heroes gain +10 Gold Unit Upkeep whenever they gain a Renown Level.
- In combat, Units in an Army without a Hero suffer -20 Morale.

## Starting bonus

- Start with +1 Hero Cap and +3 Hero Cap for Pantheon heroes.

## Description

They believe in the might of their heroes, the chosen few, elevated above the masses.
They elevate their heroes above the masses, empowered through dark elation.
