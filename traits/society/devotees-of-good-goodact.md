# Devotees of Good

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Order 1, reputation4 10
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Scions of Evil, Ritual Cannibals, Ruthless Raiders

## Effects

- Cities gain +10 Gold and your empire gains +5 Imperium per level of Good Alignment.
- +10 Alignment.
- Units gain +10 XP Experience per Turn while you are at the maximum good Alignment.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Support Unit or Polearm Unit.

## Description

They share pure intentions, compassion and an unerring devotion to the cause of Good.
They share pure intentions, compassion and an unerring devotion to the good cause.
