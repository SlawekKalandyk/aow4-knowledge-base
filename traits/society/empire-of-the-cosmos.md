# Empire of the Cosmos

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Astral 1
- DLC: COSMICWANDERER

## Effects

- Having 1 of each Affinity grants: ; +20 Mana; Tier III tomes have no affinity requirements.
- Having 2 of each Affinity grants: ; +20 Knowledge; Tier IV tomes have no affinity requirements.
- Having 3 of each Affinity grants: ; +20 Imperium; Tier V tomes have no affinity requirements.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Astral Wisp unit.

## Description

Their study of the Astral Sea and the sources of magic make for an imposing empire.
Their empire is said to resonate with the cosmos and the very sources of magic.
