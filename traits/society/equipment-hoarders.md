# Equipment Hoarders

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Gain a tier II-III Hero Item after clearing an Ancient Wonder or an Infestation.
- Gain extra Mana with Hero Items via the Equipment Hoard.

## Starting bonus

- Get 2 random hero items.

## Description

They pursue and covet magical equipment, using such items as inspiration for their magical feats.
