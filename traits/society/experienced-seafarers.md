# Experienced Seafarers

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Nature 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Cities gain +2 Gold, +2 Food, and +2 Draft for each Water province in their Domain.
- Embarked units: Deal +20% damage. Gain +40% Experience from combat.

## Starting bonus

- basic_seafaring

## Description

With strong sea legs, they are unrivaled on the water and know how to dredge up extra value from its depths.
They are experienced seafarers, sharing many stories of adventurous sailors and sea monsters.
