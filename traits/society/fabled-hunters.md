# Fabled Hunters

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Nature 1
- DLC: Base
- Not available to cultures: Mystic - School of Attunement, Mystic, Mystic - School of Potential, Mystic - School of Summoning, Oathsworn - Oath of Strife, Oathsworn - Oath of Righteousness, Oathsworn - Oath of Harmony

## Effects

- Gain +75% resources from clearing an Infestation, Ancient Wonder, or Resource Node.
- Units regenerate an additional +5 HP Hit Points per Turn.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Ranged Unit.

## Description

They are legendary hunters, excelling in ranged combat and exploration.
