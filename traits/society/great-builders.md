# Great Builders

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Cities gain +2 Gold per Quarry in their Domain.
- Special Province Improvements cost -50% Production.

## Starting bonus

- workshop
- vendor

## Description

They form a society of builders and makers who take pride in the things they construct.
They honor their builders and artisans, taking pride in their grand constructions.
