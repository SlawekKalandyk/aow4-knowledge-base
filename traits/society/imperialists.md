# Imperialists

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Order 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- The Throne City and cities that share a border with the Throne City gain +20 City Stability and +20 Gold income.

## Starting bonus

- Capital city starts with +1 Population.

## Description

They are focused on the glory of their empire and the development of their cities.
They are known imperialists, proud of their people and their developed culture.
