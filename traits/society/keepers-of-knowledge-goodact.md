# Keepers of Knowledge

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: reputation4 10, Shadow 1
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Scions of Evil

## Effects

- Unlocking a new Research Skill grants +10 Allegiance with all Free Cities to whom you have assigned a Whispering Stone.
- Unlocking a new Tome grants +100 Relation with all Empires.
- Gain +10 Knowledge income for each empire you have Open Borders with.
- +10 Alignment.

## Starting bonus

- seeing_stones

## Description

They know knowledge is power, and power demands respect.
Their vast knowledge earns them power and respect throughout the realms.
