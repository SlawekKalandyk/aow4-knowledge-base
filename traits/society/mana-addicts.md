# Mana Addicts

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Astral 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- All Units gain Attunement: Mana Addicted.
- Provinces with Mana Resource Nodes grant +10 City Stability.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Battle Mage Unit or Support Unit.

## Description

They have embraced magic to a degree few can match, gaining more than most from its prowess... but becoming dependent on its marvel in return.
A people who have embraced magic to a degree few can match, gaining more than most from spells, but becoming dependent on them in turn.
