# Mana Channelers

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Astral 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Summoning Spells cost -50% Mana to cast.
- Magic Origin gain +30% more XP Experience from combat.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Magic Origin unit.

## Description

They are attuned to the Astral Flows and support their armies by channeling arcane powers.
Their society is attuned to the Astral Flows, enabling them to channel arcane powers.
