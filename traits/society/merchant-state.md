# Merchant State

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Chosen Destroyers

## Effects

- Cannot obtain or found new Cities.
- Gain +2 Trade Route capacity for each Vassal.
- Trade Routes connecting their city to a Foreign City grant +5 Imperium.
- Trade Routes connecting to or from this city grant +10 City Stability.
- Trade Routes cost -30% less to establish in this city.

## Description

They focus not on expansion but on profit, expanding their influence through clever trade deals.
They are talented merchants and control their Vassals through trade.
