# Merciless Slavers (evil act)

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Chaos 1, Alignment -10
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Devotees of Good

## Effects

- -25% discount on recruiting Dominated and Captured Units.
- -10 Alignment.

## Starting bonus

- turncoat_masters

## Description

They are merciless; to them, life is a commodity that may be taken by blade or chain.
They are merciless, viewing life as another prize to be claimed.
