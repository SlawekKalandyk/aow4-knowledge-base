# Perfectionist Artisans

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- City Structures cost 100% more Production but grant +1 City Stability and +5 Gold in addition to their normal effects.
- Racial Unit tier 3 Units have +1 Rank

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra tier 3 Unit.

## Description

They demand perfection of their artisans, taking their time to build marvels like no other.
They hold artisans in high regard. Every mason or thatcher is an artist in their own right, given the time and resources needed to work their art.
