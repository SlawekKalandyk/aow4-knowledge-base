# Prolific Swarmers

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Chaos 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Cities require -10% Food for gaining a new Population.
- Racial Tier I units have +1 Rank.
- Non-Magic Origin units require -20% base Unit Upkeep.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Tier I unit.

## Description

They find their strength in fast population growth and overwhelming numbers.
