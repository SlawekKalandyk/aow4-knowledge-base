# Reclaimers

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Gain Binding Essence and Fragments for clearing Infestation Spawners, clearing Ancient Wonders, Razing Cities, and Pillaging Provinces.

## Starting bonus

- wizard_tower_item_forge
- 200

## Description

They believe anything can be reforged into something better, but before that can be done, it must be reclaimed.
They believe everything must be reclaimed and reforged stronger.
