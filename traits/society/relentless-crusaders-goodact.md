# Relentless Crusaders

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Order 1, reputation4 10
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Chosen Destroyers, Scions of Evil

## Effects

- Gain 2 Imperium for each Empire Unit killed after successful combat. This reward increases by +1 for each level of evil Alignment of the Faction they belonged to.
- Gain +30 Grievances against players with an evil Alignment.
- +10 Alignment.

## Description

They are absolute in their dedication to rooting out evil, sparing no mercy for the wicked.
They are absolute in their crusade to root out evil.
