# Ritual Cannibals (evil act)

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Chaos 1, Alignment -10
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Units gain the Corpse Eating ability.
- -10 Alignment.
- The nearest City owned gains +3 Mana and +3 Food per tier of non-Magic Origin units killed or lost after successful combat.

## Description

They believe both in the spiritual power of life... and in the value of a good banquet.
They sacrifice their people to gain favor from their gods.
