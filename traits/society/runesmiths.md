# Runesmiths

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- [World Map] Unit Enchantment Research cost -40% Knowledge to unlock.
- [World Map] Unit Enchantments cost -30% upkeep.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Shield Unit or Polearm Unit.

## Description

They are legendary smiths, weaving magic into their finely crafted creations through enchantments.
They are legendary smiths, capable of crafting the finest armor and weapons.
