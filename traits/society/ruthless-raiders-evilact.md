# Ruthless Raiders (evil act)

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Chaos 1, -10 Alignment
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- The nearest City owned gains +3 Gold and +3 Draft per tier of units killed after successful combat.
- -10 Alignment.

## Starting bonus

- Get 2 random hero items.

## Description

They murder without remorse, more interested in loot and plunder than right or wrong.
