# Scions of Evil (evil act)

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Shadow 1, -10 Alignment
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Devotees of Good, Chosen Uniters, Keepers of Knowledge

## Effects

- Cities gain +10 Draft and your empire gains +5 Imperium per level of Evil Alignment.
- Units cost -20% less Gold and Draft to recruit while you are at the maximum evil Alignment.
- No Imperium income penalties from War without War Justification.
- -10 Alignment.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Shock Unit, Shield Unit, or Polearm Unit.

## Description

They share a thirst for knowledge and power, thriving in their pursuit of Evil.
They dismiss silly ideas of morals and compassion, knowing that knowledge and power are the true virtues worth striving for.
