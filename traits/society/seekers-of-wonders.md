# Seekers of Wonders

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Ancient Wonders do not require Population in order to be annexed to a City or Outpost.
- Cities gain +20% Production per annexed Ancient Wonders.

## Starting bonus

- 1 nearby ancient wonders cleared.

## Description

They are inspired by unique constructions and the Ancient Wonders of old.
