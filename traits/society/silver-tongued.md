# Silver Tongued

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Shadow 1
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Chosen Destroyers

## Effects

- Pronouncements cost -50% less
- Trade options with Free Cities to increase income from Trade Routes no longer cost resources.

## Starting bonus

- Start with an extra Scout Unit.
- court_of_whispers

## Description

They know when to flatter and when to extort, elevating intrigue itself to a lucrative art.
