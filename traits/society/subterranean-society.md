# Subterranean Society

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Materium 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Underground Passages grant +2 Imperium.
- Underground Cities gain +20% Food income.
- Excavation of Underground provinces has a chance of uncovering an additional Resource Node.

## Starting bonus

- excavation

## Description

They know the earth like no other, favoring the depths of the realms to the turbulent surface.
Beneath the earth, they can be found, prospering and digging deeper still.
