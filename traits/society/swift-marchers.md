# Swift Marchers

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Chaos 1
- DLC: HERALDOFGLORY

## Effects

- Forced March inflicts no damage to your Units.
- Routed units are never lost.
- All units gain Road Walk.

## Starting bonus

- forced_march
- Start with an extra Scout Unit.

## Description

They are ever on the move, with travel at the heart of their culture.
