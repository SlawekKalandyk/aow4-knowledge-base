# Talented Collectors

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Nature 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Cities gain +5 Gold, Mana, Knowledge, Food, Production, and Draft for each Magic Material in their Domain.

## Starting bonus

- Start with a Magic Material nearby.

## Description

They are fascinated by rare materials and spend large parts of their lives collecting and studying them.
