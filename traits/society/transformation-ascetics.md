# Transformation Ascetics

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Order 1
- DLC: Base
- Incompatible with: Runesmiths

## Effects

- Units of your Race start with the Transformation Ascetics applied which cannot be disabled.

## Starting bonus

- Transformation Ascetics

## Description

They believe magic should not interfere with their natural development, so they shun its use on themselves and look for more material ways to improve themselves.
For them, magic should not shape the living; so they shun its use on themselves, focusing on material paths to improvement.
