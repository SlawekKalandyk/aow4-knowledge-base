# Umbral Disciples (evil act)

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: -10 Alignment, Shadow 1
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS
- Incompatible with: Devotees of Good, Transformation Ascetics
- Not available to cultures: Oathsworn - Oath of Righteousness

## Effects

- Start with Ritual of the Gloomveil unlocked.
- Cities may build the Conduit Altar of Marching Gloom.
- Your Ruler gains Tyrant's Retinue.
- -10 Alignment.

## Starting bonus

- Start with Umbral Flesh active.

## Description

The shadows whisper, and the disciples listen.
They know the shadows are not to be feared, but to be studied and used.
