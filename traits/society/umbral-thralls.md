# Umbral Thralls

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: -100 Alignment
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS
- Note: disabled in game data
- Incompatible with: Chosen Uniters, Devotees of Good, Keepers of Knowledge

## Effects

- This race are designated thralls for all Umbral Dwellings in the realm and will primarily appear in the Umbral Abyss.

## Description

These are the Initiates of the Umbral Abyss, who use otherworldly magic that mutates them with extra limbs. Their pact with the abyss makes their flesh twisted and immune to the Umbral Malady.
It is said that the Umbral Thralls were once a free civilization governed by a benevolent hivemind. Yet through Urrath's eldritch lashes, the minds of the Umbral Thralls became enchained, and their hive was twisted into an instrument of enslavement. As will-less drones they serve as unceasing warriors, laborers, or as a harvest of souls to satiate the hunger of the Umbral Demons. Their swarm carries the gloom to other realms, so that other free peoples across the Astral Sea might share the same terrible fate.
