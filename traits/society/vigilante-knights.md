# Vigilante Knights

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- Affinity: Order 1
- DLC: Base

## Effects

- Completing Bounties grants +50% Gold and Mana and an additional 100 Imperium as rewards.
- Whenever you have no Bounties active, gain a bounty offer targeting a nearby existing Infestation that hasn't been targeted before. It will not generate a new bounty if no more Infestations can be found.

## Starting bonus

- Starting Units gain +1 rank.

## Description

They keep the lands safe and restore balance between warring neighbors.
Where the land is infested, they strike with lawful strength.
