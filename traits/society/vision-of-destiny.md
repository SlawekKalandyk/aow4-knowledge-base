# Vision of Destiny

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

## Effects

- On arrival in the Realm, you must interpret a vision of what your people are destined to become: ; Order Affinity: Peacemakers.; Chaos Affinity: Conquerors.; Astral Affinity: Scholars.; Materium Affinity: Artisans.; Nature Affinity: Cultivators.; Shadow Affinity: Deathbringers.
- Permanently empower your units upon completion.
- +2 Affinity based on the chosen interpretation.

## Description

Bearing the mark of an affinity, they are destined for greatness.
Marked by an affinity, they are set upon a path they do not yet see.
