# Vision of Promise

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

## Effects

- On arrival in the Realm, you must interpret a vision of Promise: Order Affinity: The promise is a land that must be conquered. Materium Affinity: The promise is a home that must be built. Nature Affinity: The promised land is occupied by a monster we must defeat.
- Gain a powerful Throne City upon completion.
- +1 Affinity based on the chosen interpretation.
