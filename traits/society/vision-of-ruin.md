# Vision of Ruin

- Type: society trait
- Point cost: 1
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY

## Effects

- On arrival in the Realm, you must interpret a vision of Ruin: Chaos Affinity: War shall engulf the Realm. Astral Affinity: The Arcane shall dominate. Shadow Affinity: Death will reign.
- Trigger a powerful global effect upon completion.
- +1 Affinity based on the chosen interpretation.
