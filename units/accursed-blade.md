# Accursed Blade

- Tier: 3
- HP 110 | Defense 5 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Shock weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Cursed Strike** (damage 8 Physical 4 Fire 4 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Ghostfire: Base 90% chance of inflicting Ghostfire for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Frozen Flames** (damage 10 Fire 10 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Adjacent enemy units sustain damage. Frozen: Base 60% chance of inflicting Frozen for 1 Turn. If unsuccessful, inflicts Chilled. Ghostfire: Inflicts Ghostfire for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Accursed Body**: When a melee range attack hits, the attacker has a 30% chance of being inflicted with Ghostfire for 3 Turns.
- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks. Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.

## Unit type traits

- **Accursed Fiend**: A Fiend unit with: +2 Fire Protection; +2 Frost Protection; -4 Lightning Protection; Accursed Body; Immunity to Burning
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Polearm Unit**: Specialists in taking out large threats and cavalry and can counter shock unit charges. Polearm Units have: First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Retaliation Damage
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
