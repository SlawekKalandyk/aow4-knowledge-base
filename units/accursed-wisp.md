## Abilities

- **Ghostfire Bolts** (damage 5 Fire 5 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Ghostfire: Base 60% chance of inflicting Ghostfire for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Damned Body**: When a melee range attack hits, the attacker has a 60% chance of being inflicted with Ghostfire for 3 Turns.  Replaces Accursed Body.
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.
