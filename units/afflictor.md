# Afflictor

- Tier: 2
- HP 65 | Defense 1 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 4
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Source: Tome: Tome of Alchemy

## Abilities

- **Shoot Repeater Crossbow** (damage 9 Physical 5 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Miasma Shot** (damage 24 Blight, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Deal damage to target unit and create Afflicting Miasma on their hex. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Missile, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Eagle Eye
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
