# Animated Lava Bow

- Tier: 2
- HP 75 | Defense 2 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: GIANTKINGS
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 5, Spirit resistance 2, Shock weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Shoot Lava Bow** (damage 7 Physical 7 Fire, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Greater Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, free action): Caster moves to target hex. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Movement, Teleport
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Construct**: A construct unit with: Reinforced; -4 Lightning Protection; 2 Spirit Protection. Heartless; Control Loss Immunity; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Distracted; Poisoned
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Eagle Eye
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
