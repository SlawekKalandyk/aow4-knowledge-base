# Animated Storm Glaive

- Tier: 2
- HP 80 | Defense 3 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: GIANTKINGS
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 1, Spirit resistance 2

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Lightning 5 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Storm Strike** (damage 25 Lightning, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Move 4 hexes forward. Enemies in the way sustain damage. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks.  Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.
