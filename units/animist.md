# Animist

- Tier: 2
- HP 60 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 2
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: PRIMALFURY
- Source: Culture: Primal / Ash Sabertooth; Culture: Primal / Dune Serpent; Culture: Primal / Glacial Mammoth; Culture: Primal / Mire Crocodile; Culture: Primal / Storm Crow; Culture: Primal / Sylvan Wolf; Culture: Primal / Tunneling Spider

## Abilities

- **Blight Blast** (damage 16 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Mire Crocodile's Boon: Gain 2 stacks of Rising Fury on use. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Summon Primal Crocodile** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, full action): Summon a Primal Crocodile onto target hex. When summoned on favored terrain gains Frenzy. This summon lasts for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Can only be used once per battle. Starts on cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Summoning
- **Spiritual Healing** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, single-use action): Target unit: Heals for 20 Temporary Hit Points. For each Primal Fury the caster has, spend them and: Heal an extra 10 Temporary Hit Points. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Mire Crocodile's Boon**: Unit is blessed by the Mire Crocodile: It gains a Rising Fury stack when attacking. At 3 stacks, these turn into stacks of Fury of the Mire Crocodile. The unit's base attacks: Gain +3 Blight Damage. Inflict Diseased. Each attack removes a stack of Fury of the Mire Crocodile.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Diseased**: Unit is immune to Diseased.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
- **Swamp Walk**: Moving through Swamps costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
