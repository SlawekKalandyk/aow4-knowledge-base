# Architect

- Tier: 3
- HP 80 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 5
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY
- Source: Culture: Architect / Architects of Astral; Culture: Architect / Architects of Chaos; Culture: Architect / Architects of Materium; Culture: Architect / Architects of Nature; Culture: Architect / Architects of Order; Culture: Architect / Architects of Shadow

## Abilities

- **Magic Blast** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Sundered Defense: Inflict Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Sundered Resistance: Inflict Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Incarnate Restoration** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit heals 20 Temporary Hit Points.  Additionally for each stack of Affinity Incarnate the caster has, it: ; Heals +20 Temporary Hit Points.; Gains +1 Regeneration. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Conjure Order Tesseract** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, full action): Summons an Affinity Tesseract onto target location. Its damage type matches the caster.  This summon lasts for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Summoning
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Affinity Incarnate**: For each stack of Affinity Incarnate this unit gains damage based on their Empire's dominant Affinity: ; Order Affinity: +1 Spirit Damage; Chaos Affinity: +1 Fire Damage; Astral Affinity: +1 Lightning Damage; Materium Affinity: +1 Physical Damage; Nature Affinity: +1 Blight Damage; Shadow Affinity: +1 Frost Damage  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Conjure Affinity Tesseract, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
