# Astral Keeper

- Tier: 2
- HP 70 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 2
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Lightning Blast** (damage 16 Lightning, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Astral Blessing** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit: ; Heals +25 Temporary Hit Points.; Gains Static Charge for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Support
- **Shatter Self** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Unit dies: ; Triggering Astral Torrent.; Heals an additional 10 Temporary Hit Points to friendly units within 2 hexes. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Astral Torrent**: When this unit dies, friendly units within a 2-hex radius heal +15 Temporary Hit Points.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.

## Unit type traits

- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: ; +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: ; Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: ; Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8.; Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
