# Autumn Fairy

- Tier: 3
- HP 75 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Mana | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 4, Blight weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Lightning Bolts** (damage 14 Lightning, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Blinding Gale** (damage 24 Lightning, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Deals damage to units in a 1-hex radius. Blind: Base 60% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Fey Guile**: At the end of its turn, if this unit is Obscured or in a Mist cloud, it gains a random Positive Status Effect.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Fey**: A Fey unit with: +4 Spirit Protection; -4 Blight Protection; Fey Guile; The ability to ignore: Vision penalties from Misty. Accuracy penalties from Clinging Mist.
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Tiny**: Unit is very small, making it 20% harder to hit by Missile and ranged Magic attacks.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Blinding Gale, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
