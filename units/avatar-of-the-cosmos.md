# Avatar of the Cosmos

- Tier: 5
- HP 150 | Defense 7 | Resistance 7 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 400 Draft 400 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Blight resistance 2, Shock resistance 2, Fire resistance 2, Spirit resistance 2, Frost resistance 2

## Abilities

- **Cosmos Bolts** (accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Deal 30 damage of a random channel to target. Breaching: Ignore 50% of the target's Resistance. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Greater Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, free action): Caster moves to target hex. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Movement, Teleport
- **Cosmos Beam** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, full action): Units in an 8-hex long and 3-hex wide line sustain damage. This ability deals damage according to the affinity points its controller has: +2 Lightning Damage per Astral Affinity; +2 Fire Damage per Chaos Affinity; +2 Physical Damage per Materium Affinity; +2 Blight Damage per Nature Affinity; +2 Spirit Damage per Order Affinity; +2 Frost Damage per Shadow Affinity. Breaching: Ignore 50% of the target's Resistance. Cooldown: 3 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Cosmic Resonance**: When a spell is cast in combat, gain +2 Protection according to the affinities of the spell.
- **Cosmos Manifest**: For each affinity at or above 5 gain the corresponding Passive Ability: Cosmos Manifest: Astral; Cosmos Manifest: Chaos; Cosmos Manifest: Materium; Cosmos Manifest: Nature; Cosmos Manifest: Order; Cosmos Manifest: Shadow
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **High Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep increased by 50%

## Unit type traits

- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
