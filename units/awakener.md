# Awakener

- Tier: 3
- HP 75 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: High

## Abilities

- **Spirit Bolts** (damage 14 Spirit, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Awaken** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Target friendly unit gains Awakened for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Exposing Light** (damage 22 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Deals damage in a 1-hex radius. Sundered Defense: Inflict Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Sundered Resistance: Inflict Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Dormant: Radiant Light**: While Awakened, this unit gains Awakened: Radiant Light, allowing it to inflict Distracted.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Exposing Light, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
