# Ballista

- Tier: 2
- HP 15 | Defense 1 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 3
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Ballista** (damage 30 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 3-12, full action): Deals damage to a target over a long range. Ignores line of sight penalties. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Starts on cooldown. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Non-Vital**: Non-Vital units are destroyed if only Non-Vital allies remain.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Soulbound**: Unit is immune to Soulbound and cannot be transformed into a Decaying Zombie by necromancy spells and abilities.

## Unit type traits

- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Siegecraft**: Normally only accessible during Siege battles. Often required construction.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
