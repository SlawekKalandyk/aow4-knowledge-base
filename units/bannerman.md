# Bannerman

- Tier: 2
- HP 70 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 2
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Feudal / Aristocracy; Culture: Feudal / Monarchy

## Abilities

- **Banner Smite** (damage 14 Spirit, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Sundered Defense: Base 60% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Soothing Standard** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Target friendly unit gains: +25 Temporary Hit Points; 10 Morale. Enter Defense Mode after use. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **War Banner**: All adjacent friendly units gain +1 Defense and +1 Resistance. This effect does not stack.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Soothing Standard, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
