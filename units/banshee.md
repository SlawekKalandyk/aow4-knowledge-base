# Banshee

- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Cruel Bolts** (damage 14 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Cruel: +30% damage against units with Morale of "Low" or worse. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6): Caster moves to target hex. Leaves 1 Action Point. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Movement, Teleport
- **Wail of the Banshee** (damage 10 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Enemy units within a 2-hex radius sustain damage and have a base 90% chance of: ; Having their Morale reduced by -10.; Becoming 2 Weakened. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Debuff, Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives
