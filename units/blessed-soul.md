# Blessed Soul

- Tier: 3
- HP 100 | Defense 7 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Blight weakness 2, Spirit weakness 2, Frost weakness 2

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Spirit 4 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Condemned: Attacks inflict Condemned. Marked: Inflict Marked for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Archon's Smite** (damage 25 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, full action): Deals damage to target unit. If the target is Condemned, deals double damage instead. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Magic, Single Shot
- **Beacon of Hope** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Target friendly unit with Morale of "Low" or worse gains 20 morale. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Shield Wall**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Defense.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Faithful**: Base Unit Upkeep is reduced by 10%.
- **Inspiring Killer**: This unit grants double the morale bonus to itself and its allies when killing an enemy.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Shield Defense**: This unit has +3 Defense against non- Flanking attacks.
