# Blood Exarch

- Tier: 5
- HP 160 | Defense 6 | Resistance 6 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 400 Draft 300 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Parasitic Cleave** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Deal damage to units in a 1-hex cone. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Blood Parasite: Base 90% chance of inflicting Blood Parasite for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Siphon Blood** (damage 28 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target unit: Sustains damage. Damage increases by 10 Physical Damage for each stack of Bleeding. Heal for 50% of the damage dealt to the target. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Grant Blood of the Exarch** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Target friendly Tier III or higher unit gains Blood of the Exarch until the end of combat. On unit kill, this ability's cooldown is reset. Cannot affect Heroes or other Blood Exarchs. Cooldown: 4 Turns. Starts on cooldown. Requires: Support
- **Blood Pull** (damage 11 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Target unit: Sustains damage. Has a base 90% chance of being pulled to an adjacent hex. Chance increases to 120% chance against Bleeding targets. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Coagulate**: Once per turn when hit by an attack, gain for 3 Turns: Bolstered Defense; Bolstered Resistance
- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Lifedrinker**: When an adjacent unit dies: Gain Strengthened for 3 Turns. Heal for 10 Temporary Hit Points.
- **Oppressing Aura**: At the end of the turn adjacent enemies have a base 90% chance of suffering: Misfortune for 3 Turns. 10 Morale loss.
- **Polearm Weapon**: Unit has: First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
- **Undead**: An undead unit with: +2 Frost Protection; +2 Blight Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Heartless; Immunity to: Poisoned; Diseased
