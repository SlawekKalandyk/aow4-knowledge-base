# Blood Maggot

- Tier: 1
- HP 50 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 90 Draft 70 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 8 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Bleeding: Inflicts Bleeding for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Corpse Gorge** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Consume target dead unit: Gain a stack of Strengthened for 3 Turns. Gain a stack of Bloated, increasing the damage of Parasitic Bloat. Cooldown: 0 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Parasitic Bloat**: When this unit dies adjacent enemies: Sustain 10 Physical Damage. Have a base 90% chance of gaining Blood Parasite for 3 Turns.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Blood Parasite**: Unit is immune to Blood Parasite.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
