# Bone Dragon

- Tier: 4
- HP 140 | Defense 7 | Resistance 5 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 210 Draft 75 Souls | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 18 Physical 11 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Poisoned: Inflicts Poisoned for 3 Turns. Life Steal: Attacks heal 5 Temporary Hit Points. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Tail Swipe** (damage 33 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Attacks 3 adjacent units and removes their Defense Mode and Retaliation Attack. Enter Defense Mode after use. Life Steal: Attacks heal 10 Temporary Hit Points. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Melee
- **Putrid Breath** (damage 30 Blight, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to all units in a 3-hex cone. Decaying: Inflicts 2 stacks of Decaying for 3 Turns. Life Steal: Attacks heal 10 Temporary Hit Points. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Draconic Rage**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 60% or lower, it deals +30% damage.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **High Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep increased by 50%
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Bleeding**: Unit is immune to Bleeding.
- **Terrifying Aura**: At the end of this unit's turn, adjacent enemy units lose -5 Morale.  Doesn't work on units with this ability.
- **Truesight**: Unit can see invisible or camouflaged units within its vision range.
