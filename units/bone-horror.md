# Bone Horror

- Tier: 3
- HP 100 | Defense 4 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 170 Draft 50 Souls | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

This amalgamation of reanimated corpses has the sole purpose of killing your foes, with no regard for its own survival.

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 24 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Consume Corpse** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Consumes target corpse and heals +30 Temporary Hit Points. Cooldown: 1 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Corpse Cage**: The first time this unit's total Hit Points reach 50% or lower, it summons a Decaying Zombie.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Status Effect Immunity: Bleeding**: Unit is immune to Bleeding.
