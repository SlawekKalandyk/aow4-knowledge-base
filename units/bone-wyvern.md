# Bone Wyvern

- Tier: 2
- HP 70 | Defense 2 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Magic Strike** (damage 13 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Poisoned: Base 60% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Poisonous Burst** (damage 20 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage to target unit. Poisoned: Base 90% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Single Shot, Magic, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Draconic Rage**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 60% or lower, it deals +30% damage.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Bleeding**: Unit is immune to Bleeding.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Dragon**: Dragon unit with: Draconic Rage
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Fighter Unit**: A melee attacker that uses Magic Strikes to target enemies with low Resistance. Magic Fighters have: Melee Mage.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Undead**: An undead unit with: +2 Frost Protection; +2 Blight Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Heartless; Immunity to: Poisoned; Diseased
