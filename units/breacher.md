# Breacher

- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 3 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Source: Culture: Reaver / Federate

## Abilities

- **Magic Strike** (damage 15 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Breaching Shot** (damage 20 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to all units in a 2-hex Cone. Sundered Defense: Base 90% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Sundered Resistance: Base 90% chance of inflicting Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns. Breaching: Ignore 50% of the target's Resistance. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Fighter Unit**: A melee attacker that uses Magic Strikes to target enemies with low Resistance. Magic Fighters have: Melee Mage.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Arcanist
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
