# Bronze Golem

- Tier: 3
- HP 100 | Defense 5 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Construct
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 2, Shock weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Weakening Cleave** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to units in a 1-hex cone. Weakened: Inflict Weakened for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks.  Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Polearm Unit**: Specialists in taking out large threats and cavalry and can counter shock unit charges. Polearm Units have: ; First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.
