# Brown Bear

- Tier: 2
- HP 70 | Defense 2 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Evolves into: [Dire Bear](../units/dire-bear.md)

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 14 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Bleed Fury: +20% damage against Bleeding units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Swipe** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage in a 1-hex cone. Bleeding: Base 90% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Evolve**: This unit will transform into a more powerful unit when it reaches Legendary rank. Evolving units can not be recruited at Legendary rank.
- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
