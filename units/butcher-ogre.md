# Butcher Ogre

- Tier: 2
- HP 95 | Defense 3 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 13 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Bleeding: Base 60% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Butcher's Cut** (damage 25 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to target unit. Based on missing health the target has an increased chance to: Gain 3 Bleeding for 3 Turns. Gain 3 Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. On a success, the ogre heals for 15 Temporary Hit Points. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Melee, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.

## Unit type traits

- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
