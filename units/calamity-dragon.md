# Calamity Dragon

- Tier: 5
- HP 150 | Defense 6 | Resistance 7 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 400 Draft 400 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Shock weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Ghostfire Spike** (damage 22 Fire 22 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 8, full action): Deals damage to target unit. Ghostfire: Inflicts Ghostfire for 3 Turns. Ignores line of sight penalties. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base, Single Shot
- **Ghostfire Storm** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, full action): Up to 3 random enemy units: Suffer 10 Fire Damage; Suffer 10 Frost Damage; Gain 2 Ghostfire for 3 Turns. This then repeats at the start of the caster's turn for the next 3 Turns. Only one of these combat enchantments can be active at once.
- **Force Mask of Calamity** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, single-use action): Link to target unit and inflict it with Mask of Calamity. While active, gain Rip Mask of Calamity. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic, Debuff
- **Ghostfire Rebuke** (damage 11 Fire 11 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to adjacent unit. Ghostfire: Inflicts Ghostfire for 3 Turns. Requires: Magic, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Accursed Body**: When a melee range attack hits, the attacker has a 30% chance of being inflicted with Ghostfire for 3 Turns.
- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Draconic Rage**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 60% or lower, it deals +30% damage.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.

## Unit type traits

- **Accursed Fiend**: A Fiend unit with: +2 Fire Protection; +2 Frost Protection; -4 Lightning Protection; Accursed Body; Immunity to Burning
- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Dragon**: Dragon unit with: Draconic Rage
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
