# Caustic Worm

- Tier: 3
- HP 100 | Defense 4 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 12 Physical 12 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. Sundered Defense: Inflict Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Caustic Eruption** (damage 20 Blight, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, full action): Deals damage to enemy units in a 2-hex radius. Sundered Defense: Base 90% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Cave Walk**: Moving through Cavern Floors costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Fungus Fields costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Mushroom Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Sprint
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
