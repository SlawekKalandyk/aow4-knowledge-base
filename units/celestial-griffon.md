# Celestial Griffon

- Tier: 2
- HP 65 | Defense 3 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 2, Blight weakness 4, Frost weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Physical 4 Spirit, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Sundered Resistance: Base 60% chance of inflicting Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns. Combo: On the third hit, move back 4 hexes. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Fling Pinion** (damage 10 Physical 10 Spirit, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Throws a projectile at target unit. Sundered Resistance: Inflict Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Inspiring Killer**: This unit grants double the morale bonus to itself and its allies when killing an enemy.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.
- **Zeal**: Attacks gain +2 Spirit Damage. Deal +10% damage against Condemned units. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Celestial**: Celestial unit with: +2 Spirit Protection; -4 Blight Protection. -4 Frost Protection; Inspiring Killer; Control Loss Immunity
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
