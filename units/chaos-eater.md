# Chaos Eater

- Tier: 4
- HP 110 | Defense 3 | Resistance 5 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Mana | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4, Frost weakness 4

Close-range Battle Mage unit that excels at exploiting \\>.

## Abilities

- **Chaos Bolts** (damage 8 Fire, accuracy 90%, range 3, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Chaos Bolts: Deals either 8 Lightning Damage, 8 Blight Damage, 8 Spirit Damage, or 8 Frost Damage at random. Havoc Magic: 60% chance of inflicting a random Negative Status Effect. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Consume Chaos** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Targets all adjacent enemies: Removes all Negative Status Effects. Deals 12 random elemental damage for each status effect removed. Life Steal: Attacks heal 10 Temporary Hit Points. Cooldown: 1 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Chaotic Rebuke**: Enemy attackers within 3 hexes have a base 60% chance of gaining a random status effect.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Desolate Walk**: Moving through Ashlands costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Chasms costs 8 Move Points instead of 12.
- **Infernal Fiend**: A Fiend unit with: +2 Fire Protection; -4 Frost Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; +15% Critical Hit Chance; Desolate Walk; Lava Walk
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Lava Walk**: Allows the unit to traverse lava at a Movement Cost of 6.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
