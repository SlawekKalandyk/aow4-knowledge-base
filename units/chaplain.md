# Chaplain

- Tier: 3
- HP 80 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 5
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Beacon

## Abilities

- **Spirit Blast** (damage 20 Spirit, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Bless** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Target friendly unit: +2 Fortune; +2 Strengthened; +2 Bolstered Resistance. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Healing Prayer** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit: Heals +35 Temporary Hit Points. Has their Negative Status Effects removed. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Faithful**: Base Unit Upkeep is reduced by 10%.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Healing Prayer, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
