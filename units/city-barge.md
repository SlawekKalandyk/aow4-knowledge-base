# City Barge

- Tier: 1
- HP 160 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 200 Draft 200 Gold 1 Population | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 18 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Moving City**: This city is moving to a new location and may deploy into a Nomad City.
- **Palisade Walls**: Palisade Walls

## Unit type traits

- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Settler Unit**: A civilian unit that seeks a location to settle into a city.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
