- **Defense Mode: Shield Wall**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Defense.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Legion**: For each other friendly unit with Legion in combat, this unit gains: ; +1 Defense; +1 Resistance; +1 Physical Damage on attacks.  This stacks up to 4 times. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.
- **Shield Defense**: This unit has +3 Defense against non- Flanking attacks.
- **Spiked Shield**: Melee range attackers sustain 2 Physical Damage when attacking this unit.
- **Undying**: When killed, this unit will revive with 50% of its total HP Hit Points: ; After 2 Combat Turns.; If side is victorious in battle.  Can only be triggered once per battle.
