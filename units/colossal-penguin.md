# Colossal Penguin

- Tier: 5
- HP 190 | Defense 7 | Resistance 7 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 300 Mana | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 11 Physical 11 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Life Steal: Attacks heal 5 Temporary Hit Points. Morale Drain: Drains 6 Morale from targets. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Parasitic Coral**: At the end of the turn, enemy units adjacent to this one suffer 10 Frost Damage. Then heal 10 Temporary Hit Points for each enemy hit.
- **Penguin Commander**: Tier 4 or lower Penguin units in the army gain: ; +20% damage.; +20 HP Hit Points.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance  Cannot stack.

## Unit type traits

- **Amphibious**: This unit: ; Can enter water hexes without movement penalties or becoming Embarked.; Has faster movement over Rivers and Swamps.
- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Arctic Walk**: Moving through Snow and Ice costs 5 Move Points instead of 6.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
