# Corrupt Soul

- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 3 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Magic Strike** (damage 15 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Morale Drain: Target gets -4 Morale. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Crushing Anguish** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, full action): Target unit with Morale of "Low" or worse: Has a chance of dying instantly. The lower the target unit's morale is, the more this chance increases. If the unit does not die, it sustains 30 Frost Damage instead. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Magic, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Armor of Despair**: For each Morale level an attacker has below neutral, this unit gains: +1 Defense; +1 Resistance
- **Fleeting**: This unit is not stopped by enemy Zone of Control.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.

## Unit type traits

- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Magic Fighter Unit**: A melee attacker that uses Magic Strikes to target enemies with low Resistance. Magic Fighters have: Melee Mage.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Undead**: An undead unit with: +2 Frost Protection; +2 Blight Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Heartless; Immunity to: Poisoned; Diseased

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Arcanist
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
