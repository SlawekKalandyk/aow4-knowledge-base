# Cultivator

- Tier: 1
- HP 50 | Defense 0 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 2
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY
- Source: Culture: Architect / Architects of Astral; Culture: Architect / Architects of Chaos; Culture: Architect / Architects of Materium; Culture: Architect / Architects of Nature; Culture: Architect / Architects of Order; Culture: Architect / Architects of Shadow

## Abilities

- **Magic Blast** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Cultivate Affinity** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Link to target friendly unit and dispel 1 Negative Status Effect.  While linked: ; Target unit gains +2 Affinity Incarnate.; At the start of their turn, both units heal for +10 Temporary Hit Points and dispel 1 Negative Status Effect. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Affinity Incarnate**: For each stack of Affinity Incarnate this unit gains damage based on their Empire's dominant Affinity: ; Order Affinity: +1 Spirit Damage; Chaos Affinity: +1 Fire Damage; Astral Affinity: +1 Lightning Damage; Materium Affinity: +1 Physical Damage; Nature Affinity: +1 Blight Damage; Shadow Affinity: +1 Frost Damage  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
