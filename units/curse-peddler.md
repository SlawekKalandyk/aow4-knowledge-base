# Curse Peddler

- Tier: 2
- HP 60 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 2
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Source: Culture: Mercantile / Cartel

## Abilities

- **Frost Blast** (damage 14 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Weakened: Base 90% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Curse Inversion** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit: ; Heals +10 Temporary Hit Points.; Heals for +10 Temporary Hit Points for each status effect on the target.; Dispel all Negative Status Effects. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Divine Weakness** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target enemy unit and all units of that type suffer: ; 1 Leverage.; 2 Weakened for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Observant**: At the end of this unit's turn, enemy units in a 4-hex range of this unit: ; Suffer Leverage.  Disabled if this unit is Engaged in Melee.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Inversion Potion, Champion Divine Weakness, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
