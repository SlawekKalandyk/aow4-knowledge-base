# Death Beetle

- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 5 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Weakened: Base 60% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Pestilent Escape** (damage 12 Blight 12 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 3, single-use action): Adjacent enemy units sustain damage. Then this unit moves to target hex and enters Defense Mode. Weakened: Base 90% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Movement, Magic
- **Deathly Exhaust** (damage 14 Blight 14 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Adjacent enemy units sustain damage. Weakened: Inflict Weakened for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Cave Walk**: Moving through Cavern Floors costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Fungus Fields costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Mushroom Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
