# Deep-Sea Nimu

- Tier: 3
- HP 80 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 5
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Blight Blast** (damage 20 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Nimu Soothing** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit: ; Heals +30 Temporary Hit Points; Gains 3 Regeneration Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Protective Symbiosis** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Links to a friendly unit. Damage sustained by that unit is transferred to this unit with a 20% reduction.
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Natural Regeneration**: Heals 10% of its maximum Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points at the end of its turn in combat, though not more than the unit started combat with. Heals 10 HP Hit Points at the start of your turn on the world map.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
- **Water Movement**: This unit has Water Movement, which allows it to traverse the water at a Movement Cost of 4.
