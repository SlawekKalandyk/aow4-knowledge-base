# Devastator Sphere

- Tier: 2
- HP 60 | Defense 3 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 2, Shock weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Detonate and Devastate** (damage 15 Physical 15 Fire, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Unit detonates itself. In a 1-hex radius: Units sustain damage. All obstacles are destroyed. Sets hexes On Fire. Burning: Inflicts Burning for 3 Turns.
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Hyper-Awareness**: Immune to Flanking
- **Non-Vital**: Non-Vital units are destroyed if only Non-Vital allies remain.
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Soulbound**: Unit is immune to Soulbound and cannot be transformed into a Decaying Zombie by necromancy spells and abilities.

## Unit type traits

- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Construct**: A construct unit with: Reinforced; -4 Lightning Protection; 2 Spirit Protection. Heartless; Control Loss Immunity; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Distracted; Poisoned
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Siegecraft**: Normally only accessible during Siege battles. Often required construction.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
