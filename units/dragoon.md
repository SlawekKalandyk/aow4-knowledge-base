# Dragoon

- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 4 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Source: Culture: Reaver / Imperial

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Focused Aggression: Inflict Marked for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Harrying Shot** (damage 28 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 3, single-use action): Fire Magelock Pistol at target unit. Distracted: Base 90% chance of inflicting Distracted for 1 Turn. Piercing: Ignore 50% of the target's Defense. Focused Aggression: +10% damage per stack of Marked on the target. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Fleeting**: This unit is not stopped by enemy Zone of Control.
- **Focused Aggression**: This unit's attacks gain: Ranged or Magic attacks: +10% damage per stack of Marked on the target. Melee attacks inflict Marked. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Master Skirmisher**: After attacking, this unit regains half their Action Points but can no longer attack.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Cavalry**: This unit is mounted on an animal.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
