# Druid of the Cycle

- Tier: 4
- HP 100 | Defense 3 | Resistance 5 | Status resistance 9
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of Cycles
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 4, Fire weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Decaying Blast** (damage 24 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Decaying: Base 90% chance of inflicting Decaying for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Restart the Cycle** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Target friendly unit: ; If alive, heals +70 Temporary Hit Points.; If dead, is brought back to life with 70% of its total Hit Points. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Support
- **Cycle's End** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, full action): Slight chance of instantly killing an injured target. This chance increases the lower the target's Hit Points are. If unsuccessful, the target gains 3 Weakened. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Magic, Single Shot
- **Sacrificial Blight** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target non-hero friendly unit is killed, adjacent enemies suffer 10 Blight Damage per tier of the unit sacrificed.  This ability always triggers Life From Death Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Life From Death**: When this unit kills another, its cooldowns are reset.

## Unit type traits

- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Plant**: A Plant unit with: ; +4 Lightning Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Forest Walk; +20% healing received in combat.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
