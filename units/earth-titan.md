# Earth Titan

- Tier: 5
- HP 160 | Defense 6 | Resistance 6 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 400 Draft 300 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 3, Blight resistance 4, Spirit weakness 3

Only obeying the command of the absolute masters of the earth, the Earth Titan is an unstoppable force and the embodiment of the earth's rage.

## Abilities

- **Heavy Charge Strike** (damage 33 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Heavy Charge attack: Gains 30% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. Heavy Charges ignore Charge Resistance. Earthen Fury: +20% damage against Immobilized and Slowed units. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Seismic Slam** (damage 33 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, full action): Targets all enemies within 2 hexes. Immobilized: Base 90% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Status Effect Immunity**: This unit is immune to Negative Status Effects.
- **Undying**: When killed, this unit will revive with 50% of its total Hit Points: After 2 Combat Turns. If side is victorious in battle. Can only be triggered once per battle.

## Unit type traits

- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
