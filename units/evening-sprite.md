# Evening Sprite

- Tier: 1
- HP 50 | Defense 0 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Mana | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 4, Blight weakness 4
- Evolves into: [Autumn Fairy](../units/autumn-fairy.md)

## Abilities

- **Moon Bolts** (damage 5 Lightning 5 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Evening Bane** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target enemy unit: Becomes Blind for 1 Turn. Becomes Slowed for 2 Turns. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Fey Guile**: At the end of its turn, if this unit is Obscured or in a Mist cloud, it gains a random Positive Status Effect.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Evolve**: This unit will transform into a more powerful unit when it reaches Legendary rank. Evolving units can not be recruited at Legendary rank.
- **Fey**: A Fey unit with: +4 Spirit Protection; -4 Blight Protection; Fey Guile; The ability to ignore: Vision penalties from Misty. Accuracy penalties from Clinging Mist.
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Tiny**: Unit is very small, making it 20% harder to hit by Missile and ranged Magic attacks.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Evening Bane, +10 HP
