# Feylor

- Tier: 5
- HP 150 | Defense 7 | Resistance 7 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 400 Draft 300 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 2, Blight weakness 4, Frost weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Magic Strike** (damage 11 Lightning 11 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Fey Tricks: Base 60% chance of inflicting a random Negative Status Effect. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Twisted Brand** (damage 18 Lightning 18 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Target unit: Sustains damage. Gains Insanity and Fey Brand until the end of battle. Cannot affect Heroes, Fey, or Siegecraft units. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cooldown: 3 Turns. Requires: Magic, Single Shot
- **Rending Mist** (damage 22 Lightning 22 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): In a 4-hex long 3-hex wide line: Units sustain damage. Units gain 3 Sundered Resistance. Create Mist. Cooldown: 3 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Fey Guile**: At the end of its turn, if this unit is Obscured or in a Mist cloud, it gains a random Positive Status Effect.
- **Glitter Explosion**: When this unit dies, units in a 2-hex radius: Sustain 15 Lightning Damage and 15 Frost Damage. Halved for friendly units. Gain a random Negative Status Effect.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.

## Unit type traits

- **Desolate Walk**: Moving through Ashlands costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Chasms costs 8 Move Points instead of 12.
- **Fey**: A Fey unit with: +4 Spirit Protection; -4 Blight Protection; Fey Guile; The ability to ignore: Vision penalties from Misty. Accuracy penalties from Clinging Mist.
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Infernal Fiend**: A Fiend unit with: +2 Fire Protection; -4 Frost Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; +15% Critical Hit Chance; Desolate Walk; Lava Walk
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Lava Walk**: Allows the unit to traverse lava at a Movement Cost of 6.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
