# Fire Giant

- Tier: 4
- HP 150 | Defense 7 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 330 Draft 250 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 6, Frost weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Heavy Charge Strike** (damage 17 Physical 17 Fire, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Heavy Charge attack: Gains 30% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. Heavy Charges ignore Charge Resistance. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Conjure Runestone** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Summons a Fire Runestone onto target location. This summon lasts for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns
- **Volcanic Smash** (damage 16 Physical 16 Fire, accuracy 100%, range 1, full action): Deals damage to target unit and enemy units within 1 hexes. Burning: Inflicts 3 stacks of Burning for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Melee, Giant Smash, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Fiery Wake**: This unit ignites Flammable obstacles it moves over On Fire. In addition, this unit is immune to Burning.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.
- **Unstoppable Juggernaut**: This unit can crush non-Fortified obstacles without slowing.

## Unit type traits

- **Amphibious**: This unit: Can enter water hexes without movement penalties or becoming Embarked. Has faster movement over Rivers and Swamps.
- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Lava Walk**: Allows the unit to traverse lava at a Movement Cost of 6.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
