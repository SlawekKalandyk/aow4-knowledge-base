# Floral Stinger

- Tier: 2
- HP 80 | Defense 2 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 3
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 4, Fire weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 9 Physical 4 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Constricting: Base 60% chance of inflicting Constricted for 1 Turn. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Poisonous Spores** (damage 10 Physical 10 Blight, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target enemy unit is dealt damage. Poisoned: Base 90% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns. Requires: Base, Missile, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Hyper-Awareness**: Immune to Flanking
- **Natural Regeneration**: Heals 10% of its maximum Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points at the end of its turn in combat, though not more than the unit started combat with. Heals 10 Hit Points at the start of your turn on the world map.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Plant**: A Plant unit with: +4 Lightning Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Forest Walk; +20% healing received in combat.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
