- Tier: 5
- HP 150 | Defense 7 | Resistance 7 | Status resistance 11

- Cost: 400 Draft 300 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium

A Fracture, byproduct of the experiments of the Unmakers.

## Abilities

- **Magic Rebuke** (damage 11 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to adjacent unit. Requires: Magic, Base, Single Shot
- **Greater Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, free action): Caster moves to target hex. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Movement, Teleport
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

- **Status Effect Immunity: Morale**: This unit is Immune to Morale-type status effects.

## Unit type traits

- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
