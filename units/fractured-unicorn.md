# Fractured Unicorn

- Tier: 3
- HP 100 | Defense 3 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 24 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. Disrupted: Base 90% chance of draining all [World Map] Unit Enchantments from target for 2 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Mana Drain Touch** (damage 8 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Target enemy unit: Sustains damage. Becomes Mana Drained for 1 Turn. On hit, all other friendly units with Mana Starved gain Strengthened and Regeneration for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 3 Turns. Requires: Debuff, Magic
- **Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6): Caster moves to target hex. Leaves 1 Action Point. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Movement, Teleport
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Mana Starved**: This unit: Gains Strengthened and Regeneration for 3 Turns: When it applies Disrupted and Mana Drain. When hit by a Magic attack. Cannot have Unit Enchantments or Transformations. Has Control Loss Immunity.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.
