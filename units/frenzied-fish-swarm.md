# Frenzied Fish Swarm

- Tier: 1
- HP 70 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: SILVERSEAS

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 5 Physical 5 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Sundered Defense: Base 60% chance of inflicting Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Frenzy: Gains a stack of Strengthened when landing a hit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Frenzy**: When this unit lands an attack, it gains a stack of Strengthened. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Tiny**: Unit is very small, making it 20% harder to hit by Missile and ranged Magic attacks.
- **Water Movement**: This unit has Water Movement, which allows it to traverse the water at a Movement Cost of 4.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
