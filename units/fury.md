# Fury

- Tier: 2
- HP 65 | Defense 1 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Barbarian

## Abilities

- **Shoot Bow** (damage 14 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Frenzy: Gains a stack of Strengthened when landing a hit. Savage Strike: Deals an additional 4 Blight Damage on a critical hit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Frenzy**: When this unit lands an attack, it gains a stack of Strengthened. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Savage Strike**: When an attack triggers a critical hit, it deals: +4 Blight Damage. +8 Blight Damage for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Eagle Eye
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
