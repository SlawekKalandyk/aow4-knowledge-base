# Geomancer

- Tier: 4
- HP 90 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: GIANTKINGS
- Source: Tome: Tome of Geomancy

## Abilities

- **Terra Bolts** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Deal damage to target enemy unit. Bleeding: Base 90% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Geo Surge** (damage 25 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Deal damage to all enemies in a 1-hex radius. Immobilized: Base 90% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Bleeding: Inflicts Bleeding for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Geomantic Connection**: The damage and effects of Terra Bolts and Geo Surge change based on the terrain this unit is standing on.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Geo Surge, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
