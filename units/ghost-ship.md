## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Limited Maneuverability**: This unit is harder to turn during combat meaning: ; Each turn made during movement costs 1 point.; Rotating the unit on the spot reduces move points to 0.; The unit cannot make Opportunity Attacks or Retaliation Attack to hexes behind it.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Spirit Beacon**: When a non-Summon non-Undead unit dies, the ability Summon Ghost can be used again. Only triggers once per turn.
