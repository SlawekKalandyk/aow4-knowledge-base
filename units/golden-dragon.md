# Golden Dragon

- Tier: 5
- HP 175 | Defense 8 | Resistance 6 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 400 Draft 400 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 10, Blight weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 19 Physical 13 Spirit, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Blind: Base 90% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Tail Swipe** (damage 39 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Attacks 3 adjacent units and removes their Defense Mode and Retaliation Attack. Enter Defense Mode after use. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Melee
- **Purifying Breath** (damage 30 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): In a 3-hex cone: ; Enemy units sustain damage.; Friendly units heal 20 Temporary Hit Points.; Friendly units have up to 2 of their Negative Status Effects removed. Blind: Base 60% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Draconic Rage**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 60% or lower, it deals +30% damage.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **High Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep increased by 50%
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Natural Regeneration**: Heals 10% of its maximum Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points at the end of its turn in combat, though not more than the unit started combat with. Heals 10 Hit Points at the start of your turn on the world map.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.
- **Spirit Aura**: At the start of the turn, adjacent enemy units: ; Sustain 8 Spirit Damage.; Have a base 90% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.
- **Terrifying Aura**: At the end of this unit's turn, adjacent enemy units lose -5 Morale.  Doesn't work on units with this ability.
- **Truesight**: Unit can see invisible or camouflaged units within its vision range.
