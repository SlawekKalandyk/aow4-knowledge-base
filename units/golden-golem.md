# Golden Golem

- Tier: 5
- HP 150 | Defense 7 | Resistance 7 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 400 Draft 400 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Golden Realm
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 2, Shock weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Golden Cleave** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Deal damage to enemy units in a 1-hex cone. When hitting a Gilded target: Heal 5 Temporary Hit Points. Deal +20% damage. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Gilded Emanation** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, full action): Enemy units in a 2-hex radius have a 60% chance of becoming Gilded for 1 Turn. Enter Defense Mode. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Golden Curse**: Enemy melee attackers have a base 60% chance of becoming Gilded.
- **Golden Retaliation**: When in Defense Mode, this unit has unlimited Retaliation Attacks.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Polearm Weapon**: Unit has: First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.

## Unit type traits

- **Construct**: A construct unit with: Reinforced; -4 Lightning Protection; 2 Spirit Protection. Heartless; Control Loss Immunity; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Distracted; Poisoned
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Lava Walk**: Allows the unit to traverse lava at a Movement Cost of 6.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
