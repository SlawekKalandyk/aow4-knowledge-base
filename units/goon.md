# Goon

- Tier: 1
- HP 60 | Defense 2 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Source: Culture: Mercantile / Cartel

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Observe** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Observe the target: Target suffers 2 Leverage. Caster gains 1 Evasion for 3 Turns. Enter Defense Mode after use. Cooldown: 2 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Expendable**: This unit's death does not affect their allies' Morale.
- **Observant**: At the end of this unit's turn, enemy units in a 4-hex range of this unit: Suffer Leverage. Disabled if this unit is Engaged in Melee.
- **Replaceable**: If this unit is slain in combat, you can spend Gold to resurrect it if you are victorious.

## Unit type traits

- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
