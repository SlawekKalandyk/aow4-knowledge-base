# Goretusk Matriarch

- Tier: 3
- HP 110 | Defense 4 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 24 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Bleeding: Inflicts Bleeding for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Violent Gorging** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Consumes target corpse and heals +30 Temporary Hit Points. Cooldown: 2 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Maternal Rage**: Whenever a friendly unit dies, this unit gains 1 Strengthened.  Does not trigger on the death of Combat Summons, Tower, and Siegecraft units.
- **Unstoppable Juggernaut**: This unit can crush non-Fortified obstacles without slowing.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.
