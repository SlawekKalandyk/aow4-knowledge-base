# Guardian

- Tier: 2
- HP 80 | Defense 5 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY
- Source: Culture: Architect / Architects of Astral; Culture: Architect / Architects of Chaos; Culture: Architect / Architects of Materium; Culture: Architect / Architects of Nature; Culture: Architect / Architects of Order; Culture: Architect / Architects of Shadow

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode: Shield Wall**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Defense.

## Passives

- **Affinity Incarnate**: For each stack of Affinity Incarnate this unit gains damage based on their Empire's dominant Affinity: Order Affinity: +1 Spirit Damage; Chaos Affinity: +1 Fire Damage; Astral Affinity: +1 Lightning Damage; Materium Affinity: +1 Physical Damage; Nature Affinity: +1 Blight Damage; Shadow Affinity: +1 Frost Damage. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.
- **Incarnate Disruption**: Adjacent enemy units suffer -1 damage type Resistance associated with each stack of Affinity Incarnate on this unit.
- **Incarnate Retaliation**: Adjacent attackers take 1 damage of the associated type per stack of Affinity Incarnate.
- **Shield Defense**: This unit has +3 Defense against non- Flanking attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Shield Unit**: Great at blocking enemies and protecting allies against physical attacks. A Shield unit gains: Shield Defense. Defense Mode: Shield Wall.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Exalted Defense
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
