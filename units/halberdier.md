# Halberdier

- Tier: 2
- HP 80 | Defense 3 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Industrious

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 14 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Bolstering**: Once per turn this unit gains: Bolstered Defense when hit by a Melee or Missile attack. Bolstered Resistance when hit by a Magic attack.
- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks. Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.
- **Rune of Retaliation**: Reflects 40% of damage sustained from Melee attacks back onto attackers.
- **Vigilant**: Unit has +1 Retaliation Attack.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Polearm Unit**: Specialists in taking out large threats and cavalry and can counter shock unit charges. Polearm Units have: First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Retaliation Damage
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
