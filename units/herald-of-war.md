# Herald of War

- Tier: 5
- HP 160 | Defense 6 | Resistance 10 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 400 Draft 300 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 5, Fire resistance 10, Spirit resistance 5

## Abilities

- **Flurry of Swords** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Deal damage in a 2-hex line. Burning: Inflicts Burning for 3 Turns. Molten Fury: Gain a stack of Molten Fury on use. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Molten Whirlwind** (damage 28 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Adjacent units sustain damage. Consume all stacks of Molten Fury. Heal 10 Temporary Hit Points per stack consumed. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.
- **Spirit of War**: When an enemy unit dies, gain Strengthened. When a friendly unit dies, heal 10 Temporary Hit Points. Does not trigger on the death of Combat Summons, Tower and Siegecraft units.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.
- **Tireless Retaliation**: Unit has unlimited Retaliation Attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
