# Hireling

- Tier: 1
- HP 55 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Source: Culture: Mercantile / Barony; Culture: Mercantile / Cartel

## Abilities

- **Shoot Carbine** (damage 24 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Slowed: Base 90% chance of inflicting Slowed for 2 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Observant**: At the end of this unit's turn, enemy units in a 4-hex range of this unit: Suffer Leverage. Disabled if this unit is Engaged in Melee.
- **Slip Away**: When this unit's Hit Points are reduced to zero, it is displaced by 3 hexes and heals +15 Temporary Hit Points. Works once per battle. Doesn't work when Embarked.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Eagle Eye
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
