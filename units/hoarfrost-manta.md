# Hoarfrost Manta

- Tier: 2
- HP 40 | Defense 0 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Damage resistances: Frost resistance 4, Fire weakness 2

## Abilities

- **Frost Bolts** (damage 12 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Chilled: Base 60% chance of inflicting Chilled for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Chilling Ray** (damage 35 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Target enemy unit suffers damage. Inflicts Chilled for 3 Turns. Has a base 90% chance of inflicting 2 additional Chilled. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Debuff, Magic, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Rime Healing**: Unit has Natural Regeneration when on Snow, Ice, and Ocean.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Chilled**: Unit is immune to Chilled.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Frozen**: Unit is immune to Frozen.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Medal: Range, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
