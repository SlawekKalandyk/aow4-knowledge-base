# Horned God

- Tier: 5
- HP 160 | Defense 5 | Resistance 7 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 400 Draft 300 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 4, Spirit resistance 4, Blight weakness 4, Fire weakness 4

A powerful warden of nature and the personification of its wrath. Capable of summoning and healing.

## Abilities

- **Reclaiming Bolt** (damage 36 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Reclaim: Heals an allied unit adjacent to the target +15 Temporary Hit Points. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base, Single Shot
- **Wild Eruption** (damage 33 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Within a 1-hex radius: ; Deals Physical Damage to all units and obstacles.; Summons Living Vines onto each unoccupied hex. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Magic
- **Animate Flora** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Target Flora Obstacle transforms into a Floral Stinger unit under your control. This summon lasts for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support, Summoning
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Fey Guile**: At the end of its turn, if this unit is Obscured or in a Mist cloud, it gains a random Positive Status Effect.
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Shepherd of the Wilds**: Grants Stalwart to friendly units that are: ; Fey; Animal; Plant; Dragon; Spider; Merfolk  Does not affect Horned Gods.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.
