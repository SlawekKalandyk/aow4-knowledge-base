# Ice Spider

- Tier: 2
- HP 75 | Defense 2 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Frost resistance 6, Fire weakness 6
- Evolves into: [Ice Spider Matriarch](../units/ice-spider-matriarch.md)

## Abilities

- **Magic Strike** (damage 13 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Chilled: Base 60% chance of inflicting Chilled for 3 Turns. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Chilling Web** (damage 16 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Deals damage to enemy units in a 2-hex cone. Chilled: Base 90% chance of inflicting Chilled for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Immobilized**: Unit is immune to Immobilized.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Slowed**: Unit is immune to Slowed.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Arctic Walk**: Moving through Snow and Ice costs 5 Move Points instead of 6.
- **Cave Walk**: Moving through Cavern Floors costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Fungus Fields costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Mushroom Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Evolve**: This unit will transform into a more powerful unit when it reaches Legendary rank. Evolving units can not be recruited at Legendary rank.
- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Fighter Unit**: A melee attacker that uses Magic Strikes to target enemies with low Resistance. Magic Fighters have: ; Melee Mage.
- **Spider**: A spider unit with: ; Swift; Forest Walk; Cave Walk; Immunity to: ; Slowed; Immobilized
