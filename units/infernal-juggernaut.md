# Infernal Juggernaut

- Tier: 4
- HP 130 | Defense 5 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: PRIMALFURY
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4, Frost weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 14 Physical 14 Fire, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Burning: Inflicts Burning for 3 Turns. Displace and Replace: Displaces the attacked target and moves onto its hex. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Infernal Stomp** (damage 35 Fire, accuracy 100%, range 1, full action): Deal damage to target unit. Adjacent enemy units sustain 50% of the damage. Burning: Base 90% chance of inflicting Burning for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Melee, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.
- **Unstoppable Juggernaut**: This unit can crush non-Fortified obstacles without slowing.
- **Vengeful Flames**: When a attacker hits at melee range, the attacker sustains 5 Fire Damage.
- **Vicious Killer**: This unit inflicts increased morale penalties when killing an enemy. Affects enemy units up to 3 hexes.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Desolate Walk**: Moving through Ashlands costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Chasms costs 8 Move Points instead of 12.
- **Infernal Fiend**: A Fiend unit with: ; +2 Fire Protection; -4 Frost Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; +15% Critical Hit Chance; Desolate Walk; Lava Walk
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
- **Lava Walk**: Allows the unit to traverse lava at a Movement Cost of 6.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.
