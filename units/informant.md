# Informant

- Tier: 1
- HP 45 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 48 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 60 Draft 50 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Source: Culture: Mercantile / Cartel

## Abilities

- **Frost Blast** (damage 14 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Weakened: Base 90% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Farsight**: The unit has +1 vision range.
- **Observant**: At the end of this unit's turn, enemy units in a 4-hex range of this unit: Suffer Leverage. Disabled if this unit is Engaged in Melee.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Scout Unit**: Though weak in battle, these swift units are useful for exploring the world. Scout units have Farsight and can move faster on the world map.
- **Universal Camouflage**: This unit cannot be seen on the World Map.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Sprint
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
