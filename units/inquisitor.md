# Inquisitor

- Tier: 3
- HP 95 | Defense 4 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Inquisition

Grants access to an aggressive Skirmisher that can inflict Condemned and Stunned.

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 16 Physical 2 Spirit, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Condemned: Base 60% chance of inflicting Condemned until the end of battle. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Shoot Crossbow** (damage 24 Physical 4 Spirit, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Bolt of Judgment** (damage 24 Physical 4 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Shoots crossbow at target enemy unit. Stunned: Base 90% chance of inflicting Stunned for 1 Turn. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.
- **Truesight**: Unit can see invisible or camouflaged units within its vision range.
- **Zeal**: Attacks gain +2 Spirit Damage. Deal +10% damage against Condemned units. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
