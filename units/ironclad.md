# Ironclad

- Tier: 4
- HP 110 | Defense 6 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Dreadnought
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 2, Shock weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Direct Fire** (damage 22 Physical 22 Fire, accuracy 90%, range 5, single-use action): Blast the target with the main gun and knock them back. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Missile, Single Shot
- **Barrage** (damage 9 Physical 9 Fire, accuracy 50%, range 5, repeating action): Deals damage in a 1-hex radius. Ignores line of sight penalties. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Base, Missile
- **Load Sundering Shot** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Gain Ammunition: Sundering Shot. Can only be used once per battle.
- **Load Incendiary Shot** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Gain Ammunition: Incendiary Shot. Can only be used once per battle.
- **Load Shrapnel Shot** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, free action): Gain Ammunition: Shrapnel Shot. Can only be used once per battle.
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **High Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep increased by 50%
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.
- **Unstoppable Juggernaut**: This unit can crush non-Fortified obstacles without slowing.

## Unit type traits

- **Amphibious**: This unit: Can enter water hexes without movement penalties or becoming Embarked. Has faster movement over Rivers and Swamps.
- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Construct**: A construct unit with: Reinforced; -4 Lightning Protection; 2 Spirit Protection. Heartless; Control Loss Immunity; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Distracted; Poisoned
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Lava Walk**: Allows the unit to traverse lava at a Movement Cost of 6.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
