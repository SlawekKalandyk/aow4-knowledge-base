# Karagh

- Tier: 5
- HP 160 | Defense 6 | Resistance 5 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 400 Draft 300 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 33 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Bone-Shattering Howl** (damage 33 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Enemy units within a 2-hex radius sustain damage and lose -5 Morale. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Terrifying Gorging** (accuracy 100%, range 1, full action): Has a base 90% chance of consuming the target Tier I, II, or III enemy unit. If successful, heals this unit +30 Temporary Hit Points. If resisted, deals 30 Physical Damage instead. When this unit is killed, the last unit that was consumed is brought back. Cooldown: 2 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.

## Unit type traits

- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
