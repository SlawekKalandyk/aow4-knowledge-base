## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 27 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Knight's Challenge** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, free action): Challenge a Target Unit; while challenged: ; This unit does 50% more damage to the target.; The target does 50% less damage to this unit. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Graceful Charge**: Charge attacks grant this unit 1 Grace. Does not trigger on Retaliation Attacks and Opportunity Attacks.
- **Inspiring Killer**: This unit grants double the morale bonus to itself and its allies when killing an enemy.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.

## Unit type traits

- **Cavalry**: This unit is mounted on an animal.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.
