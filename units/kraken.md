# Kraken

- Tier: 4
- HP 140 | Defense 6 | Resistance 6 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 330 Draft 250 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 22 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Immobilized: Base 30% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Naval Wrecker: +20% damage against Embarked Units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Tentacle Vortex** (damage 29 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Deals damage to adjacent enemies. Enemies that are 2 hexes away sustain half of the damage and are pulled toward this unit. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Melee
- **Pull of the Deep** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, full action): Attempts to submerge an Embarked Unit to kill it. The lower the target's Hit Points, the greater the chance. If the attempt fails, the target sustains 40 Physical Damage. Cooldown: 3 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
- **Water Movement**: This unit has Water Movement, which allows it to traverse the water at a Movement Cost of 4.
