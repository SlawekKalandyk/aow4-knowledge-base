# Lesser Magma Spirit

- Tier: 1
- HP 50 | Defense 0 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 6, Spirit resistance 3, Frost weakness 6
- Evolves into: [Magma Spirit](../units/magma-spirit.md)

## Abilities

- **Fire Bolts** (damage 10 Fire, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Burning: Base 30% chance of inflicting Burning for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Fiery Wake**: This unit ignites Flammable obstacles it moves over On Fire. In addition, this unit is immune to Burning.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.
- **Vengeful Flames**: When a attacker hits at melee range, the attacker sustains 5 Fire Damage.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Evolve**: This unit will transform into a more powerful unit when it reaches Legendary rank. Evolving units can not be recruited at Legendary rank.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Medal: Evoker, +10 HP
