# Lesser Stone Spirit

- Tier: 1
- HP 65 | Defense 4 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 3, Blight resistance 4, Spirit weakness 3
- Evolves into: [Stone Spirit](../units/stone-spirit.md)

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Earthen Fury: +20% damage against Immobilized and Slowed units. Slowed: Base 30% chance of inflicting Slowed for 2 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode: Shield Wall**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Defense.

## Passives

- **Shield Defense**: This unit has +3 Defense against non- Flanking attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Evolve**: This unit will transform into a more powerful unit when it reaches Legendary rank. Evolving units can not be recruited at Legendary rank.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: ; Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8.; Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mountain Camouflage**: This unit cannot be detected on Mountains hexes.
- **Mountain Walk**: Moving through Mountains and Cliffs costs 8 Move Points instead of 16.
- **Shield Unit**: Great at blocking enemies and protecting allies against physical attacks. A Shield unit gains: ; Shield Defense.; Defense Mode: Shield Wall.
