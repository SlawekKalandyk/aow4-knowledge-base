# Lesser Tide Spirit

- Tier: 1
- HP 80 | Defense 1 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 6, Spirit resistance 3, Shock weakness 2
- Evolves into: [Tide Spirit](../units/tide-spirit.md)

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Wet: Inflicts Wet. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.
- **Wet**: Unit has: -2 Lightning Protection; -2 Frost Protection; 2 Fire Protection. Wet counters Burning

## Unit type traits

- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Evolve**: This unit will transform into a more powerful unit when it reaches Legendary rank. Evolving units can not be recruited at Legendary rank.
- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Water Camouflage**: This unit cannot be detected when standing on Water and River hexes.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
