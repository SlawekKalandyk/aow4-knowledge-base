# Liege Guard

- Tier: 3
- HP 100 | Defense 6 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Feudal / Aristocracy

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 14 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Sworn Bond** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Link to target friendly Hero unit: Damage sustained by the linked unit is dealt to this unit as well but reduced by 50%. At the start of each turn, the linked unit gains Strengthened. Cooldown: 2 Turns
- **Defense Mode: Shield Wall**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Defense.

## Passives

- **Heavy Shield**: +1 Defense and +1 Resistance. Ignored by Flanking attacks.
- **Shield Defense**: This unit has +3 Defense against non- Flanking attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Shield Unit**: Great at blocking enemies and protecting allies against physical attacks. A Shield unit gains: Shield Defense. Defense Mode: Shield Wall.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Exalted Defense
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
