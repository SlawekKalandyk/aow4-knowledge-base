# Lieutenant

- Tier: 2
- HP 85 | Defense 3 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Warband

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 14 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Discern Weakness** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, leave-one action): Target enemy unit and all units of that type gain: 2 Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. 2 Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 3 Turns. Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Lieutenant's Lead**: When this unit attacks, a random friendly unit within 2 hexes gains Strengthened for 3 Turns. When this unit enters Defense Mode, a random friendly unit within 2 hexes gains Bolstered Defense and Bolstered Resistance for 3 Turns.

## Unit type traits

- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
