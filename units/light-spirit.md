# Light Spirit

- Tier: 3
- HP 80 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 5
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Mana | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 2, Spirit resistance 6, Blight weakness 4, Frost weakness 4

- **Condemning Blast** (damage 20 Spirit, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Condemned: Attacks inflict Condemned. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Heal** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit: ; Heals for 50 Temporary Hit Points.; Has all Negative Status Effects removed. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Brilliant Phase** (damage 10 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Deals damage and applies Blind to units in a 1-hex radius. The unit then teleports to target hex. Blind: Inflict Blind for 1 Turn. Cooldown: 2 Turns Starts on cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Magic, Movement, Teleport
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Inspiring Killer**: This unit grants double the morale bonus to itself and its allies when killing an enemy.
- **Zeal**: - Attacks gain +2 Spirit Damage.; Deal +10% damage against Condemned units.  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
