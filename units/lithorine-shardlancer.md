# Lithorine Shardlancer

- Tier: 3
- HP 100 | Defense 5 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Mana | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: GIANTKINGS
- Source: Tome: Crystal Pact
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 2, Fire resistance 2, Blight weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Physical 6 Lightning, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Volatile Charge: Base 60% chance of inflicting Volatile Charge for 2 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Pinning Thrust** (damage 12 Physical 12 Lightning, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to enemy units in a 2-hex line. Immobilized: Inflicts Immobilized for 1 Turn. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks.  Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **Crystal Reflection**: Non-Melee attacks are reflected back at the attacker for 20% of the damage.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.

## Unit type traits

- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Polearm Unit**: Specialists in taking out large threats and cavalry and can counter shock unit charges. Polearm Units have: ; First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.
