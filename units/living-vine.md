# Living Vine

- Tier: 1
- HP 1 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 60 Draft 50 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 4, Fire weakness 4

A stationary plant that entangles anything that comes within its range.

## Abilities

- **Entangle** (accuracy 100%, range 1, full action): Target has a base 30% chance of becoming Immobilized.
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Berserk**: The unit: Is uncontrollable and always attacks the closest enemy target. If no enemy is in range, it will attack its allies. Is immune to Morale effects. Damage penalties from Casualty are negated.
- **Deathless**: This unit does not trigger "on unit death" effects when it dies.
- **Hyper-Awareness**: Immune to Flanking
- **Status Effect Immunity: Bleeding**: Unit is immune to Bleeding.

## Unit type traits

- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Plant**: A Plant unit with: +4 Lightning Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Forest Walk; +20% healing received in combat.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +5 HP
- Rank 3: +5 HP
- Rank 4: +5 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +5 HP
- Rank 6: +5 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
