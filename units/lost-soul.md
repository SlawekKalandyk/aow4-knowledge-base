# Lost Soul

- Tier: 2
- HP 60 | Defense 2 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 3
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 2, Spirit weakness 4

Ghostly presences, bound to this world until their unfinished business is put to rest.

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Physical 4 Spirit, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Fleeting**: This unit is not stopped by enemy Zone of Control.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.

## Unit type traits

- **Amphibious**: This unit: Can enter water hexes without movement penalties or becoming Embarked. Has faster movement over Rivers and Swamps.
- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Undead**: An undead unit with: +2 Frost Protection; +2 Blight Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Heartless; Immunity to: Poisoned; Diseased

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
