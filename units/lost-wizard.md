# Lost Wizard

- Tier: 5
- HP 160 | Defense 6 | Resistance 7 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 400 Draft 400 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Cosmic Bolts** (damage 7 Lightning 7 Fire 7 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 6, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Forbidden Tome** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0): Unit gains a forgotten spell, which can then be used as a single action until the end of the turn. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 0 Turns
- **Greater Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, free action): Caster moves to target hex. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Movement, Teleport
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.

## Unit type traits

- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
