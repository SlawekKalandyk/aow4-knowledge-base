# Magma Spirit

- Tier: 3
- HP 85 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 6, Spirit resistance 3, Frost weakness 6

## Abilities

- **Fire Bolts** (damage 14 Fire, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Burning: Base 30% chance of inflicting Burning for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Incinerate** (damage 25 Fire, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage in a 1-hex radius. Burning: Base 90% chance of inflicting Burning for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Explosive Phase** (damage 5 Fire, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Deals damage and leaves fiery terrain in a 1-hex radius around the unit. The unit then teleports to target hex. Burning: Base 30% chance of inflicting Burning for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Starts on cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic, Movement, Teleport
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fiery Wake**: This unit ignites Flammable obstacles it moves over On Fire. In addition, this unit is immune to Burning.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.
- **Vengeful Flames**: When a attacker hits at melee range, the attacker sustains 5 Fire Damage.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Incinerate, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
