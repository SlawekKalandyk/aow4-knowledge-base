# Mercenary

- Tier: 1
- HP 80 | Defense 3 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Source: Culture: Reaver / Federate; Culture: Reaver / Imperial

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Focused Aggression: Inflict Marked for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Drive Back** (damage 10 Physical 10 Fire, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to target, push them back and move onto their hex. Enter Defense Mode after use. Piercing: Ignore 50% of the target's Defense. Focused Aggression: Inflict 2 Marked for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Melee, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks. Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.
- **Focused Aggression**: This unit's attacks gain: Ranged or Magic attacks: +10% damage per stack of Marked on the target. Melee attacks inflict Marked. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **High Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep is increased by 2 Gold.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Polearm Unit**: Specialists in taking out large threats and cavalry and can counter shock unit charges. Polearm Units have: First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Retaliation Damage
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
