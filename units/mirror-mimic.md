# Mirror Mimic

- Tier: 4
- HP 120 | Defense 4 | Resistance 5 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 11 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Mimic** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, free action): Transform into target enemy non-Hero unit: Status effects are copied. Enchantments are copied. Hit Points are copied if higher than the unit's. Becomes stronger when transforming into lower tier units. Becomes weaker when transforming into higher tier units.
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
- **Universal Camouflage**: This unit cannot be seen on the World Map.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
