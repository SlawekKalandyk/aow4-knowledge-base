- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 3 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3

- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

## Abilities

- **Misty Strike** (damage 13 Blight 13 Lightning, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Strikes target enemy unit and then teleports back 3 hexes. Requires: Single Shot, Magic, Base
- **Fey Trick** (damage 12 Blight 12 Lightning, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Deal damage to target enemy with a base 90% of inflicting a random Negative Status Effect. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Single Shot, Magic, Base

## Passives

- **Fey Guile**: At the end of its turn, if this unit is Obscured or in a Mist cloud, it gains a random Positive Status Effect.
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.
- **Trail of Mists**: At the end of the turn create a Mist cloud on the unit's hex.
