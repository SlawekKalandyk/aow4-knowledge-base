# Mystic Projection

- Tier: 1
- HP 45 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 48 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 60 Draft 50 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Mystic / Attunement; Culture: Mystic / Potential; Culture: Mystic / Summoning
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Cosmic Bolts** (damage 1 Lightning 1 Fire 1 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Attunement: Star Blades**: When a friendly spell is cast, this unit's base attack gains +1 damage based on the affinity of the spell: Order Affinity - Spirit Damage; Chaos Affinity - Fire Damage; Astral Affinity - Lightning Damage; Materium Affinity - Physical Damage; Nature Affinity - Blight Damage; Shadow Affinity - Frost Damage. Stacks up to 3 times. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Farsight**: The unit has +1 vision range.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.

## Unit type traits

- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Scout Unit**: Though weak in battle, these swift units are useful for exploring the world. Scout units have Farsight and can move faster on the world map.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Sprint
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
