# Necromancer

- Tier: 2
- HP 60 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 2
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 50 Gold 20 Souls | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of Necromancy

## Abilities

- **Death Blast** (damage 9 Blight 9 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Strengthen Undead** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Target Undead gains: 2 Strengthened; Hastened. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Raise Undead** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target: Friendly Undead corpse is revived with 60% of its total Hit Points. Non-Undead corpse is raised as a Zombie. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Strengthen Undead, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
