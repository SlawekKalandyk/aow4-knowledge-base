# Nymph

- Tier: 3
- HP 80 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 5
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Mana | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 4, Spirit resistance 4, Blight weakness 4, Fire weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Blight Blast** (damage 20 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Revitalize** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, single-use action): Friendly units in a 1-hex radius: ; Gain 2 Regeneration.; Have 3 Negative Status Effects removed. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Seduce** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, single-use action): Grants a base 90% chance of making target enemy unit become Dominated for 3 Turns. If unsuccessful, the unit is Distracted instead. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Fey Guile**: At the end of its turn, if this unit is Obscured or in a Mist cloud, it gains a random Positive Status Effect.

## Unit type traits

- **Fey**: A Fey unit with: ; +4 Spirit Protection; -4 Blight Protection; Fey Guile; The ability to ignore: ; Vision penalties from Misty.; Accuracy penalties from Clinging Mist.
- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Plant**: A Plant unit with: ; +4 Lightning Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Forest Walk; +20% healing received in combat.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
