# Observer

- Tier: 1
- HP 45 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 48 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 60 Draft 50 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Source: Culture: Reaver / Federate; Culture: Reaver / Imperial
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 2, Shock weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Mark** (accuracy Always Hits, range 10, single-use action): Inflict Marked on the target. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Focused Aggression**: This unit's attacks gain: Ranged or Magic attacks: +10% damage per stack of Marked on the target. Melee attacks inflict Marked. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Non-Vital**: Non-Vital units are destroyed if only Non-Vital allies remain.
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.
- **Sensing Scope**: Unit has +3 Sensing Range.

## Unit type traits

- **Construct**: A construct unit with: Reinforced; -4 Lightning Protection; 2 Spirit Protection. Heartless; Control Loss Immunity; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Distracted; Poisoned
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Scout Unit**: Though weak in battle, these swift units are useful for exploring the world. Scout units have Farsight and can move faster on the world map.
