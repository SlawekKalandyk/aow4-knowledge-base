# Outrider

- Tier: 1
- HP 45 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 48 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 60 Draft 50 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Dark / Cult of Death; Culture: Dark / Cult of Tyranny

## Abilities

- **Shoot Bow** (damage 8 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Exploit Vulnerability: Base 60% chance of: Inflicting Status Vulnerability for 3 Turns. Healing self for 5 Temporary Hit Points. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Aura of Tyranny**: Adjacent enemies have -1 Status Resistance.
- **Farsight**: The unit has +1 vision range.
- **Infiltrate**: Gain +2 vision when in enemy domain.

## Unit type traits

- **Cavalry**: This unit is mounted on an animal.
- **Forest Camouflage**: This unit cannot be detected when standing on Forest hexes.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Mountain Camouflage**: This unit cannot be detected on Mountains hexes.
- **Scout Unit**: Though weak in battle, these swift units are useful for exploring the world. Scout units have Farsight and can move faster on the world map.
- **Swamp Camouflage**: This unit cannot be detected when standing on Wetland hexes.

- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Sprint
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
