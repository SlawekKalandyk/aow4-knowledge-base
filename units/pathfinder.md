Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base

- **Outpost Builder**: This unit is capable of creating Outposts on the world map.
- **Savage Strike**: When an attack triggers a critical hit, it deals: ; +4 Blight Damage.; +8 Blight Damage for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Cavalry**: This unit is mounted on an animal.
- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.

- **Scout Unit**: Though weak in battle, these swift units are useful for exploring the world. Scout units have Farsight and can move faster on the world map.
