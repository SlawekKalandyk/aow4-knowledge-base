# Pestilence Crocodile

- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 3 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: PRIMALFURY
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 5 Physical 5 Blight 5 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Diseased: Base 90% chance of inflicting Diseased for 3 Turns. Decaying: Inflicts Decaying for 3 Turns. Thrashing: +25% damage per attack until the end of the turn. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Aura of Disease**: At the end of this unit's turn, adjacent enemy units have a base 60% chance of becoming Diseased.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%

## Unit type traits

- **Amphibious**: This unit: Can enter water hexes without movement penalties or becoming Embarked. Has faster movement over Rivers and Swamps.
- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Undead**: An undead unit with: +2 Frost Protection; +2 Blight Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Heartless; Immunity to: Poisoned; Diseased

- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
