# Phase Beast

- Tier: 4
- HP 120 | Defense 4 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4

An elusive and fast shock unit, capable of teleportation.

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 27 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Fast Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Caster moves to target hex. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Movement, Teleport
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Displacement**: Upon sustaining damage, this unit teleports to a location away from its enemies within 3 hexes. Works once per turn.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: ; +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: ; Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.
- **Universal Camouflage**: This unit cannot be seen on the World Map.
