# Pirate Cannon Tower

- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 3 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 3
- Cost: 140 Draft 110 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Damage resistances: Blight

## Abilities

- **Fire Cannon** (damage 40 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 3-12, full action): Units in a 3-hex line: ; Sustain damage.; Have their Defense Mode canceled and Retaliation Attacks removed. Piercing: Ignore 50% of the target's Defense. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Requires: Missile, Base

## Passives

- **All-round Awareness**: Immune to Flanking
- **Blight Immunity**: This unit is immune to Blight Damage.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Immobilized**: This unit cannot move or use Movement abilities.
- **Non-Vital**: Non-Vital units are destroyed if only Non-Vital allies remain.
- **Reinforced**: Unit has +3 Defense against Missile attacks.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Morale**: This unit is Immune to Morale-type status effects.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Soulbound**: Unit is immune to Soulbound and cannot be transformed into a Decaying Zombie by necromancy spells and abilities.

## Unit type traits

- **Amphibious**: This unit: ; Can enter water hexes without movement penalties or becoming Embarked.; Has faster movement over Rivers and Swamps.
- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
- **Siegecraft**: Normally only accessible during Siege battles. Often required construction.
