# Pistoleer

- Tier: 1
- HP 60 | Defense 2 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: EMPIRESANDASHES
- Source: Culture: Reaver / Federate

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Versatile Skirmisher: Grants Prepared Shot for 2 Turn. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Fire Magelock** (damage 18 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Fire Magelock Pistol at target unit. Piercing: Ignore 50% of the target's Defense. Versatile Skirmisher: Grants Prepared Strike for 2 Turn. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.
- **Versatile Skirmisher**: Melee attacks grant Prepared Shot for 2 Turn. Missile attacks grant Prepared Strike for 2 Turn.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
