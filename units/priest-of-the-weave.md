# Priest of the Weave

- Tier: 4
- HP 100 | Defense 3 | Resistance 5 | Status resistance 9
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Weaver

## Abilities

- **Web Blast** (damage 12 Blight 12 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Immobilized: Base 90% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Webbed Transfusion** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit: Heals 70 Temporary Hit Points. Has its Negative Status Effects removed. Then an enemy unit within 2 hexes: Sustains 15 Blight Damage and 15 Frost Damage. Has a base 120% chance of becoming Immobilized for 1 Turn. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Spider Rebirth** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Target friendly dead unit: Revives into a Weaver Spider or Hunter Spider unit. If Tier IV or Tier V, revives into a Weaver Spider Matriarch or Hunter Spider Matriarch unit instead. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Can only be used once per battle. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
