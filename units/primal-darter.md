# Primal Darter

- Tier: 1
- HP 60 | Defense 1 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: PRIMALFURY
- Source: Culture: Primal / Ash Sabertooth; Culture: Primal / Dune Serpent; Culture: Primal / Glacial Mammoth; Culture: Primal / Mire Crocodile; Culture: Primal / Storm Crow; Culture: Primal / Sylvan Wolf; Culture: Primal / Tunneling Spider

## Abilities

- **Shoot Blowgun** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 3, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Mire Crocodile's Boon: Gain 1 stack of Rising Fury on use. Ignores cover and unit penalties. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Disengaging Shot** (damage 12 Physical 12 Blight, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage to target unit and jump back 3 hexes. Mire Crocodile's Boon: Gain 2 stacks of Rising Fury on use. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot, Movement
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Mire Crocodile's Boon**: Unit is blessed by the Mire Crocodile: It gains a Rising Fury stack when attacking. At 3 stacks, these turn into stacks of Fury of the Mire Crocodile. The unit's base attacks: Gain +3 Blight Damage. Inflict Diseased. Each attack removes a stack of Fury of the Mire Crocodile.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Diseased**: Unit is immune to Diseased.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.
- **Swamp Walk**: Moving through Swamps costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
