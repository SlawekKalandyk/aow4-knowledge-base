# Primal Mammoth

- Tier: 2
- HP 80 | Defense 1 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: PRIMALFURY
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Frost resistance 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 21 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. Displace and Replace: Displaces the attacked target and moves onto its hex. Chilled: Inflicts 2 stacks of Chilled for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Glacial Stomp** (damage 20 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, full action): Deal damage to target unit. Adjacent enemy units sustain 50% of the damage. Frozen: Base 50% chance of inflicting Frozen for 1 Turn. If unsuccessful, inflicts Chilled. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Melee, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Mammoth Primal Connection**: Unit gains Frenzy when in a province with Snow terrain.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Chilled**: Unit is immune to Chilled.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Frozen**: Unit is immune to Frozen.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Arctic Walk**: Moving through Snow and Ice costs 5 Move Points instead of 6.
- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.

- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
