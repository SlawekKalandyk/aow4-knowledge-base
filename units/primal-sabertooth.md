# Primal Sabertooth

- Tier: 2
- HP 70 | Defense 2 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: PRIMALFURY
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Fire resistance 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 11 Fire, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit and deals an extra 4 Fire Damage to another enemy unit within 2 hexes. Burning: Base 90% chance of inflicting Burning for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Pounce** (damage 14 Fire, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Jump and deal damage to target unit. Deal 50% of the damage to another enemy unit up to 2 hexes away. Burning: Inflicts Burning for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Movement, Single Shot, Base, Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Fiery Wake**: This unit ignites Flammable obstacles it moves over On Fire. In addition, this unit is immune to Burning.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Sabertooth Primal Connection**: Unit gains Frenzy when in a province with Ashlands terrain.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Desolate Walk**: Moving through Ashlands costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Chasms costs 8 Move Points instead of 12.
- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: ; +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: ; Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
