# Primal Spider

- Tier: 2
- HP 70 | Defense 2 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: PRIMALFURY
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Blight resistance 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 14 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Poisoned: Base 60% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns. Life Steal: Attacks heal 5 Temporary Hit Points. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Poison Webs** (damage 14 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Enemy units in a 2-hex cone: ; Sustain damage.; Become Slowed for 1 Turn. Poisoned: Base 90% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns. Immobilized: Base 60% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Life Steal: Attacks heal 10 Temporary Hit Points. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Missile
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Spider Primal Connection**: Unit gains Frenzy when in a province with Mushroom Forest terrain.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Immobilized**: Unit is immune to Immobilized.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Slowed**: Unit is immune to Slowed.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.
- **Trail of Mists**: At the end of the turn create a Mist cloud on the unit's hex.
