# Primordial Basilisk

- Tier: 5
- HP 115 | Defense 3 | Resistance 5 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 400 Draft 400 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: GIANTKINGS
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 6

## Abilities

- **Blight Coil** (damage 18 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Deal damage to target unit and up to 2 other enemies within 3 hexes. Immobilized: Base 70% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Doom Gaze** (damage 25 Blight, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius sustain damage. Stunned: Base 120% chance of inflicting Stunned for 1 Turn. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Magic
- **Serpentine Curse** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Target enemy non-hero unit has a base 120% chance of transforming into a Plague Serpent under the caster's control.  Cannot be used in Water battles. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 5 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Natural Regeneration**: Heals 10% of its maximum Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points at the end of its turn in combat, though not more than the unit started combat with. Heals 10 HP Hit Points at the start of your turn on the world map.
- **Primordial Envy**: While this unit is in battle, all friendly units' attacks gain: ; A 60% chance of stealing one Positive Status Effect.  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. ; Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight.; Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
