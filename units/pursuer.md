# Pursuer

- Tier: 1
- HP 55 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Dark / Cult of Death; Culture: Dark / Cult of Tyranny

## Abilities

- **Shoot Bow** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Exploit Vulnerability: Base 60% chance of: Inflicting Status Vulnerability for 3 Turns. Healing self for 5 Temporary Hit Points. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Relentless Pursuit** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Caster moves to target hex. This ability recharges on kill. Can only be used once per battle. Starts on cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Movement, Relentless Pursuit
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Aura of Tyranny**: Adjacent enemies have -1 Status Resistance.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.
