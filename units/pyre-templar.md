# Pyre Templar

- Tier: 4
- HP 110 | Defense 5 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Cleansing Flame
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 4

## Abilities

- **Fire Cleave** (damage 7 Physical 7 Fire 2 Spirit, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Deal damage to units in a 1-hex cone, effectiveness against secondary targets is reduced. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Pyre Cloak** (damage 20 Fire 2 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): This unit enters Defense Mode and: All adjacent enemy units suffer Fire Damage damage. All adjacent enemy units have a 90% chance of suffering Burning. Create Cleansing Flames on this and adjacent hexes for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Debuff, Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks. Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Burning**: Unit is immune to Burning.
- **Zeal**: Attacks gain +2 Spirit Damage. Deal +10% damage against Condemned units. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Polearm Unit**: Specialists in taking out large threats and cavalry and can counter shock unit charges. Polearm Units have: First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.

- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
