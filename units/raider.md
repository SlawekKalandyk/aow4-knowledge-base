# Raider

- Tier: 1
- HP 75 | Defense 3 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN
- Source: Culture: Nomad / Conquerors

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 18 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Empowering Momentum**: After moving at least 3 hexes, gain Momentum until the end of the Turn.
- **Fiery Momentum**: While this unit has Momentum: ; Attacks inflict guaranteed Burning.; Gain Demolisher.
- **Slip Away**: When this unit's HP Hit Points are reduced to zero, it is displaced by 3 hexes and heals +15 Temporary Hit Points.  Works once per battle. Doesn't work when Embarked.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.
