# Reaper

- Tier: 5
- HP 150 | Defense 6 | Resistance 6 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 280 Draft 100 Souls | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 11 Physical 11 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Weakened: Base 90% chance of inflicting Weakened for 3 Turns. Piercing: Ignore 50% of the target's Defense. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Finger of Death** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, single-use action): Target non-Hero enemy unit has a base 60% chance of dying. If a non-Undead unit dies this way, it transforms into a Decaying Zombie under your control until the end of the battle. If resisted, the unit sustains 45 Frost Damage that ignores Resistance. Cooldown: 4 Turns. Requires: Debuff
- **Greater Corpse Consumption** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Consume target corpse: Heal +40 Temporary Hit Points. Gain 3 Strengthened. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Dark Rites**: When an enemy unit dies: Heal 10 Temporary Hit Points. Gain Strengthened. Does not trigger on the death of Combat Summons, Tower and Siegecraft units.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Soul Maintenance**: Has an added 5 Soul upkeep. Gold and Mana base Unit Upkeep is reduced by -50%

## Unit type traits

- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
- **Undead**: An undead unit with: +2 Frost Protection; +2 Blight Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Heartless; Immunity to: Poisoned; Diseased
