# River Troll

- Tier: 3
- HP 105 | Defense 4 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 15 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Bleeding: Base 60% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Razor Net** (damage 24 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage to target unit. Immobilized: Base 90% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.
- **Troll Natural Regeneration**: Heals 10% of their maximum Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points at the end of its turn in combat. Heals 10 Hit Points at the start of your turn on the world map. If this unit sustains Fire Damage, the regeneration stops for 1 Turn.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
