- Tier: 4
- HP 120 | Defense 6 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 27 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Bleeding: Inflicts 2 Bleeding for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot

- **Raise Thorned Skeleton** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, free action): Target corpse revives as a Thorned Skeleton under your control. Cooldown: 2 Turns
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Cursed Rose Command**: While this unit is in combat, all friendly undead units have Razor Thorns.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Razor Thorns**: Melee attackers suffer a stack of Bleeding for 3 Turns.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Bleeding**: Unit is immune to Bleeding.
