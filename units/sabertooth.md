# Sabertooth

- Tier: 3
- HP 95 | Defense 3 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: PRIMALFURY

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Bleeding: Base 90% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Pounce** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Jump and deal damage to target unit. Bleeding: Base 90% chance of inflicting Bleeding for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Movement, Single Shot, Base, Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Fighter Unit**: An outright melee unit with no obvious specialization or counters.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Brawler
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
