# Sand Worm

- Tier: 4
- HP 130 | Defense 5 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 27 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Swallow Whole: Base 60% chance of Swallowing a non-hero unit. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Earth Tremor** (damage 30 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, full action): Enemy units in a 2-hex radius sustain damage.  Deals double damage to obstacles. Immobilized: Base 60% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Juggernaut**: This unit may crush non-Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Siege Breaker**: When part of a besieging army, this unit contributes +1 Fortification Damage.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit.  Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Cannot be Displaced**: This unit is immune to effects that would forcefully and physically move it.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.
