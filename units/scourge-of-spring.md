- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Physical 10 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Poisoned: Base 90% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Phantom Charge** (damage 25 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Deal damage in a 6-hex line. Decaying: Inflicts Decaying for 3 Turns. Weakened: Inflict Weakened for 3 Turns. Momentum: +20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Melee
- **Captive Glamor** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): - Target damaged unit has a chance of becoming Dominated until the end of combat.; Chance increases with the targets' missing health. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Fearless**: Unit cannot rout due to low morale.
- **Fey Guile**: At the end of its turn, if this unit is Obscured or in a Mist cloud, it gains a random Positive Status Effect.
