# Sealbearer

- Tier: 2
- HP 55 | Defense 0 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Source: Culture: Oathsworn / Harmony; Culture: Oathsworn / Righteousness; Culture: Oathsworn / Strife

## Abilities

- **Seal Bolts** (damage 12 Spirit, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Cannot miss against targets with Seal of Transference. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Transference Seal** (damage 30 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Deals damage and inflicts Seal of Transference, a link effect that lasts for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Base, Magic, Debuff, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Nobility**: Unit's non-flanking attacks deal extra damage based on the culture's Devotion: Paragon: +5 Spirit Damage; Devotee: +4 Spirit Damage; Adherent: +3 Spirit Damage; Neutral: +2 Spirit Damage; Oathbreaker: -20% damage. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 5: Champion Seals, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
