## Abilities

- **Lightning Blast** (damage 24 Lightning, accuracy 90%, range 5, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Phase** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6): Caster moves to target hex. Leaves 1 Action Point. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Movement, Teleport
- **Reveal Fears** (accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius have a base 90% chance of losing -40 Morale. Requires: Debuff
- **Reveal Weakness** (accuracy Always Hits, range 9, single-use action): Target enemy units gains: ; 5 Sundered Defense for 3 Turns.; 5 Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns; 5 Status Vulnerability for 3 Turns Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance
