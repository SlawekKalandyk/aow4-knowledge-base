# Shade

- Tier: 3
- HP 95 | Defense 4 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Source: Tome: Tome of Shades

Shade

## Abilities

- **Fatal Strike** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Single Melee Strike. Piercing: Ignore 50% of the target's Defense. Fatality: Damage increases based on target's lost Hit Points. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Throw Shadow Dagger** (damage 20 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Throws a projectile at target unit. Blind: Base 90% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Shadowed Escape**: When this unit's Hit Points drop below 30%: Inflict Blind on all adjacent enemy units. Teleport up to 5 hexes in a random direction.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.
- **Universal Camouflage**: This unit cannot be seen on the World Map.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
