# Shademaker

- Tier: 2
- HP 65 | Defense 1 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY
- Source: Culture: Architect / Architects of Astral; Culture: Architect / Architects of Chaos; Culture: Architect / Architects of Materium; Culture: Architect / Architects of Nature; Culture: Architect / Architects of Order; Culture: Architect / Architects of Shadow

## Abilities

- **Shoot Bow** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Giant Slayer: Deal +40% damage against creatures with Large Target. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Incarnate Shot** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Fires upon a 1-hex radius. Incarnate Shot: 90% chance of inflicting Weakened per Affinity Incarnate this unit has. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Missile
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Affinity Incarnate**: For each stack of Affinity Incarnate this unit gains damage based on their Empire's dominant Affinity: Order Affinity: +1 Spirit Damage; Chaos Affinity: +1 Fire Damage; Astral Affinity: +1 Lightning Damage; Materium Affinity: +1 Physical Damage; Nature Affinity: +1 Blight Damage; Shadow Affinity: +1 Frost Damage. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.
