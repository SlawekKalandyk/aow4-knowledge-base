- Tier: 3
- HP 80 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 5
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of Revelry
- Damage resistances: Fire resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4, Frost weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Heat of the Revel** (damage 18 Fire, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Insanity: Base 60% chance of inflicting Insanity for 1 Turn. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Song of Revelry** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): This unit and adjacent friendly units gain: ; 3 Rally.; 20 Temporary Hit Points Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Song of Carnage** (accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): This unit and adjacent friendly units gain: ; 2 Strengthened.; 2 Fortune; Hastened Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; +2 Defense.; +2 Resistance.; Immune to Flanking.; All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Unit type traits

- **Desolate Walk**: Moving through Ashlands costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Chasms costs 8 Move Points instead of 12.
- **Infernal Fiend**: A Fiend unit with: ; +2 Fire Protection; -4 Frost Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; +15% Critical Hit Chance; Desolate Walk; Lava Walk
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Lava Walk**: Allows the unit to traverse lava at a Movement Cost of 6.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
