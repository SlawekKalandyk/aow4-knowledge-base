## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 8 Physical 4 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Venom Shock: +20% damage against Poisoned units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Venomous Spit** (damage 7 Physical 7 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage to target unit. Poisoned: Base 90% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Missile, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Draconic Rage**: When this unit's total HP Hit Points reach 60% or lower, it deals +30% damage.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.
