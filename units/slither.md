# Slither

- Tier: 3
- HP 95 | Defense 4 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 9 Physical 6 Blight, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Venom Shock: +20% damage against Poisoned units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Venomous Spit** (damage 14 Blight 10 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage to target unit. Poisoned: Inflict Poisoned for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Missile, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Draconic Rage**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 60% or lower, it deals +30% damage.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Slip Away**: When this unit's Hit Points are reduced to zero, it is displaced by 3 hexes and heals +15 Temporary Hit Points. Works once per battle. Doesn't work when Embarked.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Dragon**: Dragon unit with: Draconic Rage
- **Forest Camouflage**: This unit cannot be detected when standing on Forest hexes.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
