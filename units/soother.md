# Soother

- Tier: 2
- HP 60 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 2
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Mystic / Attunement; Culture: Mystic / Potential; Culture: Mystic / Summoning

## Abilities

- **Cosmic Blast** (damage 5 Lightning 5 Fire 5 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Soothing Breeze** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Target friendly unit and another within 2 hexes heal +20 Temporary Hit Points. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Attunement: Star Blades**: When a friendly spell is cast, this unit's base attack gains +1 damage based on the affinity of the spell: Order Affinity - Spirit Damage; Chaos Affinity - Fire Damage; Astral Affinity - Lightning Damage; Materium Affinity - Physical Damage; Nature Affinity - Blight Damage; Shadow Affinity - Frost Damage. Stacks up to 3 times. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Spell Channeling**: While this unit is in battle, spells are 20% cheaper to cast. Doesn't stack.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Soothing Breeze, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
