# Spirit Hawk

- Tier: 1
- HP 50 | Defense 1 | Resistance 1 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Magic Strike** (damage 10 Spirit, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Farsight**: The unit has +1 vision range.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Magic Fighter Unit**: A melee attacker that uses Magic Strikes to target enemies with low Resistance. Magic Fighters have: Melee Mage.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Arcanist
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
