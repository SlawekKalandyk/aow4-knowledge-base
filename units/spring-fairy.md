# Spring Fairy

- Tier: 3
- HP 80 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 5
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Mana | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Spirit resistance 4, Blight weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Blight Blast** (damage 18 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack at target unit. Poisoned: Base 90% chance of inflicting Poisoned for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Budding Strength** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Friendly units in a 1-hex radius gain: 2 Strengthened; True Strike. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Warding**: Ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Resistance and +3 Status Resistance

## Passives

- **Fey Guile**: At the end of its turn, if this unit is Obscured or in a Mist cloud, it gains a random Positive Status Effect.
- **Feygrowth**: At the end of this unit's turn, gain Obscured until moved and create Obscuring flora in adjacent spaces. Does not work in water battles.

## Unit type traits

- **Fey**: A Fey unit with: +4 Spirit Protection; -4 Blight Protection; Fey Guile; The ability to ignore: Vision penalties from Misty. Accuracy penalties from Clinging Mist.
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Support Unit**: A defensive specialist that supports friendly units from afar and protects them from magical attacks. A Support unit has Defense Mode: Warding.
- **Tiny**: Unit is very small, making it 20% harder to hit by Missile and ranged Magic attacks.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Budding Strength, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
