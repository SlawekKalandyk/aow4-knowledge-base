# Storm Rune Crab

- Tier: 4
- HP 120 | Defense 8 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 6

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 7 Physical 7 Lightning, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Piercing: Ignore 50% of the target's Defense. Electrified: Base 90% chance of inflicting Electrified for 3 Turns. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Storming Defense** (damage 22 Lightning, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, single-use action): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius: Sustain damage. Gain 2 Electrified for 3 Turns. Become Wet for 3 Turns. Enter Defense Mode. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode: Shield Wall**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Defense.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Shield Defense**: This unit has +3 Defense against non- Flanking attacks.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Electrified**: Unit is immune to Electrified.
- **Storm Rune**: Adjacent friendly units gain: Static Charge. +2 Lightning Protection. +1 Resistance

## Unit type traits

- **Amphibious**: This unit: Can enter water hexes without movement penalties or becoming Embarked. Has faster movement over Rivers and Swamps.
- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Shield Unit**: Great at blocking enemies and protecting allies against physical attacks. A Shield unit gains: Shield Defense. Defense Mode: Shield Wall.
