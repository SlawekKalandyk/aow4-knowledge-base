# Storm Spirit

- Tier: 3
- HP 95 | Defense 4 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Shock resistance 6, Spirit resistance 3, Frost weakness 6

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 11 Physical 11 Lightning, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Shocking Phase** (damage 5 Lightning, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): This unit teleports to target hex and deals damage and creates Electrified terrain in a 1-hex radius around it. Electrified: Base 60% chance of inflicting Electrified for 3 Turns. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Starts on cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Magic, Movement, Teleport
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Electrified**: Unit is immune to Electrified.
- **Wind Barrier**: This unit is 60% harder to hit by Missile and ranged Magic attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Elemental**: An Elemental unit with immunity to Bleeding.
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.

- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Sprint
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
