# Sunderer

- Tier: 1
- HP 60 | Defense 2 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 80 Draft 60 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Culture: Barbarian

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Savage Strike: Deals an additional 4 Blight Damage on a critical hit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Javelin** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Throws a projectile at target unit. Sundered Defense: Inflict Sundered Defense for 3 Turns. Savage Strike: Deals an additional 8 Blight Damage on a critical hit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Savage Strike**: When an attack triggers a critical hit, it deals: +4 Blight Damage. +8 Blight Damage for Single Shot attacks.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
