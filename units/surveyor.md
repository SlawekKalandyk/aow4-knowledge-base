# Surveyor

- Tier: 1
- HP 45 | Defense 0 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 48 | Combat speed 6
- Cost: 60 Draft 50 Gold | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY
- Source: Culture: Architect / Architects of Astral; Culture: Architect / Architects of Chaos; Culture: Architect / Architects of Materium; Culture: Architect / Architects of Nature; Culture: Architect / Architects of Order; Culture: Architect / Architects of Shadow

## Abilities

- **Magic Bolts** (damage 10 Lightning, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Affinity Incarnate**: For each stack of Affinity Incarnate this unit gains damage based on their Empire's dominant Affinity: ; Order Affinity: +1 Spirit Damage; Chaos Affinity: +1 Fire Damage; Astral Affinity: +1 Lightning Damage; Materium Affinity: +1 Physical Damage; Nature Affinity: +1 Blight Damage; Shadow Affinity: +1 Frost Damage  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.
- **Farsight**: The unit has +1 vision range.
- **Survey Materials**: This unit can survey Provinces that contain Magic Materials. This rewards Wonderstone.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.
- **Scout Unit**: Though weak in battle, these swift units are useful for exploring the world. Scout units have Farsight and can move faster on the world map.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Sprint
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
