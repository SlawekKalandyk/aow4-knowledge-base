# Swamp Troll

- Tier: 4
- HP 105 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 6, Fire weakness 3

## Abilities

- **Blight Bolts** (damage 16 Blight, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Diseased: Base 60% chance of inflicting Diseased for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Plague Spores** (damage 24 Blight, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Deals damage to units in a 1-hex radius. Diseased: Base 90% chance of inflicting Diseased for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Troll Natural Regeneration**: - Heals 10% of their maximum Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points at the end of its turn in combat.; Heals 10 HP Hit Points at the start of your turn on the world map.; If this unit sustains Fire Damage, the regeneration stops for 1 Turn.
