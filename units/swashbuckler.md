# Swashbuckler

- Tier: 3
- HP 90 | Defense 4 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Source: Tome: Tome of Piracy

## Abilities

- **Dash Strike** (damage 14 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 3, repeating action): Dash to target enemy and deal damage. If caster is Immobilized, range is reduced. Rob: Base 60% chance to Rob the target. Ignores line of sight penalties. Requires: Melee, Base, Movement
- **Shoot Pistol** (damage 12 Physical 12 Fire, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Shoot at enemy unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base, Single Shot
- **Dust Disengage** (accuracy Always Hits, range 1, leave-one action): Jump 3 hexes back from target unit. Blind: Base 90% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn. Ember Dust: Inflicts Ember Dust. Rob: Base 90% chance to Rob the target. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Debuff, Movement
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Feint**: Ignore enemy Zone of Control. Can pass through enemy units.
- **Slippery**: This unit does not trigger Opportunity Attack.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Skirmisher Unit**: Cheap and swift units that are useful at Flanking and targeting lone enemies. Skirmisher units gain: Swift. Slippery.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Critical
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
