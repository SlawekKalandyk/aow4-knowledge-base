# Thorned Skeleton Militia

- Tier: 1
- HP 60 | Defense 2 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 35 Draft 10 Souls | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 12 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Polearm: +40% damage to Cavalry and Large Target units. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks.  Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **Expendable**: This unit's death does not affect their allies' Morale.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Razor Thorns**: Melee attackers suffer a stack of Bleeding for 3 Turns.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Bleeding**: Unit is immune to Bleeding.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Polearm Unit**: Specialists in taking out large threats and cavalry and can counter shock unit charges. Polearm Units have: ; First Strike; Charge Resistance; +40% damage against Cavalry and Large Target units.
- **Undead**: An undead unit with: ; +2 Frost Protection; +2 Blight Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Heartless; Immunity to: ; Poisoned; Diseased
