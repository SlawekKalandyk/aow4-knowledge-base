# Thorned Skeleton Warrior

- Tier: 1
- HP 55 | Defense 5 | Resistance 0 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 35 Draft 10 Souls | Upkeep: 8 Gold
- DLC: THRONESOFBLOOD
- Damage resistances: Blight resistance 2, Frost resistance 2, Fire weakness 4, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 10 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Defense Mode: Shield Wall**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. +2 Defense. +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +3 Defense.

## Passives

- **Expendable**: This unit's death does not affect their allies' Morale.
- **Heartless**: The unit has a -50% modifier to Morale changes
- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Razor Thorns**: Melee attackers suffer a stack of Bleeding for 3 Turns.
- **Shield Defense**: This unit has +3 Defense against non- Flanking attacks.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Bleeding**: Unit is immune to Bleeding.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Shield Unit**: Great at blocking enemies and protecting allies against physical attacks. A Shield unit gains: Shield Defense. Defense Mode: Shield Wall.
- **Undead**: An undead unit with: +2 Frost Protection; +2 Blight Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; -4 Fire Protection; Heartless; Immunity to: Poisoned; Diseased

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Resurgence, +10 HP, Champion Medal: Exalted Defense
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
