# Trainer Ogre

- Tier: 4
- HP 130 | Defense 5 | Resistance 5 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: GIANTKINGS

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 18 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Expert Advice** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Target friendly unit gains 5 random Positive Status Effects. Cooldown: 0 Turns Requires: Support
- **Get Back in the Fight** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, full action): Target friendly unit regains full health as Temporary Hit Points. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Spur to Action** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Target friendly unit regains all its Action Points. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Defensive Masters**: Unit always enters Defense Mode at the end of its Turn.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Expert Trainer**: Other units in the army gain 50% extra XP Experience from combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: ; Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks.; Has Demolisher.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
