# Transmuter

- Tier: 4
- HP 95 | Defense 2 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of Transmutation

A Battle Mage that turns enemies to gold and can steal their armor and grant it to friendly units.

## Abilities

- **Molten Bolts** (damage 7 Physical 7 Fire, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Gilded: Base 30% chance of inflicting Gilded for 1 Turn. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Transmute** (damage 12 Physical 12 Fire, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Enemy units in a 1-hex radius: ; Sustain damage.; Gain Sundered Defense for 3 Turns.; Gain Sundered Resistance for 3 Turns.  If successful, grant Bolstered Defense and Bolstered Resistance for 3 Turns to all friendly units within 2-hexes of the caster. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns Requires: Magic, Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. ; Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight.; Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
