# Umbral Tyrant

- Tier: 5
- HP 150 | Defense 6 | Resistance 6 | Status resistance 11
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 400 Draft 300 Gold | Upkeep: 60 Gold 7 Imperium
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Fire weakness 2, Spirit weakness 2

## Abilities

- **Umbral Oppression** (damage 11 Blight 11 Spirit 11 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, single-use action): Single magical attack on target unit. Deals +10% damage per friendly unit with Tyrant's Mark. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Base, Magic, Single Shot
- **Tyrant's Mark** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, free action): Grant Tyrant's Mark to target friendly unit until the end of combat. Cooldown: 0 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Tyrant's Assault** (damage 15 Blight 15 Spirit 15 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 8, full action): Target a friendly unit with Tyrant's Mark: Adjacent enemies sustain damage. Spread Umbral Taint around it. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Curse Eater**: At the start of each turn: Unit loses one random Negative Status Effect and heals 10 Temporary Hit Points from it. Disabled if the unit is Burning.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Tyrant's Oversight**: At the start of each turn, all friendly units with Tyrant's Mark: Gain Strengthened for 3 Turns. Heal 10 Temporary Hit Points.
- **Tyrant's Retinue**: At the start of battle, summon 2 Splitterlings.
- **Umbral Malady Immunity**: This unit is immune to Umbral Malady.

## Unit type traits

- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
- **Umbral Demon**: An Umbral Demon unit with: -2 Fire Protection; -2 Spirit Protection; Curse Eater; Immunity to Umbral Malady
