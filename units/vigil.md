# Vigil

- Tier: 3
- HP 75 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: ARCHONPROPHECY
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 2, Blight weakness 4, Frost weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Radiant Bolts** (damage 7 Fire 7 Spirit, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Blind: Base 60% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Purifying Blast** (damage 20 Fire 20 Spirit, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Target unit: Sustains Damage. Has all Positive Status Effects removed. Units adjacent to target: Sustain 50% damage. Have 2 Positive Status Effects removed. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Inspiring Killer**: This unit grants double the morale bonus to itself and its allies when killing an enemy.
- **Zeal**: Attacks gain +2 Spirit Damage. Deal +10% damage against Condemned units. Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Celestial**: Celestial unit with: +2 Spirit Protection; -4 Blight Protection. -4 Frost Protection; Inspiring Killer; Control Loss Immunity
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
