# Warbound

- Tier: 3
- HP 100 | Defense 4 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: WAYSOFWAR
- Source: Culture: Oathsworn / Strife

## Abilities

- **Flaming Charge Strike** (damage 24 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: ; Gains +5 Fire Damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes.; Cancels Defense Mode.; Removes Retaliation Attack. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Bulldoze** (damage 10 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 1, leave-one action): Move 4 hexes forward. Enemies in the way sustain damage. Cooldown: 2 Turns Requires: Melee, Movement
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Paragon of Strife**: At Paragon level Devotion, gain the Infernal Blaze ability.
- **Warrior's Soul**: At the start of the unit’s turn, if friendly units do not outnumber enemy units, the unit gains Warrior's Fury until the end of combat.  Warrior's Fury increases damage of attacks, based on the culture's Devotion: ; Paragon: +5 Fire Damage; Devotee: +4 Fire Damage; Adherent: +3 Fire Damage; Neutral: +2 Fire Damage; Oathbreaker: Warrior's Fury is disabled, attacks do -20% damage.  Effects are increased for Single Shot attacks.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Shock Unit**: An offensive unit specialized in disrupting the enemy line. A Shock Unit has a Charge attack, which counters Shield Units, but can be countered by Polearm Units.

- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Sprint
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
