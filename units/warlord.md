# Warlord

- Tier: 4
- HP 140 | Defense 5 | Resistance 6 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: RISEFROMRUIN
- Source: Tome: Tome of the Warlord

## Abilities

- **Charge Strike** (damage 33 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): A Charge attack: Gains 20% damage per hex traveled, up to 3 hexes. Cancels Defense Mode. Removes Retaliation Attack. War Veteran: +20% Critical Hit chance. Requires: Melee, Base, Single Shot
- **Command: Attack** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Target friendly Tier I, Tier II, or Tier III unit gains: 5 Strengthened for 3 Turns. 3 Fortune for 3 Turns. All Command abilities on this unit go into cooldown. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Command: Defend** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Target friendly Tier I, Tier II, or Tier III unit gains: +1 Retaliation Attack for 1 Turn; 5 Bolstered Defense for 3 Turns. 5 Bolstered Resistance for 3 Turns. All Command abilities on this unit go into cooldown. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Command: Retreat** (accuracy Always Hits, range 3, free action): Target friendly Tier I, Tier II, or Tier III unit gains: Heals 40 Temporary Hit Points; Gains Very Fast Movement for 1 Turn. Gains Slippery for 1 Turn. All Command abilities on this unit go into cooldown. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Support
- **Defense Mode: Hold your Ground**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Immune to Flanking. All adjacent friendly units gain +1 Defense. All adjacent friendly units gain +1 Resistance. All adjacent friendly units gain Charge Resistance.

## Passives

- **Charge Resistance**: Unaffected by the bonus effects of Charge attacks. Can be counteracted by using Heavy Charge Strike.
- **Command Momentum**: On unit kill, reset the cooldowns of all Command abilities.
- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Field Tactics**: Tier I, Tier II, and Tier III units in the army have: +1 Defense; +1 Resistance. Does not stack.
- **First Strike**: When attacked, a unit with First Strike will use a Retaliation Attack to attack first.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.
