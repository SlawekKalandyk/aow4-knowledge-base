# Watcher

- Tier: 3
- HP 75 | Defense 1 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Spirit weakness 4

## Abilities

- **Lightning Bolts** (damage 14 Lightning, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Psychic Gaze** (damage 40 Lightning, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Target enemy unit sustains damage. Stunned: Base 60% chance of inflicting Stunned for 1 Turn. Marked: Inflict 2 Marked for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Base, Debuff, Magic, Single Shot
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Demolisher**: This unit's attacks damage Fortified obstacles.
- **Farsight**: The unit has +1 vision range.
- **Pass Through**: This unit can move through obstacles. Does not function when the unit is in a transport.
- **Scrying Eye**: Unit is immune to Flanking and is immune to becoming Distracted.
- **Truesight**: Unit can see invisible or camouflaged units within its vision range.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Ethereal**: An Ethereal unit with: +2 Physical Protection; -4 Spirit Protection; Pass Through; Immunity to: Bleeding; Diseased; Immobilized; Poisoned
- **Floating**: This unit is Floating: Moves through any type of terrain at a movement cost of 6, apart from Mountains, which cost 8. Requires Seafaring to traverse the water.
- **Large Target**: A large unit that: Is 20% easier to hit with Missile and Magic attacks. Has Demolisher.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.

- Rank 5: Champion Psychic Gaze, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
