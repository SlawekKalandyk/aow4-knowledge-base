# Wavemaster

- Tier: 4
- HP 110 | Defense 4 | Resistance 4 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: SILVERSEAS
- Source: Tome: Pirate Bond

## Abilities

- **Tidal Strike** (damage 25 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Attacks enemy units in a 1-hex cone. Pushes units 1 hex back. Inflicts Wet. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Vortex Pull** (damage 15 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 0, leave-one action): Damage enemy units in a 2-hex radius around the unit. Cannot be resisted during Water combat. Immobilized: Base 90% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Pull: Base 90% chance of pulling enemy units to caster. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Guiding Currents**: In water battles, units in this army gain +1 Combat Speed. This effect does not stack.
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Fighter Unit**: A melee attacker that uses Magic Strikes to target enemies with low Resistance. Magic Fighters have: Melee Mage.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Arcanist
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
