# Weaver Spider Matriarch

- Tier: 4
- HP 105 | Defense 3 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 7
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 300 Draft 200 Gold | Upkeep: 30 Gold 3 Imperium
- DLC: SECRETSOFTHEARCHMAGES

## Abilities

- **Shoot Web** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Immobilized: Base 60% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Weaver's Retreat** (damage 10 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 4, leave-one action): Adjacent units sustain damage. Then teleport to target hex. Immobilized: Base 90% chance of inflicting Immobilized for 1 Turn. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Movement, Melee, Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Low Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep reduced by -25%
- **Status Effect Immunity: Immobilized**: Unit is immune to Immobilized.
- **Status Effect Immunity: Slowed**: Unit is immune to Slowed.
- **Swift**: This unit ignores the effects of Slowing terrain in combat.

## Unit type traits

- **Animal**: An Animal unit. Animals that belong to Players and Free Cities gain Combat Trained, making them more effective in battle.
- **Cave Walk**: Moving through Cavern Floors costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Fungus Fields costs 5 Move Points instead of 6. Moving through Mushroom Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Forest Walk**: Moving through Forests costs 6 Move Points instead of 8.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.
- **Spider**: A spider unit with: Swift; Forest Walk; Cave Walk; Immunity to: Slowed; Immobilized
