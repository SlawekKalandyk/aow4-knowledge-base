# White Witch

- Tier: 2
- HP 55 | Defense 0 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of Cryomancy
- Damage resistances: Frost resistance 4

Grants access to a Battle Mage that inflicts Chilled and Frozen.

## Abilities

- **Frost Bolts** (damage 12 Frost, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Unleashes a magical bolt at target enemy. Chilled: Base 60% chance of inflicting Chilled for 3 Turns. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Freezing Blast** (damage 20 Frost, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Target unit: Sustains damage. Has a base 60% chance of becoming Frozen for 1 Turn. If unsuccessful, inflict Chilled. Adjacent enemy units: Sustain 50% of the damage. Become Chilled for 3 Turns. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Debuff, Magic
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Unit type traits

- **Battle Mage Unit**: Magic users who specialize in ranged offense and weakening enemies. Their Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot use their main Magic attacks when Engaged in Melee.
- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Optional Cavalry**: This unit will become Cavalry if the race has a special mount trait.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: Champion Freezing Blast, +10 HP
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
