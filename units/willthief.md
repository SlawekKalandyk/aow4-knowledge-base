# Willthief

- Tier: 2
- HP 70 | Defense 2 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 0
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 4
- Cost: 120 Draft 100 Gold | Upkeep: 12 Gold
- DLC: ELDRITCHREALMS
- Damage resistances: Physical resistance 2, Fire weakness 2, Spirit weakness 2

## Abilities

- **Magic Strike** (damage 13 Frost, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Requires: Magic, Base
- **Umbral Lash** (accuracy Always Hits, range 4, single-use action): Link to target enemy unit. They become inflicted with Umbral Drain.  Only one unit can be linked at a time. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 3 Turns Requires: Debuff
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: ; Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes.; Gains +2 Defense.; Gains +2 Resistance.; Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Curse Eater**: At the start of each turn: ; Unit loses one random Negative Status Effect and heals 10 Temporary Hit Points from it.  Disabled if the unit is Burning.
- **Melee Mage**: Unit can use Magic and Support abilities while Engaged in Melee.
- **Umbral Malady Immunity**: This unit is immune to Umbral Malady.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Magic Fighter Unit**: A melee attacker that uses Magic Strikes to target enemies with low Resistance. Magic Fighters have: ; Melee Mage.
- **Magic Origin**: This unit was summoned or comes from another dimension and requires mana upkeep.
- **Umbral Demon**: An Umbral Demon unit with: ; -2 Fire Protection; -2 Spirit Protection; Curse Eater; Immunity to Umbral Malady
