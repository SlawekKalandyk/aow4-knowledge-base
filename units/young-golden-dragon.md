# Young Golden Dragon

- Tier: 3
- HP 135 | Defense 5 | Resistance 3 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 240 Draft 160 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: DRAGONLORDS
- Damage resistances: Spirit resistance 4, Blight weakness 2
- Evolves into: [Golden Dragon](../units/golden-dragon.md)

## Abilities

- **Melee Strike** (damage 14 Physical 6 Spirit, accuracy 100%, range 1, repeating action): Strikes target enemy unit. Blind: Base 30% chance of inflicting Blind for 1 Turn. Requires: Melee, Base
- **Tail Swipe** (damage 29 Physical, accuracy 100%, range 1, single-use action): Attacks 3 adjacent units and removes their Defense Mode and Retaliation Attack. Enter Defense Mode after use. Cooldown: 1 Turns. Requires: Melee
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Passives

- **Control Loss Immunity**: Immune to effects that make you lose control of a unit, such as Berserk and Dominated. Also grants immunity to being Captured.
- **Draconic Rage**: When this unit's total Hit Points reach 60% or lower, it deals +30% damage.
- **High Maintenance**: Base Unit Upkeep increased by 50%
- **Intimidating Aura**: At the end of this unit's turn, adjacent enemy units have a base 90% chance of losing -5 Morale. Doesn't work on units with this ability or Terrifying Aura.
- **Natural Regeneration**: Heals 10% of its maximum Hit Points as Temporary Hit Points at the end of its turn in combat, though not more than the unit started combat with. Heals 10 Hit Points at the start of your turn on the world map.
- **Truesight**: Unit can see invisible or camouflaged units within its vision range.

## Unit type traits

- **Dragon**: Dragon unit with: Draconic Rage
- **Evolve**: This unit will transform into a more powerful unit when it reaches Legendary rank. Evolving units can not be recruited at Legendary rank.
- **Flying**: This unit is Flying: It has +2 Vision Range; Can move in any terrain at a movement cost of 6; Requires Seafaring to traverse Water hexes; Can safely move past most other units and obstacles in combat.
- **Mythic Unit**: A powerful unit that doesn't conform to a particular fighting style. All mythic units have Control Loss Immunity.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Killing Momentum
