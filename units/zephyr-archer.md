# Zephyr Archer

- Tier: 3
- HP 85 | Defense 2 | Resistance 2 | Status resistance 3
- Movement 40 | Combat speed 5
- Cost: 220 Draft 140 Gold | Upkeep: 20 Gold
- DLC: Base
- Source: Tome: Tome of Winds

## Abilities

- **Shoot Bow** (damage 16 Physical, accuracy 90%, range 4, repeating action): Fires ranged projectiles at target enemy unit. Affected by Line of Sight rules. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Requires: Missile, Base
- **Zephyr Shot** (damage 26 Physical, accuracy Always Hits, range 6, full action): Deals damage to target unit. Adjacent enemy units sustain 50% of the damage. Cannot be used when within an enemy Zone of Control. Cooldown: 2 Turns. Requires: Missile
- **Defense Mode**: This unit ends its turn and goes into a defensive mode. The unit: Extends its Zone of Control to all adjacent hexes. Gains +2 Defense. Gains +2 Resistance. Becomes immune to Flanking.

## Unit type traits

- **Land Movement**: This unit can move over land hexes.
- **Ranged Unit**: An attack unit that takes out targets from afar. It is somewhat vulnerable. Its Accuracy is affected by Line of Sight. Cannot fire its main ranged attack when Engaged in Melee.

## Rank-up rewards

- Rank 2: +10 HP
- Rank 3: +10 HP
- Rank 4: +10 HP, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
- Rank 5: +10 HP, Champion Medal: Eagle Eye
- Rank 6: +10 HP, +10% damage, +1 Defense, +1 Resistance
