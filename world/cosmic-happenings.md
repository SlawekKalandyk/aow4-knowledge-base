# Cosmic happenings

## Dawn Of The Lodestar (Order)

(Major Major, 12 turns)

Global Effects:
- Celestial units and units with Faithful or Zeal gain Angelic Potential, granting: +2 Status Resistance and +20 Morale.
- Free Cities spawn armies to attack their targets.
- For the rest of the game, all units have -2 Spirit Resistance.

## Drifting Ley Lines (Astral)

(Major Major, 12 turns)

Global Effects:
- Magic Origin units gain Magic Origin Potential, granting: Attacks inflict Sundered Resistance and gain +5 [Combat] Combat Casting Points at the start of combat.
- Combat spells are always Overcharged the first time they are cast each combat.
- For the rest of the game, all units have -2 Lightning Resistance.

## Eye Of Fire (Chaos)

(Major Major, 12 turns)

Global Effects:
- Fiend and Demonkin units gain Demonic Potential, granting: Base attacks have a 30% chance to inflict a random negative status effect, this effect is doubled for non-repeating attacks.
- Free Cities lose -10 Relations per Turn and are able to declare war on their overlords when at Vassalage or lower.
- For the rest of the game, all units have -2 Fire Resistance.

## Toll Of Seasons

(Major Toll of Seasons, -1 turns)

Mysterious Fey folk appear from the mists to attack the cities of the unwary.

## Astral Eclipse

(Minor Constellation, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Mana income is reduced by -30%.
- World Map Spells cost +50% [World Map] World Map Casting Points.

## Blightfall Conjunction

(Minor Planet Phase, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Cities lose -5 Food per Population.
- Blightfall is active in combat: Each turn in combat a random unit takes 15 Blight Damage and becomes inflicted with Diseased.

## Colossus Moon

(Minor Planet Phase, 8 turns)

Global Effects:
- Provinces turn into Swamps around the world.
- Thunder Storms is active in combat: Each turn in combat all units become Wet and 1 random unit takes 20 Lightning Damage.

## Cosmic Winds

(Minor Weather, 8 turns)

Global Effects:
- Provinces turn into Deserts around the world.
- Blinding Sands is active in combat: Each turn in combat, each unit has a 60% chance of becoming Blind for 1 Turn.

## Invading Sylviculture

(Minor Asteroid, 8 turns)

Global Effects:
- A World Tree seedling causes forests to spread into adjacent provinces.
- Grasping Vines is active in combat: Every 2 turns in combat, all units have a 50% chance of becoming Immobilized for 1 Turn.
- Units on Forest provinces suffer from -5 HP Hit Point regeneration per Turn and -2 Vision Range.

## Melenis's Kiss

(Minor Entity, 8 turns)

Global Effects:
- All units have a 30% chance of turning slain non-Undead units into Zombies under your control which stay after combat. These Zombies cannot regenerate HP Hit Points.

## Phase-shift Nixing

(Minor Entity, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Teleporter province improvements, Teleportation spells, and Teleportation abilities are disabled. Phasing units cannot use teleporting abilities.

## Portent of Rousing

(Minor Asteroid, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- All units gain Portent of Rousing, granting: Unit Upkeep is reduced by 50%.
- Cities gain +100% Draft income.
- At the end of the happening, gain 20 Gold for each unit in your empire.

## Ravenous Visitants

(Minor Entity, 8 turns)

Global Effects:
- Armies of hostile astral creatures appear across the world.
- Ethereal units gain Attunement: Enhancement, granting: Whenever a spell is cast, this unit gains a random positive status effect.

## Rays Of Winter

(Minor Asteroid, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Provinces turn into Arctic around the world.
- Arctic Blizzards is active in combat: Every turn in combat, all units take 8 Frost Damage damage and all Wet units have a 90% chance of becoming Frozen.
- Units on Arctic provinces suffer from -5 HP Hit Point regeneration per Turn and -2 Vision Range.

## Sign Of The Huntress

(Minor Constellation, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Ranged, Battle Mage, and Support units gain Sign of the Huntress, granting: +30% damage and -1 range.

## Sign Of The Lumbering Staff

(Minor Constellation, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Battle Mage and Support units gain Sign of the Lumbering Staff, granting: +1 range and Slow Movement in combat.

## Sign Of The Vanguard

(Minor Constellation, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Shock units gain Sign of the Vanguard, granting:
- Very Fast Movement in combat.
- Magic and Physical Ranged attacks against this unit have -20% Accuracy.
- Melee attacks deal -20% damage.

## Sign of the Cleft Shield

(Minor Constellation, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Shield units gain Sign of the Cleft Shield, granting: +40% damage and -4 Defense.

## Starry-eyed Enlightenment

(Minor Constellation, 5 turns)

Global Effects:
- Cities gain +100% Knowledge income.
- Declaring wars is always Unjust.
- Ending wars grants 200 Imperium.

## Stirring Stone Conjunction

(Minor Planet Phase, 8 turns)

Global Effects:
- Cities gain +100% Production income.
- All units have Siege Breaker.

## Sunstruck Eruptions

(Minor Entity, 8 turns)

Global Effects:
- Provinces turn into Desolate around the world.
-
Volcanic Eruptions is active in combat:
- Each turn in combat, 1-3 markers are placed. Then, after 1 Turn the ground will erupt and units within 1 range of the marker will suffer 10 Physical Damage and 10 Fire Damage.
- Random affected hexes will become On Fire.
