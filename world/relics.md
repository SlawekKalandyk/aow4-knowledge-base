## Lithorine Prism

DLC: GIANTKINGS

- Your City gain:

- +20 City Stability if you have an Order Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Food if you have a Nature Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Gold if you have a Materium Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Draft if you have a Chaos Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Knowledge if you have a Shadow Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Mana if you have an Astral Affinity Tome in your library.
- For dual affinity Tome these bonuses are halved.
