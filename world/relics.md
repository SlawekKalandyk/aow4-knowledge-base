## Compass of Bounteous Coasts

DLC: SILVERSEAS

- Rewards from Pickups on the water are increased by +100%.
- Every 4-6 Turns, a new Pickup is revealed in a water province close to your Domain.

## Conch of Fair Winds

DLC: SILVERSEAS | Spells: [Gust of the Seven Seas](../spells/gust-of-the-seven-seas.md)

## Essence Sheath

DLC: RISEFROMRUIN | Spells: [Mend Fracture](../spells/mend-fracture.md)

- Unlocks the spell Mend Fracture.
- Restoring a Ruined Node is 20% cheaper. Scales with the amount of Artifacts of the Unmakers owned by the player.
- Receive additional +3 Gold and +3 Production in Astral Barrens and Sand.

## Flowrock Cornucopia

DLC: GIANTKINGS

- Unlocks the production of the Giant Forum Special Province Improvement in your Cities.
- The Giant Forum receives the following buffs:

- +10 Production income.
- +5 Food per adjacent Farm or Forester.
- Counts as a Farm and Forester in addition to its other types.

## Free Captain's Demonic Contract

DLC: SILVERSEAS

At the start of water combat, a random pirate unit with Demonkin joins you in battle.

## Head of Ceaseless Laments

DLC: SILVERSEAS | Units: [Accursed Wisp](../units/accursed-wisp.md), [Accursed Lamenter](../units/accursed-lamenter.md)

- Unlocks recruitment of the Accursed Wisp and Accursed Lamenter in all of your Cities.
- Your Ruler and Heroes gain Damned Body, Damned Conduit, and Dirge of the Damned.

## Heart of the Maer

DLC: SILVERSEAS | Spells: [Nevermoor's Curse](../spells/nevermoors-curse.md) | Units: [Ghost Ship](../units/ghost-ship.md)

- Unlocks the Nevermoor's Curse [World Map] Major Race Transformation.
- Unlocks the recruitment of Ghost Ships in all your cities.
- Yields +20 Souls each Turn.

## Hoarfrozen Paw

DLC: SILVERSEAS

- You have +50 World Map Casting Points and +50 Combat Casting Points.
- Whenever you cast a strategic spell, the Province of your Ruler is affected by the following:

- Alter land provinces to Snow and Ice.

- Loses Chasm, Swamp, Ashlands, Sand, Cavern Floor, and Gloom.

- All Units in the Province gain Whiteout for 2 Turns.

## Horn of Mercy

## Jade of Prosperous Reign

DLC: SILVERSEAS

- Trade Routes between your Cities and Outposts grant +3 Imperiums.
- When obtaining this Artifact for the first time, you unlock 2 random high Tier Empire Skills.

## Lithorine Globe

DLC: GIANTKINGS

- Unlocks Eternal Studies in your Cities, which converts the city's Production into Knowledge.

## Lithorine Prism

DLC: GIANTKINGS

- Your Cities gain:

- +20 City Stability if you have an Order Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Food if you have a Nature Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Gold if you have a Materium Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Draft if you have a Chaos Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Knowledge if you have a Shadow Affinity Tome in your library.
- +10 Manas if you have an Astral Affinity Tome in your library.
- For dual affinity Tomes these bonuses are halved.

## Lithorine Scroll

DLC: GIANTKINGS

- +1 Order Affinity
- +1 Nature Affinity
- +1 Materium Affinity
- +1 Chaos Affinity
- +1 Shadow Affinity
- +1 Astral Affinity

## Soulcursed Cauldron

DLC: GIANTKINGS | Spells: [Soulcurse of Gluttony](../spells/soulcurse-of-gluttony.md)

- Unlocks the Soulcurse of Gluttony spell.
- Whenever you cast a soulcurse spell there is a 25% chance of your ruler gaining some Experience.

- The amount of Experience gained this way scales with the tier of the cast soulcurse spell.
- When multiple of these extra soulcurse casting effects could trigger, their triggering chance is summed up, but a maximum of 1 effects triggers.
